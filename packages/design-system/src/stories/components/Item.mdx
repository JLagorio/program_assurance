# Item

**Status: Stable.**

A compact record row with aligned identifiers, descriptions, dates and independent actions. Item retains Ledger's record-list layout; Base UI owns link composition and disclosure behavior.

## Anatomy

Item.Group renders an ordered list inside a div. Rows share a six-column grid: disclosure, leading mark, record id, title/body, trailing value and actions. Expanded content starts under the title. A group can supply its heading, count (capped at `countMax`, Count's `max`: 99 unsaid, `9999` for a count of rows), trailing action and empty message.

## Variants and states

Use `link` to navigate, `onSelect` for an action, or neither for a plain row. `isActive` marks the row open beside the list: a selected fill (in forced colours, which remove the fill, a Highlight outline inside the row's edge), and `aria-current="true"` on its link or button. A link takes precedence over onSelect. Secondary actions and disclosure buttons remain outside the title link/button.

A row that opens a record is a link, never an `onSelect` that changes the address: a button cannot be opened in a new tab or copied.

<Canvas of={Stories.DoDont} />

The title is one line by default. A title, an id or a meta that is cut shows in full in a tooltip on hover, and a cut title also when its row's link or button takes keyboard focus. `maxTitleLines` gives a list of long names two or three lines before the ellipsis. Beside a meta, the title gives way first: a short meta ("CAT I") keeps its words, and a long one is cut only past half the line, so it cannot crowd the title out. A group heading wraps rather than cutting.

`children` display below the row; add `isCollapsible` to fold them. Use `defaultOpen` or controlled `open` with Base UI's `onOpenChange(open, details)`. Call `details.cancel()` to reject a change.

Group `size="compact"` reduces vertical padding. `flush` extends separators and hover backgrounds to the card edges, and draws a row's focus ring inside its edge, so the card's clipping cannot cut its sides. `idWidth` defaults to 72px (`dimension.part.itemId`); align it across related rows.

A group shows its `empty` when its children render nothing: empty arrays, `false` and empty fragments count as nothing, so a list fed from several mapped sources that are all empty shows its empty state.

## Accessibility

The title is a real anchor or button. Enter follows links; Space activates buttons. Disclosure buttons announce expanded state and reference the linked title's id. The chevron is the kit's 20px row control (`IconButton size="xxsmall"`), named by the title and with no tooltip of its own; it sits above the title's stretched link, so a click or a tap on it opens the row and never follows the link, and where any pointer is coarse its hit area is 24px. Actions are independent tab stops. Plain rows stay out of the tab order unless the caller explicitly supplies tabIndex for programmatic focus.

A group title names the list. It is a heading at the contextual level: an h3 when nothing sets one, one below a titled Section, an h2 in a rail that starts its outline at 2 with `HeadingLevelProvider level={2}`. When a surrounding card or section already draws the heading, point `aria-labelledby` at it; with no visible heading, `aria-label` names the list. Both reach the `ol`, not the wrapper, and the group's own `title` wins over them. `labelledBy` is the deprecated name of `aria-labelledby`, kept for one version. A compact `empty` message is body text, not a heading.

Keep link titles descriptive; meta and trailing values are separate from the link's accessible name. The id, the meta and the description are the link's or button's accessible description, in that order, so rows that share a name ("personnel or roles") read apart: "personnel or roles, button, ac-01_odp.01 Assignment". The trailing value is not.

## Props

<ArgTypes of={Item} />
<ArgTypes of={ItemGroup} />

Item forwards native li attributes, styles, events and refs. Its existing `id`, `title` and `onSelect` props keep their record-content/action meanings. Item.Group forwards native div attributes and refs to the outer wrapper, except `aria-label` and `aria-labelledby`, which name the list; its `title` remains the visible group heading. Each part names itself last with `data-slot` (`item`, `item-group`). Styles merge with the standalone row grid.

`link={<Link to="/record" />}` uses Base UI useRender to preserve the supplied link's attributes, ref, handlers and id. Leave the link empty to use Item's configured title. Explicit link children follow Base UI's rendered-element precedence. No router dependency is added.
