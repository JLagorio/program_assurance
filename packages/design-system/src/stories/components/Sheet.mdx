# Sheet

**Status: Stable.**

A modal panel at an edge of the window: a selection, a review, or a task that must hold focus beside the page's context. Built on [Base UI Dialog](https://base-ui.com/react/components/dialog) with [shadcn’s Sheet composition](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/base/sheet) and Ledger's tokens.

<Canvas of={Stories.EdgesAndScrolling} />

## When to use it

- **A modal selection or review** that needs the height of the window, such as choosing many records ([PickerSheet](?path=/docs/patterns-pickersheet--docs)) or reviewing one ([PreviewSheet](?path=/docs/patterns-previewsheet--docs)).
- **Filters or a short form** that should sit at the edge while the reader keeps the page in mind.

Not for work beside a list the reader keeps using: that is the Shell's panel, which blocks nothing. Not for a create or edit form: that is a [Dialog](?path=/docs/components-dialog--docs). Not for a phone's bottom sheet with a swipe: that is a [Drawer](?path=/docs/components-drawer--docs).

## Anatomy

- **SheetContent** brings its portal, the blanket and a close button at the top end (an IconButton named Close, with no tooltip, first in the Tab order where it is drawn, while the first field still takes focus on open), and slides from `side`: `right` by default, `left`, `top` or `bottom`, or `start` and `end`, which follow the reading direction. It records the overlay surface as the current one, so a sticky header or pinned cell inside matches the sheet.
- **SheetHeader** holds the title (the sheet's h2, a [Heading](?path=/docs/primitives-heading--docs) at `overlay`, 15/22 medium; headings inside start at h3) and the description, which wrap an unbroken value. A record's name as the title is a PageHeader.Title rendered as SheetTitle, and keeps the page title's size.
- **SheetBody** is the one scrolling region, with the sheet's inset. A toolbar row that should not scroll sits between the header and the body.
- **SheetFooter** stays outside the scroll, on the sheet's own surface below a hairline. The header, the body and the footer share the sheet's `space.200` inline inset.
- **SheetPortal** and **SheetOverlay** are exported for a custom popup, as Dialog's are.

## Widths

`width` on SheetContent picks a step for a sheet at the start or end edge; do not set pixels in `style`.

| `width`      | Size               | For                                |
| ------------ | ------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| `small`      | 320px              | Filters, a short form              |
| `medium`     | 420px, the default | A form, a record's details         |
| `large`      | 760px              | A table of about four columns      |
| `xlarge`     | 960px              | A table beside a preview           |
| `fullscreen` | The window         | A task that needs the whole window |

Each step is capped by the window. `large` and `xlarge` match Dialog's.

## Scrolling and short windows

The body takes the height the header, any toolbar row and the footer leave, at least 80px, and scrolls. Read-only content that overflows makes it a tab stop, "Content, scrolls". In a window under 30rem tall the sheet scrolls as one: the header and any toolbar scroll away with the body, and the footer stays at the bottom, so a list is never squeezed to a single row. The footer keeps its height as the sheet's scroll padding, so a control that takes focus scrolls clear of it.

## Pending

`pending` on the Sheet holds it while a command runs: Escape, the blanket, the close button and every SheetClose are cancelled or disabled, the close button and a SheetClose rendered as a kit Button stay focusable while they are (`aria-disabled`), and the popup is `aria-busy`. Close the sheet with `open={false}` when the command succeeds.

## Accessibility

- **Give the sheet a title.** Base UI traps focus, handles Escape and nested dialogs, and returns focus to the opener, or to `finalFocus` when the opener went away. A Sheet opened from state records the element that had focus as it opens, so focus comes back to it even when a field took focus as it mounted. Move focus to a field with `initialFocus`, never `autoFocus`.
- **Keep a close control available**; the content includes one by default.
- **The one stack.** An AlertDialog for the decision the sheet owns renders inside SheetContent; the sheet dims under it and the page keeps one blanket.
- **Forced colours keep the edge**: the popup draws a CanvasText outline, and the blanket stays a veil of Canvas.

## Content

- The title names what the sheet holds: the record under review ("CTRL-0412 Segregation of duties"), or the selection and what it is for ("Add controls to Flight computer"). A selection's title, its trigger and its primary say the same words.
- The primary says the operation and its object ("Add 12 controls", "Apply filters"), never OK, Submit or Done alone; the secondary is Cancel, or Close for a sheet whose choices apply as they are made.
- The description says what the sheet acts on or what it leaves out ("Controls already in the baseline are not listed"), never an instruction the reader must keep in mind while the sheet is open.
- A title that names a record may hold its code, and wraps rather than running past the edge. A failure is said in the body, through an Alert, never in the title.

## Props

Sheet is Base UI Dialog at an edge, so it takes the same root props, `open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange` (with the change's `reason` and `cancel()` in its details), `onOpenChangeComplete`, `modal`, `disablePointerDismissal`, `actionsRef` and `handle`, and Ledger's `pending`. SheetContent takes the popup's `initialFocus`, `finalFocus`, `render`, `className` and `style`, and Ledger's `side`, `width` and `showCloseButton`. The types are in [Base UI's API reference](https://base-ui.com/react/components/dialog#api-reference).
