# Avatar

**Status: Stable.**

A mark that a person, a team or a system is meant: initials by default, a photo when there is one, in five sizes, built on Base UI's Avatar and shadcn's flat parts. Use it beside a name (an owner, an assessor, a reviewer), as a group of the people on a record, or as the account in the side nav. A person's name in a row is a `Person`: the small avatar and the visible name.

It is not a status: presence, health and verification are words beside the person, and a status is a [Badge](?path=/docs/components-badge--docs). It is not a picture of a record; a record's kind is an [Icon](?path=/docs/components-icon--docs).

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Avatar role="img" aria-label="Dana Whitfield" size="medium">
  <AvatarImage src="/dana.png" alt="" />
  <AvatarFallback>DW</AvatarFallback>
  <AvatarBadge tone="success" />
</Avatar>
```

| Part               | What it is                                                                                                       |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Avatar`           | The circle or square. Base UI's Root, which tracks the photo's loading.                                          |
| `AvatarImage`      | The photo, drawn only once it has loaded.                                                                        |
| `AvatarFallback`   | The initials (or an icon) the caller writes, shown while the photo loads, when it fails, and when there is none. |
| `AvatarBadge`      | A mark on the corner, ringed in the colour of the surface it sits on: a dot, or an icon from `medium` up.        |
| `AvatarGroup`      | Overlapping avatars; the overlap is a quarter of their size, near enough on the spacing scale.                   |
| `AvatarGroupCount` | The +n circle after the avatars the group draws, at their size. With `render={<button />}`, it opens the rest.   |
| `Person`           | An xsmall avatar, hidden, beside the person's visible name.                                                      |

The caller chooses the members and writes the overflow count; there is no `max`.

## Variants

| `variant`  | Use                                                                                                       |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `neutral`  | The default. The fill of a Badge with a hairline, so a person never reads as a status.                    |
| `tinted`   | A soft accent fill, the hue stable per person (`avatarHue(name)`), where several people sit side by side. |
| `bold`     | A strong accent fill with inverse initials, for the one person a view is about.                           |
| `gradient` | Two neighbouring accents, for the account in the shell.                                                   |

Shapes are `circle` for a person and `square` for a team, a system or an organization. The accent colours carry no meaning; a hue only tells two people apart.

## Sizes

| `size`   | Box  | Initials | Initials' type                      | Use                                         |
| -------- | ---- | -------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `xsmall` | 16px | one      | `font.body.xsmall` at medium, 11/14 | Beside a name in a row: `Person`.           |
| `small`  | 24px | two      | `font.body.xsmall` at medium, 11/14 | The default. A group of reviewers in a row. |
| `medium` | 32px | two      | `font.body` at medium, 13/18        | A card, a rail, the account menu.           |
| `large`  | 40px | two      | `font.body.large` at medium, 15/22  | A record's owner in its header.             |
| `xlarge` | 64px | two      | `font.heading.page`, 20/26 semibold | A person's own record.                      |

In a group, the +n circle takes its members' size and a type size under theirs, and the overlap scales with them: 4px at xsmall, 6px at small, 8px at medium and large, 16px at xlarge.

<Canvas of={Stories.GroupSizes} />

The ring between overlapping circles, and around a badge, is 2px (`border.width.selected`) of the surface the avatars sit on, `utility.elevation.surface.current`: the page's colour on the page, a card's on a card, a dialog's in a dialog, in both modes.

## States

Base UI shows the fallback while the image loads or fails. `AvatarFallback.delay` can delay its appearance so a fast photo never flashes initials. Changing Image's `src` retries loading. Once the photo has loaded, the circle takes the neutral paint behind it.

## Modifiers

- **`size`**, **`shape`**, **`variant`** and **`hue`** on Avatar; `hue` matters only to `tinted`, `bold` and `gradient`.
- **`avatarInitials(name, count)`** writes the fallback: one letter at xsmall, two above. **`avatarHue(name)`** gives a person the same hue everywhere.
- **AvatarBadge `tone`**: `neutral`, the default, for a mark that is not a status (a lock, a role, an invite); `success` for a verified or active person; `warning` for one who needs attention.
- **AvatarGroupCount `render`**: a button, for a Popover or HoverCard that lists the members the group does not draw.

## Content

- **Initials from the name as the person writes it**, one or two letters. Never a placeholder silhouette in place of a known name.
- **Presence is its own vocabulary**, in words: "online", "away", "invited". Say it in the avatar's `aria-label` ("Dana Whitfield, online") or beside the name, never as a red or green dot alone. A tone on the corner is a status, and presence is not one.
- **A group says who is in it.** Name the group ("Reviewers: Dana Whitfield, Grace Hoppel and three others"), and let the +n open the rest.

A corner mark with no word for it is colour alone: say what it marks in the avatar's name ("Dana Whitfield, verified").

## Long content

An avatar holds one or two letters and never overflows. A `Person`'s name is a row field: longer than its column, it ends in an ellipsis and shows whole on hover while it is cut. The group does not count its room: where it would run past its container, the caller draws fewer avatars and a larger +n, and makes the +n a button whose Popover lists the hidden names.

## Accessibility

- **Name an informative avatar** with `role="img"` and `aria-label`, including meaningful presence ("Dana Whitfield, online"). Give Image an empty `alt` when the root supplies the name.
- **Hide an avatar beside its visible name** with `aria-hidden="true"`; `Person` does. A named group can hide its avatars and count when its own name describes the whole group.
- **The +n as a button** is named for what it holds ("3 more reviewers"), opens with Enter or Space, and its Popover returns focus to it on Escape. It takes the focus ring and a 24px hit area where a pointer is coarse.
- **Not colour alone.** A corner mark in a tone always has words beside it or in the avatar's name.
- **Contrast:** tinted initials on their fill and inverse initials on the bold fill pass 4.5:1 in every accent, and the neutral paint is a Badge's; the contrast gate holds them.

## Props

Root, Image and Fallback take their native Base UI props, refs, `render`, and state-based classes and styles. Badge takes a span's native props and `tone`; Group takes a div's; GroupCount a div's and `render`. Each part names itself last with its `data-slot` (`avatar`, `avatar-image`, `avatar-fallback`, `avatar-badge`, `avatar-group`, `avatar-group-count`, `person`), and Avatar reports `data-size`, `data-variant` and `data-shape`.
