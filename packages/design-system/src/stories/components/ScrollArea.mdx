# ScrollArea

A region that scrolls with the kit's thin bar instead of the platform's, so a rail looks the same on every OS and the bar takes no room from the content. A rail of facts in a fixed height, a list in a Resizable pane, a table wider than its card. The viewport is a tab stop, so a keyboard reader scrolls it too.

The page scrolls on its own; a ScrollArea is for a region inside the page that has a height of its own. A register is a [DataTable](?path=/docs/patterns-datatable--docs), which scrolls itself with its header held. Source is a [CodeBlock](?path=/docs/components-codeblock--docs), which keeps the platform's bar so its gutter can stick.

## Anatomy

| Part     | What it is                                                                                                                                         |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Region   | The box, sized by `className`; it clips and never grows taller than its content. Radix ScrollArea underneath.                                      |
| Viewport | What scrolls: the content at its full size. A tab stop, and a region named by `label` when one is given.                                           |
| Bar      | Over the content's edge, `space.100` wide with `space.025` around the thumb; vertical, horizontal or both. It does not take room from the content. |
| Thumb    | `color.background.neutral.pressed`, `radius.full`; `color.background.neutral.bold` under the pointer. Its length is the visible share.             |
| Corner   | Where two bars meet, when there are two.                                                                                                           |

## Variants

By orientation. **Vertical**, the default: a rail, a list, a pane. **Horizontal**: a table wider than its card, a strip. **Both**: a large figure. The bar is the same in each.

<Canvas of={Stories.Wide} />

Where it lives: the [Inspector](?path=/docs/shapes-inspector--docs)'s body, so a long rail scrolls under the sticky header; a list in a [Resizable](?path=/docs/components-resizable--docs) pane; a card with a wide table.

## Sizes

None of its own: `className` sizes it (`h-full` in a pane, `max-h-full` in a rail, a fixed height on a card), and it is never taller than its content. The bar is 8px, `space.100`, and the thumb 4px inside it.

## States

| State     | Look                                                                                                                |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rest      | The content; no bar, under `hover`.                                                                                 |
| Hover     | The bar fades in while the pointer is over the region, and stays 600ms after the last scroll.                       |
| Always    | `bar="always"`: the bar stays, so the reader sees there is more.                                                    |
| Scrolling | The thumb moves; under the pointer it darkens to `color.background.neutral.bold`.                                   |
| Focus     | The kit's focus outline on the viewport, on keyboard focus, so a keyboard reader sees which region the arrows move. |
| Fits      | The content fits: no bar, nothing to scroll, the region is exactly its content.                                     |

## Modifiers

- **`orientation`**, as in Variants.
- **`bar`**: `hover`, the default, or `always`, for a list whose length matters, where a reader should see there is more without touching it.
- **`label`**: names the region for a screen reader ("Facts", "Related"), so a keyboard reader who lands on it knows what scrolls. Unsaid, it is a plain scrolling box that still takes focus.

<Canvas of={Stories.Always} />

## Content

- **One region of a kind per view.** Two scrolling boxes side by side is a layout the reader has to manage.
- **The region has a reason for its height:** a rail beside a page, a pane in a split, a card of fixed size. Not a box that could have been taller.
- **Nothing important below the fold of a hover bar.** A control the reader must find sits above it, or the bar is `always`.
- **Horizontal scrolling is for tables and figures,** never for text.

## Accessibility

- **The viewport is a tab stop,** so a region that scrolls can be reached and moved with the arrows, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End, without a pointer. This is the rule a scrollable region must meet.
- **With `label` it is a region** named by it, so a screen reader announces "Facts, region" on arrival and lists it among the landmarks. Give one wherever the region is not the only thing in its container.
- **The bar is not in the tab order** and has no role: it is a drawing of where the reader is, and the viewport is the control.
- **The bar takes no room,** so nothing reflows when it appears.
- **The region reaches its edge and hands on.** Scrolling past its end moves the page behind it, as any scroller in the flow of a page does, and a swipe never becomes the browser's back gesture: the page holds `overscroll-behavior: none` at the root, from `base.css`. The surfaces with nothing behind them (the shell's side nav and panel, a sheet's, dialog's or drawer's body, a menu, a listbox) stop at their edge instead, with `overscroll-none` on their own element. A region in the flow must not: Chrome contains a gesture in any scroller whose overflow is auto, whether or not it has anything to scroll and in every axis the gesture touches, so a region that fits its content would swallow the wheel and the page would not scroll under the pointer.
- **The a11y gate** checks the focusability and the name in the matrix.
