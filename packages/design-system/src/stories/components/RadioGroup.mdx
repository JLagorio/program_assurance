# RadioGroup

**Checkbox**, **Switch** and **RadioGroup** are Radix underneath, with the tokens on top. Give a Checkbox, Switch or RadioGroup.Item children and the children become a label that toggles the control. Without children, pass `aria-label`.

## States

A checked state is the blue budget's "selection" use. Disabled is its own set of tokens, not an opacity.
