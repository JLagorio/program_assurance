# Treemap

Part-to-whole with a hierarchy: a tile per leaf, sized by value, in the tone of its top-level branch. Findings by system and component; controls by family and enhancement. Where the total goes, and where inside that. A name shows on a tile when it fits; the rest is the tooltip's. A click on a tile drills into its branch, or opens its card. It lives in a [Chart](?path=/docs/components-chart-overview--docs) Frame, whose legend names the branches.

<Canvas of={Stories.TreemapMatrix} />

## Anatomy

| Part    | What it is                                                                                                                          |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tile    | A leaf, `radius` 2px, in its branch's tone, with a 2px `elevation.surface` gap from its neighbours. Sized by value, 4:3 as far as the layout allows. |
| Name    | The tile's name on an `elevation.surface` chip at 92% in `color.text`, when the tile is at least 64 by 28; cut with the whole in a title. |
| Tooltip | The tile's name and value, keyed by a square.                                                                                       |
| Card    | On a chosen tile: its name, its branch and its value, then the caller's facts.                                                      |

## Variants

| Variant     | Use                                                                                                                              |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Branches    | Two levels: systems in the categorical set, their components inside each in the same hue.                                        |
| One branch  | One system alone, its components in its one hue: the level under a drill-down.                                                   |
| Drill-down  | A click on a tile redraws the treemap with its branch, the way back in the Frame's `path`; a click on a component opens its card. |

## Sizes

`size` is the plot's height: `small` 120px, `medium` 200px, `large` 320px (the right one: tiles need room for their names), or `height` in pixels. A treemap should be at least 420px wide; narrower, most names fall to the tooltip.

## States

| State    | What changes                                                                                                            |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hover    | The tooltip.                                                                                                            |
| Isolated | A branch hidden from the Frame's legend leaves the treemap; the rest re-tile. A hovered legend item dims the other branches. |
| Chosen   | With `onSelect` or `details`: the tiles take the pointer, a click chooses one, the others dim; the card opens on the tile. |
| Loading  | Four skeleton tiles, pulsing, at the plot's height.                                                                     |
| Entering | The tiles arrive over `motion.duration.slow`; a drill-down re-tiles the same way.                                       |

## Modifiers

- **`data`** as nodes: a name, a value on a leaf, children on a branch, a `tone` on a top-level node when the categorical order is not wanted.
- **`format`** for the tooltip and the card.
- **`onSelect`** and **`details`** as in States; the selection is `{ name, value, group }`, `group` being the top-level branch.
- **`loading`** draws the skeleton; the Frame sets it from `status="loading"`.
- **`label`** names the plot outside a Frame.

## Content

- **A hierarchy, or nothing.** A flat list of five is a Bar; a treemap earns its place when the reader asks "where, and where inside that".
- **Six branches at most**, in the categorical set; more folds into Other.
- **Names are short**: a component, a family. What does not fit is the tooltip's, and the table's.
- **Values are one thing**: a count, a cost. Not a score.

## Style

| Element  | Token                                                                                     |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tile     | `color.chart.<tone>` · `radius` 2px · 2px `elevation.surface` gap                         |
| Name     | `font.body.xsmall` · `color.text` on an `elevation.surface` chip at 92% · `radius` 2px    |
| Dimmed   | `opacity.disabled`, over `motion.duration.fast`                                           |
| Skeleton | `color.skeleton` tiles                                                                    |

## Accessibility

- **Named by the Frame**, or by `label`: the plot is a group and focusable. The tiles are not walked by the keyboard; the Frame's legend and table are the way in, one row per leaf.
- **Text never sits on a tile's colour**: the name is on a surface chip, in the text colour.
- **Every tile has a title** with its whole name and value, whether or not the name fits.
- **A chosen tile's card is a dialog**, and focus returns to the plot when it closes.
- **Loading is `aria-busy`** and named "[the plot's name], loading".
