# HoverCard

**Status: Stable.**

A supplementary preview of a link's destination, built from shadcn's Base UI PreviewCard composition. The trigger stays a link; the preview can contain a [Glance](?path=/docs/patterns-glance--docs) or other supporting content available at that destination.

```tsx
<HoverCard>
  <HoverCardTrigger href="/controls/CTRL-0412">CTRL-0412</HoverCardTrigger>
  <HoverCardContent style={{ width: 300 }}>
    <Glance id="CTRL-0412" title="Segregation of duties, payables" />
  </HoverCardContent>
</HoverCard>
```

## When to use it

- **A peek at a link's destination**: a record's code, name, state and owner in a [Glance](?path=/docs/patterns-glance--docs), so the reader can decide whether to follow the link.

Not for an action: the card holds no button the reader must reach, since a keyboard or touch reader may never open it; actions sit at the destination or in a [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs). Not for a short label: that is a [Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-tooltip--docs). Not for the record itself: the link goes there, and a preview the reader works in is the Shell's panel or a [PreviewSheet](?path=/docs/patterns-previewsheet--docs). [Overlays](?path=/docs/components-overlays--docs) compares them all.

Hold the card on a link to the record, never on plain text, and put no action in it: a keyboard or a touch never opens a card on text.

## Content

- The record's own words, as its page has them: its code and name first, then the two or three facts a reader decides on.
- Nothing that is not also at the destination, and no instruction the reader needs.

## Composition and placement

`HoverCard` owns the open state. `HoverCardTrigger` renders an anchor and accepts native link props, a ref, state callbacks and `render` for a custom link. `HoverCardContent` includes the portal and positioner; its native props, ref and `render` target the popup.

An action trigger can use `render={<button type="button" />}`. Its native button owns click and keyboard activation; Trigger has no `nativeButton` prop.

Content is 256px wide by default (`dimension.part.hoverCard`) and centred under its trigger: `side="bottom"`, `sideOffset={4}`, `align="center"` and `alignOffset={0}`. Override its width through `style`, including a state callback. It is capped at the height the window leaves on its side and scrolls within it. Content also takes the positioner's `anchor` (a cell or a virtual point instead of the trigger), `collisionPadding`, `collisionBoundary`, `collisionAvoidance`, `sticky` and `positionMethod`. The positioner handles collisions; logical sides and alignment follow the Ledger locale, with Content's `dir` available as an override.

The trigger draws the kit's focus ring. Compose it with [TextLink](?path=/docs/components-textlink--docs) (`<TextLink render={<HoverCardTrigger href="…" />}>`) for link styling.

The surface uses Ledger's overlay, border, text in `font.body` (it previews the record's own words), radius, spacing and motion tokens, and records the overlay surface as the current one, so a child painted with `bg-surface-current` (a sticky label, a fade at an edge) matches the card. Content `className` and `style` callbacks receive Base UI popup state; render props, classes, styles and refs are merged.

## Interaction

The trigger opens the preview after pointer hover or keyboard focus. `delay` defaults to 600ms and `closeDelay` to 300ms, both on Trigger. Pointer passage from trigger to popup keeps the preview open. Leaving both, moving focus away, an outside press or Escape dismisses it. Escape leaves focus where it was.

Clicking or pressing Enter on the trigger follows the link. A trigger press does not itself guarantee dismissal; navigation or caller-controlled state may close the card. Touch follows the link without the mouse-hover behavior.

Use Root's `defaultOpen`, or controlled `open` with `onOpenChange(open, details)`. `details.cancel()` rejects a change. Multiple triggers can share one Root: pass `payload` on each trigger and render the active payload through Root's children function.

## Accessibility

Keep the preview supplementary. Essential information and actions belong at the linked destination or in a [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs). Opening the preview does not move focus or add a popup Tab stop, and it is not a dialog announcement. The link remains the way to navigate for keyboard, touch and assistive-technology users.

## Props

HoverCard inherits Base UI's [Root props](https://base-ui.com/react/components/preview-card#root), and HoverCardTrigger inherits its [Trigger props](https://base-ui.com/react/components/preview-card#trigger). HoverCardContent inherits [Popup props](https://base-ui.com/react/components/preview-card#popup) plus the positioner's props described above.
