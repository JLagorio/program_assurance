# Drawer

The bottom sheet: a surface that rises from the bottom edge with a drag handle, for a narrow screen and a quick task. Three things to do with the record under the thumb, the filters of a list, a short choice. It is the small-screen form of a [Sheet](?path=/docs/components-sheet--docs) and a [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs); on a wide screen those are the answer.

It is vaul underneath, on Radix's dialog, so it is the one overlay the reader can drag. Base Web's drawer anchored to the bottom is the same part; Carbon and Atlassian have none.

## Anatomy

| Part    | What it is                                                                                                                            |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blanket | `color.blanket` over the page; a click on it closes the drawer.                                                                       |
| Surface | `elevation.surface.overlay` from the bottom edge, up to 640px wide and 85% of the viewport tall, `radius.xxlarge` on its top corners. |
| Handle  | A short bar in `color.background.neutral.bold` centred at the top: drag it down to close.                                             |
| Header  | The title in `font.heading.xsmall`, an optional description under it. A hairline under.                                               |
| Body    | The task. It scrolls; nothing else does.                                                                                              |
| Footer  | The buttons at the end, on `elevation.surface.sunken`.                                                                                |

There is no close button: the handle, the blanket and Escape close it.

## Variants

Drawer has one appearance. What varies is the task:

| The task                           | Use                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A few things to do with one record | A list of subtle, full-width Buttons.                                      |
| The filters of a list              | Checkboxes, with Clear and Apply in the footer.                            |
| A short choice                     | A RadioGroup or a few Buttons.                                             |
| A form                             | Not a Drawer: a Dialog, or a page. The keyboard covers half a drawer.      |
| A record's detail                  | Not a Drawer: a Sheet on a wide screen, the record's page on a narrow one. |
| The same on a wide screen          | Not a Drawer: a Popover or a DropdownMenu at the button.                   |

Not built, on purpose: snap points (a drawer is open or closed), a drawer from the top or the sides (the Sheet is the side one), and a drawer that stays open under the page.

## Sizes

One: the viewport's width up to 640px, centred; as tall as its content up to 85% of the viewport, then the body scrolls under the handle and the header.

## States

An open drawer covers the page, so the states are one click away in the matrix above and one is held open in the Open story.

| State     | What changes                                                                                   |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Closed    | Nothing on the page.                                                                           |
| Opening   | The blanket fades in; the drawer rises from the bottom edge. Focus lands on the first control. |
| Open      | The page behind is inert and does not scroll.                                                  |
| Dragging  | The drawer follows the thumb; past a threshold it closes, else it springs back.                |
| Scrolling | A body taller than the drawer scrolls; the handle, the header and the footer stay.             |
| Closing   | A drag down, the blanket, Escape, or a footer button. Focus goes back to what opened it.       |

## Modifiers

- **`open`** and **`onClose`**: the caller's state.
- **`title`** and **`description`**.
- **`footer`**: the buttons.
- **`className`** for a product's own width.

## Content

- **The title is the task or the object**: Quick actions, Filters, Sort by.
- **The description says which record**, when the title does not: "For CTRL-0412."
- **Three to five things**, each a full-width subtle Button; the verb first, the object omitted.
- **The footer's verb says the act**: Apply, not OK.

## Style

| Element | Token                                                                                                                |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blanket | `color.blanket`                                                                                                      |
| Surface | `elevation.surface.overlay` · `radius.xxlarge` top corners · `elevation.shadow.overlay` · up to 640px wide, 85% tall |
| Handle  | `space.050` tall · `space.500` wide · `color.background.neutral.bold` · `space.150` from the top                     |
| Header  | `space.250` sides · `space.150` top and bottom · `font.heading.xsmall` · description `color.text.subtle`             |
| Body    | `color.border` above · `space.250` sides · `space.200` top and bottom                                                |
| Footer  | `elevation.surface.sunken` · `color.border` above · `space.250` sides · `space.150` top and bottom                   |
| Motion  | vaul's spring on the drag; the blanket fades                                                                         |

## Accessibility

- **It is a modal dialog** (vaul on Radix): `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, named by its title and described by its description.
- **Focus moves in** to the first control and **returns** to what opened it. Focus stays inside; the page behind is hidden and does not scroll.
- **The handle is for the thumb.** A keyboard reader closes with Escape; a screen reader with the blanket or a footer button. Nothing is only behind the drag.
- **Escape closes it**, as does the blanket.
- **The Open story turns off axe's `aria-hidden-focus` rule**, since Radix hides the page and axe cannot see the trap.
- **Contrast.** The overlay surface with the default text; the handle is decoration and has no text.
