# Collapsible

Reference material: present, addressable, closed. The catalog statement, the assessment objectives and the discussion are things a reader occasionally needs and never needs first; rendering them expanded is most of why a control page reads as a novel. A **Collapsible** is one such section: a title row that opens and closes, the body under it. Several that know about each other are an [Accordion](?path=/docs/components-accordion--docs); the two share the row, so a page can mix them. Carbon's accordion (flush alignment, chevron at the end, closed by default) is the model; HubSpot's Accordion is this part under that name.

What a Collapsible is not: the record's work. Implementation statements, the assessment, the evidence table are a [Section](?path=/docs/patterns-section--docs) or a [Block](?path=/docs/shapes-block--docs), open, in the outline, read by every reader. Carbon's rule: if a user is likely to read all of the content, do not fold it.

## Anatomy

| Part    | What it is                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Row     | The trigger: a button the full width, `space.100` above and below the title, a 1px rule in `color.border` above it. Under the pointer it tints.               |
| Title   | Flush left in `font.body` semibold, `color.text`, one line that truncates. A heading of `headingLevel` when the section is in the page's outline.             |
| Count   | After the title, a Count: how many are inside. Zero shows nothing.                                                                                            |
| Chevron | At the end of the row, `dimension.icon.small`, `color.icon.subtle`. Down while closed, up while open, turning in `motion.duration.fast`.                       |
| Body    | Under the row, from the rule to `space.200` below it. It opens to its measured height in `motion.duration.medium` on the enter curve and closes on the exit.   |

## Variants

One row, in two alignments. **Flush**, the default, puts the title on the text column: a rail, a page, a Section's body. The hover tint reaches `space.100` past the title at each end, as Carbon's flush accordion does, so the title stays on the column. **Inset** steps the row and the body in by `space.300`, for a surface whose rules run edge to edge: a Card, a Panel. The first row on a surface drops its rule with `border-t-0`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Inset} />

Carbon's start-aligned chevron, for a fold that behaves as a tree, is not built: a hierarchy is a [Tree](?path=/docs/components-tree--docs).

## Sizes

One: the row is 36px, `space.100` around a line of body text, so a rail of rows is one rhythm with the KeyValue rows beside it. Carbon's is 40px; the kit's rails are denser.

## States

| State    | Look                                                                                                      |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Closed   | The default. The chevron points down; the body is absent from the page.                                   |
| Open     | The chevron points up; the body is present and in the tab order.                                          |
| Hover    | The row tints `color.background.neutral.subtle.hovered`; pressed, `.pressed`.                             |
| Focus    | The kit's focus outline on the row.                                                                       |
| Disabled | Title and chevron in the disabled colours, no pointer. Rare: prefer the row open with an Empty inside.     |

## Modifiers

- **`defaultOpen`**, or **`open`** and **`onOpenChange`** when the caller holds it: a rail whose sections remember their state, a "show all" that opens every row.
- **`count`**: how many are inside, hidden at zero.
- **`headingLevel`**: the title as an h2 to h6, so the section is in the page's outline and a screen reader's heading list. Under a Section's h2 the rows are h3s.
- **`inset`**, as in Variants.
- **`disabled`**, as in States.

<Canvas of={Stories.Headings} />

## Content

- **The title is what is inside, as a noun:** "Catalog statement", "Assessment objectives", "Discussion". Not "Show more", not a question.
- **Closed is the default.** The titles are the overview; the reader opens what they came for. Open one row when the reader arrives for it, never all of them.
- **The body is reference:** Text, KeyValue rows, a short list. Not a table the reader works in, not a form.
- **Nothing inside is an Empty**, compact, saying what would be here and what fills it. Never a grey word.
- **Never nested.** A fold inside a fold is two clicks to a paragraph; flatten it, or a Tree for a hierarchy.
- **The whole page scrolls,** never the body of one row.

## Style

| Element | Token                                                                                                                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Row     | `space.100` block · `space.100` gap · 1px `color.border` above · `radius.small` for the tint and the focus outline       |
| Title   | `font.body` semibold · `color.text` · `color.text.disabled` disabled                                                      |
| Chevron | `dimension.icon.small` 14px · `color.icon.subtle` · `motion.duration.fast` on `motion.easing.standard`                   |
| Tint    | `color.background.neutral.subtle.hovered`, `.pressed`, reaching `space.100` past a flush title                            |
| Body    | `space.200` below · opens in `motion.duration.medium` on `motion.easing.enter`, closes on `motion.easing.exit`             |
| Inset   | `space.300` inline on the row and the body                                                                                |

## Accessibility

- **The row is a button with `aria-expanded`** and `aria-controls` to the body; Radix Collapsible sets them, so the chevron needs no text of its own.
- **Keyboard:** Tab reaches the row; Enter or Space toggles it. Controls inside an open body join the tab order after the row. There is no arrow model between Collapsibles: they do not know about each other, which is what an Accordion adds.
- **Headings:** with `headingLevel` the title is a heading, and a screen reader's heading list shows the rail's sections. Carbon asks for this on the first accordion in a product; the kit asks for it wherever the rows are in a Section.
- **Reduced motion** shows the body at once.
- **The a11y gate** checks the roles, the names and the heading order in the matrix; a disabled row is skipped by its contrast check as a disabled control.
