# Status surfaces

Alert, Banner, Progress and Stat read the same tone table as Badge, so a tone means one thing everywhere.

## Alert

A tinted callout in a rail or above a table: a Dot, a title in the tone colour, an optional body. Danger announces itself (`role="alert"`); the others are polite.

<Canvas of={Stories.Alerts} />

## Banner

A message about the whole site, in the shell's Banner area: the loss of data or a function, or something site-wide that changes what the reader can do. One at a time, never dismissible, gone when no longer true. It is one line and truncates rather than wraps; the `action` is one link or button drawn in the banner's own colour. Alert is inline in a rail or above a table; Banner is the screen's.

<Canvas of={Stories.Banners} />

## Progress

One value out of 100 as a thin bar, or a Stacked bar of segments for a coverage read-out. The track is `color.background.neutral`; a segment is the tone's bold fill, as Atlassian's ProgressBar. A `hatched` segment is what is not known or not covered: a hole in the record, never a flat fill.

<Canvas of={Stories.Bars} />

## Stat and Tiles

A Tile is label, big tabular number, one-line note; zero reads muted. Tiles is a row of them separated by hairlines, framed as a card or run edge to edge as a band. A bare Stat is the number over its label for an unframed summary row.

<Canvas of={Stories.Stats} />

## Gates

The conditions an action waits on, what a record still needs, a phase's entry and exit criteria. A met gate is a check and its label, muted. An unmet gate is a Dot in its tone, the label, the reason under it and the action that meets it. A list, never a score.
