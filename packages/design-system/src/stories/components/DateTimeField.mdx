# DateTimeField

**Status: Stable.**

A moment: a day and a time together, typed or picked, in the LedgerProvider's time zone, which it names after the time ("PDT"). It holds and reports an ISO instant in UTC (`"2026-09-19T00:30:00.000Z"`), so the moment a reader enters is the moment stored, and every reader sees it in their provider's zone.

Use it for a point in time: evidence collected at, a response due at 5 PM, a scheduled start. A day with no time (a due date, a planned completion) is a [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs); a wall time that repeats is a [TimeField](?path=/docs/components-timefield--docs). It replaces `input type="datetime-local"`, which ignores the provider and leaves the zone unsaid.

```tsx
<Field>
  <FieldLabel>Evidence collected</FieldLabel>
  <DateTimeField name="collected_at" value={collectedAt} onValueChange={setCollectedAt} />
</Field>
```

## The two halves

The day is a typed [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs) input with its month, and the time a [TimeField](?path=/docs/components-timefield--docs) input with its stepping; each reads what the reader types in the locale, and the time shows the zone's short name. Where the field's container holds them they sit side by side; below 256px the time goes under the day.

The field reports an instant once both halves read, and `""` while either is missing or not valid. `defaultTime` (an ISO time such as `"17:00"`) fills the time when the reader picks a day first. When focus leaves the field with one half filled, it says "Enter both a date and a time." Enter in either input with one half filled says the same and does not submit the form around the field, so focus stays where the reader is typing; so does Enter on a day or a time that does not read, or a moment outside the limits, with that message. With both halves read, Enter submits as in any text field. A day and a wall time become an instant in the provider's zone: a time a daylight-saving change skips moves forward by the gap, and a time that happens twice takes the earlier moment.

## Limits

`min` and `max` are instants. Days outside them are disabled in the month with their reason; a time before `min` on its first day is an error that names the limit: "Enter Sep 21, 2026, 9:00 AM or later." `isDateUnavailable` works as on DatePicker.

## Values and forms

`value` is an instant with an offset or `Z`. A value with no offset (`"2026-09-18T17:30"`, what `datetime-local` holds) is read as a wall time in the provider's zone, which makes moving a native field over a one-line change; anything else warns once and is treated as empty. A hidden input carries the instant under `name`; put `name` here, not on the Field. A native form reset puts the field back as it was, a half entry and its message included. One message under the field speaks for both halves: a day or time that does not read, a moment outside the limits, or a missing half.

## Accessibility

- Inside a Field the day input is the Field's control, so the FieldLabel points at it. The whole field is a group named by the label; the day input is named "Evidence collected Date" and the time "Evidence collected Time Pacific Daylight Time", so a screen reader hears the zone that the eye reads as "PDT".
- The Field's hint and error describe both inputs, and its invalid state reaches both.
- Keep `showTimeZone` on. A time without its zone reads as two moments to readers in two offices.

## Don't

Keep `showTimeZone` on, so the zone is in view and what the reader enters is what is stored. With the zone hidden, readers in two offices read one time as two moments.
