# Toaster

Feedback after an act. The reader linked the evidence, archived the program, exported the report; a toast says so at the bottom right and goes. **Toaster** is the stack, rendered once near the root; **`toast`** fires one from anywhere: `toast.success("Evidence linked")`. sonner underneath for the stack, the timer, the swipe and the live region; the kit owns the look and the defaults.

A toast is one of three messages. A condition of the record is an [Alert](?path=/docs/components-alert--docs) on the record; a message about the whole site is a [Banner](?path=/docs/components-banner--docs). A toast is never a question: that is an [AlertDialog](?path=/docs/components-alertdialog--docs), and the toast comes after it.

## Anatomy

| Part        | What it is                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Card        | 356px on `elevation.surface.overlay` with a 1px `color.border`, `radius.large`, the overlay shadow, `space.150` inside. It slides in from the edge.             |
| Mark        | Before the text, `dimension.icon.medium`, in the tone's icon colour: a check, an info, a warning, a danger. A plain toast has none; a working one has the Spinner. |
| Title       | What happened, `font.body` medium, one line.                                                                                                                 |
| Description | Under it when there is more to say, `font.body.small`, `color.text.subtle`.                                                                                  |
| Action      | At the end: one verb the reader may still take, Undo mostly, as a small bold button. `cancel` is a quiet second beside it.                                    |
| Close       | An error's, at the end, so a reader can be rid of it; `closeButton` puts one on every toast.                                                                 |
| Stack       | Newest at the bottom right, the older ones behind it, four at most; the pointer over the stack fans them out.                                                 |

## Variants

Five kinds, by what happened. **Plain**, `toast("Saved")`: no mark, for the small confirmations. **Success**: the act landed. **Info**: something the reader should know now, "3 controls updated". **Warning**: the act landed with a caveat, "Due in 2 days". **Error**: the act did not land; it stays longer and carries a close. And **working**, `toast.loading` or `toast.promise`: the Spinner while it runs, then the outcome in the same card.

The card is one colour in every kind; the mark carries the tone. There is no tinted toast, for the reason there is no tinted Stat: a tone fills a Badge or an Alert, and a toast is neither.

## Sizes

One: 356px wide, so a title and a line of description fit, and as tall as its text. On a narrow screen it spans the width. The stack sits `space.200` from the edges with `space.100` between cards.

## States

| State    | Look                                                                                                                          |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arriving | Slides in from the bottom in `motion.duration.moderate`; the stack lifts to make room.                                        |
| Resting  | Four seconds for most; eight for an error; until it settles for working; `duration: Infinity` for one that must be closed.     |
| Hover    | The timer pauses and the stack fans out, so a reader who looks can read.                                                      |
| Focus    | The card is a tab stop with the kit's focus outline; the action, the cancel and the close are reached inside it.               |
| Leaving  | Fades and drops in `motion.duration.moderate`; a swipe to the side dismisses it.                                              |
| Expanded | `expand` on the Toaster: every card at full height instead of stacked.                                                         |

## Modifiers

- **`description`**: the second line, the object or the consequence.
- **`action`**: `{ label, onClick }`, one verb; **`cancel`**: the quiet second.
- **`duration`**: milliseconds; `Infinity` for a toast the reader must close. The kit's defaults are in States.
- **`closeButton`** per toast, or on the Toaster for every toast.
- **`id`**: fire again with the same id to replace a toast in place, as `loading` then `success` does.
- **`toast.promise(p, { loading, success, error })`**: one card through the three.
- **`toast.dismiss(id)`**: take one down, or all with no id.
- **`position`** and **`expand`** on the Toaster, chosen once per product.

## Content

- **The title is what happened, past tense, with the object:** "Evidence linked", "Archived PRG-1041", "Could not save". Not "Success!", not "Error", not a sentence.
- **The description names the thing or the consequence,** one line: "Bank reconciliation, July", "Its controls stay readable."
- **An error says what to do** when there is something: "Add the SAR and export again."
- **One action, a verb:** Undo, View, Retry. Never Yes or No: a toast does not ask.
- **Say what the product did, not what the reader clicked.** The click is known; the outcome is the news.
- **One toast per act.** An act that touched twelve records says "12 controls updated", not twelve toasts.

## Accessibility

- **The stack is a live region,** `aria-live="polite"`, named "Notifications", so a screen reader hears a toast when it arrives without being interrupted. An error is polite too: it says what failed, and the failed control says it as well.
- **Keyboard:** Alt+T moves focus to the stack; each card is a tab stop, and Tab reaches its action, its cancel and its close. Escape does nothing to a toast; the close and the timer do.
- **The timer pauses on hover and on focus,** so a reader who reaches a toast can read it.
- **An error stays eight seconds and has a close,** because four is not enough to read why and a reader who looked away must still find it. A toast that must be acted on is `Infinity` with an action.
- **Reduced motion** shows and hides the card at once.
- **The a11y gate** checks the specimens' contrast and the buttons' names; the live region is sonner's and is checked by hand.
