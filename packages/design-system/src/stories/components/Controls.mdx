# Controls

The form controls. **Field** wraps one control with its label, hint and error. **Input**, **NativeSelect** and **Textarea** share the hairline control on the input surface. **Checkbox**, **Switch** and **RadioGroup** are Radix underneath, with the tokens on top.

<Canvas of={Stories.Fields} />

## Field

- `label` is required. It is a real `label`, so clicking it focuses the control.
- `hint` sits under the control. `error` replaces the hint, turns it danger and marks the field invalid.
- `isRequired` adds the asterisk. It does not set `required` on the control; do that on the control.

## Input, NativeSelect, Textarea

All three are the browser's own element with the kit's look. Every attribute passes through. NativeSelect draws its chevron with a real icon so it follows the colour mode; `className` goes to its wrapper.

A `controlBase` string is exported for the button triggers of Select, Combobox and DatePicker, so a trigger and a field are the same shape.

## Choice controls

Give a Checkbox, Switch or RadioGroup.Item children and the children become a label that toggles the control. Without children, pass `aria-label`.
