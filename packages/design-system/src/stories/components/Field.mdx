# Field

One control with its label, hint and error. Field is Base UI's Field underneath, so the parts tie themselves to the control: no ids, no `htmlFor`, no `aria-describedby` and no `aria-invalid` written by hand. The form still owns values and validation; the Field carries what the form decided.

```tsx
<Field invalid={invalid} required>
  <FieldLabel>Owner</FieldLabel>
  <Input value={owner} onValueChange={setOwner} />
  <FieldDescription>Use the full name.</FieldDescription>
  <FieldError errors={errors} />
</Field>
```

The label names the control and its click focuses it; the hint and the error describe it, in that order; `invalid` reaches the control as `aria-invalid` and `data-invalid` and turns the label and the error to the danger colour; `required` announces the requirement and adds the label's asterisk, which is hidden from the accessible name. The same binding reaches Input, Textarea, InputGroupInput, the Select trigger, the Combobox input, Checkbox, RadioGroup and Switch.

## Parts

`FieldLabel`, `FieldDescription` and `FieldError` bind inside a Field and stay plain elements outside one. `FieldSet` and `FieldLegend` group related controls, and a RadioGroup inside a FieldSet is named by its legend. `FieldGroup` spaces fields and is a size container, so a form's grid can collapse by the form's own width (see [Forms](?path=/docs/patterns-forms--docs), Layout); `FieldContent` stacks text beside a control; `FieldTitle` is a choice card's title, where a wrapping label makes the whole card choose: it names the control on its own, so the card's FieldDescription describes the control once instead of also becoming its name. `FieldSeparator` divides sections, with optional text; its rules stop on either side of the text, so it works on any surface.

## States

- **`invalid`** comes from the form library (TanStack Form: `isTouched && !isValid`). A FieldError that shows a message also marks the Field invalid, so `<FieldError errors={errors} />` is enough when the errors are already filtered. A hand-set `data-invalid` is read as `invalid`.
- **`required`** is announced (`aria-required`) and marked; it is not a native constraint, so a form library that checks it is not second-guessed by the browser. Put native `required` on the control itself when Base UI or the browser should check it. `FieldLabel required={false}` hides the asterisk where most of a form is required and only the optional fields say so. A group Field requires the group, not each checkbox in it.
- **`disabled`** disables the control and dims the label. A Field or a FieldSet inside a disabled Field is disabled too, so a disabled group's legend and choices follow it. `disabled` on a FieldSet reaches every control inside, including the Checkbox, Radio and Switch roots that a native `<fieldset disabled>` leaves live. The label also dims when its control is disabled on its own or by a native fieldset, and a disabled choice dims only its own label.
- **Read-only** is the control's own `readOnly`: the sunken surface with a dashed edge and no hover on the text fields, Checkbox and Radio, and the neutral fill in place of the brand one on a Switch that is on. The label keeps its colour, since the value can still be read and copied. A value never edited where it is shown is a [KeyValue](?path=/docs/components-keyvalue--docs) instead.

<Canvas of={Stories.Disabled} />

A focused invalid text field thickens its danger border to the 2px focus edge; a focused invalid Checkbox or Radio keeps its danger border with the focus outline around it, and a Switch swaps its danger outline for the focus outline, so focus is always visible.

A FieldSet is a form's lock while it saves: `disabled` holds every field and choice inside it, and it adds no border, padding or content-sized minimum, so a wide table inside it scrolls or clips within its frame instead of widening a dialog. It stacks its children `space.100` apart; `className` changes the gap.

<Canvas of={Stories.SavingLock} />

A FieldLabel wrapping a horizontal Field becomes a choice card, with hover, the chosen state and one keyboard focus ring, drawn by the card rather than the control; a disabled card keeps a quiet surface. See the choice cards on [Checkbox](?path=/docs/components-checkbox--docs) and [RadioGroup](?path=/docs/components-radiogroup--docs).

## Validation

With a form library, pass `invalid` and the messages. Validate on submit, then on change; focus the first invalid control after a failed submission, or an [ErrorSummary](?path=/docs/components-errorsummary--docs) when the form is long.

<Canvas of={Stories.Validation} />

Without one, Base UI validates: native constraints on the control, `validate` on the Field for your own rules, `validationMode` for when (`onSubmit`, then on change, by default), and a FieldError with `match` for each message. A FieldError with no children shows Base UI's own message whenever the Field fails.

<Canvas of={Stories.NativeValidation} />

FieldError takes children or an `errors` array of message objects, drops duplicates and empty entries, and renders nothing when none remain; children win. It is not a live region: focus and the control's description carry it, and a form-level failure belongs in an Alert or an ErrorSummary.

## Groups

A group of choices is a Field around a FieldSet: the legend names the group, the Field's hint and error describe it, and `invalid` and `required` reach the RadioGroup (or the checkboxes of a [CheckboxGroup](?path=/docs/components-checkboxgroup--docs)) once; `disabled` reaches every choice. Each choice is its own horizontal Field with a FieldLabel, so it keeps its own name. A RadioGroup announces the requirement itself; a CheckboxGroup's legend says "(required)" to assistive technology, since a group cannot.

```tsx
<Field invalid={invalid} required>
  <FieldSet>
    <FieldLegend variant="label">Review frequency</FieldLegend>
    <RadioGroup value={frequency} onValueChange={setFrequency}>
      <Field orientation="horizontal">
        <RadioGroupItem value="quarterly" />
        <FieldLabel>Quarterly</FieldLabel>
      </Field>
    </RadioGroup>
    <FieldError errors={errors} />
  </FieldSet>
</Field>
```

## Accessibility

- The label names the control through `htmlFor` and `aria-labelledby`, and a Select or Combobox list inside the Field is named by it too. Ids are generated per instance, so a form can appear more than once.
- Hint and error ids reach `aria-describedby` in the order they render; ids the caller already references are listed once.
- Explicit ids, `htmlFor` and ARIA on the parts still win, and wrappers between Field and control do not break the binding. Hand-written ids, `aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid` and `data-invalid` keep working; a Field needs none of them.
- The date and number fields (DatePicker, DateRangePicker, TimeField, DateTimeField, NumberField) and SearchField take the binding as Input does.
- A Field is not a group of its own: it takes `role="group"` only when it is given a name (`aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`), so one control is not wrapped in an unnamed group.

Field and FieldSet take `render`. A test finds a FieldError by its text or `data-slot="field-error"`, not by a live role.
