# Tabs

An underline tab strip. **Tabs** is the rail: a tablist with a rule underneath. **Tabs.Tab** is one tab.

Which tab is selected is the caller's state. In this product a tab is a route far more often than an in-page panel, so Tabs does not manage panels; it draws the strip and reports the selection through aria.

<Canvas of={Stories.Buttons} />

## As links

Pass `asChild` and the child element takes the tab's classes and aria. This is how a router's Link becomes a tab.

<Canvas of={Stories.AsLinks} />

## Details

- Tabs are 32px tall, on `dimension.control.medium`, so a strip lines up with a row of small controls beside it.
- `count` puts a Count after the label; null or undefined shows nothing, so `count={open || null}` hides a zero. `trailing` takes anything else, e.g. a Badge.
- A disabled tab keeps its place and loses its pointer.
- The underline is `color.background.brand.bold`, two pixels, sitting on the rail's rule.
