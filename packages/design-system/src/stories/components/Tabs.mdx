# Tabs

**Status: Stable.**

Related views within one page, using [shadcn's Base UI Tabs](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/base/tabs) and [Base UI Tabs](https://base-ui.com/react/components/tabs).

## When to use it

- **Views of one record or one page** that the reader moves between, each filling the panel: a record's Overview, Controls, Evidence and History.
- **A collection per view**, each a register of its own, with its count in the tab.

Not for narrowing the rows the reader is already in (All, Open, Closed): that is a [ToggleGroup](?path=/docs/components-togglegroup--docs), a filter or a saved view in the [Toolbar](?path=/docs/patterns-toolbar--docs). Not for steps taken in order: that is a [Stepper](?path=/docs/components-stepper--docs). Not for sections read down the page: those are [Sections](?path=/docs/layout-section--docs), or a [Collapsible](?path=/docs/components-collapsible--docs) for one the reader opens. Not for moving between the product's pages: that is the Shell's side nav.

**At most about seven primary tabs.** Each tab is a view a reader comes back to. Beyond about seven, group or fold: the strip scrolls rather than folding the rest into a menu, so the reader no longer sees every view at once and hunts for one. Merge views that hold the same kind of work into one tab under Section headings, fold a view read rarely into the Overview (its Details, or a section such as the record's activity), and fold a view with little of its own into the view it belongs with. A strip never wraps onto a second row.

## Content

- A tab is one or two words, a noun in sentence case that names what its panel shows ("Controls", "Evidence", "Activity"). Never a verb, and never the word tab.
- The tabs on a strip name views of the same thing, in the order the reader works through them, Overview first where there is one.
- A count after the label says how many records the panel holds: a [Count](?path=/docs/components-count--docs) with `max={9999}`, the one cap on every strip in a product, never a number written into the words. It shows `0` once the rows have loaded, and no count while they load or after they failed: a count is never a guess.
- A state after the label is a [Badge](?path=/docs/components-badge--docs) ("Draft"); an icon is decoration, never the only name.

## Anatomy

Compose `Tabs`, `TabsList`, `TabsTrigger` and `TabsContent`. Match each Trigger's `value` to its Content. Put counts, badges and icons in the Trigger's children: a count is a [Count](?path=/docs/components-count--docs) after the label, `max={9999}` (Content), and a state is a Badge. Name the List with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.

```tsx
<Tabs defaultValue="controls">
  <TabsList variant="line" activateOnFocus aria-label="Record views">
    <TabsTrigger value="controls">
      Controls <Count value={340} max={9999} />
    </TabsTrigger>
    <TabsTrigger value="history">History</TabsTrigger>
  </TabsList>
  <TabsContent value="controls">Control register</TabsContent>
  <TabsContent value="history">Change history</TabsContent>
</Tabs>
```

Routes compose Tabs, TabsList and TabsContent directly; route state controls the selected value. Supporting context can be contributed separately with Shell.Aside.

## Variants

`TabsList` supports shadcn's `default` filled strip and `line` underline. Ledger retains its color, spacing and focus tokens; line tabs remain 32px tall on a border, with a 2px brand underline. One Base UI Indicator follows the selected tab, sliding and resizing over the moderate motion duration (240ms). The filled strip moves its selected chip in the same way: the chip is outlined in `color.border.selected`, 3:1 against the track in both modes, since its fill alone is about 1.1:1. Horizontal line tabs fill the available width and align to the start. Their border continues across the container even when only a few tabs exist, and each tab's hit area reaches half way across the gap to its neighbour, so a click between two labels chooses the nearer tab.

Horizontal tabs, filled or line, use the kit's `ScrollArea` and horizontal `ScrollBar` inside a horizontal [Scroller](?path=/docs/components-scroller--docs), whose click arrows mark the overflowed edge for pointer readers and replace the scrollbar while they show; touch readers swipe with the bar. They stay on one row at every container width, including narrow preview panels. Touch, trackpad, scrollbar dragging and arrow-key focus can reach the overflowed tabs, and the Scroller reveals the focused tab clear of its arrows. The selected tab stays in view as the strip narrows, and as counts arrive after the first layout until the reader scrolls the strip; a tab chosen from outside the strip scrolls into view. Do not add `flex-wrap`, an overflow utility or another scroller to `TabsList`. Size the `Tabs` container when a bounded example is needed; the List handles its own width and scrolling.

The default filled strip fits its content and scrolls, from its start, in a container narrower than its tabs. Record pages and previews explicitly use `variant="line"`; `activateOnFocus` remains an independent choice. Vertical lists retain their vertical layout and do not receive a horizontal scroller. The exported `tabsListVariants` recipe supplies list styling; the `TabsList` component also supplies the horizontal scrolling composition.

Under a horizontal strip, filled or line, the panel's content starts `space.150` (12px) below the strip's edge. TabsContent carries that space as its own top padding, so it is the same on every screen and stays with the panel wherever the strip sits. Do not add padding, a margin or a gap to Tabs or TabsContent for it, and do not set `display: contents` on either, which drops the panel's entrance. Beside a vertical strip, Tabs puts `space.100` between the strip and the panel.

Set `orientation="vertical"` on Tabs for a vertical strip and Up/Down navigation. Direction follows LedgerProvider; `dir="ltr"` or `dir="rtl"` overrides both layout and keyboard direction.

## Accessibility

Base UI owns the tablist, tab and tabpanel roles, selection, focus and panel associations. Keep a panel for the selected tab. Each strip is one Tab stop; arrows move within it and Home/End reach the ends. Focus wraps unless `loopFocus={false}` is set on the List. A horizontal strip clips, so a focused tab draws its ring inside itself; a vertical strip draws it outside, as other controls do.

Activation is **manual by default**: arrows move focus, then Enter or Space selects. `activateOnFocus` on the List selects as focus moves; use it when the panel can appear immediately. Disabled tabs remain arrow-focusable in the installed Base UI 1.7 contract and cannot activate.

A navigation tab uses `nativeButton={false}` and `render={<Link ... />}` or an anchor. It keeps its href and native modified-click navigation while receiving the tab role. The caller owns synchronization between route state and the controlled value.

<Canvas of={Stories.Links} />

Inactive panels unmount by default. `keepMounted` retains their state; hidden panels remain hidden even with a display class, and Base UI makes their controls inert. The arriving panel fades from 0.65 opacity and rises 4px over the medium duration (150ms). The outgoing panel hides immediately so two views never occupy layout together. A controlled route that reuses one panel with a changing value replays its entrance without remounting children or resetting retained input state.

Reduced motion disables both indicator movement and panel entrance. The Motion story samples rendered frames for filled, line, RTL, vertical and reused-panel examples; the dark test project uses reduced motion. Keyboard, link and retained-state stories run in both color modes.

The indicator's position and size round to the layout unit (1/64px). A resize of the strip's container that leaves the tabs where they were, such as a record's Details rail leaving with its Overview, then writes the same values, so the slide runs once and arrives in its 240ms rather than starting over on every frame of the resize.

In forced-colors mode, system-color outlines and underlines preserve the selected tab in both variants.

## Props

The four parts expose their corresponding Base UI props, native refs, `render`, events, and state-based `className`/`style`. `TabsList` props and its ref still target the element with `role="tablist"`, inside the horizontal scroller. The scroller adds no separate Tab stop; keyboard users enter the tablist and arrows or Home/End reveal the focused tab. Public types are `TabsProps`, `TabsListProps`, `TabsTriggerProps` and `TabsContentProps`.

Root accepts `value`/`onValueChange` or `defaultValue`, including numeric values; `null` means no selection. `onValueChange(value, details)` can cancel user activation with `details.cancel()`. Uncontrolled selection falls back when a selected tab disappears or becomes disabled; those automatic notifications are not cancellable. For predictable server rendering, give an explicit enabled initial value.

## Don't

A filter over one list is a ToggleGroup or a toolbar filter, never tabs. A tab is a noun that names its view, with a Count for its records, never a verb or a count typed into the words.
