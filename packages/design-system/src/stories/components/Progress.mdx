# Progress

**Status: Stable.**

How far a task has gone, as a bar: an upload, an import, a package build. `Progress` is a progressbar on Base UI's Progress, with Ledger's tones and sizes. `ProgressStacked` is the other bar: a share of a whole, such as control coverage, drawn as segments.

<Canvas of={Stories.ValuesAndRanges} />

## Choosing between them

| What the reader is watching                                  | Part                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A task that finishes, with a known amount done               | `Progress` with `value`                                                                               |
| A task that finishes, of unknown length, in a region         | `Progress` with `value={null}`: a hatched bar                                                         |
| A short wait for an action, beside its control               | [Spinner](?path=/docs/components-spinner--docs), or the Button's own `isLoading`                      |
| Content on its way, whose shape is known                     | [Skeleton](?path=/docs/components-skeleton--docs)                                                     |
| A share of a whole: coverage, controls by state, a breakdown | `ProgressStacked`, or the number in words                                                             |
| A measure against a target that does not finish (a score)    | A number in words, or a [Chart](?path=/docs/patterns-chart-overview--docs) gauge; never a progressbar |

A progressbar says a task is under way and will end. A quantity that is not a task's completion is not one: a screen reader would hear "73 percent" of a job that is not running.

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Progress value={41} max={80} tone="success">
  <ProgressLabel>Controls verified</ProgressLabel>
  <ProgressValue>{(_, value) => `${value} of 80`}</ProgressValue>
</Progress>
```

| Part                | What it is                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Progress`          | The progressbar: Base UI's root, a row that wraps, with its own track and indicator after the children.                                        |
| `ProgressLabel`     | The visible name, in small medium text. It names the bar.                                                                                      |
| `ProgressValue`     | The value after the label, at the row's end, in small subtle tabular figures: the formatted percentage, or what its children function returns. |
| `ProgressTrack`     | The track, `color.background.neutral`, rounded, at the size's height. For a custom composition under Base UI's root.                           |
| `ProgressIndicator` | The fill, in the tone's bar colour; the hatched bar while the value is unknown. For a custom composition.                                      |
| `ProgressStacked`   | The segmented bar: each segment its share of the total, solid or hatched, optionally a button that filters what is under it.                   |

## Variants and sizes

`tone` is the Ledger meaning: `information` by default, `success` for a task that is going well, `warning` or `danger` for one at risk, `neutral` for one that carries no judgement. Each fill holds 3:1 against its track on every surface, `elevation.surface.sunken` included, in both modes. A warning bar, filled or waiting, is `color.chart.warning.bold`, a step darker than a chart's warning series in light, because `color.background.warning.bold` is a light orange made to carry dark text and the series orange is under 3:1 on the track over the sunken surface. `size` is `small` (4px, in a cell or beside a name), `medium` (6px, the default, in a row) or `large` (8px, a band).

## States

- **Determinate**: `value` between `min` and `max` (0 and 100 unless set). Base UI clamps a value past either end, and the bar's width and its accessible value follow.
- **Unknown length**: `value={null}` is a wait. The bar is hatched the width of the track, in the tone's icon colour (a warning bar's in its fill's orange), and its stripes move one tile every `motion.duration.slower`; under reduced motion they stand still and the bar stays hatched. It never looks like a finished bar.
- **Done**: remove the bar, or replace it with what the task made. A full bar left on the page reads as a task still running.

## Content

- **Name what is progressing**, with `ProgressLabel` or `aria-label`: "Uploading evidence", "Importing the catalogue". Not "Progress", not "Loading".
- **Show the value when the reader needs it**, as a count where a count means more than a percentage ("41 of 80").
- **One bar per task.** Several uploads are several bars, each named for its file.

## Stacked

`ProgressStacked` draws each segment as its share of the total; a zero segment is skipped. A `hatched` segment is what is not known or not covered, a hole in the record rather than a colour. With `label`, the bar is an image named by the label and its segments' titles; when segments have `onClick`, it is a group of buttons named by the label, each segment's button named by its title, and a segment that does not click is read as its title, so no part of the breakdown is lost. Without `label` it is decorative and the numbers beside it carry the values.

## Accessibility

- **Progress is a progressbar** with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax`, and `aria-valuetext` from Base UI's formatting in the provider's locale. While the value is unknown it has no `aria-valuenow`, and its value text is the locale's "In progress". `getAriaValueText` replaces either.
- **A clickable segment is a button** in the tab order. The bar does not clip its segments, so a focused segment's ring shows whole around it, in Highlight under forced colours. On a touch screen its hit area is 24px tall across the segment's own width, so neighbours never overlap.
- **Reduced motion** stops the stripes and the width transition; the hatching and the value stay.
- **Forced colours** keep the track's boundary and the fill, the segments and the hatching in system colours.

## Don't

A share of a whole, such as coverage, is a ProgressStacked or a number in words, never a progressbar, which a screen reader hears as a task part done. A wait of unknown length is `value={null}`, never a full bar.
