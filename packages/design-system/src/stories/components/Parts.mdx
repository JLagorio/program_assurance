# Small parts

## Toggle, ToggleGroup, ButtonGroup

A Toggle is one thing on or off: the subtle button with a recessed on state. A ToggleGroup is one of several views, always exactly one on: the recessed segmented control. A ButtonGroup joins Buttons that belong together with a hairline between.
