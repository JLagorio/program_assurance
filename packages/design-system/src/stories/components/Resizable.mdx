# Resizable

Panes a reader sizes for themselves: a list beside the record it opens, a tree beside the record beside its preview, a log under a form. **Resizable** is the group, **Resizable.Panel** a pane, **Resizable.Handle** the hairline between two panes that the reader drags or moves with the keys. Sizes are shares of the group.

It is for a trade the reader makes between two things they look at together. A page's sections stack and the page scrolls; a rail the reader only hides is the shell's Panel with its toggle; a rule that divides is a [Separator](?path=/docs/components-separator--docs).

## Anatomy

| Part   | What it is                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Group  | `Resizable`: a flex row, or a column when `vertical`, filling its container. The container sets the height; the group has none of its own.                                               |
| Panel  | `Resizable.Panel`: one pane, clipping its content. A list inside is a [ScrollArea](?path=/docs/components-scrollarea--docs), so the pane scrolls and the page does not.                 |
| Handle | `Resizable.Handle`: a 1px rule in `color.border` with an 8px hit area either side. Under the pointer and on focus it turns `color.border.brand`. A `separator` with the pane's size.     |

## Variants

By orientation. **Horizontal**, the default: panes side by side, the handle a vertical hairline, Left and Right move it. **Vertical**: panes stacked, the handle horizontal, Up and Down. Two panes, or three with two handles; past three the reader is managing a layout rather than reading.

<Canvas of={Stories.Vertical} />

Where it lives: the [PreviewSplit](?path=/docs/patterns-previewsplit--docs), an index beside its preview rail, is a Resizable with the list at a floor of 55% and the rail between 18% and 45%. The shell's SideNav and Panel have their own splitters and are not this.

## Sizes

None of the group's own: it fills its container, and the container says how tall. A pane's `defaultSize`, `minSize` and `maxSize` are shares of the group in percent as numbers, or strings with a unit ("240px") for a pane that must hold a fixed width. Unsaid, the panes share the group equally. The handle is 1px; its hit area is `space.100` either side.

## States

| State      | Look                                                                                                                             |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rest       | The hairline, `color.border`.                                                                                                    |
| Hover      | `color.border.brand` and the resize cursor, in `motion.duration.fast`.                                                           |
| Dragging   | `color.border.brand` while the pointer is down; the panes follow it within their floors and ceilings.                            |
| Focus      | `color.border.brand`, so the keyboard reader sees which handle the arrows move. The kit's outline is not drawn on a 1px rule.    |
| Collapsed  | A `collapsible` pane at nothing; its handle stays, and Enter on it brings the pane before it back to its floor.                  |

## Modifiers

- **`orientation`**, as in Variants.
- **`defaultSize`, `minSize`, `maxSize`** on a Panel, as in Sizes. A floor keeps a list readable; a ceiling keeps a rail a rail.
- **`collapsible`** on a Panel: dragged under its floor it folds to nothing, and Enter on the handle after it folds and unfolds it. Enter works on the pane before the handle only, so a pane that folds by key comes first: a tree, a list. A rail after the handle folds by drag.
- **`persist`** on the group: a key under which the reader's sizes are kept, one per split, so it opens as they left it. Give each Panel an `id` so the sizes find their panes. The shell keeps its widths the same way.
- **`onResize`** on a Panel: called as it resizes, with its share and its pixels, for a pane that lays itself out by width.
- **`label`** on a Handle: its accessible name, "Resize" unsaid; say what it sizes when a page has two.

## Content

- **Two things the reader looks at together:** a list and the row it opens, a tree and the record, a form and its log. Never two sections of one record.
- **The list has a floor** that keeps its names readable, and the rail a ceiling that keeps it a rail.
- **Defaults favour the reading pane:** the list at a third, the rail at a quarter.
- **A pane's content scrolls inside the pane,** in a ScrollArea. The page never scrolls because a pane grew.
- **Persist a split the reader returns to** (an index they work in), not one they see once (a dialog).

## Style

| Element | Token                                                                                                  |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Handle  | 1px `color.border` · `color.border.brand` hovered, dragging, focused · `motion.duration.fast` on `motion.easing.standard` |
| Hit     | `space.100` either side of the rule                                                                     |
| Cursor  | `col-resize`, `row-resize` when vertical                                                                |
| Panes   | No paint of their own; the content's. A frame around the group is the caller's border and radius.       |

## Accessibility

- **The handle is a `separator`** with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` for the pane before it, `aria-orientation`, and `aria-controls` to the panes; its name is `label`. The library sets the values.
- **Keyboard:** Tab reaches each handle. Left and Right (Up and Down when vertical) move it by 5%; Home and End take the pane to its floor and its ceiling; Enter folds and unfolds a `collapsible` pane before the handle; F6 and Shift+F6 move between the handles of one group. A double-click puts the pane back to its default.
- **Focus shows in the brand colour** on the rule, since an outline around a 1px line is the line.
- **The panes are ordinary content:** a ScrollArea inside is a tab stop named by its label, so a keyboard reader scrolls a list without a pointer.
- **The a11y gate** checks the roles, the names and the values in the matrix; the keys are the library's and are checked by hand.

### Resizable.Panel

| Prop          | Type                                       | What it is                                                             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `defaultSize` | `number \| string`                         | The share at first, in percent; a string carries its unit.             |
| `minSize`     | `number \| string`                         | The floor.                                                             |
| `maxSize`     | `number \| string`                         | The ceiling.                                                           |
| `collapsible` | `boolean`                                  | Folds to nothing under the floor; Enter on the handle after it folds and unfolds. |
| `id`          | `string`                                   | The pane's name for a persisted split.                                  |
| `onResize`    | `(size: { asPercentage, inPixels }) => void` | Called as it resizes.                                                |
| `className`   | `string`                                   | Layout only.                                                           |
| `children`    | `ReactNode`                                | The pane's content, in a ScrollArea when it is a list.                 |

### Resizable.Handle

| Prop        | Type     | What it is                                                     |
| ----------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `label`     | `string` | The accessible name. "Resize" unsaid.                          |
| `className` | `string` | Layout only.                                                   |
