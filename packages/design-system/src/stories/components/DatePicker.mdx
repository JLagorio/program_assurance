# DatePicker

One calendar day in a form, held and reported as an ISO day (`"2026-09-18"`, the contract of `input type="date"`) and shown in the LedgerProvider's locale ("Sep 18, 2026"). A day has no time and no zone, so it never shifts between readers. It is Ledger's composition of [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs) and [Calendar](?path=/docs/components-calendar--docs), with Today and Clear under the month.

Use it for a day: a due date, a planned completion, a milestone. For a moment in time (collected at, due at 5 PM) use [DateTimeField](?path=/docs/components-datetimefield--docs); for a time of day alone, [TimeField](?path=/docs/components-timefield--docs); for a range, [DateRangePicker](?path=/docs/components-daterangepicker--docs). An approximate or remembered date ("March 2024") is an [Input](?path=/docs/components-input--docs) with the format in its hint.

```tsx
<Field>
  <FieldLabel>Scheduled completion</FieldLabel>
  <DatePicker name="due" value={due} onValueChange={setDue} />
  <FieldDescription>When the milestone is due.</FieldDescription>
</Field>
```

## Picking or typing

`entry` chooses how the reader gives the day.

| `entry`          | What the reader sees                            | For                                                            |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `pick` (default) | A button that reads the day and opens the month | A day the reader looks for: the next review, a due date        |
| `type`           | A text field with a button that opens the month | A day the reader already knows: a signed date, a date of birth |

A typed day is read in the provider's locale when the reader presses Tab or Enter, never while typing: "Sep 18, 2026", "9/18/2026" (en-US order), "18.9.2026" (de-DE), "2026-09-18" and "9/18" (this year) all read, in the locale's digits or Latin ones. The field then shows the day in the locale's words. Text that is not a day, or a day outside `min`, `max` or `isDateUnavailable`, stays as the reader typed it, the field reports `""` and says what fixes it under itself: "Enter a date such as Sep 25, 2026.", "Enter Sep 1, 2026 or later." Escape puts back the last day. Alt+Down opens the month from the text.

The message is a FieldError inside the Field, so it describes the input and marks the Field invalid. `onEntryError` receives it (and `null` once the entry reads), for a form library that should show it in place of its own "Required.".

## Days the reader may not choose

`min` and `max` are ISO days; `isDateUnavailable(iso)` returns `true` or the reason as a sentence ("Weekends are closed."). Those days are disabled in the month and their names carry the reason ("Saturday, September 19, 2026, Not available. Weekends are closed."); the month does not page before `min` or after `max`; when today may not be chosen, Today is disabled but stays in the tab order and says why ("The latest date is Dec 31, 2025."), as Button's `disabledReason`; a typed day outside them is an error that names the limit. Say the rule in the hint as well, where every reader sees it before choosing.

`calendarProps` passes the month's own options: `captionLayout="dropdown"` for the year dropdown when the day is a year or more away, `startMonth` and `endMonth`, `showWeekNumber`, `fixedWeeks`.

## Values and forms

Use `value` and `onValueChange` for controlled state or `defaultValue` for uncontrolled; `onChange` is the older spelling of `onValueChange`, deprecated and kept for one version. Choosing reports an ISO day and clearing reports `""`. A value that is not an ISO day warns once in the console: a timestamp is read as its day in the provider's zone, and anything else ("2026-02-30") as empty, so the field never shows one day and submits another. A hidden input carries the day under `name`, including for an owning form named by `form`; a disabled picker submits nothing. A native form reset restores an uncontrolled default; a controlled caller resets its own value.

## Accessibility

- Inside a Field the picker is the Field's control: the FieldLabel names it without ids, and the hint, the error and the Field's `invalid` and `disabled` reach it. Outside one, give it `aria-label`.
- The picking button's name is its label, and it is described by the chosen day first, then the hint and the error, so a reader tabbing through a form hears the value without opening the month. The month is named after its field.
- The month takes focus when it opens. Choosing, Clear and Escape return focus to the field, including inside a Dialog, which stays open.
- A typed field is a text input and announces required; a button cannot carry `aria-required`, so the picking form says required in the label's asterisk and the form's check.
- The month keeps the Calendar's keyboard: arrows by day and week, Page Up and Page Down by month, Shift with them by year, Home and End by week, one tab stop.
