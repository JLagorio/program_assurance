# Overlays

Everything that floats over the page, from the smallest label to a full task. Four are anchored to the thing that opened them and four cover the page under a blanket. The anchored and modal overlays all use Base UI. Modal overlays manage focus and page scrolling; anchored previews keep focus on their trigger. The surfaces share one recipe, `elevation.surface.overlay`, `elevation.shadow.overlay`, the large radius, the enter and exit motion, so a product's overlays read as one family.

Each part has its own page: [Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-tooltip--docs), [HoverCard](?path=/docs/components-hovercard--docs), [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs), [DropdownMenu](?path=/docs/components-dropdownmenu--docs), [Dialog](?path=/docs/components-dialog--docs), [Sheet](?path=/docs/components-sheet--docs), [Drawer](?path=/docs/components-drawer--docs), [AlertDialog](?path=/docs/components-alertdialog--docs).

## Variants

| Need                                                             | Use              | Opens on                            | Closes on                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| A short label                                                    | **Tooltip**      | Hover after 300ms; focus at once    | Leaving, Escape                        |
| A peek at a record: a few facts, no actions                      | **HoverCard**    | Hover or focus, after 600ms         | Leaving, Escape                        |
| A small task at its button: a filter, a reason, a picker         | **Popover**      | Click                               | Escape, outside click, Done            |
| A list of actions                                                | **DropdownMenu** | Click, Enter, Space, the down arrow | Choosing, Escape, outside click        |
| A focused task over the page                                     | **Dialog**       | The caller's state                  | Escape, the blanket, Cancel, the verb  |
| A modal selection, review or explicitly scoped parent-draft task | **Sheet**        | The caller's state                  | Escape, the blanket, the footer's verb |
| The bottom sheet, for a narrow screen and a quick task           | **Drawer**       | The caller's state                  | A drag down, Escape, the blanket       |
| A decision that needs a word before it happens                   | **AlertDialog**  | The caller's state                  | Cancel, Escape, the verb               |

## Anatomy

- **The anchored four** sit beside their trigger, 4px off it and centred on it, flipping when there is no room, with Tooltip adding a small arrow. A Tooltip is the dark one, `color.background.neutral.bold` with inverse text; the other three are the overlay surface with a hairline border. Popover, HoverCard and DropdownMenu are capped at the height the window leaves on their side and scroll within it, so a calendar or a long list never opens past the edge. Content can anchor to something other than its trigger (a cell, a chart mark, a virtual point) through `anchor`, and takes `collisionPadding`, `collisionBoundary`, `collisionAvoidance`, `sticky` and `positionMethod`.
- **Text size follows the job.** A Tooltip's label and a Popover's loose text are `font.body.small`, since they sit beside the control they explain and a Popover's own fields set their size; a HoverCard is `font.body`, since it previews the record's own words.
- **The blanketed four** paint `color.blanket` over the page; nothing else paints it. Their surface is `elevation.surface.overlay` with `radius.xxlarge` (a Sheet has square corners, since it is an edge), a header with the title in `font.heading.xsmall`, a body part that scrolls (DialogBody, SheetBody, DrawerBody, AlertDialogBody), and a footer on the same surface, divided by a hairline, with the buttons at the end. The title is the overlay's h2; headings inside start at h3. Titles and descriptions wrap an unbroken value, such as a code or a server's error, instead of running past the edge.
- **Overlays record their surface.** The Dialog, Sheet, Drawer, AlertDialog, Popover and HoverCard popups set `utility.elevation.surface.current` to the overlay surface, so a sticky table header, a pinned cell or a scroll arrow inside paints the overlay's colour, not the page's.
- **The close** is one shared icon button at the top end, `dimension.control.small`, and first in the Tab order, where it is drawn; the first field still takes focus when the overlay opens. AlertDialog and Drawer compose explicit action buttons; a Drawer’s optional swipe handle is decorative.

## Accessibility

- **Focus moves in** when a blanketed overlay opens (Drawer focuses its popup by default; initialFocus can target an action or field) and **returns** to what opened it when it closes. A Dialog, Sheet or AlertDialog opened from state records the element that had focus as it opens, so focus comes back to it even when a field inside took focus as it mounted; `finalFocus` names another element when the opener goes away with the task. The page behind is hidden from assistive technology and does not scroll.
- **Escape closes** every overlay. An outside click closes the anchored ones and the blanket closes a Dialog, a Sheet and a Drawer; an AlertDialog ignores it. A press on a blanket that does not close its overlay (an AlertDialog's, a pending one's) leaves focus where it was.
- **Forced colours keep the edges.** A Dialog, AlertDialog, Sheet, Drawer and Tooltip draw a CanvasText outline, since forced colours replace their fill and remove their shadow, and the blanket stays a veil of Canvas; Popover and HoverCard keep their border.
- **Pending holds.** `pending` on a Dialog, Sheet or AlertDialog cancels every request to close (Escape, the blanket, Close), disables the close controls while keeping them focusable (`aria-disabled`), so focus on one is kept, and marks the popup `aria-busy` until the command returns; PickerSheet's `pending` is its Sheet's. A Drawer cancels in its own `onOpenChange`.
- **A tooltip never holds a control**, a hover card never holds an action, a popover never holds a task the reader must finish.

## Style

- **One blanket at a time.** An AlertDialog may open over a Sheet or a Dialog, for the decision the task ends in; that is the only stack. Render it inside the Dialog or Sheet that owns the decision: only the top dialog then dismisses, the page keeps its one blanket, and the Dialog or Sheet under the prompt dims under `color.blanket`, fading on the motion tokens, so its own buttons read as out of play in both modes. Independent stacked record workflows should navigate or replace the current frame. A selection workflow owns its internal preview.
- **A sheet's next record is its next frame**, not a second sheet: the same surface, a Back chevron before the title (PreviewSheet's and PickerSheet's `onBack`), the stack in the URL so the browser's back agrees.
- **Anchored overlays stack on anything.** A Tooltip in a Dialog, a DropdownMenu in a Sheet, a Popover in a Drawer are fine; a Popover in a Popover is not.

## Props

- **Trigger composition follows each component's API**: Popover uses `PopoverTrigger render={<Button />}`; DropdownMenu uses `DropdownMenuTrigger render` and `DropdownMenuContent`; Tooltip uses `TooltipTrigger render` and `TooltipContent`. HoverCard uses `HoverCardTrigger`, an anchor by default, with `render` for a custom link. Custom trigger components must forward their props and ref.
- **The blanketed four are opened by state**: `open` and `onOpenChange`. An act sets it; the caller clears it. Nothing opens a Dialog for the reader who did not ask.
- **Where the task lives decides the overlay.** At the button: Popover. An independent focused form: Dialog. Beside a usable list: Shell.Panel. A specifically modal selection or review task: Sheet. Under the thumb: Drawer.

## Content

- The blanket is `color.blanket`; nothing else paints it.
- A blanketed overlay's title is the act's verb (a Dialog), the record's name (a Sheet), or the question (an AlertDialog). Its primary button says the verb, never OK.
- What the reader must know is on the page or in the body, never only in a tooltip or a hover card.
- An overlay is never the record. The record has a page; the overlay is the way to it or the task on it.

## Sizes

Choose the smallest surface that fits the task. Dialog and Sheet take a `width` step (`small`, `medium`, `large`, `xlarge`, `fullscreen`) rather than pixels, so tasks of the same kind open at the same size; AlertDialog has `size`. Popover and HoverCard keep a runtime `style.width`. Content must reflow at a 320 CSS pixel viewport: the body part scrolls between a fixed header and footer, and in a window under 30rem tall the whole popup scrolls with the footer held in view. Check long titles, translated buttons, and browser zoom. A large task with navigation belongs on a page.

## States

Closed, open, pending, failed and completed are separate states. Keep the surface and draft open on failure and explain how to retry. A pending operation must settle or expose a recovery path. Close only after a successful command or an explicit cancel.

## Modifiers

Modal content takes Base UI's `initialFocus` and `finalFocus`. Base UI returns focus to the element that had it before the surface opened; pass `finalFocus` when that element goes away with the task, and move focus to a first field with `initialFocus`, never with `autoFocus`, which becomes the return target instead. `pending` on Dialog, Sheet and AlertDialog prevents dismissal while a command is in flight.

## Related

See Forms for validation and focus on error, Button for activation semantics, and Toaster for nonblocking completion feedback. Each overlay's page documents its exact dismissal contract.

## Don't

Do not rely on a toast as the only record of a failure, hide required instructions in a tooltip, nest independent modal tasks, or remove the opener without specifying a meaningful focus destination. Do not make a hover-only surface the sole route to an action.
