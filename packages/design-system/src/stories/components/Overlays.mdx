# Overlays

Everything that floats over the page, from the smallest label to a full task. Four are anchored to the thing that opened them and four cover the page under a blanket. The anchored and modal overlays all use Base UI. Modal overlays manage focus and page scrolling; anchored previews keep focus on their trigger. The surfaces share one recipe, `elevation.surface.overlay`, `elevation.shadow.overlay`, the large radius, the enter and exit motion, so a product's overlays read as one family.

Each part has its own page: [Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-tooltip--docs), [HoverCard](?path=/docs/components-hovercard--docs), [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs), [DropdownMenu](?path=/docs/components-dropdownmenu--docs), [Dialog](?path=/docs/components-dialog--docs), [Sheet](?path=/docs/components-sheet--docs), [Drawer](?path=/docs/components-drawer--docs), [AlertDialog](?path=/docs/components-alertdialog--docs).

## Variants

| Need                                                       | Use              | Opens on                            | Closes on                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| A short label                                              | **Tooltip**      | Hover or focus, after 300ms         | Leaving, Escape                        |
| A peek at a record: a few facts, no actions                | **HoverCard**    | Hover or focus, after 600ms         | Leaving, Escape                        |
| A small task at its button: a filter, a reason, a picker   | **Popover**      | Click                               | Escape, outside click, Done            |
| A list of actions                                          | **DropdownMenu** | Click, Enter, Space, the down arrow | Choosing, Escape, outside click        |
| A focused task over the page                               | **Dialog**       | The caller's state                  | Escape, the blanket, Cancel, the verb  |
| A record's detail beside its list; a picker; a form behind | **Sheet**        | The caller's state                  | Escape, the blanket, the footer's verb |
| The bottom sheet, for a narrow screen and a quick task     | **Drawer**       | The caller's state                  | A drag down, Escape, the blanket       |
| A decision that needs a word before it happens             | **AlertDialog**  | The caller's state                  | Cancel, Escape, the verb               |

## Anatomy

- **The anchored four** sit beside their trigger, 4 to 6px off it, flipping when there is no room, with Tooltip adding a small arrow. A Tooltip is the dark one, `color.background.neutral.bold` with inverse text; the other three are the overlay surface with a hairline border.
- **The blanketed four** paint `color.blanket` over the page; nothing else paints it. Their surface is `elevation.surface.overlay` with `radius.xxlarge` (a Sheet has square corners, since it is an edge), a header with the title in `font.heading.xsmall`, a body that scrolls, a footer on `elevation.surface.sunken` with the buttons at the end.
- **The close** is one shared icon button at the top end, `dimension.control.small`. AlertDialog and Drawer compose explicit action buttons; a Drawer’s optional swipe handle is decorative.

## Accessibility

- **Focus moves in** when a blanketed overlay opens (Drawer focuses its popup by default; initialFocus can target an action or field) and **returns** to what opened it when it closes. The page behind is hidden from assistive technology and does not scroll.
- **Escape closes** every overlay. An outside click closes the anchored ones and the blanket closes a Dialog, a Sheet and a Drawer; an AlertDialog ignores it.
- **Pending holds.** A Dialog or an AlertDialog saving takes `pending`: Escape, the blanket and the close do nothing until the save returns.
- **A tooltip never holds a control**, a hover card never holds an action, a popover never holds a task the reader must finish.

## Style

- **One blanket at a time.** An AlertDialog may open over a Sheet or a Dialog, for the decision the task ends in; that is the only stack. A Dialog over a Dialog, a Sheet over a Sheet, is a sign the first should have been a page.
- **A sheet's next record is its next frame**, not a second sheet: the same surface, the chevron before the title, `onBack`, the stack in the URL so the browser's back agrees.
- **Anchored overlays stack on anything.** A Tooltip in a Dialog, a DropdownMenu in a Sheet, a Popover in a Drawer are fine; a Popover in a Popover is not.

## Props

- **Trigger composition follows each component's API**: Popover uses `PopoverTrigger render={<Button />}`; DropdownMenu uses `DropdownMenuTrigger render` and `DropdownMenuContent`; Tooltip uses `TooltipTrigger render` and `TooltipContent`. HoverCard uses `HoverCardTrigger`, an anchor by default, with `render` for a custom link. Custom trigger components must forward their props and ref.
- **The blanketed four are opened by state**: `open` and `onClose`. An act sets it; the caller clears it. Nothing opens a Dialog for the reader who did not ask.
- **Where the task lives decides the overlay.** At the button: Popover. Over the page: Dialog. Beside the list: Sheet. Under the thumb: Drawer.

## Content

- The blanket is `color.blanket`; nothing else paints it.
- A blanketed overlay's title is the act's verb (a Dialog), the record's name (a Sheet), or the question (an AlertDialog). Its primary button says the verb, never OK.
- What the reader must know is on the page or in the body, never only in a tooltip or a hover card.
- An overlay is never the record. The record has a page; the overlay is the way to it or the task on it.

## Sizes

Choose the smallest surface that fits the task. Content must reflow at a 320 CSS pixel viewport; the body scrolls independently when a header and footer must remain available. Check long titles, translated buttons, and browser zoom. A large task with navigation belongs on a page.

## States

Closed, open, pending, failed and completed are separate states. Keep the surface and draft open on failure and explain how to retry. A pending operation must settle or expose a recovery path. Close only after a successful command or an explicit cancel.

## Modifiers

Controlled modal surfaces accept `returnFocusRef` for asynchronous opening, a removed trigger, or a deliberate replacement target. Without it, they capture the active opener before moving focus into the surface. The target must remain connected and focusable after closing. `pending` prevents dismissal while supported saves are in flight.

## Related

See Forms for validation and focus on error, Button for activation semantics, and Toaster for nonblocking completion feedback. Each overlay's page documents its exact dismissal contract.

## Don't

Do not rely on a toast as the only record of a failure, hide required instructions in a tooltip, nest independent modal tasks, or remove the opener without specifying a meaningful focus destination. Do not make a hover-only surface the sole route to an action.
