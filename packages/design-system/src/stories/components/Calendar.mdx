# Calendar

A month, or two, the reader picks a day or a range from. It is react-day-picker with the tokens on it: 32px cells, today semibold, the chosen day in the brand fill, a range painted in the selection colour between its ends. On its own it is a surface, not a field: it lives inside a [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs), inside a [Popover](?path=/docs/components-popover--docs) as a range filter, or inside a [Dialog](?path=/docs/components-dialog--docs) that exists to pick a day. It never sits inline in a form.

## Anatomy

| Part          | What it is                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Caption       | The month and the year, `font.body` at `font.weight.medium`, centred. It is a label, not a menu.                                            |
| Previous, next| Two `dimension.control.small` buttons at the corners, chevrons in `color.icon.subtle`, paging one month.                                    |
| Weekdays      | The seven names, `font.body.xsmall` at `font.weight.medium` in `color.text.subtle`, 32px wide each.                                          |
| Days          | 32px cells, `radius.medium`, `font.body`. Today is `font.weight.semibold` with no dot. Days of the neighbouring months show in `color.text.subtlest`. |
| Selection     | One day: `color.background.brand.bold` with `color.text.inverse`. A range: the ends in the brand fill, the days between on `color.background.selected`. |

## Variants

| Mode     | Use                                                                                                          |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `single` | One day. What a DatePicker holds, and what a Dialog asks for when the day is the whole task.                 |
| `range`  | A start and an end. A filter over a table's dates, in a Popover whose trigger shows the range in words.       |

One month is the rule; `numberOfMonths={2}` for a range that may cross a month. There is no `multiple` mode, no month or year menu in the caption, and no week numbers. The picker is for near days, reached by paging; a far date is typed into an [Input](?path=/docs/components-input--docs).

## Sizes

One size. A cell is 32px, a month is seven cells and `space.150` of padding wide, about 248px, and as tall as its weeks. Two months sit side by side and stack on a narrow screen. The Calendar does not scale with a field; a DatePicker's month is this size whatever the field's width.

## States

| State        | What changes                                                                                                   |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rest         | The day's number in `color.text`.                                                                              |
| Hover        | The cell takes `color.background.neutral.subtle.hovered`.                                                      |
| Focus        | The focus outline on the cell, on keyboard focus.                                                              |
| Today        | `font.weight.semibold`. Nothing else: no ring, no dot.                                                         |
| Selected     | `color.background.brand.bold`, the number in `color.text.inverse`.                                             |
| In a range   | `color.background.selected` behind the number, the ends in the brand fill with the outer corners rounded.      |
| Outside      | A day of the month before or after, in `color.text.subtlest`; it can be picked.                                |
| Disabled     | `color.text.disabled`, not clickable, skipped by the keyboard.                                                 |

## Modifiers

- **`mode`** `single` or `range`, with **`selected`** and **`onSelect`** for the value. Controlled in a form; uncontrolled in a Dialog that returns the day on its primary Button.
- **`defaultMonth`** for the month shown first: the chosen day's, else today's.
- **`disabled`** with a matcher for the days the reader cannot pick: `{ before: today }` for a completion date, `{ after: today }` for an observed date.
- **`numberOfMonths`** `2` for a range.
- **`required`** in single mode, so a second click on the chosen day does not clear it. The DatePicker clears with its own button instead.
- **`showOutsideDays`** is on; turn it off only when the grid must be exactly the month.

## Content

The Calendar has no words of its own beyond the month's name, the weekday names and the numbers, all in the reader's locale through date-fns. The task's words belong to what holds it: the Popover's label ("Choose a date"), the Dialog's title, the DatePicker's Today and Clear.

## Accessibility

- **It is a grid.** react-day-picker renders the month as a grid labelled with the month's name, the weekdays as column headers, and every day as a button named with its full date. Today is marked, the chosen day is selected, disabled days are disabled.
- **Keyboard:** with a day focused, Left and Right move a day, Up and Down a week, PageUp and PageDown a month, Shift with them a year, Home and End to the week's ends; Enter or Space picks. Tab moves between the previous button, the next button and the grid.
- **Previous and next are named** "Previous month" and "Next month".
- **The selection colours pass**: the brand fill carries inverse text at 4.5:1, and the range's selection background carries the default text; the contrast test covers both in both modes.
- **Three months on one page** each carry their own navigation region, which the accessibility gate reports as duplicate landmarks; the matrix story turns that rule off and says so.

## Props

Every react-day-picker prop passes through. The ones the kit uses:

| Prop                    | Use                                                                          |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `mode`                  | `single` or `range`.                                                         |
| `selected`, `onSelect`  | The value: a `Date`, or `{ from, to }`.                                      |
| `defaultMonth`          | The month shown first.                                                       |
| `disabled`              | The days that cannot be picked: `{ before }`, `{ after }`, a list, a function. |
| `numberOfMonths`        | `1`, or `2` for a range.                                                     |
| `required`              | Single mode: a second click does not clear.                                  |
| `showOutsideDays`       | On by default.                                                               |
| `className`, `classNames` | Layout only; the look is the kit's.                                        |
