# Calendar

**Status: Stable.**

A month to pick a day, several days or a range from, following [shadcn’s Calendar](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/base/calendar) on React DayPicker, with Ledger tokens and the LedgerProvider's locale.

Use it inside a picker. A day in a form is a [DatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs), a range a [DateRangePicker](?path=/docs/components-daterangepicker--docs); an inline month takes the room of six fields for one answer. Use Calendar on its own where the month is the content, such as a schedule panel.

<Canvas of={Stories.DoDont} />

```tsx
const [date, setDate] = useState<Date>();
<Calendar mode="single" selected={date} onSelect={setDate} />;
```

Calendar takes DayPicker's own props: `mode` (single, multiple or range), `selected` and `onSelect`, disabled matchers, `startMonth` and `endMonth`, `numberOfMonths`, `captionLayout="dropdown"`, `showWeekNumber`. Custom `components`, `formatters`, `labels` and `classNames` merge with Calendar's defaults; customize a day with the exported CalendarDayButton so DayPicker's focus, selection and events stay intact. `buttonVariant` sets the navigation buttons. Days are 32px cells, above the 24px touch minimum.

## Days and zones

Calendar's Dates stand for calendar days: the local year, month and day, never an instant. They are labelled with `formatCalendarDate`, so a provider's `timeZone` never moves a day. Today is the reader's own day.

## Locale

Under a LedgerProvider the month speaks its locale: `lang` on the root, the first day of the week (Monday in de-DE, Saturday in ar-EG, Sunday in en-US), the caption, weekday names and their spoken names (the short form heads each column, or the narrow one where the short names are whole words, as in Arabic, and the full name is always the spoken one), the day digits, the year dropdown and week numbers. A caller's own date-fns `locale`, `weekStartsOn`, `lang`, `labels` and `formatters` win. Two calendars on one page name their navigation apart through `labels.labelNav`, or the navigation landmark repeats.

## Ranges

With `mode="range"` the first and last days are the blue budget and the days between take the selected tint; `numberOfMonths={2}` spans a range across months. Below the `sm` window the months always stack; above it the calendar's own space decides, so a range in a 320px panel stacks on a wide screen. The range's ends say which end they are ("Start of range", "End of range") and a completed range is announced once, politely: "September 7, 2026 to October 9, 2026 selected", or "September 7, 2026 selected" for a one-day range.

## Disabled days

A disabled matcher makes a day unchoosable and skips it in the keyboard order. `describeDay(date, modifiers)` adds the reason to its name ("Weekends are closed."), after the kit's date, today, selected and range words. Keep the rule in the field's hint too; DatePicker and DateRangePicker do both from `min`, `max` and `isDateUnavailable`.

## Navigation

`navLayout="around"` places Previous beside the first month's caption and Next beside the last, in either direction. The default and `"after"` share one navigation bar; `"after"` puts it later in the keyboard order.

## Content

- **Name a month that stands alone by its task**: a visible heading over it, or an `aria-label` such as "Review days", never "Calendar". In a DatePicker the month's dialog takes the field's label.
- **A disabled day says why** in a sentence through `describeDay` ("Weekends are closed."), and the field's hint states the same rule, since the reader meets the hint before the month.
- **Two months on one page name their navigation apart** through `labels.labelNav` ("Review window navigation").
- **Leave the caption and the weekday names to the locale.** A `formatters` override wins over the LedgerProvider, so one that writes English words stays English under every locale.

## Accessibility

- DayPicker owns the grid keyboard: arrows by day and week, Page Up and Page Down by month, Shift with them by year, Home and End by week, one tab stop. The day button takes focus when DayPicker marks it focused.
- Every day's name is the full date in the locale, with today, selected or its range end, and any reason from `describeDay`.
- Selection is shown by fill and survives forced colours: the selected day and the range keep their own colours there.
- Give a standalone Calendar a visible heading or an accessible name.
