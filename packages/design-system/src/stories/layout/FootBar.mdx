# FootBar

**Status: Experimental.** New with the November 2026 proposal's workspaces; the props may change while its first screens try it. Owned by the design system.

A workspace's one finalizing act, docked under the work: what is judged and what it found at the start, what is left and the act at the end. It takes [DataTable](?path=/docs/patterns-data-table--docs)'s SelectionBar anatomy (the count, then the verbs) at the 60px of `dimension.layout.footer`, and sticks to the foot of the window, or of the scroller it sits in, while the work scrolls above it.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />

## Anatomy

| Part    | Prop      | What it is                                                                                                           |
| ------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Summary | `summary` | What is judged, a count in its words, at medium weight: "56 of 58 recorded", "3 decided".                            |
| Detail  | `detail`  | After the summary, subtle: what the work found ("5 controls not satisfied") or what is left ("85 left").             |
| Reason  | `reason`  | Before the act, what is left and so why it waits: "To judge" and a link to each record left.                         |
| Act     | `actions` | The one act: a 36px primary Button, a sign Button with Return beside it, or a PageHeader.Slot that waits on someone. |

## States

The bar is the same in every state; only what it holds changes. While work is left, `reason` links to what is left and the act keeps its place with Button's `disabledReason`. Ready, the act is the 36px primary. Under way, the act shows `isLoading`. If it fails, `failed` draws the summary in the danger tone with its icon ("Not sent"), the detail says the draft is kept ("58 determinations kept as a draft"), and the act retries ("Send again"). Sent, the end is a [PageHeader.Slot](?path=/docs/layout-pageheader--docs) `waitingOn` the person who acts next, with Remind. A signature is Return beside a Button with `intent="sign"`, whose Command+Enter signs.

## Counts read

The summary and its detail are said whenever they change, politely, so a reader who judges by key hears the count move without looking down; a failure is said at once. Nothing is said as the bar first draws.

<Canvas of={Stories.CountsRead} />

## Content

- **The summary is a count with its noun:** "56 of 58 recorded", never a percentage alone.
- **One act.** The bar never holds two primaries; the record's other commands stay in its header.
- **Say what was kept** when the act fails, so the reader knows nothing was lost.

## Accessibility

- The bar is not a landmark; it comes after the work in reading order, and its act is a Tab stop like any other.
- A waiting act keeps its place and is described by its `disabledReason`; the records left are links.
- In a narrow bar the end takes the next line, and a wait and its Remind take a line each.

## Props

FootBar is a `div` that forwards native props and its ref, and names itself with `data-slot` (`foot-bar`, with `foot-bar-summary`, `foot-bar-end`, `foot-bar-reason` and `foot-bar-actions` inside). A failed bar carries `data-failed`.
