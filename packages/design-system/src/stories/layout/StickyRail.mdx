# StickyRail

**Status: Stable.**

A column of a page's grid that stays in view under the shell's banner and top nav while the page scrolls: a wizard's steps beside its form. It stops `space.300` under them (the Shell's `--shell-top`; outside a Shell, the top nav's height) and is at most the window's height under them. In a scroller of its own, a dialog's body or a panel, it sticks to that scroller instead: an ancestor sets `--rail-top`, where the rail stops in it, and `--rail-max-height`, the scroller's height.

`from="lg"` is for a rail the page's Grid puts beside the content from the large breakpoint and stacks above it below: below `lg` it stays in the flow and scrolls away with the page, so a phone never keeps the steps over the form. Without `from` it always sticks.

A rail that the grid stacks above the content on a phone and has no `from` sticks there too, and covers the form the reader is filling in.

<Canvas of={Stories.DoDont} />

```tsx
<Grid gap="space.300" templateColumns={{ lg: "200px minmax(0,1fr)" }}>
  <StickyRail from="lg">
    <Stepper orientation="responsive" label="Program setup">
      …
    </Stepper>
  </StickyRail>
  <Stack space="space.250">…the step's form…</Stack>
</Grid>
```

## When to use it

- **Use it** for a short column that orients the reader while they work down a long page beside it: a wizard's steps, the contents of a long document.
- **Not** for a record's Details: those are the [Shell](?path=/docs/layout-shell--docs)'s Aside, which the Shell places beside Main and turns into a Details disclosure on a phone.
- **Not** for a list beside its detail: that is a [WorkPane](?path=/docs/patterns-workpane--docs), whose list sticks the same way.
- **Not** for a page frame or a register's table: Main scrolls the page, and DataTable `fill` takes the rest of the window.

## Accessibility

The rail is a plain `div`: it adds no landmark or role. What it holds names itself, as a Stepper's list does by its `label`. Its content keeps the reading and tab order of where it is rendered, before the content beside it.

## Props

<ArgTypes of={StickyRail} />

StickyRail renders one `div`, forwards its native props, a `className` and its ref to it, shrinks in its grid track (`min-w-0`) and names itself last with `data-slot="sticky-rail"`.
