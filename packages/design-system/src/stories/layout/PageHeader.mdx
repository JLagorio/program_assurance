# PageHeader

**Status: Stable.**

One composable page header for collections and records. Two columns: Heading, what names the page, on the left; Actions on the right; Lead across both above them. Native props and refs pass through every part.

<Canvas of={Stories.Constrained} />

Compose a Heading with a Title, an optional Lead and one primary action or an Actions menu. A breadcrumb is the Lead through `render={<Breadcrumb />}`; a category line is a Lead with text. A register's header is its name alone: its create action is the toolbar's primary. A record's header is the trail, the name, its facts line and its actions: a code goes in the trail, and under the name PageHeader.Facts says where the record stands and the few facts the reader acts on, each labelled (below). Editors, property strips and the record's other facts belong in the work area or the Aside, not in PageHeader or a preview header.

<Canvas of={Stories.DoDont} />

Use PageHeader.Title once for the page's h1. Actions sit at the end of the title's row while the row can give the title its measure (14rem) beside them; when it cannot, on a phone or in a narrow panel, they take the next row, right-aligned, and the title wraps at its full width. The same rule holds on a page, in a Shell.Panel and in a Sheet (the Stacked story). Keep action groups on one line; use a menu when several actions compete for space.

## When to use

- **Every page and every preview's record.** A page's header names it; a preview's inner header names its record, below the panel's navigation-only header.
- **Not for a section of a page.** A titled block inside the page is a [Section](?path=/docs/layout-section--docs).
- **Not for a dialog's title.** A dialog or a sheet has its own header part; PageHeader.Title renders through it with `render` only where a sheet's body starts with a record header, as in PreviewSheet.

## Actions and their sizes

| Where                    | Actions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A register               | None. The create action is the Toolbar's small primary.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| A record page            | One default-size primary Button, or one default-size Actions menu: a Button labelled Actions with a trailing ChevronDown, its items the record's operations, and a link item for each destination. Or the action slot, whose act, sign and Remind are 36px. |
| A preview's inner header | One small primary, then the rest behind a subtle small IconButton that opens a menu.                                                                                                                                                                        |

A destination in an Actions menu is a link item (`DropdownMenuLinkItem`, or an item rendered as a router link), so it opens in a new tab; a Button in the header acts, never navigates.

## The action slot

`PageHeader.Slot` is a record's one place for what happens next, at the end of the title's row in PageHeader.Actions' place, and it keeps Actions' wrap rule. It has four forms and no others, one prop each, and TypeScript refuses two at once:

| Form    | Prop        | What it draws                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Advance | `advance`   | The reader's own next step: why it is next (`reason`, at medium weight, and a smaller `detail` under it), then the act, a 36px primary named by `label`. The reason is the act's description. `onAction` runs it here; `link` makes it a LinkButton to another page. |
| Sign    | `sign`      | A signature the reader owes: "Signing as" and their `role`, then a [Button](?path=/docs/components-button--docs) with `intent="sign"` and the reader as `signer`, which Command+Enter presses. The words are its description.                                        |
| Waiting | `waitingOn` | Who the record waits on, by `name` and `role`, and since when (`since`, said relative to now with the full moment in its tooltip), then Remind, a 36px secondary described by the wait, where the reader may remind (`onRemind`).                                    |
| None    | none        | Nothing of its own: the record has no next step here, and the slot holds only `more`.                                                                                                                                                                                |

`more` is the record's other commands behind a subtle 36px icon button at the slot's end: `label` names the record ("More control actions") and `items` are the menu's items, Inspect record last. Every form may have it. A slot with no form and no `more` draws nothing.

The slot never shows a disabled primary: when the reader cannot take the next step, the record waits on someone (Waiting) or has none (None). One slot a record, and nothing else in the header's actions. The slot, the facts line and the standing are experimental: their API may change before they are stable.

## The facts line and the standing

`PageHeader.Facts` is the line under the name, inside PageHeader.Heading after the Title: a short row of labelled facts in the body size, four at most, each a [Fact](?path=/docs/components-fact--docs) (its label small and subtle, its value at medium weight), in the order the reader needs them. It is a definition list, so a screen reader hears each label with its value. Where the line has the room the facts share it; narrower, on a phone or in a panel, it wraps between facts, and every fact stays whole on its line.

`PageHeader.Standing` is one fact of that line, the one that says where the record stands: its `status` in its own short words ("ATO with conditions"), coloured by the `tone` its badge would take, and after it the record's most urgent `date`, flagged as [DateLabel](?path=/docs/components-datetime--docs) flags one: late in the danger tone with its icon and how long ago ("PDR 35d late", heard "35 days"), due today and due soon in the warning tone with theirs, a later day plainly ("TRR due Dec 4, 2026"). A date's `name` says what falls due; `kind="expiry"` reads expires and expired, and `soonWithin` flags a decision that expires a quarter ahead (90). Its `label` ("Authorization") is heard before its words and not drawn, since the status words name themselves, as a status beside a name does. `isLoading` holds its place with a line while it is read, and with no status it is an Absent.

The status in the header is this one fact in the body size: never a headline, never a badge among the actions, and never a coloured title. The name stays the h1 and the only heading.

Description is for a page outside the register and record shapes, such as a settings page or a setup step: one line of orientation under the title. It wraps, and a long unbroken value breaks rather than widening the page.

## Title levels

The Title is a [Heading](?path=/docs/primitives-heading--docs) at `page`, 24/30 semibold, and the h1 on a page. Inside a [HeadingLevelProvider](?path=/docs/primitives-heading--docs) it takes that level instead: a preview's inner record header, in a panel or a sheet whose outline starts at 2, renders the record's name as an h2 with the same type style. The content after that header goes one level below through a HeadingLevelProvider without `level`, so its Sections are h3s. `render` sets the element outright, `render={<h2 />}`, or a sheet's or a dialog's own title part where the surface needs its title for its accessible name; either keeps the `page` size. A title the PageHeader does not draw is a Heading at the size it matches, never a raw heading with the Title's classes: a change to the ramp would miss it.

## Accessibility

- **Not a landmark.** The root is a `div`: the page's landmarks are the shell's (Main, the navigation, the Aside and the Panel), and a `header` element inside a dialog or a sheet would become a second banner. `render={<header />}` gives a page the element where it wants one.
- **One h1 a page.** The Title is the page's h1; a preview's record title is an h2 through the heading-level context, never a second h1.
- **The trail is navigation.** A Breadcrumb Lead is a named navigation landmark whose last level is the current page (see [Breadcrumb](?path=/docs/components-breadcrumb--docs)).
- **A page change is announced by the Shell.** When the route changes, `Shell locationKey` moves focus to Main and says the page's name; the header needs no focus handling of its own.
- **Actions keep their names.** An Actions menu's trigger is named by its visible label; an icon-only overflow takes a `label` that names the record ("More artifact actions").

## Props

The root and Lead are a `div` or the element given to `render`, Heading and Actions are `div`s, and Description a `p`; each takes its element's native props and ref, and names itself last with `data-slot`. Title takes `render` and its element's props. Facts is a `dl` and Slot and Standing are `div`s, each with props of its own, below; the Standing holds its `dt` and `dd`. Every part is also exported by name (`PageHeaderLead`, `PageHeaderHeading`, `PageHeaderTitle`, `PageHeaderDescription`, `PageHeaderActions`, `PageHeaderSlot`, `PageHeaderFacts`, `PageHeaderStanding`), the same component as the member.
