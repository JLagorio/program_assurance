# Heading

A title. The size is the design's and the level is the page's: `size` picks the style from the heading ramp and an element to go with it, and `as` overrides the element when the outline needs another level. Four sizes, four jobs: a displayed number, a page title, a section heading, a card or dialog title. The colour is the text colour, or its inverse on a bold fill, which a Box sets for you; a tone is not a heading colour. A required `size`, a default element per size, `as`, and `color` limited to three. The sizes are fixed, and the level is semantic and separate from the style.

## Anatomy

| Part    | What it is                                                                                              |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Element | By size: `large` a `div`, `medium` an `h1`, `small` an `h2`, `xsmall` an `h3`. `as` overrides it with a `HeadingElement`: a level, a `div` or a `span`. |
| Size    | `large`, `medium`, `small`, `xsmall`: the four `font.heading` tokens.                                   |
| Colour  | `color.text`, inherited; `color.text.inverse` or `color.text.warning.inverse` from a bold Box.          |

## Variants

| Heading  | Use                                                                                                     |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `large`  | A displayed number: a Stat's value, a dashboard figure. A `div`, because a number is not a title.       |
| `medium` | The page's title, the record's name. One per page, the `h1`.                                            |
| `small`  | A section heading in a page body, a tab's heading. The `h2`.                                            |
| `xsmall` | A card's title, a dialog's title, a rail section's heading. The `h3`, or `h4` under a section.          |

Not built, and why: `xxlarge` and `xlarge` (the product has no hero, and 22px is the biggest text on a page); `xxsmall` as a Heading (it is the Eyebrow, uppercase and subtle, on the Typography page); a subtitle slot (the line under a title is a Text, and the RecordHeader draws it); an icon before (a Heading is words; the mark beside a record's title is the RecordHeader's); a tone (a red heading says the word is the problem; the count beside it is).

## Sizes

| Size     | Token                 | Size / leading / weight | Element | Where                                        |
| -------- | --------------------- | ----------------------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `large`  | `font.heading.large`  | 28 / 34 / semibold      | `div`   | A displayed number.                          |
| `medium` | `font.heading.medium` | 22 / 28 / semibold      | `h1`    | The page title.                              |
| `small`  | `font.heading.small`  | 20 / 26 / medium        | `h2`    | A section heading.                           |
| `xsmall` | `font.heading.xsmall` | 15 / 22 / medium        | `h3`    | A card, a dialog, a rail section.            |

Under it, `font.heading.xxsmall` at 11 / 14, uppercase, is the Eyebrow. Four are enough here because a page is a title, its sections and their cards.

## States

Not applicable: a heading has no states. A collapsible section's heading is the Accordion's trigger, and the trigger has the states.

## Modifiers

- **`size`**: required. The design's choice.
- **`as`**: the page's choice. `h2` for a section at the card size in a rail; `h4` for a subsection at the section size; `div` for a title-sized line where the outline already has its `h1`.
- **`color`**: `color.text`, `color.text.inverse`, `color.text.warning.inverse`. Left off, it follows the surface.
- **`id`**, for a section's `aria-labelledby`.
- **No `style`.** A heading has no computed dimension.

## Content

- **One `h1`.** The page's title; every other title is `as="h2"` or below, whatever its size.
- **The outline in order.** No `h3` under an `h1` without an `h2` between, and no level chosen for its size.
- **A title is a noun phrase**, sentence case, no full stop: "Assessment results", "Schedule assessment".
- **`large` is a number**, not a title. The 28px size is a Stat's.
- **A tone goes on the fact beside the title**, never on the title.

## Style

| Element | Token                                                                                     |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Size    | `font.heading.large` · `.medium` · `.small` · `.xsmall`, family, size, leading, weight and tracking in one |
| Colour  | `color.text` · `color.text.inverse` · `color.text.warning.inverse`                        |

## Accessibility

- **The level is the element**, and the element is the page's decision through `as`. The size says nothing to a screen reader.
- **A section is named by its heading.** `<Box as="section" aria-labelledby>` pointing at the Heading's `id`.
- **The outline is a map.** A screen reader's user jumps by heading; two `h1`s are two pages, a skipped level is a missing floor.
- **`large` is a `div`** so a displayed number is not a heading in the outline. A Stat's label is its heading, if it needs one.
- **Contrast is the token's.** `color.text` on every surface and `color.text.inverse` on every bold fill are measured on the Color sheet.
