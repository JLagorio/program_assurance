# Bar

**Status: Stable.**

Bars per category: the size of each thing, compared. Several series sit side by side or stack into parts of a whole; a bar runs down the side when its name is long; a value that is a `[from, to]` pair floats, which is how a phase window is drawn. Simple, grouped, stacked, horizontal and bullet bar charts are all this one part; a histogram is a bar chart of bins the data layer makes. It lives in a [Chart](?path=/docs/patterns-chart-overview--docs) Frame, which names it, keys it and lays it out as a table.

## Anatomy

| Part         | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bar          | 24px thick at most, the data end rounded 2px and the baseline end square; `barCategoryGap` a quarter of the slot. Grouped bars sit 2px apart.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Segment      | In a stack, a part of the bar in its series' tone, with a 2px gap in the surface it sits on from the next, so the parts count. A part below zero stacks down from zero.                                                                                                                                        |
| Floating bar | A `[from, to]` value: the bar starts at `from`. For windows in time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Negative bar | A value below zero hangs from the zero line, which is drawn in `color.border.bold`; its rounded end and its label are at the data end, below.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Texture      | With `texture`, each series' fill is its pattern in its colour; the first series stays solid.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| End label    | The value at the bar's end in `font.body.xsmall` and `color.text.subtle`, outside the bar; on a stack, its total at its end. Left out where it is wider than its bar's share of the category.                                                                                                                  |
| Target       | A 2px mark in `color.text` across the bar at the datum's `target` value: actual reads against planned.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Line         | A series drawn as a 2px line over the bars on the same axis: a cumulative, a rate, a plan. It is never part of a stack's total.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Axes         | The category axis with a hairline, a label for every category, on two lines or cut with an ellipsis where the band is narrow; the value axis from zero, no line, ticks in `color.text.subtlest`, round steps through zero on a pinned `domain`. Titles in `color.text.subtle` when the keys do not say enough. |
| Tooltip      | The category, every series at it keyed by a square, and the total of the stacked parts when stacked. A hovered bar takes its tone's `hovered` step.                                                                                                                                                            |

## Variants

| Variant             | Use                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| One series          | Findings by source. `brand` when the reader is asked to look, `neutral` when it is context. No legend; the title names it.     |
| Grouped             | Two or three series side by side, in the categorical set. Past three the groups blur: stack, or emphasise one as a Line.       |
| `stacked`           | Parts of each whole, in the status tones or the categorical set. The tooltip totals them, and `labels="end"` prints the total. |
| `horizontal`        | Long names, or many categories: the names read whole down the side.                                                            |
| `target`            | A mark across each bar at its target: a bullet chart, without the qualitative bands.                                           |
| `[from, to]` values | Floating bars: phase windows, with `horizontal` and a `reference` for today.                                                   |
| `line`              | One series as a line over the bars, on the same axis: closed against the plan.                                                 |
| Below zero          | Variance, change, balance: the bars hang from the zero line. The axis reaches below zero on its own.                           |
| `domain`            | The value axis pinned, so charts side by side share a scale: small multiples. Its ticks are round steps through zero.          |
| `texture`           | Patterns as well as colours, for print and colour-vision loss.                                                                 |

## Sizes

`size` is the plot's height: `small` 120px, `medium` 200px (the default), `large` 320px, or `height` in pixels; unsaid, the Frame's, and `large` in the Frame's expanded Dialog. A bar never grows past 24px however wide the plot; the slots grow instead, so a wide chart of four bars still reads as four bars and not four slabs. A horizontal chart gives its category axis the width its longest name needs, to 160px, and cuts the rest with the whole name in a title. A column chart keeps a label under every bar: when a name will not fit its category it takes two lines, broken at a space, and each line past its band is cut with an ellipsis, the whole name in the tick's title, the tooltip and the table.

## States

| State    | What changes                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hover    | The tooltip for the category; a `color.background.neutral.subtle.hovered` band behind the slot; the bar under the pointer in its tone's `hovered` step (a textured bar at 85%). Synced charts hover together.              |
| Hidden   | A series hidden from the Frame's legend leaves the plot and the table; the others keep their colours, a stack closes up over zero, and the value axis keeps its scale.                                                     |
| Chosen   | With `onSelect` or `details`: the bars take the pointer, a click on one chooses it and the other categories dim; Enter or Space on the focused plot chooses the category the arrow keys are on. The card opens on the bar. |
| Loading  | Eight skeleton columns on a hairline, pulsing, at the plot's height; five skeleton bars when `horizontal`.                                                                                                                 |
| Entering | The bars grow from the baseline over `motion.duration.slow`; a change of data moves them.                                                                                                                                  |

## Modifiers

- **`stacked`**, **`horizontal`**, **`labels="end"`**, **`target`**, **`line`** as in Variants.
- **`xLabel`**, **`yLabel`** print axis titles: the unit on the value axis when the title does not say it.
- **`domain`** pins the value axis, `[0, "auto"]` when unsaid; a value below zero extends it on its own. **`texture`** and **`syncId`** as on the Frame. A series' own **`format`** wins over the plot's in the tooltip, the card and the table: a fraction printed as a percentage.
- **`reference`** draws dashed lines: a target across every bar, a milestone on the category axis, labelled at the end. A `y` reference on a horizontal chart is a vertical line, labelled above the plot.
- **`format`**, **`formatX`** for the numbers and the categories, inherited from the Frame; the Frame's table prints the plot's.
- **`data`**, **`x`**, **`series`**: Inside a Frame, `data`, `x` and `series` may be left to the Frame, and the Frame learns them from the part when they are given here instead: say them once, to either.
- **`onSelect`** and **`details`** as in States; the selection is `{ datum, series?, index }`, and `series` is absent when Enter chose the whole category.
- **`loading`** draws the skeleton; the Frame sets it from `state="loading"`.
- **`label`** names the plot outside a Frame.

## Content

- **Categories are short**: a code, a name, a month. A long name goes down the side with `horizontal`.
- **Print a value only at the end** of a few bars, or a stack's total at its end. A stack's parts are the tooltip's and the table's.
- **A target is a fact about the datum**, in its own key, not a series: the mark is ink.
- **The value axis starts at zero.** A bar's length is its meaning; there is no `baseline="auto"` here on purpose. Below zero is allowed, and the zero line is drawn, because a negative bar's length still means something.

## Style

| Element   | Token                                                                                                      |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bar       | `color.chart.<tone>` · 24px at most · `radius` 2px at the data end · hover in `color.chart.<tone>.hovered` |
| Zero line | `color.border.bold` · 1px                                                                                  |
| Texture   | The colour at 30% under 1.5px marks · 8px repeat                                                           |
| Stack gap | 2px of the surface it sits on (`utility.elevation.surface.current`)                                        |
| Group gap | 2px                                                                                                        |
| End label | `font.body.xsmall` · `color.text.subtle` · tabular · 4px off the end                                       |
| Target    | `color.text` · 2px · 24px across                                                                           |
| Line      | `color.chart.<tone>` · 2px · round joins · a ringed 8px marker on hover                                    |
| Cursor    | `color.background.neutral.subtle.hovered` behind the slot                                                  |
| Dimmed    | `opacity.disabled`, over `motion.duration.fast`                                                            |
| Skeleton  | `color.skeleton` columns on a `color.border` hairline                                                      |

## Accessibility

- **A plot that chooses is one tab stop.** With `onSelect` or `details`, the svg is named by the Frame's title (or `label`), with the role description "chart" and its keys as its description. Left and Right move between the categories of a column chart; Up and Down (and Left and Right) down the rows of a horizontal one; Home and End go to the first and the last. Each step says the category and every visible value in a polite live region: "AU: Satisfied 18, Partial 4, Other than satisfied 3, Not assessed 1, total 26". Enter or Space chooses it; the card that opens is a dialog, and focus returns to the plot after.
- **A plot that chooses nothing is an image**, named by the Frame's title, with no tab stop: the table twin carries its numbers. Unnamed, it is decoration; one with `onSelect` or `details` and no name is named "Chart" and warns in development.
- **The table is the other copy** of the same numbers, one toggle away in the Frame; a stack's parts and its total are columns there.
- **A status series says its status** in the legend, the tooltip and the table; the tone reinforces.
- **The target is a mark in ink**, not a colour, so it reads under every vision.
- **Loading is `aria-busy`** and named "[the plot's name], loading".
- **`texture` gives a stack a second channel**, so its parts read in print and where fills are flattened.
