# Panel

The surface in the shell's Panel area. Flush, with no header, for the detail of a selected row beside its table, gone when the selection clears. With a header (a back chevron, an icon, up to two actions and the close), an optional subheader and footer, and `Panel.Trigger` to open it, for a panel the reader opens: a thread, a form. The area scrolls; the header and footer stay put. Never the record's rail, which is the ShowPage's.
