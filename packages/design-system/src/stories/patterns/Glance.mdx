# Glance

The body of a hover card: what the reader sees when the pointer rests on a record's id or name. The id and one status on the first line, the name on at most two, the meta line, at most four facts. Facts only, no actions: it is the first rung of the hover ladder, the click is the peek in a [PreviewSheet](?path=/docs/patterns-previewsheet--docs) and the footer link there is the record. A record reads the same on every rung because the same parts draw it, and this is the smallest of them. The same card serves a record and a person.

## Anatomy

| Part   | What it is                                                                  |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Id     | First, `font.body.small` in `color.text.subtle`.                            |
| Status | At the end of the id's line: a Badge or an Indicator, one.                  |
| Title  | The record's name, `font.body` medium, at most two lines.                   |
| Meta   | Under it, `font.body.small` subtle, one line: kind, path, owner.            |
| Facts  | At most four KeyValue rows, the labels 88px wide.                           |

## Variants

Glance has one appearance. What varies is the record type, which is the product's: an element, a requirement, a control, each with the four facts that say whether to open it. The three the prototype draws:

<Canvas of={Stories.GlanceMatrix} />

Not built, and why: an action row, because a hover card has no controls and a keyboard never reaches them; an avatar or an image, because a record is its id and its name; a fifth fact, because the part draws four and the rest belong to the peek; a link in the card, because the trigger is the link.

## Sizes

One size: the [HoverCard](?path=/docs/components-hovercard--docs) is 300px wide for a Glance (280 for a few lines of text). The card is as tall as its content, at most a two-line title and four facts.

## States

A glance has none. It appears when the card opens and goes when it closes; nothing in it hovers, focuses or presses.

## Modifiers

- **`id`**, **`title`**, **`meta`**, **`status`**, **`facts`** as in Anatomy. Facts past the fourth are dropped.

## Content

- **The title is the record's name** as it reads on its page; a shall statement in full, two lines at most.
- **The meta is kind, path, owner** in a few words with middle dots: "Subsystem · Atlas payments platform", "Derived · revision 1".
- **One status**, the one the reader decides by. A second is a fact.
- **Four facts**, the ones that decide whether to open the record; the label a word or two, the value a word, a number or a name.

## Style

| Element | Token                                                              |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Id      | `font.body.small` · `color.text.subtle`                            |
| Status  | At the end of the id's line, `space.100` from it                   |
| Title   | `font.body` medium · `color.text` · two lines, then an ellipsis    |
| Meta    | `font.body.small` · `color.text.subtle` · one line                 |
| Facts   | KeyValue, label 88px · `space.100` above the group                 |
| Layout  | `space.025` between the lines of the head                          |

## Accessibility

- **The card's behaviour is the HoverCard's**: it opens on hover after a rest and on focus of the trigger, and closes on Escape and when the pointer leaves. The trigger is the link, so a keyboard reader reaches the record without the card.
- **The glance is text**, read in order: id, status, title, meta, then the facts as terms and values.
- **The status carries its word**: a Badge or an Indicator with text, never a bare Dot.
- **Nothing in it is interactive**, so nothing in it is unreachable.
