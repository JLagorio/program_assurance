# Shell

The navigation system: how a reader gets around a product. `Shell` is the root, and its areas are its immediate children in a fixed order, which is also the keyboard, landmark and skip-link order: **Banner, TopNav, SideNav, Main, Panel**. The package owns the areas and how they behave: the side nav collapses, resizes, flies out on hover and overlays the page on a narrow viewport, the panel resizes and overlays, the banner pushes everything down. The product owns what goes in them: the nav data, the router, the search, the actions, whatever fills the panel. Every product on the system shares one brand and one behaviour, and brings its own name and mark.

## Anatomy

The areas, in their order:

| Area            | Does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Shell.Banner`  | Above everything, `dimension.layout.banner` tall, holding a Banner. Render it while the message is true; it pushes the rest down and is gone when it unmounts.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `Shell.TopNav`  | `dimension.layout.topnav` tall, the full width, sticky. Three slots: `Start`, `Middle`, `End`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `Shell.SideNav` | `dimension.layout.sidenav` wide on the sunken surface, sticky under the top nav. Three slots: `Header` and `Footer` stay put, `Body` scrolls and pushes the footer down.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `Shell.Main`    | The page. Fills what the others leave, body scroll, `space.200` inset and `space.300` on a desktop.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `Shell.Panel`   | Beside the page at the end, under the top nav, `dimension.layout.panel` wide. Render it anywhere under the Shell, a route or a page: it is fixed beside the page and the root reserves its column, on the server as on the client. Mounted when there is something to show and unmounted when there is not. It is an area, not a feature. |

The top nav's slots and items:

| Slot or item        | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `TopNav.Start`      | `toggle` takes the `SideNav.ToggleButton`; children are the `AppSwitcher` and the `AppLogo`. While the side nav is expanded the slot takes the side nav's width and surface, so the logo heads that column and the side nav reads full height; the toggle moves to the slot's end. |
| `TopNav.Middle`     | The search first, then the create action. Centred while the side nav is collapsed, start-aligned while it is expanded.                                                                                                                                                             |
| `TopNav.End`        | A list of icon buttons, right-aligned with no gaps: mode, help, notifications, settings. Below the medium breakpoint they fold into one More button.                                                                                                                               |
| `Shell.AppLogo`     | The product's mark and name with a `secondaryName` under it, hidden with the name below the large breakpoint. Use `render={<Link />}` for home or `render={<button type="button" />}` for a switcher. Additional children can supply a chevron. `Shell.Mark` is the default mark.  |
| `Shell.AppSwitcher` | The grid button that opens the switcher between products.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `Shell.Profile`     | The person, avatar, name and role, for the side nav's footer. A button with `onClick`; a label without.                                                                                                                                                                            |

The side nav's slots and items:

| Part                   | Does                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SideNav.Header`       | Fixed at the top: a container switcher, a search, a title.                                                                                                                                   |
| `SideNav.Body`         | The sections and items. It scrolls.                                                                                                                                                          |
| `SideNav.Footer`       | Fixed at the bottom, above a rule: the Profile, a settings link.                                                                                                                             |
| `SideNav.Section`      | A group of items under an eyebrow `heading`, which is the group's name.                                                                                                                      |
| `SideNav.Item`         | One destination: `href` for an anchor, `render={<Link />}` for routing, or `render={<button type="button" />}` for an action. `icon`, `badge` on the right, `isActive` for the current page. |
| `SideNav.Expandable`   | A row that opens a level of items under it, indented one step per level. Uncontrolled with `defaultOpen`, or `open` and `onOpenChange`.                                                      |
| `SideNav.ToggleButton` | Shows and hides the side nav, in `TopNav.Start`'s `toggle` slot. Its tooltip shows the shortcut when one is on.                                                                              |
| `SideNav.Splitter`     | Makes the side nav resizable from its end edge. A double-click collapses it.                                                                                                                 |
| `Panel.Splitter`       | Makes the panel resizable from its start edge.                                                                                                                                               |

## Variants

The frame has one appearance. What varies is which areas are on and how the side nav sits:

| Frame                   | Use                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Top nav, side nav, main | Every page: the default frame.                                                                                                              |
| With a banner           | A message true of the whole product for now: the audit window closing. It pushes the frame down; nothing covers anything.                   |
| With a panel            | The detail of a selected row beside its table, a thread, a form: the product mounts `Shell.Panel` with a Panel in it and unmounts it after. |
| Collapsed               | The side nav hidden, the logo mark alone in the start slot, the middle slot centred. Hover the toggle for the flyout.                       |
| Overlay                 | Below the large breakpoint: the side nav opens over the page behind a scrim, the panel over the page without one.                           |

## States

| State      | What changes                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Expanded   | The side nav inline beside the page; the start slot takes its width and surface; the toggle at the slot's end.                                                                                         |
| Collapsed  | The side nav hidden; the toggle beside the logo; the middle slot centred. The toggle button, `useSideNav()` from product code, Ctrl+[ when `sideNavShortcut` is on, or a double-click on the splitter. |
| Flyout     | Hovering the toggle while collapsed flies the side nav out over the page; moving away closes it. A popup opened inside the flyout holds it open; the next click outside closes both.                   |
| Overlay    | Below the large breakpoint the side nav opens over the page behind a scrim; Escape or the scrim closes it and focus returns to the toggle.                                                             |
| Dragging   | The splitter in `color.background.brand.bold` while dragged or focused; the arrow keys move it 16px; `onResizeEnd` reports the final width. The width survives a collapse.                             |
| Remembered | `persist` keeps the collapsed state and the dragged widths in this browser; `shellScript` in the document head applies them before the first paint, so nothing flashes open on reload.                 |
| Active     | A side nav item with `isActive`: `color.background.neutral`, medium weight, `aria-current="page"`.                                                                                                     |

## Modifiers

- **Root**: `defaultSideNavCollapsed`, `sideNavShortcut`, `persist` (`true` for the default key, or a key of your own).
- **Areas**: `id` and `label` on each, the landmark's name; `defaultWidth` on the side nav and the panel; `onCollapse` and `onExpand` on the side nav.
- **Top nav**: `toggle` on Start; `label` and `moreLabel` on End.
- **Logo**: `mark`, `name`, `secondaryName`, `render`, native props/ref and children.
- **Items**: `icon`, `badge`, `isActive`, `href` or `render`, native props/ref; `label`, `defaultOpen`, `open`, `onOpenChange` on an expandable.
- **Splitters**: `label`, `onResizeStart`, `onResizeEnd`.
- **`useSideNav()`** from product code: `isExpanded`, `expand`, `collapse`, `toggle`.

## Content

- **The side nav holds objects and queues**: Programs, Findings, My queue, Evidence. Never phases; a phase is a state of a program, reached by opening it.
- **Items are nouns**, the kind of thing behind them: no verbs, nothing that reads as an action.
- **Section headings are the product's areas**: Work, Risk, Libraries, System. An eyebrow, one or two words.
- **A badge is a count**: how many wait in the queue. Grey, so the column stays quiet; a tone only when the count is an alarm.
- **The landmarks say what is in them**: "Preview", "Comments", "Details" on the panel; never "Panel". The side nav is "Side navigation", the top nav "Top navigation", the page "Main content", each a skip link.
- **The logo is the product and the tenant**: "Equinox" over "Northwind Corp". The mark alone below the large breakpoint.

## Accessibility

- **Each area is a landmark with a skip link**: the banner a region, the top nav a `header`, the side nav a `nav`, the page `main`, the panel an `aside`, each named by its `label` and each first in a "Skip to" list of links that show on focus. A "Skip to main content" link as the first focusable element, one per area.
- **The current page says so**: `aria-current="page"` on the active item; an expandable says `aria-expanded`; the toggle says `aria-expanded` for the side nav and names the shortcut in its tooltip.
- **Keyboard**: Ctrl+[ toggles the side nav when the shortcut is on, and not while a dialog is open; Escape closes the overlay and focus returns to the toggle; the splitters are separators that take the arrow keys, 16px a step; Tab runs the areas in their order.
- **The flyout is hover only**, and everything in it is reachable by keyboard through the toggle: expand, use, collapse.
- **The overlay's scrim is a button** named "Close side navigation", so a screen reader finds the way out as well as Escape.
- **The end slot is a list** named "Actions", of icon buttons each with a name; folded into More it is the same list in a popover.
- **The stored state is applied before paint**, so a reader who collapsed the side nav does not have it announced open and then gone.

## The record's rail

A record page keeps at most six facts in its header. The rest, details and related information in every Inspector group, is the rail, and the rail is the page's: `rail` on `ShowPage`, beside the body of the overview tab, under the tab strip, so the strip runs the full width and every other tab is full width. It is not the shell's panel. The panel holds the detail of a selected row beside its table, which comes and goes with the selection, and the panels a reader opens and closes, a thread or a form, with a title, a close and a `Panel.Trigger`. The peek is a fourth thing: a Sheet over the page and the nav, about half the screen, previewing one row.

### Navigation composition migration

Replace AppLogo and SideNav.Item `asChild` with Base UI `render`. AppLogo defaults to a span; SideNav.Item defaults to an anchor. Actions explicitly render a button, including `type="button"`; an onClick handler does not change the element. AppLogo switchers compose their own chevron as children. Put the label inside SideNav.Item and leave the rendered router Link empty so the item keeps its icon and badge. Native refs, events, ARIA and styles merge onto that element; custom style preserves depth indentation unless it explicitly overrides paddingInlineStart.

Sources: [shadcn Sidebar implementation](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/bases/base/ui/sidebar.tsx), [Base UI useRender](https://base-ui.com/react/utils/use-render).
