# Due rail

**Status: Experimental.**

Dated items as dots on one time axis, above the table that lists them, with today ruled across: a program's POA&M items by the day each is due, the evidence by the day it expires. The reader sees at a glance how much is late, how much is due this week and how the rest spreads over the months ahead; the table under it says which and opens each record. The API may change before it is stable.

<Canvas of={Stories.AboveItsTable} />

## Anatomy

| Part  | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Axis  | A hairline in `color.border.bold` from the first item's day to the last, today among them, with a little room at each end. Its ticks fall on the starts of days or months by the span, labelled in `font.body.xsmall` in `color.text.subtlest`.                                        |
| Today | A rule in `color.border.current` at `border.width.selected` across the dots and the axis, with "Today" above it in `font.body.xsmall` medium: after the rule, or before it near the rail's end.                                                                                        |
| Dot   | An item, 8px, on its day. Neutral (`color.chart.neutral`) when it is not due yet; ringed in the warning tone (`color.chart.warning`) when it is due today or within two days; hollow and hatched in the danger tone (`color.chart.danger`) when it is late, as a Meter's late mark is. |
| Stack | Dots that would touch stand on one another, a pitch (`space.150`) apart, so the rail is as tall as its busiest stretch. A late dot always stands before the rule and a dot not yet late after it.                                                                                      |

Late and soon are told by shape as well as colour, and by place: a late dot is hatched and stands before today's rule; a soon dot is ringed and stands just after it.

## The rail and its table

The rail is never the only way to a date. Put it above the table that lists the same items, one row a dot, keyed by the dot's `id`, and say each day there with a [DateLabel](?path=/docs/components-datetime--docs), which uses the same words (Overdue, Due today, Due tomorrow). In a Frame the rail is a chart like any other: its Table toggle lays the items out as the table twin (the item, then its day with those words), and the CSV holds the ISO days. The Frame's `xLabel` names the item column ("POA&M item").

A dot does not open anything: at 8px it is no target, and the row under it is the way to the record. Its title shows its name on hover.

## Sizes

One size. The width is the container's; the height follows the tallest stack, from about 56px for one dot a column. On a phone the dots stand closer and on one another more, and the tick labels thin out so none runs past the rail's end.

## States

| State   | What changes                                                                       |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hover   | A dot's title: its item, its day and, when late or soon, the words.                |
| Loading | A skeleton block in the rail's place, `aria-busy`; the Frame sets it from `state`. |
| Empty   | The Frame's `state="empty"`, with why and what to do.                              |

## Modifiers

- **`items`**: each `{ id, label, due }`, `due` an ISO day ("2026-12-13"). An item with no ISO day is left off the rail; list it in the table.
- **`now`**: the moment today is judged from, in the provider's zone. By default the clock at render; pass it where the page already holds one, so the rail and the table's DateLabels agree.
- **`label`** names the dots' list outside a Frame; inside one, the Frame's title does.
- **`loading`** draws the skeleton; the Frame sets it from `state="loading"`.
- Native props and a ref reach its box.

## Content

- **Open items only.** A done item has no due; it leaves the rail and stays in the register.
- **One kind of item a rail**: POA&M items, or evidence, not both. The table under it is that kind's register.
- **The Frame's description counts what matters**: "12 open · 4 late".

## Style

| Element | Token                                                                                            |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Dot     | `space.100` · `radius.full` · `color.chart.neutral`                                              |
| Soon    | `color.chart.warning` · a ring of the surface, then of the tone, `border.width` each             |
| Late    | the surface · an inset `border.width` edge and a hatch every `space.025` in `color.chart.danger` |
| Pitch   | `space.150`                                                                                      |
| Today   | `color.border.current` · `border.width.selected` · "Today" `font.body.xsmall` medium             |
| Axis    | `color.border.bold` · `border.width` · ticks `font.body.xsmall` in `color.text.subtlest`         |

## Accessibility

- **The dots are a list in date order**, named by the Frame's title or `label`; a screen reader hears "POA&M items by due date, list, 12 items".
- **Each dot is an image named by its item, its day and, when late or soon, the words**: "Withdraw the password-only local logon, Oct 28, 2026, Overdue"; "Ship the offline credential verifier, due Nov 25, 2026".
- **Not a tab stop.** The dots open nothing; the table's rows are the stops.
- **Colour is never alone.** Late is hatched and before the rule, soon is ringed; in forced colours the hatch, the ring and the rule stay in the system's text colour.
