# Area

**Status: Stable.**

A [Line](?path=/docs/patterns-chart-line--docs) with a wash under it: the hue at 12%, so the plot says "how much" where a line alone says "which way". Stacked, the washes are parts of a whole over time, and the top edge is the total. Everything a Line takes, an Area takes; the difference is the fill and `stacked`. It lives in a [Chart](?path=/docs/patterns-chart-overview--docs) Frame.

<Canvas of={Stories.AreaMatrix} />

## Anatomy

| Part     | What it is                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Line     | 2px in the series' tone, round joins, as on a Line.                                                                                                              |
| Wash     | The series' tone at 12% from the line to the baseline; in a stack, from the line to the series below. With `texture`, the series' pattern in its colour instead. |
| Marker   | 8px ringed, on hover, or on every point with `dots`.                                                                                                             |
| The rest | End labels, bands, references, gaps and axes as on a Line.                                                                                                       |

## Variants

| Variant                                                                                   | Use                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One series                                                                                | A count whose size matters: open findings, month by month. The wash gives it weight.                                                                                          |
| `stacked`                                                                                 | Parts of a whole over time: open and closed as parts of the month's total. The tooltip totals the stack, and each end label sits on its band's top with the band's own value. |
| `texture`                                                                                 | Each wash its pattern: four assessors stacked read apart in print.                                                                                                            |
| `curve="smooth"`, `labels="end"`, `domain`, `scale="time"`, `delta`, `bands`, `reference` | As on a Line.                                                                                                                                                                 |

## Sizes

`size` is the plot's height: `small` 120px, `medium` 200px (the default), `large` 320px, or `height` in pixels; unsaid, the Frame's, and `large` in the Frame's expanded Dialog. As on a Line, `data`, `x` and `series` may be left to the Frame, or given here and learned by it, and every category keeps a label fitted to the room up to the next point.

<Canvas of={Stories.LongCategories} />

The value axis starts at zero, since a wash measures from it: a cropped wash exaggerates as a cropped bar does. There is no `baseline` here (the prop is ignored, and deprecated); a trend where the change matters more than the size is a [Line](?path=/docs/patterns-chart-line--docs) with `baseline="auto"`.

## States

| State    | What changes                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hover    | A hairline across the plot at the category, a marker on every line there, the tooltip.                                                                                                  |
| Hidden   | A series hidden from the Frame's legend leaves the plot and the table; a stack closes up, and the value axis keeps its scale.                                                           |
| Chosen   | With `onSelect` or `details`: a click in a category's column, or Enter on the focused plot, chooses it; the card opens on the point. On an unstacked area every series is ringed there. |
| Loading  | A skeleton line with a `color.skeleton.subtle` wash, pulsing, at the plot's height.                                                                                                     |
| Entering | The areas draw in over `motion.duration.slow`.                                                                                                                                          |

## Modifiers

- **`stacked`** for parts of a whole; **`texture`** for the patterns.
- **`curve`**, **`dots`**, **`labels`**, **`bands`**, **`reference`**, **`connectNulls`**, **`xLabel`**, **`yLabel`**, **`format`**, **`formatX`**, **`onSelect`**, **`details`**, **`loading`**, **`label`** as on a Line, and its native props and ref.

## Content

- **Stack only parts of one whole.** Series that are not parts (three assessors' counts) are lines.
- **Two series that cross are lines**, not two washes: where washes overlap they mix into a colour on nobody's legend.
- **One series or a stack**; never several unstacked areas.
- The rest as on a Line: label the end, declare a cropped axis, keep a gap.

## Style

| Element  | Token                                                                             |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Line     | `color.chart.<tone>` · 2px · round joins                                          |
| Wash     | `color.chart.<tone>` at 12% · or its pattern: the colour at 30% under 1.5px marks |
| Marker   | 8px · 2px ring in the surface it sits on                                          |
| Skeleton | `color.skeleton` path over a `color.skeleton.subtle` wash                         |
| The rest | As on a Line                                                                      |

## Accessibility

- **As a Line**: named by the Frame or `label`; one tab stop with the arrow keys, Home, End, Enter and a live region when it chooses, an image with no tab stop when it does not; the table one toggle away, a legend for two or more series, `aria-busy` while loading.
- **A stack's total is a column in the table**, since the top edge is the only place the plot shows it.
- **The wash is decoration**: the line carries the value, so a wash at 12% that fails 3:1 against the surface costs nothing.
