# PageHeader

The top of an index page: the area it belongs to, the page's name, one line, and the actions. This one is the head only: the filters and the table are the [IndexPage](?path=/docs/patterns-pages--docs)'s, and a page has no tabs, a record does.

<Canvas of={Stories.PageHeaderMatrix} />

## Anatomy

| Part        | What it is                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Eyebrow     | The area, above the title, `font.body` in `color.text.subtle`: "Libraries", "Risk". A word or two.          |
| Title       | The page's name, the h1, `font.heading.medium`: "Programs".                                                                                         |
| Description | One line under the title, `font.body` in `color.text.subtle`: what the rows are, a constraint, a count. It truncates; the full text is its tooltip. |
| Actions     | At the end of the row, `space.100` apart: one primary and at most two beside it.                                                                    |

## Variants

PageHeader has one appearance. What varies is what it carries:

| Header          | Use                                                                                       |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Title alone     | A page that is its name: "Programs".                                                      |
| With an eyebrow | A page inside an area of the product: "Libraries" over "Control catalog".                 |
| With a line     | A count or a constraint the reader needs before the rows: "5 programs · 2 in assessment". |
| With actions    | The page's one thing to do, and an export or a view beside it.                            |

Not built, and why: breadcrumbs, because an index page is one level deep and the side nav says where it is; a hero image or an icon, because the product's pages are registers; a bottom bar of filters, because the filters are the IndexPage's own row; a subtitle that wraps, because a line that needs two is an explanation.

## Sizes

One size. The title is `font.heading.medium` (22px); the header is as wide as the page and its actions stay on the title's line. Below `md` the actions wrap under the title.

## States

A header has no state. Its actions have theirs.

## Modifiers

- **`eyebrow`**, **`title`**, **`description`**, **`actions`** as in Anatomy.

## Content

- **The title is a noun**, the plural of the rows: "Programs", "Findings and assets".
- **The line is a count or a constraint**, never an explanation of the model: "One technical fact per row", "5 programs · 2 in assessment". If it needs two lines it does not belong here.
- **The eyebrow is the area**, one or two words.
- **One primary action**, the page's one thing to do: "New program". The rest are secondary, and past two they are a menu.

## Style

| Element     | Token                                                                                        |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Eyebrow     | `font.body` · `color.text.subtle`                                                            |
| Title       | `font.heading.medium` · `color.text`                                                         |
| Description | `font.body` · `color.text.subtle` · one line, truncating                                     |
| Layout      | `space.050` between the lines · `space.300` before the actions · `space.100` between actions |

## Accessibility

- **The title is the page's `h1`**, the one heading at that level; Sections and Cards under it are `h2`.
- **The description is read as text under it**; when it truncates, the full text is the tooltip and is read whole.
- **The actions are their own stops** in reading order after the title.
- **The eyebrow is text**, not a heading: it names the area, it does not open it.
