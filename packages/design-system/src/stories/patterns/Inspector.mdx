# Inspector

The facts stay put while the content scrolls: in a ShowPage's rail or a WorkPane's detail, sticky under the top nav, every group open until the reader folds it. Inside a Panel, the detail of a selected row, it scrolls with the panel. Sticky, it wraps in a plain `<div className="lg:sticky-rail">`, not an aside: the rail it sits in, a ShowPage's or a WorkPane's detail, is the landmark, so a rail is exactly one landmark.

<Canvas of={Stories.InspectorMatrix} />

`Inspector.Group` is one group of facts on its own, for a rail that stacks several.
