# Inspector

**Status: Stable.**

Groups of a record's facts: state, owner, identifiers and dates, as label and value rows under a title that folds. It is the Details rail beside a record's work area, and the Details block in a preview or a panel. The surrounding layout owns where it sits and how it scrolls.

`Inspector` is the root: it holds `Inspector.Group` children in the order the reader needs them, then a `footer`. Each group is one name and its facts as children, with an action beside its title. A group's KeyValue rows go in one [KeyValue.Group](?path=/docs/components-keyvalue--docs), so a screen reader hears one list of pairs per group, and the group gives them one label width; KeyValues given directly, and nothing else, become one KeyValue.Group. A row of Badges or a short list sits beside or instead of it.

```tsx
<Inspector
  footer={
    <Button size="small" variant="link">
      Edit properties
    </Button>
  }
>
  <Inspector.Group title="Ownership">
    <KeyValue label="Owner">Dana Whitfield</KeyValue>
    <KeyValue label="Sponsor">Alex Morgan</KeyValue>
  </Inspector.Group>
  <Inspector.Group title="Schedule">
    <KeyValue label="Due">30 Sept 2026</KeyValue>
  </Inspector.Group>
</Inspector>
```

<Canvas of={Stories.Composition} />

`groups` is deprecated: the same groups as data (`{ title, rows: [{ label, value }] }`), which the root renders as open Inspector.Groups before any children. It goes in the next minor version; compose the groups instead, which also gives each group its action, its `defaultOpen` and rows that are more than a label and a value. `ledger/no-deprecated-name` reports it.

## Group

Each group's title is a [CollapsibleHeader](?path=/docs/components-collapsible--docs): a button inside a heading, the title at the start and a chevron at the end that turns while the group is open. A group is open by default; `defaultOpen={false}` makes the collapsed Details a reader opens when they need provenance, counts or derivation, and `open` with `onOpenChange` hands the state to the caller. The action, an IconButton or a link button, sits beside the title while the whole title fits beside it on one line, and takes the next row, at the end, otherwise.

## Heading level

A group's heading takes the contextual level, an h3 when nothing sets one. In a [Shell](?path=/docs/layout-shell--docs) Aside rail the groups sit under the page's h1, so the Aside starts its content at 2 and the groups are h2s; in a titled Shell.Panel the body already starts at 3, under the panel's h2; under a preview's h2 record title, the content after the header goes one below with a HeadingLevelProvider, and the groups are h3s. A rail outside the Shell sets the level itself with `HeadingLevelProvider level={2}`.

## In the Details disclosure

Below the aside breakpoint a record's Shell.Aside is a Details disclosure in the page, closed until the reader opens it (Shell's Record details at 390px). Its row is the h2 and its content one level below, so the groups there are h3s. A group named as the disclosure ("Details", or the Aside's `heading`) is the disclosure's own content: its title, which would repeat the row's, steps aside, and its rows show while the disclosure is open. Its action stays, at the end of a row above them. The other groups keep their titles and fold as in the rail.

## When to use it

- **Use it** for the handful of facts that identify a record and its state, beside the work or at the top of a preview.
- **Not** for a record's own content, the statement, the narrative, the evidence: those are [Section](?path=/docs/layout-section--docs)s in the work area. **Not** for editing more than one fact at a time: that is a form in a Dialog; a single supported fact is an [Editable](?path=/docs/patterns-editable--docs) in a row.
- **Not** for linked records: a [Related](?path=/docs/patterns-related--docs) card lists them with their own links.
- On a page body, a collapsed block of details is a collapsible [Section](?path=/docs/layout-section--docs); in a rail of facts, it is an Inspector.Group with `defaultOpen={false}`.

## Accessibility

- Every group title is a button inside a heading, so the rail is in the heading outline and each group's state is announced ("Provenance, collapsed, button"). Enter and Space toggle it; focus stays on it.
- A group's facts are one definition list ("list, 4 items"), not one list per fact.
- Open and closed look different by more than colour: the chevron turns. Under reduced motion it turns without moving and the rows appear without the fold.
- The action is its own tab stop after the title, outside the heading, and names what it acts on ("Edit details").
- Give the rail's region a label (Shell.Aside's `label`), and set the level so the groups sit under the right parent, as above.
