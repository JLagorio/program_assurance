# PreviewNavigation

**Status: Stable.**

The collection controls for a record preview beside a register: previous and next through the table's displayed results, the announced record and position, and a link that opens the full record in a new tab. It belongs in the preview's outer bar, `Shell.Panel.Actions`, immediately before Close. That bar is navigation only: the record's visible name and its actions are an inner PageHeader at the start of the panel's body, its title an h2, as [Shell](?path=/docs/layout-shell--docs) describes.

<Canvas of={Stories.Collection} />

## In the panel

```tsx
<Shell.Panel label="Requirement preview" onClose={closePreview}>
  <Shell.Panel.Header>
    <Shell.Panel.Actions>
      <PreviewNavigation
        position={position}
        total={rows.length}
        recordLabel={record.name}
        onPrevious={position > 1 ? showPrevious : undefined}
        onNext={position < rows.length ? showNext : undefined}
        openLink={<Link to={recordPath} target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" />}
      />
    </Shell.Panel.Actions>
    <Shell.Panel.Close />
  </Shell.Panel.Header>
  <Shell.Panel.Body>
    <PageHeader>
      <PageHeader.Heading>
        <PageHeader.Title>{record.name}</PageHeader.Title>
      </PageHeader.Heading>
      <PageHeader.Actions>
        <Button size="small" variant="primary">
          Edit requirement
        </Button>
      </PageHeader.Actions>
    </PageHeader>
    {/* Labelled properties, then the record's sections. */}
  </Shell.Panel.Body>
</Shell.Panel>
```

The outer bar has no `Shell.Panel.Title`, so the panel takes its accessible name from `label`, and its body's outline starts at 2: the PageHeader.Title is the h2. Do not repeat the record's name in the outer bar, and do not repeat the full-record link at the bottom of the body. A nested record frame adds Back before the navigation.

## What the caller owns

The application owns selection, the row order and real record destinations; the part imports no router and holds no rows. The order is the whole filtered and sorted set, with expanded tree rows in place, not the table's current page: supply a one-based `position` and the `total` of that set, and a callback for each adjacent record. When Next crosses the edge of a table page, the caller turns the table to the page that holds the new record, so the active row stays in view. Leave a callback out at an endpoint, or pass a position at the end, and that button is unavailable. Pass the record's name as `recordLabel`, so the announcement says which record arrived.

A position and a total counted on the table's current page are wrong as soon as the result has a second page: the reader hears 3 of 25 where the result holds 62, and Next stops at the page's end. Without `recordLabel` every step is heard as a number alone.

<Canvas of={Stories.DoDont} />

`openLink` is an anchor or router link with the record's real destination, `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`; it must lead to that record, not its collection. The part gives it its icon, accessible name and tooltip. A preview inside a selection task (RecordBrowser, PickerSheet) belongs to the task and opens no record of its own: leave `openLink` out, and the bar is previous and next alone.

## Outside the results

If filtering removes the selected record, keep the preview open with `position={0}`. Both navigation buttons are unavailable and say why in their tooltip ("Record outside the current results"), which opens on hover, on keyboard focus and on a tap; the status says so too. The full-record link stays available.

## Showing the position

The position is always announced. `showPosition` draws it as well, before the buttons ("2 of 6"), and "Not in results" while the record is outside them. Turn it on where the bar has the room: it adds about four characters' width, more for large totals.

## Focus and announcements

- The controls are a group named "Record navigation": "Previous record", "Next record" and "Open full record in new tab", each with its name in a tooltip.
- A polite status says the record and its position, "Access review evidence, 2 of 6 records", when the preview opens and on each step. It mounts empty and speaks once assistive technology has seen it, so the first record is heard too.
- Focus stays on the control the reader used while the preview's content changes, so arrowing through a collection needs no hunt for the button.
- At an endpoint the control the reader used stays focused and says it is unavailable (`aria-disabled`); pressing it again does nothing, so a repeated Enter at the last record stays put instead of walking back. Tab still reaches both buttons, and Escape still reaches the panel or sheet around them. In forced colours an unavailable button is GrayText, as a native disabled one is.

## Words

Every label and template is a LedgerProvider message (`previousRecord`, `nextRecord`, `openFullRecord`, `recordNavigation`, `recordPosition`, `recordPositionNamed`, `recordPositionShort`, `recordOutsideResults`, `recordOutsideResultsNamed`, `recordNotInResults`), translated once for the product; see [Locale](?path=/docs/components-locale--docs). Numbers follow the locale. In a right-to-left provider the chevrons turn to face the reading direction, as Pagination's do.
