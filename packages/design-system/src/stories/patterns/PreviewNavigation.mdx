# PreviewNavigation

The collection controls for a record preview beside a register: previous and next through the table's displayed results, the announced position, and a link that opens the full record in a new tab. It belongs in the preview's outer bar, `Shell.Panel.Actions`, immediately before Close. That bar is navigation only: the record's visible name and its actions are an inner PageHeader at the start of the panel's body, its title an h2, as [Shell](?path=/docs/layout-shell--docs) describes.

## In the panel

```tsx
<Shell.Panel label="Requirement preview" onClose={closePreview}>
  <Shell.Panel.Header>
    <Shell.Panel.Actions>
      <PreviewNavigation
        position={position}
        total={rows.length}
        onPrevious={position > 1 ? showPrevious : undefined}
        onNext={position < rows.length ? showNext : undefined}
        openLink={<Link to={recordPath} target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" />}
      />
    </Shell.Panel.Actions>
    <Shell.Panel.Close />
  </Shell.Panel.Header>
  <Shell.Panel.Body>
    <PageHeader>
      <PageHeader.Title>{record.name}</PageHeader.Title>
      <PageHeader.Actions>
        <Button size="small" variant="primary">
          Edit requirement
        </Button>
      </PageHeader.Actions>
    </PageHeader>
    {/* Labelled properties, then the record's sections. */}
  </Shell.Panel.Body>
</Shell.Panel>
```

The outer bar has no `Shell.Panel.Title`, so the panel takes its accessible name from `label`, and its body's outline starts at 2: the PageHeader.Title is the h2. Do not repeat the record's name in the outer bar, and do not repeat the full-record link at the bottom of the body. A nested record frame adds Back before the navigation.

## What the caller owns

The application owns selection, the filtered, sorted and expanded row order, and real record destinations; the part imports no router and holds no rows. Supply a one-based `position` and the `total` of the displayed rows, and a callback for each adjacent record. Leave a callback out at an endpoint, or pass a position at the end, and that button is disabled. `openLink` is an anchor or router link with the record's real destination, `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`; it must lead to that record, not its collection. The part gives it its icon and accessible name.

If filtering removes the selected record, keep the preview open with `position={0}`. Both navigation buttons become disabled and the status says that the record is outside the current results. The full-record link stays available.

## Focus and announcements

- The controls are named "Previous record", "Next record" and "Open full record in new tab", and a polite status announces "n of m records" as the position changes.
- Focus stays on the control the reader used while the preview's content changes, so arrowing through a collection needs no hunt for the button.
- At an endpoint the control the reader used becomes disabled. Focus moves to the remaining enabled navigation button, or to the full-record link when neither is enabled, so Escape and the next key stay inside the preview.

## Words

Every label and the position template are LedgerProvider messages (`previousRecord`, `nextRecord`, `openFullRecord`, `recordPosition`, `recordOutsideResults`), translated once for the product; see [Locale](?path=/docs/components-locale--docs).
