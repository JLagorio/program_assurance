# Related

A card of linked records, in a rail or in the body of a page. Its header is the kind of record, a Count and the way to add one. Its body is a row per record in a rail, or a card per record on a page: the name as the link, a mark, one status, the properties the reader decides by, and the actions that show on hover. Its footer is "See all" when it shows a handful of many. When nothing is linked it is a proper [Empty](?path=/docs/components-empty--docs), the mark, a statement and the way to fill it, never a line of grey text.

On the body of a page: a header with the kind and "+ Add", a card per related record with its name as the link and up to six properties, the actions in a menu that shows on hover, "view all" at the bottom. In the rail, one row per record. A card is metadata with actions on hover, and a card that carries actions of its own cannot be one click target. So the title is the link and the actions are stops of their own, in both layouts.

## Anatomy

The header and the footer are the card's; the rows are [Item](?path=/docs/components-item--docs)s; the cards are `Related.Card`.

| Part                   | What it is                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Title                  | The kind of record, a noun, an h3 in `font.body` semibold: "Linked findings", "Systems", "Team".                                                                  |
| Count                  | After the title: how many are linked.                                                                                                                            |
| Action                 | At the end of the header's line: one small button, "Add" or "Link" with a plus, or a TextLink. The way to add one; it stays when the card is empty.               |
| Row (`list`)           | An Item, flush: a mark or an id, the name as the link, and the state or the date at the end. The hairlines and the hover fill span the card; the text sits at its inset. |
| Card (`cards`)         | A `Related.Card` in a grid: the mark, the name as the link, the status and the meta under it, the properties, the actions at the top end, a line of its own under. |
| Card · Leading         | The mark before the name, 32px, spanning the name and the meta line: a medium Avatar, square for a thing, round for a person.                                      |
| Card · Title           | The record's name, `font.body` medium, one line. With `link`, the link, underlined on hover.                                                                       |
| Card · Meta            | Under the name, `font.body.small` subtle: kind, path, owner.                                                                                                       |
| Card · Status          | One Badge or Indicator at the start of the meta line.                                                                                                              |
| Card · Properties      | Label and value pairs under the head, KeyValue rows with a 96px label: four fit, six is the most.                                                                  |
| Card · Actions         | Icon buttons at the top end, their space kept so nothing shifts: open in a new tab, a menu with the rest. Hidden until hover or focus, always shown on a touch screen, kept shown while a menu is open. |
| Card · Children        | A line of its own under the properties: a row of Badges, a sentence.                                                                                              |
| Footer                 | Under the rows or the cards, after a rule: "See all 14" as a TextLink.                                                                                             |
| Empty                  | Where the rows would be: a compact Empty with a link icon, the title, a line and an action, from `empty`.                                                          |

## Variants

| Layout          | Use                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `list`          | A rail beside a record: one Item row per record, compact. What a row can carry at 270px is an id, a name and one thing at the end.                    |
| `cards`         | The body of a page: a grid of cards, as many across as fit at 240px each, sharing the row's width. A card carries the mark, the name, the status and the meta, four properties and the actions. |
| A related table | When the records need columns to compare: a Table in a [Card](?path=/docs/components-card--docs), headed by CardHeader. Not a Related; it is a section of the page. |

Not built, and why: the whole card as the link, because a card that carries its own actions cannot be one click target and the actions are here; a card that scrolls, because a rail shows a handful and the footer says See all; search, sort and filters in the header, because a card long enough to need them is the record's tab, where the Table has them; a card that folds, because a handful of rows has nothing to fold; drag to reorder, because links have no order of their own.

## Sizes

| Size      | Row                             | Where                                             |
| --------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `compact` | 28px for one line, the default. | The rail beside a record's overview.              |
| `default` | 36px for one line.              | A list beside a page's body, in a wider column.   |

The size is the list's; every row in one card is the same height. A card in the grid is at least 240px wide and as many share a row as fit; it is as tall as its properties, and four properties keep a row of cards one height. The inset is `space.150` in a card, `space.200` in the header.

## States

| State           | What changes                                                                                                                         |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Hover on a card | `elevation.shadow.raised` under it; the actions appear; the name underlines under the pointer.                                       |
| Focus in a card | The actions appear while anything in the card has focus, so a keyboard reaches them; the focus ring is on the name or the button.    |
| Touch           | The actions are always shown; there is no hover to reveal them.                                                                      |
| A menu open     | The actions stay shown while the card's menu is open.                                                                                |
| Hover on a row  | The Item's: `color.background.neutral.subtle.hovered` across the row.                                                                |
| Empty           | The compact Empty where the rows would be; the header and its action stay.                                                           |

## Modifiers

- **`title`**, **`count`**, **`action`**, **`footer`**, **`layout`**, **`size`**, **`empty`** as in Anatomy. `empty` is a string, the Empty's title, or the Empty's title, description, action and icon.
- **The rows** take the Item's props: `id`, `leading`, `title`, `meta`, `trailing`, `link` or `onSelect`.
- **The cards** take **`leading`**, **`title`**, **`link`**, **`meta`**, **`status`**, **`properties`**, **`actions`** and children.

## Content

- **The title is a noun**, the kind of record: "Linked findings", "Systems", "Team". Never a sentence.
- **A row is an id and a name**, and one thing at the end: the state as a word, or the date. A rail is too narrow for meta beside the name; a role beside a person's name is as much as fits.
- **A card is the name, the meta, one status and four properties**: the ones the reader decides by. Six is the most; past that the record has them.
- **The actions are what is done to the link**, not to the record: open in a new tab, edit the link, unlink. Never "Open"; the name is that. Unlink is the menu's last item, in danger.
- **A handful, then See all.** Five rows in a rail, a row of cards on a page; the footer says how many there are, and the record's tab lists them.
- **The empty state says what would be here and how to fill it**: "Nothing linked yet", "Not in a package yet", with the line and a link button. The header's action stays, so the way to add one is there twice at most, never nowhere.
- **People are neutral.** A round Avatar and the name; colour is for state.

## Accessibility

- **The heading is an `h3`** under the rail's or the section's h2, and the rows or the cards are a list named by it: a screen reader hears "Systems, list, 3 items".
- **The name is the link**, in a row and in a card, named by the record's name alone; the id, the status, the meta and the properties are read as the text beside it. A row's link is stretched over the row; a card's is the name, because the card has actions of its own.
- **The actions are always in the tab order.** They are hidden by opacity, not removed, and they show while any of them has focus; a keyboard reader reaches "Open Ground segment in a new tab" and "More for Ground segment" after the name. On a touch screen they are always shown.
- **Each action is a labelled icon button** that names the record: "Unlink" alone would be six identical buttons on a page of cards.
- **The count is text**, read after the title; the footer's link says how many.
- **The empty state is text, then the action**: the icon is hidden, the title and the line are read first, the button is a stop after them.
- **The hover shadow is decoration.** Nothing is said by it.

## Migration

`Related.Row` and `RelatedRowProps` are removed. Use `Item`: `leading` replaces `lead`, `title` replaces `label`, and `onSelect` or `link` replaces `onClick`. Card links compose native props and refs through Base UI; leave the supplied link empty to use the card title, or supply its own children to override the link text.
