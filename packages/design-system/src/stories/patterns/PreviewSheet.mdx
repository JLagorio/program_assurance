# PreviewSheet

The peek panel: a Sheet that previews a row over the page. Its header is the compact RecordHeader: the Preview eyebrow with the id and one `status`, the title, the meta line, at most three `facts`. The footer's first item opens the full record, more `links` follow, `actions` sit on the right. `onBack` when it is one frame of a stack.
