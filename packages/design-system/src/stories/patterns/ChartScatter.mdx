# Scatter

A point per record on two value axes: where each thing sits on two measures at once. Findings by how long they have been open and what they expose; systems by cost and open findings. Points fall into groups of a tone, three at most; a bubble's area follows a third value; two references make quadrants. Each point is a record, so a click opens its card. It lives in a [Chart](?path=/docs/patterns-chart-overview--docs) Frame.

## Anatomy

| Part      | What it is                                                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Point     | 8px across in its group's tone, ringed 2px in the surface so overlapping points stay apart, with a hit area 24px across.                         |
| Bubble    | With `z`, a point whose area follows the value (twice the value, twice the ink), at 70% so what is under it shows.                               |
| Axes      | Both value axes, a hairline on the category side, ticks in `color.text.subtlest`, titles in `color.text.subtle` when the keys do not say enough. |
| Grid      | Hairlines both ways, since both axes are values.                                                                                                 |
| Reference | A dashed hairline at a value on either axis; one on each makes quadrants.                                                                        |
| Tooltip   | The point's name and group, then each axis as name and value in its format, then any other point at the same place. Per point, not per column.   |
| Card      | On a chosen point: its name, group and axes, then the caller's facts and a way to the record.                                                    |

## Variants

| Variant   | Use                                                                                                                     |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One tone  | Every point in `brand`: the shape of the cloud is the point.                                                            |
| `groups`  | Up to three groups by a key, each a tone: status groups in the status tones, kinds in the first three categorical hues. |
| `z`       | A bubble: exposure, cost, count.                                                                                        |
| Quadrants | A `reference` on each axis: the four corners the reader names ("treat", "accept").                                      |

## Sizes

`size` is the plot's height: `small` 120px, `medium` 200px, `large` 320px (the right one for a scatter that is a page's point), or `height` in pixels. Unsaid, it is the Frame's; in the Frame's expanded Dialog it is `large`. Bubbles range from 64 to 900 square pixels whatever the plot's size.

## States

| State    | What changes                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hover    | The tooltip at the point. No cursor band, since there is no category.                                                                                                                                               |
| Isolated | A group hidden from the Frame's legend leaves the plot.                                                                                                                                                             |
| Focused  | With `onSelect` or `details`, on the keyboard: a 2px ring in `color.border.focused` around the point, outside its surface ring.                                                                                     |
| Chosen   | With `onSelect` or `details`: the points take the pointer, a click, Enter or Space chooses one, the others dim and the chosen one gains a halo; the card opens on it, and offers any other point at the same place. |
| Loading  | Eight skeleton dots on two hairlines, pulsing, at the plot's height.                                                                                                                                                |
| Entering | The points arrive over `motion.duration.slow`.                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Modifiers

- **`z`** for a bubble, **`zLabel`** for what its size is called; **`nameKey`** for the key that names a point. `name` still names the key and is deprecated for `nameKey`, since `name` and `label` name the plot on every other part.
- **`groupBy`** and **`groups`** for the groups and their tones; **`tone`** for every point when there are none.
- **`xDomain`** and **`yDomain`** pin an axis' ends (a number or `"auto"` each); with both ends pinned the ticks are round steps across them, and **`xTicks`** and **`yTicks`** set them outright.
- **`format`** for every axis without its own; **`formatX`**, **`formatY`** and **`formatZ`** for each axis, in its ticks, the tooltip, the card and the table.
- **`reference`**, **`xLabel`**, **`yLabel`** as on the other parts.
- **`onSelect`** and **`details`** as in States; the selection is `{ datum, group, index }`.
- **`loading`** draws the skeleton; the Frame sets it from `state="loading"`.
- **`label`** names the plot outside a Frame.

## Content

- **Both axes are measures.** A category on one axis is a Bar or a strip; two categories are a Heatmap.
- **Three groups at most.** The first three hues validate against each other in every pairing; a fourth does not. Finer distinctions are the tooltip's or a filter's.
- **Name the points** with `nameKey`, so the tooltip, the card and a point's button say which record.
- **Pin a bounded measure's axis**: a 0–100 score takes `yDomain={[0, 100]}`, so the plot does not run past the scale.
- **Points that share a place are listed together**: the tooltip and the card name each, and the card offers the others. Where many share places, the data is a Heatmap.
- **Title the axes** when the keys are not words the reader would say: "Likelihood", "Impact".
- **A quadrant's meaning is in the description**, not drawn: "the lines split the register into quadrants".

## Style

| Element   | Token                                                                                   |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Point     | `color.chart.<tone>` · 8px · 2px `elevation.surface` ring · 24px hit area               |
| Bubble    | The same at 70% · area from 64 to 900 square pixels                                     |
| Grid      | `color.border` · 1px both ways                                                          |
| Reference | `color.chart.neutral` · 1px dashed 4 3                                                  |
| Chosen    | The point's tone: a halo 8px wider at 20%                                               |
| Dimmed    | `opacity.disabled`, over `motion.duration.fast`                                         |
| Focused   | 2px `color.border.focused` ring, 3px out from the point · `Highlight` in forced colours |
| Skeleton  | `color.skeleton` dots on `color.border` hairlines                                       |

## Accessibility

- **Named by the Frame**, or by `label`: a scatter that chooses nothing is an image (`role="img"`) with no tab stop, and the table is the way in: one row per point, a column per axis. When `onSelect` or `details` is set, the plot is a group and each point a named button and a tab stop ("POA-102, Open: Days open 212 d, Exposure $420K"); Enter and Space choose it as a click does. One that chooses and has no name is named "Chart", with a warning in development.
- **The table is one toggle away.** In a Frame the scatter lays its points out as the Frame's table twin and CSV: the point's `name` under "Point", its group's label under "Group", the Frame's `columns` with `place: "before"`, a column per axis headed by `xLabel`, `yLabel` and `zLabel`, then the columns after. The groups stay one column of words: the legend's groups never become a column each. The legend keys the groups when the Frame is given no `series`.
- **A chosen point's card is a dialog**: the point says it opens one (`aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded`), and focus returns to that point when it closes.
- **Three groups keep every pair apart** under deutan and protan vision; the legend carries their names, and the tooltip and the card say the group in words.
- **A point's hit area is 24px** whatever its size, so a small point still takes a click.
- **Loading is `aria-busy`** and named "[the plot's name], loading".
