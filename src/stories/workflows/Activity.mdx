# Activity

Application-owned workflow composition. Its states, actions and data conventions belong to this prototype; it adapts shared Ledger patterns for presentation and interaction.

Everything that happened to a record, in one feed, and the way to add to it. The work happens outside the platform: a call, a hallway answer, a file someone sends. It comes back in through the **log bar**: a note, a task, a request to a person, a link to what arrived. What the system did, a field changed, a stage moved, a task closed, sits in the same feed with an icon where a person's event has a face. One feed, filtered by record, by person or by program; a record's page shows its own slice, last.

**Activity** is the feed, **Activity.Item** one event, **Activity.Group** a run under a sticky label. **Activity.Composer** is the box that adds to it, with `@` completion over the people who can be mentioned, and **Activity.Text** draws a body with its mentions tinted. **Activity.LogBar** is the row of ways in.

## Anatomy

| Part     | What it is                                                                                                                                     |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Feed     | A [Timeline](http://localhost:6007/?path=/docs/components-timeline--docs) at `large`, the rail on the left, newest first.                      |
| Marker   | A `small` Avatar for a person's event, 24px. For the system's, a disc in the kind's tone with its icon.                                        |
| Sentence | The name in weight 500, then what they did in the past tense: "asked Joel Barrantes for the account review procedure". One line; it truncates. |
| Meta     | Under the sentence, subtle: the kind's icon and word, then the record it was on when the feed spans records.                                   |
| Time     | At the end of the sentence's line, as the reader would say it: "2h ago", "27 Aug". The full stamp is its tooltip.                              |
| Body     | Under the meta: an Activity.Text, an attachment row, a before and after.                                                                       |
| Footer   | The last line: a Badge, or the actions on the event.                                                                                           |
| Composer | The author's Avatar, a three-line field, the people list that `@` opens, and the line under it with the shortcut and the button.               |
| Log bar  | Small secondary Buttons in a row, one pressed while its composer or dialog is open.                                                            |
| Mention  | `@Name` in the information tint, inline. A button when it opens the person.                                                                    |

## Variants

Two kinds of event and one feed. A **person's** event carries their face and their name leads the sentence. The **system's** carries the kind's icon and starts with what happened. They are not two lists: a request and the task it closed sit next to each other.

The kinds, each an icon and a tone: `note`, `comment`, `task`, `request`, `link`, `change`, `stage`, `assign`, `done`, `created`. Four come in through the log bar. The rest the system writes.

## Modifiers

- **`actor`** on an Item: the person, by full name. Without it, the system.
- **`kind`** on an Item: the icon, the tone and the word.
- **`meta`** on an Item: where it happened, when the feed spans records.
- **`emphasis`**, **`link`**, **`onSelect`**, **`footer`** on an Item, as in States and Anatomy.
- **`people`** on the Composer: who `@` offers. Without it, no list.
- **`onCancel`** on the Composer: a Cancel beside the button, when the log bar opened it.
- **`children`** on the Composer: fields above the body, a "To" for a request.
- **`submitLabel`**, **`placeholder`**, **`actor`** on the Composer.
- **`value`** on the LogBar: the item that is open, pressed.
- **`mention`** on Text: how to draw a mention, when it should open the person.

## Content

- **The sentence is past tense, the person first:** "asked", "linked", "took ownership". Not "Request sent", not a code.
- **The system's sentence starts with what moved:** "Implementation moved from Planned to Partially implemented".
- **The body is what was said or sent.** A note, a reply, the file's name. Not a restatement of the sentence.
- **A mention is written `@[Full Name]`** and drawn as `@Name`. The person sees it in their queue; prose with a first name tells nobody.
- **The log bar has four items at most,** nouns: Note, Task, Request, Evidence.
- **Group by when the reader would:** Today, Yesterday, Last week, then the month.
- **Newest first.** The feed is what happened since they last looked.

## Accessibility

- **The feed is a list** named `label`; each row is an item. The Timeline sets it.
- **The time is a `<time>`** when `dateTime` is given, its full stamp the tooltip.
- **The composer's field is a textbox** named by `label` or the placeholder, with `aria-autocomplete="list"`. While the people list is open it is the field's `aria-controls`, and the active row its `aria-activedescendant`; the list is a listbox named "People", each name an option.
- **Keyboard in the composer:** type `@` to open the list; Up and Down move, Enter or Tab write the mention, Escape closes. ⌘↵ or Ctrl+↵ posts. Tab leaves the field for the buttons.
- **A mention is text**, or a button when it opens the person.
- **The log bar is a group** named `label`; the open item says `aria-pressed`.
- **The a11y gate** checks the matrix: the feed's roles and names, the composer closed and its controls, the log bar's group.

### Activity.Text and Activity.Mention

`Activity.Text` takes the body as a string with mentions written `@[Full Name]`, and `mention` to draw one. `Activity.Mention` takes `name` and `onSelect`. `parseMentions(text)` returns the names, once each, for the caller that stores them.
