# Task

Application-owned workflow composition. Its states, actions and data conventions belong to this prototype; it adapts shared Ledger patterns for presentation and interaction.

The atom of the work: a verb, an object, a name and a date. A task is asked on a record, listed on that record and in the assignee's queue, and it closes when what it asked for exists. **Task** is one row: the box, the ask, then who has it, when it is due and what it waits on. **Task.List** stacks them with the empty state when there are none.

A row is one line. Done is a line through the title and nothing else. Waiting is the one badge, because a question sent is not a question answered.

## Anatomy

| Part     | What it is                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Box      | A Checkbox in the [Item](http://localhost:6007/?path=/docs/components-item--docs)'s leading slot, named "Complete: " or "Reopen: " and the title. Absent without `onDoneChange`. |
| Title    | The ask, `font.body`, one line; the link or button of the row when it opens. Struck through when done.                                                                           |
| Subject  | Inline after the title, subtle: the record it sits on, when the list spans records.                                                                                              |
| Assignee | At the end, a Person: the `xsmall` Avatar and the name, subtle.                                                                                                                  |
| Due      | After the assignee, tabular: as the reader would say it. Danger and weight 500 when overdue.                                                                                     |
| Badge    | Last: "Waiting on Name" in information, "Blocked" in danger. Nothing for open or done.                                                                                           |
| Actions  | Buttons at the row's end, beside the row's link, never inside it.                                                                                                                |
| List     | An Item.Group: hairlines between rows, a heading with a Count and one action, the Empty when there are none.                                                                     |

## Variants

One row, listed two ways. **On a record**, the rows are that record's tasks and the subject is not shown. **In a queue**, the list spans records and every row says where it sits with `subject`. Both are the same Task.

<Canvas of={Stories.InAQueue} />

A task is not a card, a percent or a Gantt bar. A plan with dates across time is a [Timeline](http://localhost:6007/?path=/docs/components-timeline--docs) or a Stepper; a checklist with met and unmet conditions is [Gates](http://localhost:6007/?path=/docs/components-gates--docs).

## Sizes

One row height, the Item's, so a task list is one rhythm with the Related rows and the Activity feed beside it. `size="compact"` on the List tightens the rows for a rail.

## States

| State   | Look                                                                                                                |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open    | The default. The box empty, the title in `color.text`, no badge.                                                    |
| Waiting | The box empty and "Waiting on Name" at the end. Asked and not answered.                                             |
| Blocked | The box empty and "Blocked" at the end.                                                                             |
| Done    | The box checked and the title struck through in `color.text.subtle`. No badge.                                      |
| Overdue | Past due and not done: the due in `color.text.danger`, weight 500. A modifier on the three open states.             |
| Opens   | With `link` or `onSelect` the title stretches over the row and tints under the pointer; the box stays its own stop. |

## Modifiers

- **`state`**, as in States.
- **`onDoneChange`**: the box. Without it, a list the reader only reads.
- **`assignee`**, **`due`**, **`dueDateTime`**, **`overdue`**, **`waitingOn`**, as in Anatomy.
- **`subject`**: where it sits, in a queue.
- **`link`** or **`onSelect`**: the row opens the record it sits on.
- **`actions`**: reassign, a menu.
- On the List: **`title`**, **`count`**, **`action`**, **`empty`**, **`size`**, **`flush`** inside a Card.

## Content

- **The title is a verb and an object:** "Confirm the account review procedure", "Link the IdP's quarterly report". Not a control id, not a noun alone, not a sentence with a because.
- **One line.** What the ask is about lives on the record the task sits on; a task that needs a paragraph is a note with a task under it.
- **The due date is a day,** as the reader would say it: "Today", "Fri 4 Sep", "3d overdue". Not a timestamp.
- **Waiting names the person.** "Waiting on Joel Barrantes", so the reader knows who to nudge.
- **Done is quiet.** The struck title is the whole message. No badge, no percent, no confetti.
- **Empty says what fills it:** "No tasks. Ask for something from the log bar."
- **Open first, then waiting, then done,** and done folded when there are many.

## Accessibility

- **The box is a checkbox** named "Complete: " or "Reopen: " and the title, so a screen reader hears what it completes. It is its own stop beside the row's link.
- **The list is the Item.Group's list,** named by its heading or `labelledBy`.
- **The due is a `<time>`** when `dueDateTime` is given.
- **Waiting and Blocked are text** in the badge, not colour alone. Overdue is the danger colour and weight 500, and the word "overdue" in the due when the caller writes it that way.
- **Keyboard:** Tab reaches the box, then the row's link or button, then its actions. Space toggles the box.
- **The a11y gate** checks the matrix: the boxes' names, the list's role, the contrast of the struck title.

### Task.List

By hand until the generated tables reach a part:

| Prop         | Type        | What it is                                                |
| ------------ | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `title`      | `ReactNode` | A heading over the rows.                                  |
| `count`      | `number`    | A Count after the title.                                  |
| `action`     | `ReactNode` | At the end of the heading's line: "Add task".             |

| `flush`      | `boolean`   | Rows edge to edge, inside a Card.                         |

| `children`   | `ReactNode` | Task rows.                                                |
