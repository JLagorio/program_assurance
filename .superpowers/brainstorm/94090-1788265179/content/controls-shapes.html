Which shape do we build?

A

WorkPane

List stays, work on the right. Family rollup becomes navigation; SCTM is a grain toggle; inheritance and currency are disclosures on the control.

B

Dense matrix with inline expand

One full-width table, facets on top, rows expand in place. Densest and closest to the export artifact, worst place to actually write a narrative.

C

Queue first

Role-shaped buckets of what you owe today; the full set and the requirement grain are sibling modes.

A+C

WorkPane, but the empty pane is the queue

Shape A exactly — except before you pick a control, the right side is C's bucketed list rather than a flat blocker table. One page, both jobs, no mode switch.
