## 3. Cross-cutting patterns

- **One owner on everything, with a fallback.** Risks, questionnaires, questions, knowledge-base items, accounts, inventory items, vendor assessments and deprovisioning tasks all take an owner; teams can own; a free-text "group owner" covers people outside Vanta; "Unassigned" is shown, never hidden.
- **Owner gaps are a worklist.** Access › Suggestions ranks "Owner is unassigned" as High with an inline Assign owner; banners count them ("Assign 47 active accounts to owners"); a template agent scans for "Controls Without Owners".
- **Due dates come from policy, not typing.** Task due = assignment + SLA; vulnerability due = first-seen timestamp + SLA by severity (to the minute); vendor reassessment = frequency by inherent risk tier; AI assessment review = approval + 1 year; answer verification = cadence; settings changes apply only to new items.
- **Review cycles are windows with statuses.** Recurring risk assessments (every 3/6/12 months, due 1–3 months later) add Not started / Due soon / Overdue / Completed beside the approval status; an old approval does not satisfy a new window.
- **Before → after on re-review.** The recurring "Complete assessment" card shows "Inherent risk 9 → 16" and requires a comment only when a score or treatment changed; the approver sees the same comparison; AI change summaries do the same for policies.
- **Approvals with a fixed grammar.** Draft → Needs review → Pending approval (read-only) → Approved; up to 5 steps × 3 approvers; Approve or Request changes / Declined; editing after approval sends it back to review; approval history is its own tab; an approved assessment changes only as a new version.
- **AI suggests, a person accepts.** "✧ Suggest" controls on a risk, "Flagged by Vanta Agent" questions, a suggested answer beside the vendor's with "select primary answer" and "mark reviewed" locking it, commitment coverage with ✓ acknowledge / ⚑ flag, conflicts with an AI summary, custom-agent runs in "Needs approval". Edited AI values are marked "user-modified"; reasons can be saved to "agent memory".
- **Provenance on every answer.** "Answered by answer library", "Edited by Madison Carter", "Imported from Answer library Apr 15, 2025", "Verified via integration on February 17, 2026, 9:52 AM", "AI answers generated on Jul 02, 2026 1:53 PM", "Sources examined".
- **Freeze points.** Risk snapshots lock; test Workpapers are immutable; a recommended vendor assessment goes read-only and its comment threads lock; contracts and imported Trust Center documents are point-in-time copies; reports and PDFs are generated, not stored.
- **Draft, review, publish.** The Trust Center editor counts draft changes, previews as each viewer type, and publishes from a side panel that lists every change with its own undo and Discard all.
- **Sharing with outsiders is graded.** Trust Center visibility Private / Shareable / Requestable / Public; access with expiry, NDA (clickwrap or DocuSign, account-level), tags, watermark, auto-approval by domain or CRM, block lists, request history and right to be forgotten; Vanta Exchange by magic link per email or domain; auditors see risk snapshots only when shared and inside the audit window; reports shared per person or on a schedule.
- **Counted segments over the table.** "Due soon or overdue 130 · Owned by me 116 · Completed 633 · All 999+", "All 87 · Needs attention 31", "Open 477 · Closed 135 · Escalated 0", "Needs answer 0 · Flagged 0 · Draft 1 · Approved 9"; filters show their count in colour ("Status 3").
- **Big numbers first, then the list.** "Gap 6 · Unclear 25 · Covered 55"; "Overall answer coverage 42% ↘ -54%"; "Systems 17 (94%) of 18 total"; twin heatmaps of current and residual risk.
- **One selection bar grammar.** "1 selected · Deselect" then the verbs (Give Access, Deny, Send reminder, Modify groups, Offboard, Deactivate monitoring, Move to managed vendor, Ignore, Reject); bulk actions explain who is skipped and why.
- **Act from Slack.** Approve or deny Trust Center requests, answer assigned questions, request access with /vanta, approve blocked agent runs; notifications batch (5, 15 or 30 minutes; digests weekly).
- **Stepper for multi-stage chores.** Deprovisioning (Review → Remove access → Complete "Removed 14 accounts"), Starter Guide (Onboarding → Program implementation → Risk remediation), employee task checklist "0 of 6 tasks completed".
- **"—" means "not specified", in the data's words.** The commitments table defines "—" as "Vanta AI determined that a value wasn't specified in the contract".

## 4. For Program Assurance

- **System boundary and inventory (CM-8, PL-2):** adopt the segment Scope card ("Systems 17 (94%) of 18 total · Configure") and its "Recent activity" line per scope change as the authorization boundary's own record; a boundary change is a significant change, so log it boldly.
- **Inventory:** adopt the asset-type rail with counts, an Owner on every item (person or group), a Framework/boundary chip column and tag mapping (VantaOwner, VantaNonProd); not Vanta's employee-laptop agent, which is outside a program's boundary.
- **Personnel and roles (PS, AT, AC-2):** adopt group → task set (union of sets) with a per-person checklist and "Last reminded on…", applied to RMF roles (AO, ISSM, ISSO, assessor) for role-based training and rules-of-behavior acceptance (inferred mapping).
- **Offboarding (PS-4, AC-2(3)):** adopt the deprovisioning stepper and per-account "Verified via integration on …" proof; it is the cleanest Vanta screen for evidence an assessor can trust.
- **Roles and permissions:** adopt object-scoped roles (Risk Scenario Owner can approve, Register Viewer cannot) beside program_role_assignments; keep RMF's six roles fixed rather than Vanta's custom-role builder.
- **ConMon:** adopt SLA due dates from first-detected time by severity, the History tab of on-time vs missed, and "Deactivate until a date / until a fix is available" with a required reason, as the POA&M deviation (operational requirement, false positive, risk adjustment) (inferred mapping).
- **ConMon reviews:** adopt recurring assessment windows (Due soon / Overdue) and the "before → after, comment required when changed" card for the monthly and annual ConMon cycle and for re-scoring risks.
- **Supply chain (SR-3, SR-5, SR-6) and component suppliers:** adopt the inherent-risk rubric (attributes by category → tier with a "Scoring explanation"), the assessment-rules matrix (tier → frequency + required evidence) and a supplier portal like Vanta Exchange for a shared component's owner; findings link to risk scenarios.
- **Supplier decision:** adopt "Make recommendation" per assessment type and "Make decision" per supplier (Approved / Conditionally approved / Not approved + residual risk + summary), frozen once made.
- **Risk register and AO acceptance:** adopt multi-step approval with an approval-history tab, treatment "Accept" requiring an approver, and locked snapshots marked "Shared with auditor"; make the AO the final step of acceptance.
- **Risk display:** adopt the twin 5×5 heatmaps (current and residual) and the rubric editor's live matrix; keep scores numeric pills, as the program already does.
- **Authorization package sharing with an AO or customer:** adopt the Trust Center mechanics (Give access with expiry, NDA, tagged resources, request history, watermark, draft → review changes → publish) as a private package room, with a live OSCAL SSP / SAR / POA&M resource, which Vanta now publishes for FedRAMP.
- **Customer requirements (the Compliance matrix):** adopt Commitments' "Analysis summary" (Gap / Unclear / Covered), "Needs attention" segment, per-row coverage rationale with "Sources examined", and Acknowledge / Flag for redline.
- **AI:** adopt "Suggest" with accept/reject and agent runs waiting in "Needs approval", attributed to the agent and the person; do not let AI write determinations, risk acceptances or supplier answers unreviewed.
- **Not:** a public marketing page, visitor chatbot, revenue-influenced reporting, G2, the referral prompts in the profile menu, or questionnaire automation for sales; Vanta's "OK %" does not replace control status by assessment objective.
