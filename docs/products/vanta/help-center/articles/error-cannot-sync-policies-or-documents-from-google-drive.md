# Error: Cannot Sync Policies or Documents from Google Drive

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345962-error-cannot-sync-policies-or-documents-from-google-drive
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

After integrating Google Drive, if you are still unable to sync over documents, or seeing a "No results found" message, please verify the following:
 
- 
Google Drive is connected, and no errors are showing on the Integrations page
[image 01]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516763236/c756f0f9c46f44e6bdb1f1dfebfe/15174245264660?expires=1794967200&signature=93e388d0de2f0e77717def8349ea6afa211d23452e14b62aad05cffdee85f778&req=dSUmEM54noNcX%2FMW1HO4zXhfOhUgw7xD1YLpnrpxRl2gWbTUEIQ7hAKfX6gS%0AXoHQtCFH1modQzfxdIY%3D%0A>
- 
Verify that there is a shared Google Drive and that the Vanta user who set up the Google Drive connection in Vanta is shared on the drive:
[image 02]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516763237/11f9fd0286fd2ffaea7a2101e0bf/15174308796052?expires=1794967200&signature=cecc31716e82ad6ac059a81503e3672044c536c7a6a45addab22889b8e631683&req=dSUmEM54noNcXvMW1HO4zdzDE3dIacygxiVk%2BBvaPe5ko9HvedDARIflhHdI%0AaWLEuYL6spV7OQIi6S8%3D%0A>
- 
Verify that the policy you are trying to sync is in Google Doc or .pdf format.
- 
Note: If your files are in .docx format, we recommend using the Upload a file from the computer <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345499> option
- 
If policies are still failing to sync, contact Vanta technical support at [email protected]
