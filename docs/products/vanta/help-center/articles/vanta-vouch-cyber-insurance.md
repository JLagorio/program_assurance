## Article text

Vanta now offers Vouch cyber insurance within our platform. You can apply for insurance from Vanta, select and customize your policy, and pay for it. Your policy will then automatically be linked back to Vanta as evidence.
 
Reach out to your Customer Success contact if you are interested in this feature.

Go to your cyber insurance document <https://app.vanta.com/tests/cybersecurity-insurance-policy>

You can optionally customize your package and select the exact coverages you want to purchase (when in doubt, we recommend just purchasing the Compliance package).
- 
Pay for your insurance using a credit card or ACH.
- 
Once you finish purchasing insurance, you’ll receive an email from Vouch with your insurance document to sign.
- 
After you sign the insurance document, your insurance will be processed by Vouch and will appear on your cyber insurance document <https://app.vanta.com/tests/cybersecurity-insurance-policy> within the next few minutes. Once the insurance is fetched by Vanta, the document will automatically start passing and your COI will be linked to that page (this is the evidence your auditor will want to see for compliance). You can also view the details of your coverage.
- 
If you’d like, you can sign into your Vouch account to view your insurance and make changes.

## Human Review
In some cases, a Vouch underwriter may need to review your application to help determine the best coverage for your business. When this happens, the purchase flow in Vanta will be paused, and a Vouch underwriter will contact you to collect additional information. In this scenario, receiving your quote can take a few business days. Once you receive your quote, you can return to the Vouch purchase flow in Vanta and continue your purchase (see steps #7 and above).
 

## Access your Vouch Account
To access your Vouch account, go to the Vouch portal <https://app.vouch.us/loginportal/send>, click Create Account, and create an account using the email address tied to your insurance policy.
 

## FAQs
There’s a problem with the Vouch insurance flow, how do I reach out to them?
- 
Please contact [email protected].
What if I already have insurance with Vouch?
- 
If you already have insurance with Vouch, you’ll be directed to sign into your Vouch account and upload your policy document directly to the document in Vanta.
Why did Vouch decline my application?
There are a variety of reasons your application may be declined including:
- 
Vouch is not filed to sell insurance in your location
- 
Your business is not currently in an industry that we insure with our proprietary coverages today
What happens if Vouch has declined my application?
- 
If Vouch has declined your application, Vouch can still help you get insured. Vouch’s brokerage arm can obtain cyber coverage from other insurance carriers. In the event you are declined, a member of the Vouch team will reach out to you. Alternatively, you can contact [email protected]
Is Cyber Insurance a hard requirement for SOC 2?
- 
No. Learn more about Cyber insurance, here <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345513>.
