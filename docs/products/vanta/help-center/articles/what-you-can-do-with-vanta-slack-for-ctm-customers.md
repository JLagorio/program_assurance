## Article text

Vanta's Slack integration unlocks three powerful workflows for Customer Trust Management (CTM) teams. This article provides a quick overview of each and links to detailed setup instructions.
 
⚠️ Prerequisite for all three: A Vanta admin must connect Vanta to your Slack workspace first. See Setting Up Slack Notifications <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345744-setting-up-slack-notifications>.
 

## 1: Approve Trust Center access requests in Slack 
When a visitor requests access to your Trust Center, you can approve or deny the request directly from a Slack DM, without opening Vanta. This dramatically speeds up access resolution time and keeps your prospects and customers moving forward.
 
Who this is for: Trust Center admins and reviewers.
 
🔗 Approving Trust Center Access Requests in Slack <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/14828845-approving-trust-center-access-requests-in-slack>
 
 

## 2: Answer security questions in Slack 
Your GTM and customer-facing teams can ask security and compliance questions directly in Slack and get instant, accurate answers sourced from your CTM knowledge base. Security teams stay in control of what's shared; everyone else gets fast, consistent answers without switching tools.
 
Who this is for: GTM, sales, and customer-facing teams; security teams who want to reduce repetitive requests.
 
🔗 Ask Vanta Questions in Slack <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/13017851-ask-vanta-questions-in-slack>
 
 

## 3: Collaborate on Questionnaires in Slack
Subject matter experts (SMEs) assigned to individual questionnaire questions receive a Slack notification and can submit their answers directly from Slack, no Vanta login required. This keeps the questionnaire review process moving even for teammates who rarely log in to Vanta.
 
Who this is for: SMEs and cross-functional contributors assigned to security questionnaires.
 
🔗Collaborating on Questionnaires <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345448-collaborating-on-questionnaires>
 
 
For general Slack setup, see Setting Up Slack Notifications <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345744-setting-up-slack-notifications>.
