# Integrate Wrike & Vanta

Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Connecting Wrike to Vanta helps automate evidence collection for your risk management tasks. This saves you time and keeps your compliance efforts on track! If you manage tasks and projects in Wrike, this integration makes it simple to pull that information into Vanta automatically.
 

## Before You Start
Make sure you have the following:
- 
Wrike admin access
- 
Vanta admin access
You’ll need admin permissions in Wrike to approve the connection with Vanta.
 

## Connecting Wrike to Vanta
- 
In Vanta, go to the Integrations page, click Add integration, and search for Wrike. For help, see our guide to the Integrations Page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345761-integrations-page>.
- 
Click Connect.
- 
A window will open, asking you to sign in to Wrike (if you’re not already signed in)
- 
Approve the connection by selecting Allow access
- 
Then select the option that lists Enable Read Access Only
- 
Click Connect

## What Happens After Connecting
- 
Vanta will automatically pull in tasks and projects from Wrike to help with your risk management evidence collection.
- 
You can review and manage this evidence directly from Vanta’s platform.
 
If you need to disconnect Wrike later, just return to the Integrations <https://app.vanta.com/integrations> page in Vanta and select Disconnect next to Wrike.
