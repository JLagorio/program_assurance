# Error: “Unable to Reconnect Zenefits to Vanta”

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345934-error-unable-to-reconnect-zenefits-to-vanta
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

## Environment Details
Trouble reconnecting Zenefits to Vanta. Vanta is stuck on "Loading your resources" and says "connected" but doesn't have the green checkbox
 

## Cause
Some permissions are disabled for the API token in use for Zenefits <https://app.vanta.com/integrations?state=available>
 

## Resolution
Enable the following permissions in Zenefits.  <https://app.vanta.com/integrations?state=available>
Once enabled, verify the users are coming through
