# Google Compliance Reports Manager: Downloading Reports for Vendors Page in Vanta

## Article text

If you are a Google user and would like to quickly find and download SOC2 reports or ISO27001 certifications for your Vendor Page in Vanta, follow these steps below:
- 
Visit Google’s Compliance Reports Manager https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/compliance-reports-manager#/ <https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/compliance-reports-manager#/>
- 
Select the appropriate filters
- 
Select the report or certification you’d like to download
- 
Click Download
Note that the report will be specific to the Google product(s) you select and will cover the relevant controls and processes related to that product(s). If you need additional information or have questions about the report, you may contact the Google Workspace support team for assistance.
