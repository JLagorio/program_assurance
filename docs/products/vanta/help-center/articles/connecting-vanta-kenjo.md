# Connecting Vanta & Kenjo

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/12418929-connecting-vanta-kenjo

Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Vanta integrates with Kenjo to sync employee data and support automated onboarding and offboarding workflows. By pulling real-time employment information, Vanta helps ensure that only current employees retain access to company systems. This simplifies access reviews and supports compliance requirements.
 
Estimated setup time: Less than 10 minutes
 

## How It Works
Vanta integrates with Kenjo to sync employee and group data. This allows you to:
- 
Monitor and manage personnel access in real time
- 
Ensure only active employees retain access to company systems
- 
Simplify access reviews and support compliance requirements
Please note: Vanta connects to Kenjo through Merge.dev <https://merge.dev/>, a secure third-party integration platform.
 

## Use Cases
Connecting Kenjo to Vanta will enable you to:
- 
Sync employees and groups from Kenjo into Vanta
- 
Monitor and manage personnel access in real time
- 
Ensure only active employees retain access to company systems
- 
Simplify access reviews and support compliance requirements
 

## Requirements
- 
Vanta administrator account
- 
Kenjo administrator account
- 
Kenjo API Key
- 
Kenjo subdomain
 

## Connect the Integration
- 
Log in to the Kenjo app
- 
In the bottom left corner, select Settings
- 
Scroll to the Integrations section
- 
Next to API, toggle the switch to enable it
- 
Select the Settings button

## Permissions
Vanta accesses the following data from Kenjo:
 
Vanta can read:
- 
Data about your users
- 
Data about your employees
Vanta can write:
- 
Nothing (Vanta does not have write permissions)
