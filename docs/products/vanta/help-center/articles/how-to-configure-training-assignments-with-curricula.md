# How to configure training assignments with Curricula

Collection: Team Compliance ; Team Compliance › Vanta Security & Privacy Training
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

## Prerequisites
- 
Training assignment must already be configured in Curricula
 

## Procedure
To configure a second Curricula campaign,
- 
go to Integrations
- 
Find Curricula
- 
Click Manage
- 
Click Edit SAT 
​
