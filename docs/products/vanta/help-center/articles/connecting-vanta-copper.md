## Article text

Vanta integrates with Copper to sync account data and support automated onboarding and offboarding workflows. By pulling real-time account information, Vanta helps ensure that only current employees retain access to company systems. This simplifies access reviews and supports compliance requirements.
 
Estimated setup time: Less than 10 minutes
 

## How It Works
Vanta integrates with Copper to sync employee and group data. This allows you to:
- 
Monitor access in real time
- 
Ensure only active employees retain access to company systems
- 
Simplify access reviews and support compliance requirements
Please note: Vanta connects to Copper through Merge.dev <https://merge.dev/>, a secure third-party integration platform.

## Use Cases
Connecting Copper to Vanta will enable you to:
- 
Sync employees and groups from Copper into Vanta
- 
Monitor and manage personnel access in real time
- 
Ensure only active employees retain access to company systems
- 
Simplify access reviews and support compliance requirements
 

## Requirements
- 
Vanta administrator account
- 
Copper API Key
- 
Copper user email

## Permissions
Vanta accesses the following data from Copper:
 
Vanta can read:
- 
Data about your users
- 
Data about your employees
Vanta can write:
- 
Nothing (Vanta does not have write permissions)
