## Integrating Vanta and Ramp
- 
In Vanta, go to the Integrations page, click Add integration, and search for Ramp. For help, see our guide to the Integrations Page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345761-integrations-page>.
- 
Select Connect.
- 
Ramp uses OAuth to authorize Vanta to monitor your account. Select Connect Ramp to continue.
- 
Sign in to Ramp

 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1554398672/65e20a71b139f3173670d497a9bf/Screenshot+2025-06-03+at+4_14_06%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1794967200&signature=f2ff6790d9adaf5e6dabaddc66419ddcbebbfaa5a06bcb73e0262c9b1a738c31&req=dSUiEsp3lYdYW%2FMW1HO4zaUY02Di0ZkUTuR9iffWhO5DecV%2BbLLje4E8NUJQ%0AN88vZ2%2BOGp4CRCK0cVc%3D%0A>
- 
Select Allow
- 
To complete the integration, you will need Admin or Owner permissions within Ramp.
