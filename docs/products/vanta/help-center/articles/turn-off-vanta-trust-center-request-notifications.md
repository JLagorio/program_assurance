# Turn off Vanta Trust Center Request Notifications

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11346015-turn-off-vanta-trust-center-request-notifications
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

## Prerequisites
- 
Administrator access on Vanta
 

## Procedure
- 
Sign into Vanta
- 
Click the gear icon in the upper right to go to Account settings
- 
Select Notifications under MY ACCOUNT
- 
Under the My Alerts section, toggle off "When a Trust Center access request is received (Sent immediately)"
Please note you can only disable notifications for your own user account. Any administrator who wishes to stop receiving Trust Center notifications must follow the same steps for themselves.
