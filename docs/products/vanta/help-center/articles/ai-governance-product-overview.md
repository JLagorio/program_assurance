✅ Feature availability: You need to be on a current Vanta plan <https://www.vanta.com/pricing?utm_source=help_center&utm_campaign=hc_pricing_cta> and have an AI governance framework <https://www.vanta.com/products/additional-frameworks> enabled to use AI Governance features.
 
AI Governance in Vanta helps you centralize and operationalize your AI governance program. Instead of maintaining spreadsheets and disconnected documents, you can document your AI use cases and conduct AI Impact Assessments or Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments directly in the platform.
 
You’ll find these features in the AI governance section of your Vanta account.
 
 

## AI use cases
 
The AI use cases page is where you document and maintain your organization’s AI use cases in Vanta. An AI use case describes the different ways your organization creates or uses AI tools—the systems that you’re using, why you use them, who manages them, and its oversight model.
 
📖 Learn more: Managing AI Use Cases <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/18419820-managing-ai-use-cases>
 
 

## Assessments
 
The Assessments page is where you create and manage assessments like AI Impact Assessments (AIIAs) and Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments (FRIAs) in Vanta. These assessments help you evaluate higher-risk AI use cases and document the safeguards and risk decisions associated with them.
- 
Author, manage, review, and approve AI impact assessments within the Vanta platform.
- 
Bring AI governance into Vanta by connecting AI use cases to AI Impact Assessments.
- 
Identify and track AI use risk in-platform using Vanta’s risk management capabilities.
​
📖 Learn more: Conducting AI Assessments <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/18420327-conducting-ai-assessments>
