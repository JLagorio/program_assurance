Vendor security reviews are a part of Third Party Risk Management <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345557> in Vanta, helping your organization evaluate vendors. In Vanta, you create questionnaires and control when and how assessments happen based on vendor risk. These settings allow you to apply consistent standards and keep a clear, audit-ready record of decisions for vendors that handle sensitive data or support critical business processes.
 
⚙️ User permissions: Editors and Admins can view and manage all Vendors settings. Collaborators assigned as a Business Owner or Assessment Owner on a vendor can manage assessment settings and make vendor decisions for that vendor. Learn more <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345590-understanding-user-roles-and-permissions-in-vanta>

## Assessment rules
Assessment rules control how vendor assessments are structured, scheduled, and automated across your organization. They use a vendor’s inherent risk score to determine assessment frequency, questionnaires, evidence requirements, and apply across vendors to keep assessments consistent while still allowing individual assessments to be adjusted when needed.
 
Vanta can automatically schedule recurring assessments based on your assessment  rules. About 30 days before an assessment’s due date, the next assessment is created and moved to in progress. At this point, Vanta can begin requesting evidence and sending reminders, depending on your automation settings.

## Creating in-app questionnaires
Questionnaires help you collect information about vendors during assessments. You create and manage questionnaires separately from questionnaires sent during individual assessments so you can reuse them consistently across vendors. 
 
From the Vendors settings, you can build a questionnaire from scratch, start from a template created by Vanta's Security team, or import a questionnaire from a spreadsheet. 
 
To access your questionnaires:
- 
Go to Settings.
- 
In the page menu, scroll to the Features section and select Vendors.
- 
From the Questionnaires tab:
- 
Select an existing questionnaire to edit.
- 
Click Add new questionnaire to create a new one.
​
ℹ️ Note: Changes are saved automatically. When you’re done editing, click Back to settings and you’ll see the questionnaire at the top of your list (you may need to refresh the page). If you edit a questionnaire, the changes do not apply to any assessment it's already been added to.

### Conditional questions
 
Conditional questions let you ask follow-up questions when a vendor selects a specific answer, so your questionnaire adapts based on how it's answered. Supported question types: Yes/No/NA and Single-select.
 
[image 15]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2524505236/5a211f8e04c769479398c7877b7a/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=2c97e7ee2c91c502153d9b83355b77cb0649aad57ac8dbaba646d6e405e3c595&req=diUlEsx%2BmINcX%2FMW1HO4zcSXc2CjLVj3%2FBoxXHUNBNL9y6ngrv%2Bv9RygL5IT%0ATw2RVFaxjpXItOxlBYM%3D%0A>
 
To add a conditional question:
- 
Open the question you want to use as the parent. 
- 
Next to the answer option that should trigger the conditional question, click the branching icon.
- 
Edit the question text and answer options as needed.
To convert a question to a conditional question:
- 
Open the question you want to use as the parent.
- 
Next to the answer option that should trigger the conditional question, click the branching icon.
- 
Click Move existing question.
- 
Select the existing question you want to move—it will become a conditional question under the parent.
To reorder conditional questions:
- 
You can reorder conditional questions underneath the parent question. From the form editor, use the up ↑ and down ↓ arrows.
- 
If you move a parent question in the form outline, its conditional questions move with it. However, you can’t move a conditional question to a different parent.
A conditional question can belong to one parent question:
- 
In the questionnaire builder, conditional questions appear under their parent question. 
- 
In Vanta Exchange, a conditional question appears under its parent question when the vendor selects an answer that triggers it.
- 
If multiple conditional questions are attached to the same answer option, they appear in the order shown in the builder.
- 
Conditional questions are one level deep—a conditional question can’t have its own conditional questions.
​
ℹ️ Note: When vendors complete the questionnaire in Vanta Exchange, the questionnaire progress total only includes questions that are currently applicable. If a vendor’s answer triggers a conditional question, that question is added to the total and the count updates.
 

### Deleting questions
- 
If you delete a question, the action is permanent—you can’t restore deleted questions.
- 
If you delete a section, the questions in the section get deleted too. You can use the form outline to drag and drop questions you want to keep to another section.
- 
If you delete a section or a question that has conditional questions attached, those conditional questions get deleted too. It’s not possible to move conditional questions from underneath their parent.

Spreadsheet questionnaires are not part of the in-app questionnaire system. They are files used in assessments as part of an evidence request. Like any uploaded document, spreadsheet questionnaires can be used by Vanta AI to help answer an in-app questionnaire, if one is included in the assessment.
 
You can upload spreadsheet questionnaire templates on this page. These files can then be added to an evidence request like any other uploaded document.

- 
Vendor responses are stored separately in the assessment and must be downloaded from there and reviewed offline.
 
 

## Conducting assessments
Conduct assessments (such as security assessments) by requesting evidence from vendors, reviewing answers, and making a recommendation to finalize the assessment.
- 
Requesting evidence: As part of conducting an assessment, you’re going to request evidence for the vendor to provide. For example, for a security assessment, this includes sending the vendor a security questionnaire, as well as resources related to their security posture.
- 
Commenting on questions: Add vendor comments to collaborate with the vendor directly on specific questions, or internal comments to discuss findings privately with your team.
- 
Reviewing answers: Once your vendor provides the evidence you requested, you’ll need to review the answers provided to the questionnaire, as well as flag any findings that might need remediation.
- 
Making a recommendation: When you’re ready to finalize your assessment, you’ll record a recommendation and residual risk to capture the outcome of your assessment and document the decision.
Learn more: Conducting Vendor Security Assessments <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345550-conducting-vendor-security-reviews>
 
 

## Making a vendor decision
After completing one or more assessments for a vendor, you can make a final decision at the vendor level. The vendor decision brings together recommendations from each assessment, along with any additional business or procurement context, to determine whether the vendor should be approved for use.
 
To make a vendor decision, open the vendor profile and click the Make decision button. You’ll choose from one of the following recommendation options, as well as provide the residual risk score <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345560>:
 | 
Decision  | 
Description
 | 
Approved | 
The vendor is approved for use. Any identified risks are acceptable based on the overall evaluation. 
 | 
Conditionally approved | 
The vendor is approved with conditions. Follow-up actions, mitigations, or monitoring may be required before or during use.
 | 
Not approved | 
The vendor is not approved due to unacceptable risk or unmet requirements.
The vendor decision represents the final outcome for that vendor, informed by all assessments. You can also include a decision summary to document context, conditions, or rationale for audit and future reference.
