# Bulk Assign Ownership to Tests, Controls, or Documents

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345889-bulk-assign-ownership-to-tests-controls-or-documents
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

- 
Login to Vanta.
- 
Open the Test, Controls, or Documents tab.
- 
You will want to tick the boxes next to the Tests, Controls, or Documents you need to take over.
- 
Click on the Assign Owner button, and select the relevant new owner.
