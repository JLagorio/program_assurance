## Article text

With Vanta Trust Center <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345469>, admins can gate confidential documentation, such as a SOC 2 report, security report, or a previously filled-out questionnaire. This will allow individuals, potential customers, and clients to request access to your Trust Center, but it requires their signature on an NDA first- keeping your information private and secure. As a Vanta admin, you can decide how to send or not send NDAs.
 

## What is a Clickwrap agreement?
A click-wrap agreement is a digital contract where you agree to terms and conditions by clicking a button, usually labeled "I agree" or "Accept," before you can use a website, service, or software. It's like signing a contract online with just a click. These agreements are standard for things like app downloads or creating accounts on websites. As long as the terms are clearly presented and you have a chance to read them before clicking, they are usually legally binding.

## Adding your Clickwrap NDA to Vanta
- 
In your account header, click the Settings icon
- 
In the Settings page menu, scroll to the Features section, select Customer trust, and go to the Trust Center tab
- 
Scroll to the Require non-disclosure agreement (NDA) section

Click on the dropdown under NDA Provider and select Clickwrap or another option if available via your Integrations (such as Docusign)

Click Browse to select your NDA file to be uploaded. DOCX and PDF files are currently supported.

Select Save

## Accessing and Downloading a Signed NDA
You can review a signed NDA directly from the Access page. This is helpful when you need a copy of the agreement someone signed before viewing your Trust Center documents.
 
Follow these steps to locate and download the signed NDA:
- 
Go to the Access page by selecting the Trust Center under the Customer Trust tab
- 
Select the Viewers granted access tab
- 
Find the Viewer Name of the person who signed the NDA
- 
Select the Clickwrap icon in the NDA column to open a copy of the signed NDA
- 
Select the Download icon in the top right corner to download the file
Please note: Downloading signed NDAs with a watermark is only supported for unencrypted PDFs.
