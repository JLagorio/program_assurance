# Encryption on Windows Home Edition

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345920-encryption-on-windows-home-edition
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

When installed on a Windows OS device, the Vanta Device Monitor specifically checks for BitLocker enablement to confirm encryption monitors; however, Windows 10 and 11 Home Edition do not support BitLocker hard drive encryption.
 
For devices using Windows 10/11 Home, we recommend using a third-party encryption solution and adding manual evidence to the related tests in Vanta. For more details, please refer to our Document Third-Party Encryption <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345994> guide.
