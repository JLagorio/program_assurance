## Article text

AI Change Summaries are here to make your annual policy review process in Vanta faster and easier. This feature is for anyone who manages or approves policies, whether you’re an admin, manager, or reviewer. With AI-powered summaries, you’ll see exactly what changed in your policy drafts so you can review and approve with confidence.
 
When you edit a policy in Vanta and submit it for approval, Vanta’s AI automatically highlights the key changes between the previous and new versions. This summary helps both editors and approvers quickly spot what’s new, saving everyone time.

## Benefits of AI Change Summaries
- 
Saves time for both editors and reviewers.
- 
Highlights essential changes clearly and quickly.
- 
Reduces back-and-forth by clarifying edits up front.
- 
Lets you personalize the summary for your organization.
 

## Tips
- 
You can always add your own details to the AI-generated summary before sending it for approval.
- 
Encourage approvers to check the summary first for a quick overview.
