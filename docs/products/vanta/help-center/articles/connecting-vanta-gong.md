## Connecting Vanta & Gong
Gong allows customers to connect tools like Vanta using OAuth. By connecting your Gong account with Vanta, Vanta can make API calls as the connecting user, allowing you to display users in Gong within Vanta under the 'Access' area.
- 
In Vanta, go to the Integrations page, click Add integration, and search for Gong. For help, see our guide to the Integrations Page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345761-integrations-page>.
- 
Click on Connect.
- 
A pop-up will inform you about using OAuth with Gong for connection and monitoring.
- 
Click Connect Gong.
