For questions requiring direct assistance, contact the appropriate team below based on the topic:
 | 
Who to contact | 
Topics
 | 
Vanta Support |

 | 
Vanta Account Manager (AM)* |

 | 
Vanta Customer Success Manager (CSM)* | 
Product adoption guidance, strategic planning, and roadmap discussions.
 | 
Vanta product feedback <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345412-sharing-product-feedback> |

 | 
Your auditor |

Your security team |

* Dedicated AMs and CSMs depend on your Vanta plan. If you’re unsure whether you have a designated contact, reach out to Vanta Support.
 
 

## Contacting Vanta Support
Contact Vanta Support through the in-product help menu—requests begin in a chat with Ask Ilma and are escalated to a support specialist as needed.

To contact Vanta Support:
- 
From within your Vanta account, click the (?) help icon in the header.
- 
From the help menu, select Chat with us.
- 
Describe your question or issue.
Ask Ilma provides initial guidance and connects you to a support specialist if needed. If live chat isn’t available, follow-up will be provided via email. If you’re unable to access chat, contact [email protected].
