## Article text

Vanta AI features <https://www.vanta.com/pricing?utm_source=help_center&utm_campaign=hc_pricing_inline>, powered by third-parties, help you work faster and smarter. With Vanta AI enabled, you unlock the ability to accelerate security reviews, streamline compliance processes, and complete questionnaires in minutes with auto-generated answers. 
 
Using Vanta AI features is voluntary. Admins or Editors <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345385-assigning-user-roles-and-permissions> can enable or disable the usage of Vanta AI features for all users within an account. For accounts with the Workspaces <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345386-vanta-workspaces> feature, this setting is managed for each workspace separately.

To enable or disable Vanta AI:
- 
In the upper-right corner of your account, click the gear icon to go to your account settings.
- 
In the AI tab, use the toggle to enable or disable Vanta AI.
- 
Enable Vanta AI: With Vanta AI enabled, you have access to the Vanta AI features available on your plan, and the third-party services that power those features can process your data.
- 
Disable Vanta AI: When you disable Vanta AI, you will no longer have access to Vanta AI-powered features, and we immediately stop processing your data through those third-party services.
You can refer to our Trust Center <https://trust.vanta.com/> for information on subprocessors and our Terms <https://www.vanta.com/legal/terms> for information on AI features.
 
Please note: Vanta AI can make mistakes—consider verifying information.
