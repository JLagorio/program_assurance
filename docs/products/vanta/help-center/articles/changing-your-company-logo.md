# Changing your Company Logo

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345393-changing-your-company-logo
Collection: Getting Started Hub ; Getting Started Hub › Account Settings
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Keeping your company logo up-to-date is essential for maintaining brand cohesion. Your logo will be visible on Policies and in your Trust Centre.
Please Note: The company logo you upload here will appear on your Policies and Trust Center pages. At this time, it does not update the logo on the employee onboarding page. Customization of the onboarding page is not currently supported, but this has been submitted as a feature request.
 

## Update your Company Logo
- 
Select the gear icon in the top right corner, you will be taken to the Company Settings page.
