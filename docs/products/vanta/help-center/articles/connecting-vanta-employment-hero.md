# Connecting Vanta & Employment Hero

## Article text

Automatically provision and de-provision users in Vanta using Employment Hero. Connecting this integration helps keep employee data current, ensures offboarding is handled promptly, and supports compliance tracking with minimal manual work.
 
Estimated setup time: Less than 8 minutes
 

## How It Works
The Employment Hero integration syncs your employee data into Vanta in just a few clicks. Connecting your Employment Hero instance allows Vanta to automatically add new employees and remove departing ones—keeping your organization’s access and records up to date.
 

## Use Cases
- 
Employee data synchronization – Keep employee information up to date across both systems
- 
Leave management integration – Sync approved leave requests for tracking availability
- 
Organizational structure mapping – Sync department and cost center information
- 
Data enrichment – Ensure Vanta has the latest employee details for compliance reporting
 

## Requirements
- 
Role and permissions
- 
Must be a Vanta admin
- 
Must be an Employment Hero admin (OAuth requires admin access)
- 
Feature plan eligibility
- 
Available on Growth and Scale plans and for GRC customers
- 
External dependencies
- 
Employment Hero OAuth integration must be enabled for your organization
- 
Audit considerations
- 
During an audit, consider setting a cutoff date to ensure employees offboarded in the audit period are tracked in Vanta
 

## Connect the Integration
- 
In Vanta, go to the Integrations page, click Add integration, and search for Employment Hero. For help, see our guide to the Integrations Page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345761-integrations-page>. 
- 
Select Connect

## Permissions
Vanta only accesses the following data from your Employment Hero organization:
 
Vanta can view:
- 
Employees (read only)
- 
Teams (read only)
- 
Organization (read only)
