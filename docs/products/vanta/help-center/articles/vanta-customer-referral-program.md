## How It Works
Have a friend or colleague who could benefit from automated security and compliance? Here’s how you can both earn rewards:
- 
Share the Referral Link
​​Spread the word by sharing our referral page via email or social media: https://www.vanta.com/referral <https://www.vanta.com/referral>
- 
Earn Your Rewards
​​When your referral becomes a Vanta customer, you’ll get a $500 Visa Gift Card, and they’ll receive $1,000 off their first year with Vanta.
- 
Submit Your Details
​​Make sure your referral includes your name, email, and company when they fill out the referral form.
