## Enable Teams notifications in Vanta
Once the bot is correctly added to your team, go to the notification settings <https://app.vanta.com/settings/company-notifications> page in Vanta to enable Teams notifications (turned off by default)

## Configuring Teams Channels
You can customize the channel you want to receive notifications in for specific categories on the Teams notification configuration page.
[image 05]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516762051/cee90d21b627eff05613c130e5a6/15730023688084?expires=1794967200&signature=b65284cbced18066e3c0efd741fc14fec91cf0987b5626588547db11bb8822a8&req=dSUmEM54n4FaWPMW1HO4zUd2ksGakneXlFloT0zDqqo8u5AF3ylWBGnWKWKQ%0AjAkCB9OyvtKp12s96tw%3D%0A>
- 
You can select a channel you’ve already configured on another category tab by selecting one of the dropdown options, or you can enter a new channel.
- 
To enter a new channel, navigate to the Teams app and the channel you’d like to use. Click the menu icon and select Get a link to the channel
[image 06]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516762049/217298944983d4be6a7082292a17/15730043849236?expires=1794967200&signature=adc6d7510836c43f57ba3fae65a787980c909d00b2001482e52bdc14ed0f6ac9&req=dSUmEM54n4FbUPMW1HO4zWAVfoFOV2M%2Be0xyNaVZF8QDMB5hJAF3K12iRIRV%0A10Jv%2F0i7NYPLv3Zskxs%3D%0A>

## FAQs
Can I integrate Vanta with a private Teams Channel?
- 
Currently, this is not supported, but you can utilize a public channel that exists within a private team.
Can I utilize Teams to send direct messages for my account notifications <https://app.vanta.com/settings/account-notifications>?
- 
This is currently not supported
