# Change the Onboarding Contact Person

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345977-change-the-onboarding-contact-person
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

The Contact Person is at the bottom of each User's onboarding page. This person can help users with issues or questions about their assigned onboarding tasks.
 

## Prerequisites
- 
The new Contact Person will need to be a Vanta Admin <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345590>
 

## Procedure
- 
Select the Group you would like to change the contact person of
- 
Select Edit group at the top right-hand corner
- 
Click on the point of contact and change it to the new assignee
- 
Select Save
