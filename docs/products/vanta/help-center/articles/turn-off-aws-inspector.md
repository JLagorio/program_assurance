# Turn off AWS Inspector

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345925-turn-off-aws-inspector
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

## Prerequisites
- 
Administrator Access to AWS and Vanta
 

## Procedure for Turning off Inspector in Vanta
Perform the steps in this section if you want to turn off Inspector solely in Vanta, Keep in mind this will still keep Inspector running on the AWS side. If you want to deactivate in both AWS and Vanta, follow the instructions for 'Procedure for Turning off Inspector in AWS' immediately after this section.
- 
Sign in to Vanta and head to the Integrations Page

## Procedure for Turning off Inspector in AWS
- 
Sign into AWS

 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516760966/db1a77794386c4b65f14ce736909/16942777597332?expires=1794967200&signature=d14d284d9395d076a7dead80d8192db46d1100169bf319cc5c0735f96bf91f96&req=dSUmEM54nYhZX%2FMW1HO4zbo%2F4HASAZjfUVdDAhy2hqNSKpiZbXStn1y5LCi4%0A8YCVuEH8LZ5KFvMFbsI%3D%0A>
 
- 
Search for Inspector and click on it from the drop-down to go to the Inspector dashboard:
 
[image 07]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516760965/9a8de17e9bf562eb9a664071a7ba/16942755056148?expires=1794967200&signature=a25b839d231a2ea423ef3d5eb5bc3253431f0a70586e5372e5d3f5b3be110303&req=dSUmEM54nYhZXPMW1HO4zbGItd10mRKhlc98AekAvq7gYjPUhtkZu7VMvuuR%0AxAjYLjXBb%2BXRkDmCaqU%3D%0A>
 
- 
On the dashboard, click General settings under account management:
 
[image 08]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516760963/46df23c2e7660e36e7f42b64ebf9/16942789504788?expires=1794967200&signature=67eb621e399b59a1cc82b4ce9925511e2924feb4fadb4f8216c0a7cd49b2ce2c&req=dSUmEM54nYhZWvMW1HO4zbvw09YCNJjNjkb4VZhcBsGQpiX0S4uaJfybt64b%0A0B%2B6MTbxjsSbYd8B7EI%3D%0A>

Then click Deactivate Inspector
