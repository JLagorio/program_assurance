## Article text

Companies need to implement technical vulnerability management measures to achieve compliance with SOC 2 and ISO 27001. This involves identifying, prioritizing, and addressing vulnerabilities in their codebase and infrastructure. Tools like Aikido can help make the vulnerability management process more effective.

## Connecting Vanta & Aikido
- 
In Vanta, go to the Integrations page, click Add integration, and search for Aikido. For help, see our guide to the Integrations Page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345761-integrations-page>.
- 
Select Connect, and follow the prompts for connecting the two platforms

Aikido continuously monitors your security posture, automates 16 Vanta tests & helps pass 5 Vanta controls.
- 
Once the integration has been connected in Vanta, you can manage the scope <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11346106> by selecting Configure Scope
