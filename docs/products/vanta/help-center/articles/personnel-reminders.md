# Personnel Reminders

Collection: Team Compliance ; Team Compliance › Managing Users
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Personnel reminders notify your employees about incomplete tasks via email or Slack. You can set up recurring reminders, send one-off reminders to individuals or groups, and customize the content of reminder emails and Slack messages.
 
⚙️ User permissions: Admins and Editors can configure personnel reminder settings. Admins, Editors, and custom roles with People Management editing permissions can send one-off reminders from the People page. Refer to Understanding User Roles and Permissions <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345590-understanding-user-roles-and-permissions-in-vanta> for more details.
 
 

## Who receives reminders
When reminders are enabled, they're sent to current personnel <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345379-adding-and-managing-personnel> who have at least one incomplete task to act on. These can include:
- 
Policy acceptance
- 
Security training
- 
Custom personnel tasks
- 
In-progress background checks
- 
Vanta Device Monitor installation
- 
Vanta Device Monitor update (when the installed version is outdated or deprecating)
- 
Hard drive encryption
- 
Password manager installation or remediation
Reminders aren't sent to personnel who are marked as a service account or who don't have any incomplete tasks to act on.
 
ℹ️ Note: Add [email protected] and [email protected] to your organization's allow list to ensure personnel receive reminder emails.
 
 

## Enabling recurring reminders
Recurring reminders notify personnel on a set schedule when they have incomplete tasks to act on.
 
[image 01]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2448380130/d5ad6db231c8f99e10d0f90870fb/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=c6e924f1e51a953d8f75df40f73b8f7dfac1ee1658a49f10743036d6a88d7908&req=diQjHsp2nYBcWfMW1HO4zcPeBlLD44iik7fnWxw2Q%2FNd6QqAkM23hu5BqH3e%0AgTZMJNtuA1Zl7SkTdFY%3D%0A>
 
To enable recurring reminders:
- 
Go to Settings, then select Notifications.
- 
Scroll to the Personnel reminders section.
- 
Select a frequency: Weekly (every Monday) or Every weekday. Reminders are sent at 11am UTC.
- 
Enable Email reminders, Slack reminders, or both.
​
📖  Learn more: To set up your Slack connection, see Setting Up Slack Notifications <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345744-setting-up-slack-notifications>.
 
 

## Sending one-off reminders
You can send a one-off reminder at any time to personnel with incomplete tasks. This doesn't affect your recurring reminders setting.
 

### Sending to all eligible personnel
 
This sends an email reminder only to eligible personnel matching your current filters.
 
[image 02]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2453553333/141c8d19c7b4f2fe2f9968d72227/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=61231ecaef2ba41ff4dc76f3dfcb2db75d19066510ffc084ed00a9c858477986&req=diQiFcx7noJcWvMW1HO4zbV%2BQB0C48SitXhg5Af4DX8qaaXw3hdAR%2BwUNBpD%0A%2F5pIBmkZ0tHkjUos3g4%3D%0A>
 
To send a reminder to all eligible personnel in your current view:
- 
Go to Personnel, then select People.
- 
Use filters to narrow the list as needed.
- 
Click the ••• menu above the table and select Send one-time reminder.
 

### Sending to selected personnel
 
This sends an email or Slack reminder only to eligible personnel you selected on the People page. Slack is only available as a method if Slack is already connected in your notification settings.
 
[image 03]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2453553778/8825ce587fcfea9a6214b370778a/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=b9ceb5b6937b09694c7769dbc0d29cc1ba4b03882d56d57a2245d176d3e2a8aa&req=diQiFcx7noZYUfMW1HO4zVub%2BPa9RYEv%2Fa6eFWGMJ1my5YjLWSih5bhqevFK%0AaD9b4NN19jy8%2FlFlM4E%3D%0A>
 
To send a reminder to multiple personnel at once:
- 
Go to Personnel, then select People.
- 
Use the checkboxes to select the personnel you want to remind.
- 
Click Send reminder in the toolbar above the table.
- 
Select a method: Email, Slack, or both.
- 
Click Send reminders.
 

### Sending to an individual
 
Individual reminders sent this way trigger an email notification only. To send via Slack, use the selected personnel flow above and select just that person.
 
[image 04]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2453563444/66209e78a3d2c6797a3dd0c3586a/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=e5c9971cc0ca2ea378294a016eaa73e3774a178ef4403a80b0647ef4659d0773&req=diQiFcx4noVbXfMW1HO4zfWWWThIr39loB%2FuGqcSzxxwM2u%2FIq%2Fv%2Bp0oLYP1%0AmZWHYMlecItSdrLXTnw%3D%0A>
 
To send a reminder to a single person:
- 
Go to Personnel, then select People.
- 
Send an email reminder in one of two ways:
- 
Click the ••• menu in the table row and select Send reminder.
- 
Open the personnel record, scroll to the Incomplete security tasks section, and click Remind.
 
 

## Customizing reminders
You can customize the content of personnel reminder email and Slack messages from the Personnel reminders settings. Changes apply to all personnel reminders—there's no per-person or per-group customization.

### Email notifications
 
To customize the email reminder:
- 
Go to Settings, then select Notifications.
- 
Scroll to the Personnel reminders section.
- 
Next to Email reminders, click Customize.
- 
On the Edit tab, update the Subject and Message fields as needed.
- 
Click the Preview tab to see how the email will appear to recipients.
- 
Click Save.
​
💡 Tip: To send reminder emails from your own domain instead of Vanta's, see Sending Emails from a Custom Domain <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/12986178-sending-emails-from-a-custom-domain>.
 

### Slack notifications
 
To customize the Slack reminder:
- 
Go to Settings, then select Notifications.
- 
Scroll to the Personnel reminders section.
- 
Next to Slack reminders, click Customize.
- 
On the Edit tab, update the Title and Message fields as needed.
- 
Click the Preview tab to see how the message will appear to recipients.
- 
Click Save.
 

### Supported variables
 
Use variables to personalize reminder content. The same variables are available in both the email and Slack editors:
 | 
Variable | 
Description
 | 
{{firstName}} | 
Recipient's first name from their personnel record. Renders as blank if not set.
 | 
{{lastName}} | 
Recipient's last name from their personnel record. Renders as blank if not set.
 | 
{{taskDueDate}} | 
The earliest due date among the recipient's highest-priority incomplete tasks. Renders as "as soon as possible" if no due date is set.
 | 
{{taskStatus}} | 
Renders as "Overdue" or "Due soon." Renders as "Due soon" if no due date is set.
 | 
{{companyName}} | 
Your organization's display name in Vanta.
 
ℹ️ Note: The preview uses sample data and isn't a personalized representation of what each employee will receive.
