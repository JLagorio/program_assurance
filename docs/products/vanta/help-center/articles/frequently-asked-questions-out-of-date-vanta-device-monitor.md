# Frequently Asked Questions: Out-of-Date Vanta Device Monitor

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345398-frequently-asked-questions-out-of-date-vanta-device-monitor
Collection: Continuous Monitoring ; Continuous Monitoring › Vanta Device Monitor
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Vanta enforces that any Vanta Device Monitor installed on employee computers must be up to date. We do this as a security best practice to ensure that Vanta Device Monitor receives quality updates. Vanta will auto-update the Vanta Device Monitor whenever possible. However, if a Vanta Device Monitor cannot be auto-updated, Vanta will assign its owner a task to update it manually <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345399>.
 
What Vanta Device Monitor versions are out of date?
- 
Vanta Device Monitor with a version below 2.3.4 is considered outdated. Vanta will continue to drop support for legacy Vanta Device Monitor versions in the future.
What happens when a Vanta Device Monitor becomes out of date?
- 
When a Vanta Device Monitor becomes outdated and cannot be auto-updated, its owner will have 30 days to update it. Vanta will notify admins and employees when a Vanta Device Monitor becomes outdated.
How will admins receive notifications? 
- 
When employees have an out-of-date Vanta Device Monitor, the admin of their Vanta account will be notified through email and/or Slack. All out-of-date Vanta Device Monitors and their owners are highlighted on the Computers Page <https://app.vanta.com/computers>.
- 
Admins can filter for Vanta Device Monitor by selecting Show me from the pop-up modal at the top of the screen.

How will employees receive notifications? 
- 
When an employee has an out-of-date Vanta Device Monitor that can’t be auto-updated, Vanta will automatically assign them a task to update it. Instructions will be provided detailing the update process.
- 
Vanta will then continue to send them reminders to update if Employee Reminders <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345593> are enabled.
What happens if an employee doesn’t update their Vanta Device Monitor?
- 
If an employee doesn’t update their Vanta Device Monitor within the 30-day grace period, Vanta will drop support for the Vanta Device Monitor. The Vanta Device Monitor will still be viewable in the Unmonitored Tab <https://app.vanta.com/computers?tab=unmonitored> on the Computers Page. However, it will not be used across tests. Its owner will be considered unmonitored and will fail the Employee computers monitored <https://app.vanta.com/tests#employees-without-laptops> with the Vanta Device Monitor test.
