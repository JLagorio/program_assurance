The integrations used by your organization power Vanta. The more you integrate, the more Vanta can monitor and help your company maintain security and compliance. Vanta supports hundreds of integrations across a wide range of tools and workflows. For more information on Integrations, check out the Integrations <https://help.vanta.com/en/collections/12575285-integrations> section in our help center.
  

## Accessing the integrations page
- 
From the left-hand panel, select Integrations. 
- 
The integrations page gives you a centralized view for your organization. The two tabs will show you: 
- 
Connected: A list of integrations that have been established with Vanta.
- 
Errors: A list of integrations that are not properly fetching data and need immediate attention. 
- 
To add a new integration, select the Add Integration button. This opens the Integration Directory.

### Curated collections
When you open the directory, you'll see curated collections to help you quickly find relevant integrations:
- 
Most popular: the integrations most commonly used across Vanta customers. 
- 
Startup Essentials: integrations commonly used by early-stage and growing companies. 
- 
Enterprise ready: integrations built for larger, more complex organizations. 
- 
Just added: the latest integrations added to Vanta.

### Integration cards
Each integration card in the directory displays:
- 
A description of what the integration does
- 
The capabilities it supports within Vanta
- 
Whether it's already connected to your account
- 
A FedRAMP badge, where applicable
 
 

## Connecting an integration
- 
From the Integration Directory, search or browse to find the integration you want to connect.
- 
Select the integration card to open its detail page.
- 
Select Connect and follow the prompts to complete setup.
- 
For integration-specific setup instructions, refer to the individual integration article in the Integrations <https://help.vanta.com/en/collections/12575285-integrations> section of our help center.

## Managing connected integrations 
- 
Your connected integrations are listed on the main Integrations page.
- 
Select Manage to edit or adjust an integration.
- 
Select Configure scope to mark resources in or out of scope.
