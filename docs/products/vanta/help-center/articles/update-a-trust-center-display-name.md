# Update a Trust Center Display Name

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345895-update-a-trust-center-display-name
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

To update the display name on Vanta's Trust Center, you need to update the Display name in your company settings.
- 
In your account header, click the Settings icon
- 
Select Information 
- 
Update the Display name field
- 
Click Update Information
