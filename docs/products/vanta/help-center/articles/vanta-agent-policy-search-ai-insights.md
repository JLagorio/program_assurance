# Vanta Agent: Policy Search & AI Insights

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/12046108-vanta-agent-policy-search-ai-insights
Collection: Policies
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

The Vanta Agent for policies allows users to search across their policy documents directly in Vanta. Instead of digging through dense documentation, users can ask natural-language questions and receive responses in seconds.
Please note: Vanta AI can make mistakes. Please verify responses.
The Vanta Agent can help you understand:
- 
Is my Risk Management Policy audit ready?
- 
Who is responsible for filing HR exceptions?
- 
What is the scope of my Physical Security Policy?
- 
My CISO just left. Which policies do I need to update?

From the Policies page, select Vanta Agent.

## AI Insights
Take advantage of AI-driven insights by having the Vanta agent automatically resolve SLA inconsistencies, address policy contradictions, and remove off-boarded employees from policies.
- 
From the policies page, select any of the pre-provided AI Insights options.
