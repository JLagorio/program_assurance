# Vanta's IP Address

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11346004-vanta-s-ip-address
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Some integrations or services require Vanta's outbound IP address to be allowlisted. You can find and copy the IP address for your instance on the Integrations page <https://app.vanta.com/integrations>.

ℹ️ Note: We recommend adding Vanta's IP to your organization's allowlists to avoid any blockers when connecting integrations.
