## Article text

Access integrations sync user access and limited account data, providing a simple way to demonstrate to auditors that access to services is only granted to known entities and promptly removes employee access upon termination. This information can be used to conduct access reviews, ensuring that employees have only the necessary level of access to applications required for their roles.
 

## Access Capabilities 
Integrations that allow Vanta to fetch account details, such as individual users or service accounts with some level of access to the end service, can be identified by the Access tag on the integrations page.
