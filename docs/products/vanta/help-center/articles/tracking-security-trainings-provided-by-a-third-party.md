# Tracking Security Trainings Provided by a Third-party

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11346094-tracking-security-trainings-provided-by-a-third-party
Collection: Team Compliance ; Team Compliance › Vanta Security & Privacy Training
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Vanta supports monitoring the completion status of various training courses for multiple standards. These include General Security Training, CCPA, GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS Security Training.
 
If your Third-party training provider does not have a supported integration with Vanta, Custom onboarding tasks can be created to help you direct your employees to your providers training, and require employees to upload a screenshot of the completed training as confirmation.
 

## Tracking Security Trainings 
- 
Navigate to the Task Sets <https://app.vanta.com/people/groups/task-sets> and select the Task Sets you would like to include third-party training in

## Frequently Asked Questions
Can third-party training be embedded on the onboarding page? 
No, Currently onboarding pages will only display Vanta in-house training videos. To preview how your custom training link will appear, please see our help guide on Task Sets Previews <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345602>
 
Can Admins upload previously completed training? 
Yes, Admins can upload evidence of completed training on behalf of a user. The option to upload a document will be visible on their user profile.  Please note, this only applies to custom training tasks where a supported integration is not connected
In cases of discrepancies where training completion is not being tracked, confirm that training intervals align with Vanta's schedule and required frameworks are active under the personnel settings. For unsupported integrations, ensure employees provide clear proof of completion.
