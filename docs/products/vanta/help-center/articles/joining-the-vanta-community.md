## Article text

The Vanta Community is a Slack-native experience where practitioners connect, share insights, solve trust management challenges, and support one another’s professional growth. This community exists to help trust management teams move faster, stay compliant, and earn trust—without reinventing the wheel.
 
Whether you're preparing for your first audit or scaling a mature compliance program, the Vanta Community gives you direct access to peers, best practices, and Vanta experts.
 
 

## Eligibility
Any Vanta customers or prospective customers can request to join the Vanta Community directly from community.vanta.com <https://community.vanta.com/?utm_source=help_center&utm_campaign=vanta_community_join>. 
- 
Vanta customers: Full access to all community channels and content in Slack, plus eligibility for digital recognition badges and member-only swag.
- 
Prospective customers: Access to the #ask-a-customer channel to connect with current customers and learn about their experience.
If you have any questions about Vanta Community membership, please email [email protected]. 
 
 

## Participation
Community participation happens in Slack <https://slack.com/>, with a companion site at community.vanta.com <https://community.vanta.com/?utm_source=help_center&utm_campaign=vanta_community_join> (hosted on Tightknit <https://tightknit.ai/>) that highlights select public Slack channels to extend visibility and engagement. Personal information beyond your first name and last initial is not shared on the companion site.

### Guidelines
The Vanta Community is peer-driven and is not a replacement for Vanta Support. For technical issues, troubleshooting, or account-specific questions, please contact Vanta Support <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11346096-vanta-support-guide>.
 
Community participation is subject to our Community Guidelines <https://community.vanta.com/community-guidelines>. Once you become a Vanta Community member, you’ll have access to more detailed information about moderation processes and participation standards directly in Slack.
