## Article text

Individual onboarding is an essential part of every security and compliance program. Ensuring your organization is aware of the process, policies, and actions to take in the event of a security incident will help you build a strong foundation of security compliance and awareness within your company.
 
- 
When assigning tasks to your personnel groups <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345836>, they will receive an email prompting them to log in to Vanta.

Once logged in to Vanta, personnel will have access to complete only their assigned tasks.
- 
When tasks are completed, the status will update in the people page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345379> under Task statu.s
