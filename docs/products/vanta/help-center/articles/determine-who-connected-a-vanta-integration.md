# Determine who connected a Vanta integration

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345888-determine-who-connected-a-vanta-integration
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

There may be a time when you need to see who connected a Vanta integration. To do this, use the following steps!
- 
Navigate to the Event Log <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345419>
- 
Click the Action menu and select Connect Integration from the dropdown

- 
The Details column will include the name of integration and the user who connected it
