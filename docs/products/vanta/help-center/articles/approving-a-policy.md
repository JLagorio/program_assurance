# Approving a Policy

Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Professional and Enterprise customers support the ability to designate multiple approvers. Customers using these editions can assign up to 5 steps of approval, with each step allowing up to 3 approvers.
 
After uploading, editing, and reviewing a policy, the last step before it is ready for employees is to accept it would be to have it approved.

Policies can be approved by any Vanta user with admin, editor, or collaborator roles. The policy approver can be anyone at the company, but it is suggested that the approver be the person responsible for enforcing the policy. If there are any further questions about who can be an approver, please contact your auditor.
 

## Procedure
Submit a Policy for Approval
​For a brand new policy:
- 
Click on the 'not started' policy on the policies page
- 
Click 'get started' or 'import an existing policy'
- 
On a following page you will be able to select the owner and approver for that policy.
- 
Select approver from the dropdown list. The approver can be yourself, or a different user. Ensure the user is a Vanta admin, editor, or collaborator. Standard users will not be able to approve policies.
For Professional and Enterprise customers: You can add up to 5 steps of approval, with each step supporting up to 3 approvers. Approvers can include any user with admin, editor, or collaborator status.

For Professional and Enterprise customers: You can assign multiple approvers and add up to 5 steps of approval, with each step allowing up to 3 approvers.
- 
If you are creating a new version, you will have the option to 'submit for approval' in the upper-right hand corner of the page.
- 
If you are the approver, click Next, and review the groups of employees that will be assigned the Policy
- 
If selecting a different user, the assignee will receive an email about this approval request. They will be able to see the Approve button once logged in with their Vanta account. Approvers can include users with admin, editor, or collaborator status. To make edits to this policy, cancel the approval request first.
- 
Review the groups of employees that will be assigned the Policy
- 
Select Submit
 
Approve a Policy 
- 
Click on the Review in Vanta link in the e-mail sent to your inbox

- 
Sign in with your company's login option
- 
Click View File and review the Policy
- 
Click Approve
For Professional and Enterprise customers: If multiple approvers are assigned, each approver will review and approve the policy according to their step in the process. All approvers must approve before the policy is ready for employees to accept.
