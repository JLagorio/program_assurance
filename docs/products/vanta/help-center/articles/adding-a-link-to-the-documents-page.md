# Adding a Link to the Documents Page

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345903-adding-a-link-to-the-documents-page
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Some document requirements may be covered on an external website, and a link to the website would be more suitable evidence than a .pdf or a .docx file. In these cases, you can use the Add URL feature.
 

## Procedure
- 
The Add URL button is at the top of the page.
