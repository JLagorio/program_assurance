## Use Vanta's Templates
- 
From the Policy page, select Start for the policy you would like to work on

 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516770856/7d8bea1ce6683bf1b23ebd847ee1/25046174164244?expires=1794967200&signature=a3a3901f1a2f8e07c44f3aa79aedf00c65adaa9491916fd11d602719141f34a7&req=dSUmEM55nYlaX%2FMW1HO4zeIrQLmGJ9FO94EkAFldB73VNfu%2BFPltvJ9HPLxU%0AOm1frjnDbrzbgM2dhxY%3D%0A>
 
 
- 
From here, you can import your policy or take advantage of Vanta's templates and policy editor
- 
Creating the policy from scratch will take you to the predefined template
[image 02]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1787267492/204e3051f786132bb69ac3c267e7/25046174199060?expires=1794967200&signature=bf44565ebd56873f9e3f40e212d619177ececb05c9d8646e41fecc13c42526d3&req=dScvEct4moVWW%2FMW1HO4zSd1fZ8yNl7p70VCBvLZ0YRmo2CJu4Wisagne6uJ%0AsUT7%2Fs9TTr%2FW%2FgvsF3w%3D%0A>
- 
Use Vanta's template to create a policy that reflects your organization's needs
- 
Using the policy editor
- 
Use a pre-established template
- 
Make any necessary edits
- 
Submit for approval
- 
You can also delete and reset the template by selecting Delete and Reset in the top right-hand corner of the policy editor page.
 
[image 03]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516770857/83524d3b12149c9fe7a204932bfb/9035193288980?expires=1794967200&signature=d98c369cb0bff07700f38d135b1c86a19005c514f1b4aceada920ad4085e6f29&req=dSUmEM55nYlaXvMW1HO4zfoRy5rkeCaQ7KG%2BChJ5XtzjRYtLyYpoU3%2FCXSeQ%0At6stPXN9TFGmVCP9n6E%3D%0A>
 
- 
Once the edits have been made, select Submit for approval
- 
Choose the approver
- 
If you are the approver, you can approve the document. You can assign the approval to someone else if you do not wish to be the approver.

Once approved, the policy can be assigned to employees in onboarding settings.
- 
If the policy has already been assigned to employees, you can ask them to reaccept it once the pop-up model approves it.

## Upload a File
- 
Select the policy title
- 
Select Upload a file from the computer 
 
[image 07]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516770872/bde2a3c9519311a05eefffee7b3d/18616910678932?expires=1794967200&signature=5d842c5e755a53a75a4f27ccc3bf59df18233bcb14f19060e804ea3cb92455db&req=dSUmEM55nYlYW%2FMW1HO4zRgrNAruOx9zhpSr7tZzdhhzpeBdLLEgrcxLm2j%2F%0AbQL2H1HH9MjSqGx5Xds%3D%0A>
 
- 
Select Upload to select the file from your device, or drag and drop the document into the highlighted space.
Please Note: Currently supported file types are .docx and .pdf, and must be 50 MB or less. Importing a .docx file may result in altered formatting from the original document.

## Renewing a Policy
- 
If you don't need to change a current policy and would like to renew it, open the policy and select Renew
- 
If the policy has already been assigned to employees, you can ask them to reaccept it once the pop-up model approves it.
- 
Select Renew
 

## Delete a Policy
To delete a policy:
- 
Go to the Policies page.
- 
Locate the policy you want to delete.
- 
Select the three-dot menu (⋯) to the right of the policy name.
- 
Click Delete policy.
 
[image 10]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1818890854/9102f203d13055a1e8348214afb4/Screenshot+2025-11-07+at+11_48_40%E2%80%AFAM.png?expires=1794967200&signature=214c266db427303fcbdd0467a567b00e6902b2910a66fa512e0eae49d21346b0&req=dSgmHsF3nYlaXfMW1HO4zXQw2kLC23oqe0BDIh4%2F7YXeDt8PGEkH6jDhIXJT%0AQzCezyYByZVY7nEgcok%3D%0A>
 
This will remove the policy from your list.
 

## Restoring a Deleted Policy
If you’ve deleted a policy and need to bring it back:
- 
Navigate to Policies in Vanta.
- 
Select Add Policy.
- 
Click + Add from Policy Library.
 
[image 11]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1818892200/69c13baa1f8f16c81c6a87c44bde/Screenshot%2B2025-11-07%2Bat%2B10_56_57-E2-80-AFAM.png?expires=1794967200&signature=8ce155bb852437090dd122c6b8509dccf95f2fcf7914aa41f88360d6e51a0a58&req=dSgmHsF3n4NfWfMW1HO4zcXZV7DIchTJyrmWHmieSKwjb33l2kkO0JRienLq%0APOncCI6aWzUSCeBaJ3c%3D%0A>
 
- 
Locate the policy you deleted and re-add it from the list of available templates.
This restores the default version of the policy template to your instance.
