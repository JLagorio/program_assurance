## Article text

If you leverage a CSPM (Cloud security posture management) tool, the integration with Vanta will allow you to monitor alerts from posture management tools and track resolution within set windows.
 
Please note: To utilize this page, you must be integrated with a CSPM in Vanta.
 

## CSPM Alerts
- 
From the Asset tab in the left-hand navigation panel, select CSPM alerts 
Please note that to utilize this page, you must be integrated with a CSPM in Vanta.

From here, you can see a list of items requiring remediation, their severity, status, and due date.
 

## Creating CSPM Alert Remediation SLAs
- 
The SLAs you set forward on the settings page will guide due dates for alerts. To create or edit SLAs (service-level agreements)
- 
In your account header, click the Settings icon
- 
In the Settings page menu, scroll to the Features section, select _item_name_, and go to the CSPM alert remediation SLAs tab
- 
When CSPM alerts are detected within your infrastructure, it is crucial to ensure they are triaged and remediated promptly. This allows you to prioritize possible threats and prevent environmental harm or loss.
- 
Please note: If you previously set your SLAs for CSPM alerts, any changes made here will update your CSPM alert settings across Vanta. Changes will be applied to new CSPM alerts and will not affect any historical or currently open CSPM alerts.
- 
Vanta will provide remediation time recommendations based on the level of criticality.

## Managing CSPM Alerts
- 
Alerts are managed from the CSPM alerts page
- 
From the open tab, all available alerts will provide additional details when clicked on
[image 03]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2108309254/e66041ba23b5b368d31e081b5ffa/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=e83e98a91235568113f8f17fab77e033434054586923b75145a37ab805e46b42&req=diEnHsp%2BlINaXfMW1HO4zdBr5T2jGMRDFoCiiXfWRcBAdPHaeyFIMAVM5Syt%0AmywcTFwu7FrwnO4gmIc%3D%0A>
- 
From the modal on the left-hand side of your screen, you can view information about the alerts, view remediation instructions, and select the asset(s).
- 
If you deactivate the monitoring of these alerts, Vanta will no longer track the remediation.
- 
You can deactivate indefinitely, or until a specified date
