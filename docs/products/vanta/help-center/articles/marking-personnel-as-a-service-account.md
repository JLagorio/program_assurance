# Marking Personnel as a Service Account

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345579-marking-personnel-as-a-service-account
Collection: Team Compliance ; Team Compliance › Managing Users
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Service accounts are accounts used for automated processes or shared accounts that don't have a specific owner, such as group email accounts. It is essential to ensure these accounts are marked as such within Vanta. Marking an account as a Service account will exclude it from email configuration checks, monitored accounts, application access reviews, onboarding tasks, and computer associations.
- 
From the People page select the three-dot menu of the desired account
- 
Select Set as a service account

 Enter a reason. Keep in mind marking this person as a service account means it will not be tracked as a person in Vanta. Tasks can’t be assigned to service accounts and they are not tracked in tests.

Select Confirm
