# Unsupported International Background Checks

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11346009-unsupported-international-background-checks
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Due to how some background check providers, processes checks outside of the US, they cannot be pulled through into Vanta. However, you have a few options to work around this limitation.
 

## Manually link a custom URL
- 
To manually link a background check to a user, you can go directly to the employee's profile on the people page, click on the onboarding tab, then hover over background check, and you should see a link icon:
​
[image 01]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516766278/36bbf77cc076673b0ab1df7ea490/27565174421780?expires=1794967200&signature=d96c43d1580029ed6dde6f6fa8b34f36023707c47fbd7b1dcf53d1854c102352&req=dSUmEM54m4NYUfMW1HO4zWO37FMkVxBzl7k5tyeV%2Bdg3KeXhPuiM2TvrY2gI%0ADJH7%0A>
- 
Click the Link button, then click Link Custom URL, and then you'll have the option to add a link to where the background check is.
- 
If Checkr has a share link for the background check, you can add that or upload it to a third party file hosting site (such as Google Drive) and share the link from there.
 

## Disable monitoring for the background check requirement for a user:
- 
You can disable monitoring for the employee from the Background check test <https://app.vanta.com/tests#background-checks-records> and upload the background check when deactivating monitoring:

- 
You can create a new group <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345594> for employees who are outside of the US by navigating to the group's page in Vanta <https://app.vanta.com/groups>. By creating a new group, you can choose not to require background checks for these employees and manually upload the background check here in the document tab <https://app.vanta.com/tests?tab=documents#bulk-background-check>
