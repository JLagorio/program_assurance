# Error: Getting “accounts deprovisioned when employees leave” for Re-Onboarded Active Personnel

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11346012-error-getting-accounts-deprovisioned-when-employees-leave-for-re-onboarded-active-personnel
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

## Environment Details
When a person is offboarded and returns to Vanta later on, the employee may fail the tests, "accounts de-provisioned when employees leave" for different services. This is due to the re-onboarded person's accounts being connected to their previous employee profile:

 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516766609/bddef5101e1b4eb7b1ebc7fed71e/27572097170580?expires=1794967200&signature=e100ec70b31052ca04092506dd4fe20c060ff18d1397a1043a14f9a5c11f50fd&req=dSUmEM54m4dfUPMW1HO4zX0BAXuUbeh0m%2BrVAwulYH7YE53LMOQoK43%2FC7n0%0AOffSQns09YKW1yYfOAE%3D%0A>
 

## Resolution
- 
Go to the Access page 
- 
Select service from the dropdown
- 
Search for an employee in the search bar
- 
Click the pencil icon ​
- 
Select the active profile from the dropdown
[image 02]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516766607/03c192f1ec2ee29f4345ba199ed2/27572093596308?expires=1794967200&signature=b7c3412e6926f7f9601397b67ad227d8f3d3d22a443b074ee9a885b8e2ffabfd&req=dSUmEM54m4dfXvMW1HO4zazvdyH3FnCGh7zSLzp8vlsgNHxfAx2%2B%2Froxa0A0%0AcwgiSjawvKpqamwnxqY%3D%0A>
- 
Once reassigned to an active profile, the employee should no longer show on tests for de-provisioned accounts.
