# Invalid Magic Link

## Article text

When attempting to access Vanta using an Email login, you are encountering an error page stating, Unexpected error: Invalid magic link.

## Cause
- 
Link is 30 minutes old, or has it already been used
- 
You requested too many links, and the link you clicked on was not the newest version
- 
Your email scanner automatically accessed the link sent to your email, rendering it useless
- 
Your email client malformed the link when opening your browser
 

## Resolution
- 
Open up an incognito/private browser and navigate to app.vanta.com
- 
Enter your email in the field box under Enter your email address
- 
Click the Continue with the email button
- 
In the "[Vanta] Log in to your Vanta account" email you receive, right-click the link provided and copy
- 
Go to your incognito/private browser and paste the link you just copied
