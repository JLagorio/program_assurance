## Article text

The Policies page has features and corresponding tests for managing policies. You will enforce processes around your company's security policies that are both good practices and required for audits. Vanta provides policy templates that you can edit to fit your organization's needs.
 
 

## Policies page
- 
From the left-hand navigation panel, select Compliance, then Policies
- 
If you are new to Vanta, the Policies page will show you a list of Templates you can leverage to being the process of drafting policies
- 
If your team has been using Vanta, you will see a list of all of the policies being used within your organization
- 
From the top-right corner, you can add custom policies <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345492>

## Policy status
Latest Policy Status Column: The status of the most recent policy version
- 
Not Started: No work has begun on drafting this policy
- 
Draft: The most recent policy version has been started but has not been sent for approval.
- 
Pending approval: The most recent policy version has been sent for approval.
- 
Approved: The most recent policy version has been approved, and the policy approval test is now being passed. Your Overall policy status is OK
- 
Renew soon: The most recent policy version has been approved, and the policy approval test is now passing. However, it will expire within 6 weeks.
- 
Expired: The most recent policy version has expired, causing the Overall Policy Status to move to Needs remediation.
Overall policy status Column: Aligns with the status of the policy approval test
- 
OK: A version of this policy has been approved and is unexpired.
- 
Needs remediation: This policy has yet to be approved and is unexpired.

## Deleting a policy
To delete a policy:
- 
Go to the Policies page.
- 
Locate the policy you want to delete.
- 
Select the three-dot menu (⋯) to the right of the policy name.
- 
Click Delete policy. This will remove the policy from your list.
⚠️ Note: Deleting a policy removes it from your Policies page. If the policy came from Vanta’s policy library, you can add it back later, but any customizations you made won’t be preserved. Custom policies and drafts can’t be restored.

## Restoring a deleted policy
You can re-add a Vanta policy template, but any customizations you made to the policy (such as edits to the content, title, or description) will not be restored. The re-added policy will use the default Vanta template. It's not possible to restore a deleted custom policy <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345492-creating-a-custom-policy>.
 
To re-add a policy from the template library:
- 
Navigate to Policies in Vanta.
- 
Select Add Policy.
- 
Click + Add from Policy Library.
- 
Locate the policy you deleted and re-add it from the list of available templates.
- 
This restores the default version of the policy template to your instance.
