## Vanta Essentials: Your Compliance Launchpad
No prerequisites required.
The problem this solves: Most new Vanta customers spend their first few weeks unsure of where to start — and arrive at their audit unprepared because of it.
Start here. This foundational session is designed for new customers and teams getting started with Vanta.
 
In this interactive session, you’ll learn the fundamentals of how Vanta works, beginning with the platform’s foundations and how evidence collection plays a critical role. You’ll explore how integrations and automation streamline compliance efforts, discover how to implement and manage policies, and navigate your account settings for a tailored experience. The session wraps with a live Q&A so you can get real-time answers from our team.
 
Why take this training?
You’ll understand the core functionality of Vanta and how the pieces fit together to support your compliance goals.
After this session, you'll know how Vanta works, how to connect your tools, and how to configure your account for compliance success — and you'll have earned the title of Vanta Navigator.

## Vanta MCP Builder Workshop: Build Your Compliance Copilot
Prerequisites: Vanta Organization Admin access and an AI tool such as Claude, Cursor, Perplexity, or a similar tool. Connecting Vanta MCP before the session is recommended.
The problem this solves: Compliance teams have valuable data in Vanta but often spend too much time searching for answers, creating reports, and moving information between tools.
Build something real. This hands-on workshop shows you how to connect Vanta to your AI tool and turn natural-language questions into useful compliance work.
In this interactive session, you’ll connect Vanta MCP, run guided queries against your compliance program, and build a practical automation. Choose from creating a shareable compliance report, adding a risk entry, or designing a workflow that connects Vanta with tools like Slack or Jira.
 
Why take this training?
You’ll learn how to turn your Vanta data into faster insights and connected actions, without relying on manual processes.
After this session, you’ll know how to connect Vanta MCP, query your compliance program with AI, and build a practical workflow, and you’ll have earned the title of Automation Architect. ## Personnel Security & Access Reviews Workshop: Manage People & Access
No prerequisites required.
The problem this solves: When the wrong people have the wrong access, it's not just a compliance issue it's a security risk.
With your policies in place, it’s time to focus on the people in your organization. Learn how to securely manage onboarding, offboarding, and access control.
 
This session shows you how to use integrations for managing personnel, set up user groups and task lists, and build smooth onboarding and offboarding workflows. You’ll also learn how to conduct access reviews that keep your organization secure. A live Q&A rounds out the training to address your team’s specific needs.
 
Why take this training?
Ensuring the right people have the right access at the right time is key to both security and audit success.
After this session, you'll be able to manage onboarding, offboarding, and access reviews so the right people have the right access at all times and you'll have earned the title of Access Guardian.
