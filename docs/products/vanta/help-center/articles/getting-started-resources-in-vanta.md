## Article text

Vanta provides in-app tools to facilitate your onboarding journey and maximize your utilization of the platform's capabilities.
 

## Vanta's Compliance Roadmap
- 
You can open Vanta's Compliance Roadmap from your account profile icon at any time.
- 
This tool will walk you through the product and outline how to complete the steps integral to using Vanta effectively.
- 
The Compliance Roadmap will be hidden three days after completion, but can be viewed by selecting your profile icon and choosing Compliance Roadmap from the drop-down.
