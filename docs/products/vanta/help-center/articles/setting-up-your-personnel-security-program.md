# Setting up Your Personnel Security Program

Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Are you new to Vanta and looking to get started? It is essential to ensure you begin your personnel onboarding process. The onboarding process ensures your team members complete the task to maintain organizational compliance.
 

## Configure your Groups
- 
The goal is to organize your personnel based on either department, job functions, or "level of access."
- 
Learn more about creating your groups here <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345594>.

## Configure & customize your Task Sets settings
- 
Groups are meant to be the best way to identify and organize your personnel, and Task Sets <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345603> are where you assign security tasks to personnel (they're different pages, both designed to work hand-in-hand)
- 
You can create a specific task set for individuals who don't handle sensitive data (such as customer data, production environment, HR/personnel data, or ePHI) and scope down their required policies and security tasks.
- 
You can always use the same task set for multiple groups
- 
 Feel free to preview the personnel onboarding workflow (app.vanta.com/onboarding <https://app.vanta.com/onboarding>)
 If you're unsure what security tasks should be required for your groups, it's a Security Best Practice to treat them as an FTE and hold them to the same standard. That way, you're covering all your bases as a company and ensuring the best security posture.
 

## Send Internal Communication
- 
Leverage Vanta's provided templates <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345608> to give your personnel more context
- 
This gives your personnel a preview of what you're doing as a company to be secure & compliant, a brief intro to Vanta, and company-wide action items
- 
We recommend sending this out as a company-wide email & in your Slack's #general channel
- 
Also, it's essential to assign a deadline. This way, you can follow up with specific teams or people managers.

## Enable Automated Personnel Reminders on the 
- 
Vanta will automatically send out email reminders <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345593> to your personnel for their required security tasks, and you can set this to weekly or daily.
- 
We recommend using weekly at first, then switching to daily reminders as the deadline approaches.
- 
There is also a Vanta<>Slack integration that you can leverage for reminders, also found on the Settings page <https://app.vanta.com/settings/company-notifications>
