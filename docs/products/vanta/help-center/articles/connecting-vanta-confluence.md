## Article text

By connecting Confluence, Vanta is able to automate the synchronization of your company policies and documents.

## Connecting Vanta & Confluence
- 
In Vanta, go to the Integrations page, click Add integration, and search for Confluence. For help, see our guide to the Integrations Page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345761-integrations-page>.
- 
Select Connect
Ensure you are selecting the 'Confluence' integration for syncing policies and documents, and not 'Confluence Access Control'
- 
A pop-up modal will appear; select Connect Confluence
