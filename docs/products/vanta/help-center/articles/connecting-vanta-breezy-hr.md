## Article text

Vanta integrates with Breezy HR to sync account data and support automated onboarding and offboarding workflows. By pulling real-time account information, Vanta helps ensure that only current employees retain access to company systems. This simplifies access reviews and supports compliance requirements.
 
Estimated setup time: Less than 10 minutes
 

## How It Works
Vanta integrates with Breezy HR to sync employee and group data. This allows you to:
- 
Monitor access in real time
- 
Ensure only active employees retain access to company systems
- 
Simplify access reviews and support compliance requirements
Please note: Vanta connects to Breezy HR through Merge.dev <https://merge.dev/>, a secure third-party integration platform.
 

## Use Cases
Connecting Breezy HR to Vanta will enable you to:
- 
Sync employees and groups from Breezy HR into Vanta
- 
Monitor and manage personnel access in real time
- 
Ensure only active employees retain access to company systems
- 
Simplify access reviews and support compliance requirements
 

## Requirements
- 
Vanta administrator account
- 
Breezy HR administrator account
 

## Connect the Integration
- 
To connect, go to the Integrations page, click Add integration, and search for Breezy HR. For help navigating the Integrations page, see Integrations Page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345761-integrations-page>.
- 
Click Connect.
- 
Enter your Breezy HR username
- 
Enter your Breezy HR password
- 
Select Save changes

## Permissions
Vanta accesses the following data from Breezy HR:
 
Vanta can read:
- 
Data about your users
- 
Data about your employees
Vanta can write:
- 
Nothing (Vanta does not have write permissions)
