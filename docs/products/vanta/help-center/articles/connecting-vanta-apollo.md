## Article text

The Apollo and Vanta integration enables seamless connection between your Apollo environment and Vanta, enhancing visibility into your sales systems and strengthening your security posture. By enabling this integration, Vanta can automatically retrieve relevant user and activity data from Apollo, allowing you to monitor account access, enforce security controls, and meet compliance requirements with minimal manual effort. This streamlined connection supports a more secure and audit-ready sales tech stack.
 

## Requirements 
- 
Change access to your Apollo environment 
- 
Ability to create API Keys

## Connecting Vanta & Apollo
- 
In Vanta, go to the Integrations page, click Add integration, and search for Apollo. For help, see our guide to the Integrations Page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345761-integrations-page>.
- 
Click Connect. 
- 
Follow the link to the Apollo Developer Portal.
