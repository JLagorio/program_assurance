## Article text

✅ Feature availability: This article discusses Trust Center features, some of which may require an upgrade or add-on. If you don’t see a feature described here, it may not be included in your plan or turned on for your organization. Refer to Vanta Plans and Pricing <https://www.vanta.com/pricing?utm_source=help_center&utm_campaign=hc_pricing_cta> for details.
 
Your Trust Center is a branded security portal where you can share your compliance documents and security information with customers. This guide explains how to customize your Trust Center’s content, layout, and access settings. You can use the Trust Center as a stand-alone tool, even if you’re not using the full Vanta product. However, this setup may limit certain editing or automation options, so some features may not be available and some content will need to be managed manually.
 
⚙️ User permissions: Trust Admins, Editors, and Admins can manage and edit the Trust Center and configure settings. Trust Collaborators can preview the Trust Center editor and review changes. Learn more: User Permissions by Product Area <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345590-user-permissions-by-product-area>
 
 

## Trust Center editor 
Use the Trust Center editor to customize your design and content. Edits save as draft changes until you publish them.

To open the Trust Center editor:
- 
Go to Customer Trust > Trust Center.
-

Review each tab to find the section you want to edit.
- 
Click the pencil icon next to a section to open the editing options.
- 
Publish your draft changes now, or close the editor to save them and publish later.

### Branding and layout
- 
Use the Customize menu on the left of the Trust Center editor to upload a header image or choose the colors and fonts you'd like to use in your Trust Center.
- 
Click the Customize layout button in the upper-right of the Trust Center editor to re-organize the sections in each tab of your Trust Center.
-

- 
Add a welcome message or notice.
 

### AWS marketplace listing
 
If you sell through AWS Marketplace, you can add your Marketplace listing to your Trust Center header. This gives buyers reviewing your security posture a direct path to purchase.
 
To link your AWS Marketplace listing:
- 
Open the Trust Center editor.
- 
Click to edit the header content.
- 
In the AWS Marketplace listing field, paste the URL of your AWS Marketplace product listing.
- 
Save your changes.
To find your listing URL, open your product listing in AWS Marketplace and copy the address from your browser.
 
The link appears regardless of your Trust Center access settings. If your Trust Center is private, viewers see the link after they log in.
ℹ️ Note: Vanta doesn't validate this URL, so confirm it points to your own Marketplace listing before saving.

### Controls
 
You can customize the controls you want to share with Trust Center viewers by adding controls to categories and managing control visibility settings.
- 
Open the Trust Center editor.
- 
In the Controls tab, click the pencil icon to open the editing options.
- 
Under Control categories, add control categories and reorder how they appear in your Trust Center.
- 
Select an existing category, then above the table:
- 
Click Edit controls to choose which controls to put in it.
- 
Click the pencil icon next to the category name to edit it.
 

### Global control visibility settings
At a global level, all controls you add to a category are visible to anyone with Trust Center access. You can configure a status visibility setting to decide how much status detail they see.
 
To configure a global status visibility setting:
- 
In the upper-right corner, click Configure global control visibility.
- 
Select one of the options for the status visibility setting:
- 
Show "OK" controls: Controls that are "OK" are displayed.
- 
Show "OK" and unmapped controls: Controls that are "OK" or without evidence mapped are displayed. Only "OK" controls display a green check.
- 
Show all controls with status: All controls are visible with status displayed. Controls without evidence mapped will not display a green check.
- 
Show all controls without status: All controls are visible with no status displayed.
​
ℹ️ Note: If you don’t have tagging enabled on your plan, you can only set the global status visibility setting.
 

### Control category visibility settings
If your plan includes tagging, each control category can have its own visibility settings that override the global defaults.
 
⚠️ Note: Changing category visibility to shareable hides it from your Trust Center. To make the category visible at the status visibility selected, (1) add a tag to the controls in the category and (2) add the same tag to the accounts you want to have access to those controls. Learn more: Customer Trust Accounts <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/13163124-customer-trust-accounts#h_b19af2df1c>

### FAQ
 
Use Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) to proactively address questions you get from prospects and customers—like commonly asked security questions, permission requirements, data storage locations, and anything else your sales or security team gets questions about.
 
To manage the FAQ for your Trust Center:
- 
Open the Trust Center editor.
- 
In the FAQ tab, click the pencil icon to open the editing options.
- 
In the menu on the left, click the + icon to add the question, answer, and metadata for the FAQ.
- 
Drag and drop FAQs to reorder how they appear in your Trust Center.
- 
The first 4 FAQ will show on the front page of the Trust Center. 
- 
Remaining FAQ will display when visitors click the FAQ tab, where you can also copy a direct link to each FAQ you’ve created for easy sharing.

## Trust Center publishing
Changes you make in the Trust Center editor save as drafts—they don't go live until you review and publish them. You can also choose whether changes to a resource in your knowledge base are auto-published to your Trust Center or saved as drafts until you publish them.

Learn more <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345452-creating-and-managing-trust-center-updates>
 

### Previewing draft changes
 
To preview your draft changes:
- 
From the Trust Center editor, click Preview.
- 
Click the Preview options drop-down menu.
- 
Under Version, select what you want to see:
- 
Draft: Your unpublished changes
- 
Published: What’s currently live now
- 
Under Viewing as, select the type of visitor you want to preview. The options depend on your Enable public access setting:
- 
Enabled:
- 
People with full access: Can view all content
- 
People with public access: Must request access to restricted content
- 
Disabled: 
- 
People with full access: Can view all content
- 
People with access: Must request access to view restricted content
- 
People without access: Must request access to view your Trust Center
 

### Publishing draft changes
 
To review and publish your draft changes:
- 
From the Trust Center editor, click Review changes:
- 
In the Design tab, review changes to your header and branding.
- 
In the Content tab, review changes to the content across the tabs in your Trust Center.
- 
Review each change:
- 
Open a change to see what was added, edited, or removed.
- 
Click the ↺ undo icon to discard a change.
- 
When you're ready, click Publish changes.
​
ℹ️ Note: If resource drafting is on, your resource changes appear in the Content tab. To discard or undo a change, edit the resource in your knowledge base.
 

### Enabling resource drafting
 
This setting applies to updates you make to resources in your knowledge base <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345465-managing-your-knowledge-base>: new file versions, titles, descriptions, tags, visibility, download settings, and new resources that are eligible to appear on your Trust Center.
 
To manage the resource drafting setting:
- 
In your account header, click the Settings icon.
- 
In the page menu, scroll to Features and select Customer trust.
- 
In the Trust Center tab, scroll to Turn on resource drafting:
- 
Disabled (default): Your resource changes go live on your Trust Center right away.
- 
Enabled: Your resource changes save as drafts within the Trust Center editor.
​
ℹ️ Note: Resource edits you make directly in the Trust Center editor, like reordering and changing categories, always save as drafts. Deleting a resource removes it from your Trust Center right away, even if resource drafting is enabled.

## Access requests 
To manage individual access requests:
- 
Go to Customer Trust > Trust Center using the navigation menu in your account.
- 
Click the Access requests tab. 
- 
Select one or more requests and click the Give Access button or Deny button. If you choose to give access, you can set an expiration date.

​

## Access settings 
To manage site-level access or auto-approval settings:
- 
In your account header, click the Settings icon.
- 
In the Settings page menu, scroll to the Features section, select Customer trust, and go to the Trust Center tab.
- 
Scroll to the Configure access section to control whether visitors see your Trust Center by default or must request access.
- 
Enable public access to your Trust Center:
- 
On: Anyone with the link can access the Trust Center.
- 
Off: Only viewers who have been manually invited or approved by a Trust Center admin can access the Trust Center. Unauthenticated visitors see an access request form.
- 
Enable full access to your Trust Center: 
- 
On: Show the Request access button. Once approved, viewers can download resources and documentation.
- 
Off: Hide the Request access button. Trust Center admins can still send invitations manually to grant access.
- 
Scroll to the Configure auto-approval section to set up rules to automate approval of access requests.
- 
You can connect Vanta to your CRM to set conditions for auto-approval.
- 
You can automatically approve access at a domain level.
When both Public Access and Full Access are disabled, only manually invited or approved users can access the Trust Center.
​
💡 Tip: To automatically approve access requests by email domain, create a Customer Trust Account <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/13163124-customer-trust-accounts> and enable account-level auto-approval so matching requesters can be automatically approved and associated with that account.
 
 

## Trust Center data deletion requests
If a Trust Center viewer requests deletion of their personal data, you can fulfill the request directly from Vanta. This workflow revokes the viewer's access and de-identifies matching Trust Center-related records stored in Vanta, and can help you fulfill viewer deletion requests under privacy regulations like GDPR.
 
[image 05]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2399319369/531f7a09241243ee3684be86157e/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=c03aef3e4d7b498ac5089f131a806a553bce1d3a05ac2c758d93c1bf5fabf271&req=diMuH8p%2FlIJZUPMW1HO4zdmsw2BBNDYHupReWhyn%2FJqlTMLakkWXcqV%2BK23%2B%0A2x9hecfoVdtLtLfHJQE%3D%0A>
 

### Fulfilling deletion requests
 
Trust Center Admins or higher permissions required. The workflow processes one email address at a time. Vanta uses the email address to identify all matching Trust Center records for that viewer.
 
To fulfill a deletion request:
- 
In your account header, click the Settings icon.
- 
In the page menu, select Customer Trust.
- 
From the Trust Center tab, scroll to the Right to be forgotten section.
- 
Enter the viewer's email address.
- 
Review the confirmation prompt and confirm the deletion.
Deletion requests are processed in the background, so updates may not appear immediately. If the same person requests access using the same email address again later, the new request will be treated as a new Trust Center record.
 

### What gets de-identified
 
When you submit a deletion request, Vanta de-identifies the viewer's personal information across the following Trust Center records:
- 
Viewer record: Access revoked, name and email de-identified
- 
Access requests: Pending requests denied, name and email de-identified in historical requests
- 
Update subscriptions: Viewer unsubscribed from all groups, name and email de-identified
- 
Update notifications: Viewer's email de-identified in past notification recipient lists
- 
Chatbot conversations: Viewer’s email is de-identified as the conversation requester (message content they typed is not de-identified)
- 
Event logs: Matching Trust Center viewer and subscriber references are redacted, while audit metadata is preserved
 

### What's still stored
- 
If a viewer typed personal information directly into a Trust Center chatbot message, that message content is not automatically de-identified by this workflow—only the viewer's email as the conversation requester is removed.
- 
Some historical request metadata may remain after the viewer's name and email are removed. The workflow preserves the underlying record structure for audit purposes while removing identifying information.
- 
This workflow applies to Trust Center data stored in Vanta. It does not automatically delete records in connected third-party systems such as your CRM, Slack, or DocuSign. If you need data removed from those systems, do that separately in those tools.
