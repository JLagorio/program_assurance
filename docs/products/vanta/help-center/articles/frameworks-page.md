## Article text

The Frameworks Page lets you view the progress of all frameworks your organization has purchased through Vanta. This page lets you view other offerings that benefit your security and compliance program.
 

## Utilizing the Frameworks Page
- 
From the left-hand navigation panel, select Frameworks.
- 
Here, you will see all of the frameworks your organization has purchased and any custom frameworks your admins have created for your company.
- 
To view the details of any frameworks, click on the Framework.

The elements of each framework will give you the controls applicable to the framework and the opportunity to add additional controls.
- 
The Frameworks page also allows you to start the process of adding custom frameworks <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345497>.

## Available Frameworks
- 
From the Frameworks page, the Available tab provides a list of Frameworks that Vanta automates and allows you to explore their details.

## Update Notes
- 
The Update notes tab on the Framework page will keep you up to date with relevant updates and changes made to Frameworks.
- 
For additional information, click on the update to reveal additional details.
