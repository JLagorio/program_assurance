## Evidence Status
- 
Not Ready for Audit: A document/policy that has not been uploaded or a test that is failing
- 
Ready for audit: A document/policy is uploaded and is responsible for passing the test.
- 
Approved: Evidence that an auditor has approved
- 
Flagged: Evidence that an auditor has flagged
- 
N/A: Evidence that has been marked as not applicable (N/A)
- 
The auditor can mark the evidence as N/A
- 
If a customer deactivates a test <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345549>, the associated evidence will be marked as N/A
- 
Additionally, evidence can become N/A if the audit observation period does not include the approval or creation dates of the provided evidence.
 

## Resubmitting Evidence for Review
When auditors flag a piece of evidence, you can update or review it and resubmit it to your auditor directly within Vanta.
- 
From the left-hand navigation panel, select Audit.
- 
Locate the audit, and select Open audit.

## Understanding Auditor Visibility in Vanta
Vanta facilitates seamless collaboration during audit processes while maintaining clear boundaries around what data external auditors can access. It's important to understand which elements, such as notes, evidence, and custom controls, are visible to auditors and how to manage this visibility effectively.
 
 

## Key Principles of Auditor Access
- 
Notes in Tests: Notes added to tests are visible only to your internal Vanta users and not automatically accessible to auditors. To share important justifications or explanations with auditors, you should include them in the supporting evidence or consult your auditor regarding preferred methods of communication.- Avoid using Vanta main application comments for auditors; instead, provide comments directly on evidence under audit engagement to ensure visibility.
- 
Custom Controls: Custom controls imported after the audit's defined end date will not be visible to auditors unless the audit end date is modified to include them. Audit timelines must align with custom control creation timelines. To resolve issues of custom controls not appearing, adjust audit end dates or initiate audits covering the control creation period.
- 
URLs as Evidence: Auditors can access URLs added as evidence, provided the content is attached to the control/framework under the audit scope. Explicit permissions might still need to be granted for content visibility.
- 
Documents and drafts: Auditors will only see document files after the document is marked as complete. If you update a document after it’s been marked as complete, those changes remain in draft and won’t be visible to auditors until the document is marked as complete again.
 

## Common Challenges and Their Solutions
- 
Issues with Trust Services Criteria (TSCs):You can activate additional TSCs (e.g., Availability, Confidentiality) after the creation of an audit engagement, but they can not be added or edited if the audit window has started. To include new TSCs after an audit window has begun, you must begin a new audit engagement.
- 
Custom Evidence Visibility: Ensure evidence and controls fit within the defined parameters of the audit timeline. Controls added after critical cutoff dates may require adjustments to audit deadlines for visibility. Request auditors to create specific custom evidence requests for newly updated or created documents  and submit the necessary evidence to the custom request, ensuring complete inclusion in the audit.
 

## Final Tips for Effective Audit Management
- 
Keep open communication with auditors about the preferred methods of presenting and sharing relevant evidence.
- 
Regularly verify that attachments, URLs, or documents have the necessary permissions for auditor access.
- 
Plan audit engagements carefully, including the intended inclusions of TSCs or control frameworks, to avoid repetitive engagement setups.
- 
Educate team members about the importance of correctly linking evidence to audit items and regularly synchronize updates to prevent oversight.
- 
Collaborate with auditors to pre-define observation periods and ensure they encompass all relevant updates.
