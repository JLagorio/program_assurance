## Article text

Vanta's Google Drive <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345637>, Microsoft SharePoint <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345634>, and Confluence integrations <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345639> allow you to sync policies directly into Vanta's Policy page. Syncing occurs automatically once an hour, with the option to manually trigger a sync from Google Drive, Microsoft SharePoint, or Confluence at any time.
- 
From the left-hand navigation panel, open the Policies page
- 
Select the policy you would like to manually sync
- 
The policy must be initially brought in from Google Drive, Microsoft SharePoint, or Confluence to perform the manual sync/refresh
- 
Select the three-dot menu, followed by Refresh sync
