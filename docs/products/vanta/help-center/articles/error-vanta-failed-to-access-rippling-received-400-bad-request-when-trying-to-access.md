# Error: “Vanta failed to access Rippling. Received 400 Bad Request when trying to access...”

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345911-error-vanta-failed-to-access-rippling-received-400-bad-request-when-trying-to-access
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

## Details
- 
When integrating Rippling, the following error message is received:Vanta failed to access Rippling. Received 400 Bad Request when trying to access https://api.rippling.com/platform/api/reports/report_data. Please reach out to Rippling Support to learn more.
- 
The endpoint referenced in the error message may differ depending on your specific situation.
 

## Cause
- 
You've selected services when connecting Rippling in Vanta that you don't have access to in Rippling.
 

## Resolution
- 
Select Reconnect at the top of the integrations page <https://app.vanta.com/integrations>. (If you've disconnected Rippling completely, you'll need to find Rippling on the available tab <https://app.vanta.com/integrations?state=available> and select connect.):
