## Article text

Feature availability: This article discusses Adaptive Scoping, which may require an upgrade or add-on. Refer to Vanta Plans and Pricing <https://www.vanta.com/pricing?utm_source=help_center&utm_campaign=hc_pricing_cta> for details.
 
Framework Scoping in Vanta helps admins streamline compliance by customizing and managing what people, assets, and integrations apply to different frameworks. This helps reduce audit cost and risk, supports scaling to multiple frameworks, and allows detailed monitoring of more assets across your organization.
 
 

## Benefits of Using Framework Scoping
- 
Cost Efficiency: Reduces audit costs and risk by auditing only relevant assets.
- 
Scalability: Enables seamless management and scaling of compliance across multiple frameworks.
- 
Enhanced Monitoring: Focuses your monitoring and compliance efforts on the assets that matter most, while enabling monitoring of more organizational assets that are out of scope for audits.

## Configure Integrations & Assets in Scope
- 
Toggle integrations: Switch integrations off to remove an entire system and its assets from the scope.
- 
Granular adjustments: Click Configure scope to specify individual accounts and their assets to exclude from your scope.
- 
Configure scope availability varies: Not all integrations support granular scoping of individual assets. If you don’t see Configure scope for an integration, you can still toggle the integration off to remove the system and its assets from scope.

## Exclude New Assets from Scope
- 
Navigate to the Scope tab of your selected framework.
- 
Click Configure scope next to the integration you want to adjust. Not all integrations support granular scoping. If Configure scope isn’t available for that integration, you won’t be able to exclude individual assets using this flow.
- 
Deselect any new assets or accounts you wish to exclude from the scope.
- 
Confirm your selection to apply the changes.

## Configure Employee Groups & People Scope
- 
Select the relevant employee groups or specific individuals to include or exclude.
- 
Go to the People page to view and manage the scope of personnel. 
[image 06]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2054054194/b7f1d4bc71a3d5baacbca4afd504/36935599827988.png?expires=1794967200&signature=75dba653feb80c4d58c53bfa83f73f001db0a83a30eb5ee11cc8233931827060&req=diAiEsl7mYBWXfMW1HO4zX7VhOApeYoda2ZyHbFcBn9JtGgE0%2BNh63Nq7RRY%0AmEOeGZj%2FoCOR1tSvaYU%3D%0A>
 
You can also use Auto Scoping to automatically include computers and accounts when people are in scope. This keeps your personnel and their assets aligned without extra manual updates.
- 
Auto Scoping for Devices only applies to eligible computers that support framework scoping (for example, computers pulled in via certain third‑party device monitoring / MDM integrations).
- 
Computers monitored via the Vanta Device Monitor aren’t currently included in framework device Auto Scoping. If you need to exclude specific Vanta Device Monitor computers from impacting particular tests, you can use Deactivate monitoring for a Resource on a Test.
 
To enable Auto Scoping:
- 
From your selected framework, go to the Scope tab and select People
- 
Select Change Settings
