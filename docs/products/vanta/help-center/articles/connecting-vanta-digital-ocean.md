## Article text

Digital Ocean allows customers to connect tools like Vanta using OAuth. By connecting your Digital Ocean account with Vanta, Vanta is able to make API calls as the connecting user, allowing you to display your droplets, load balancers, and more within Vanta under the Inventory page.

## Integrating with Digital Ocean
- 
In Vanta, go to the Integrations page, click Add integration, and search for Digital Ocean. For help, see our guide to the Integrations Page <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345761-integrations-page>.
- 
Select Connect
- 
A pop-up modal will appear; select Connect Digital Ocean
- 
Vanta will then redirect you to Digital Ocean. If you are not logged in, you will need to log in first.
- 
Digital Ocean will then ask you to select an account and authorize the application to Vanta.
