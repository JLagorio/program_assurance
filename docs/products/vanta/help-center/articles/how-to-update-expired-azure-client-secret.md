# How to Update Expired Azure Client Secret

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345906-how-to-update-expired-azure-client-secret
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

When a client secret is created for an app registration in Azure, it's required that an expiration is set. When the expiration date has passed, you must create a new client secret in the current app registration and update your integration connection in Vanta.

## Prerequisites
- 
A new Client Secret was generated in the previously created app registration.
 

## Procedure
- 
On the Integrations page, locate your Azure integration and click Manage, then Edit
- 
Click the pencil icon on the Subscription bar
 
[image 02]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1531628507/d150c57d58a6b18d7b1e72e845e0/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=300d305ff20bd649b61146609898e453ff09692b67e2ec125d5123a255badb9d&req=dSUkF898lYRfXvMW1HO4zTSmIr%2FpR2ZE1c%2Bmp5MuG2gzJ6YFDiIraGbJY8IT%0AAvBqaG%2Fq5ck%2FnhD49VU%3D%0A>
 
- 
Select Change Secret, then click Next
