# Offboard an Asana Account

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11346005-offboard-an-asana-account
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

When the Asana integration is connected to your Vanta instance, all user accounts from Asana are tracked on the Access page <https://app.vanta.com/access?credentialKey=asana>. An administrator must deprovision the account from their Asana workspace to trigger offboarding for one of these accounts rather than just removing them. This article will review the steps needed to trigger offboarding for an Asana account.
 

## Prerequisites
- 
Admin access to Asana workspace/team
 

## Procedure
- 
Go to your Asana workspace
- 
Navigate to your Team Settings, then Edit Teams settings
- 
Select the Members tab
- 
Click Remove next to the user account you would like to remove
- 
Once successfully removed, select deprovision
 

## Keep in Mind
If you removed the user account without de-provisioning it, you must re-add the user's account and follow the instructions above.
