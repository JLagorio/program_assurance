What should I expect from Vanta on social media?
- 
We only use the official handles listed above to communicate news, announcements, and updates about Vanta. We’ll never go into the specifics of your account over social media.
- 
If a social media handle reaches out to you asking for these details or claiming that they work for Vanta, please do not engage. Never share your account login details over social media. Instead, ignore the message and report the incident to [email protected].
