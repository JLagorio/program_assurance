## Configuring Tag Settings
Trust Center tags help you organize and segment your content by product, business line, deployment type, or any category that makes sense for your team. You can apply tags across all major Trust Center content types, including compliance frameworks, controls, FAQs, resources, updates, and subprocessors. This ensures visitors only see the content that is relevant to them and improves filtering and chatbot responses.
- 
Create your tags <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345444> from your account settings page and apply tags to resources  <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345456>accordingly.
- 
In your account header, click the Settings icon.
In the Settings page menu, scroll to the Features section, select Customer trust, and go to the Tags tab.
- 
In the Show on Trust Center field, select the relevant tag categories you want to be shown on your Trust Center for filtering.
 

## Tag Content in the Trust Center
To tag controls
Go to Trust Center, select Edit, then open Controls. Click the pencil icon, then select Edit controls. Choose the controls you want to update. Open the three dot menu and add or remove tags within a category.

## Grant Access to Resources by Tags
- 
Go to the Customer Trust > Overview page and click Give Access.
- 
Enter the recipients, access expiration, and NDA requirement.
- 
For resources, click Select resources to share and select tags based on what you want to share.
- 
Users can remove individual resources even if tied to the selected tags
- 
Optionally, you can select additional resources that may not be tied to the selected tags.
- 
Click Give Access, and the recipients will receive a request via e-mail.

-
