## Article text

Feature availability: This article discusses Team-based ownership, which may require an upgrade or add-on. Refer to Vanta Plans and Pricing <https://www.vanta.com/pricing?utm_source=help_center&utm_campaign=hc_pricing_cta> for details.
 
In Vanta, you can create teams to manage test and document ownership more efficiently. Before creating a team, ensure that the users you want to add already have an account in Vanta.
 

## Before You Begin
- 
To be a team member, users must have the Collaborator role or above <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345590>. To create users in Vanta, go to the user permissions page <https://app.vanta.com/settings/users>.
- 
From this page, you can upgrade existing Employee users (which solely appear on the People page) to the Collaborator role or above, or add new users.
- 
Learn more about how to create users, here <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345385>.
- 
Once users are added, you can proceed with creating teams.

## Option 3: SCIM
If you’re in the Scale package, you can use SCIM to sync groups in your identity provider to Teams in Vanta, allowing you to automate team creation, membership, and deletion. Visit this article <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345647> on how to get started setting up SCIM in Vanta.

## Notification Preferences
In addition to setting a destination, Team Admins can now manage which types of notifications their team receives as a part of our Growth, Scale, Enterprise packages.. From the Team Settings tab, you can toggle the following on or off:
- 
Notify members when they've been added to the team or their membership status has change
- 
Notify the team when it’s been assigned ownership of an item
- 
Notify the team when an item it's assigned to needs attention
- 
Notify the team when a task it owns is completed

Keeping your teams updated ensures the right people have access and receive notifications where they need them.
 
Using Vanta teams, you can simplify test ownership, improve organization, and ensure compliance efforts run smoothly.

What are the notification options?
At least one notification destination must be enabled, but you can select multiple options. The default setting is that all team members will be cc’ed on an email thread. Additional options include:
- 
Send notifications to a team email
- 
Use an email alias such as [email protected] or [email protected]
- 
Send notifications to a Slack channel (if Slack is integrated)
- 
If you haven’t integrated Slack yet and wish to, learn more here. <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345744>
- 
Send notifications to a Microsoft Teams channel (if Microsoft Teams is integrated)
- 
If you haven’t integrated Microsoft Teams yet and wish to, learn more here. <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345745>
These options allow teams to stay informed in the best way for them.
 
In addition to choosing where notifications are sent, Team Admins can now configure which types of notifications the team receives from the new Notification Preferences section in Team Settings.
