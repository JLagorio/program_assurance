## Article text

Details for individual users on the People Page <https://app.vanta.com/people/people> can be populated from different sources, below is a breakdown of where this information comes from and how to confirm.
 

## Creating user profiles on the People Page
Currently, there are two ways to create users on the People page <https://app.vanta.com/people/people>. The first is manually creating the user from the People page ( the article here walks through that process <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345379> ). The second is through a connected Identity Provider (Google Workspace, Okta, Office365, etc.).
 

## Validating Where Information is Pulling From
To see how a person was made and to determine where information like Start/End dates, emails, or job titles are pulled from, you can do so by clicking on a user's profile on the people page, then hovering over the 'i' icon on the card that appears on the right-hand side of the page:
