# Update Trust Center Logo

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345873-update-trust-center-logo
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Keeping your company logo up-to-date on your Vanta Trust Center is a crucial element for maintaining brand consistency.
Please Note: Updating your company logo here will only affect your Vanta Trust Center. 
 

## Update Trust Center Logo
- 
Select the gear icon in the top right corner, you will be taken to the Company Settings page
​
[image 01]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516757258/e3512bfdd10b3d1dfaac9e181277/28297431363604?expires=1794967200&signature=d07f9413122a30ce5ead54068d3bed87a0d2d577df8472e6c4181fa6e0a01428&req=dSUmEM57moNaUfMW1HO4zdgKCPy4A3EkTFuzKNeQ%2BmGRlG2yRh8jzFvrEPGd%0AaQfu5cwFKHJg3MPpAUs%3D%0A>
- 
Select Information and scroll down to Company logo
​
[image 02]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1752796042/3545b8f0457d4c7962ebdd800046/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=e645878877e8f975fb9db92c44547a1e5aa26f4bccf3ee700bfeb61788b3de8d&req=dSciFM53m4FbW%2FMW1HO4zTjti5Ql1Oli4YYHG7pPyHOhcFFjjBFWUogiUhgr%0AIPYO%0A>
- 
Select Upload to import the correct logo file
- 
Note: You can upload files up to 1 MB
- 
After the file is uploaded, ensure the Update Information button is selected to save this change.
 

## Confirm Logo Update on Trust Center
- 
Select Overview under the Customer Trust tab in the left-hand navigation panel.
- 
Select the View button in the top right corner.
[image 03]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2113119148/5e329b2db940485e77aa17d1da56/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=72e30fa47d9d06850686425143438ab2fd2997ad25246ef25085aafbd6afa0cb&req=diEmFch%2FlIBbUfMW1HO4zQLFz0iAutp4UkcAyZ3olXzozxuGLutFGzsermwB%0ABoyigZB4m%2BXJiB6FM70%3D%0A>
- 
The new logo is ready for use on your Trust Center.
