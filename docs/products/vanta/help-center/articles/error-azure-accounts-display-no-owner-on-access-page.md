# Error: Azure accounts display 'No Owner' on access page

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345894-error-azure-accounts-display-no-owner-on-access-page
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

## Environment Details
Azure accounts are displayed as 'No Owner' on the access page and cannot be reassigned.
 

## Cause
Vanta cannot pull in the owner assignment for Azure accounts.
 

## Resolution
Unfortunately, this limitation cannot be resolved at the moment. However, our team is continuing to work on improvements in this area.
