# Adding Custom Notes to Policies

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345491-adding-custom-notes-to-policies
Collection: Policies
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Adding custom notes to a policy in Vanta helps teams capture important context, clarify intent, and ensure the policy reflects how things work in your organization. While Vanta’s templates provide a strong starting point, custom notes let you layer in practical details, like tool-specific instructions, team ownership, or company-specific exceptions. This is especially helpful for aligning policies with real-world processes, helping auditors understand how a control is implemented, or guiding internal teams responsible for following the policy. In short, custom notes make your policies more useful, more actionable, and more aligned with your day-to-day operations.
 

## Adding Custom Notes to Policies
- 
From the left-hand navigation panel, select Policies 
- 
Open the policy you would like to add a custom note to
- 
Add a note to provide additional context to Vanta users
