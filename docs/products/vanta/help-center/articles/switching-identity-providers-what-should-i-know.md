## Article text

We suggest contacting support <https://help.vanta.com/hc/en-us/requests/new> before you make any changes to your Identity Provider (IdP).
 

## Changing Identity Providers
- 
Confirm that the new Identity Provider <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345653> you will use is one of Vanta's current integration options <https://app.vanta.com/integrations?state=available&servicecategory=IDENTITY_PROVIDER>
- 
The switch will be more manageable if the employee emails are identical from the original IdP to the new IdP. This means that the structure of the email domain should be the same—i.e., [email protected] or [email protected]. 
If the emails differ, Vanta may have difficulty with the matching logic, which would lead to issues. Please reach out to Vanta Support before starting the connection process.
 
If the emails are identical, follow these steps:
- 
Do not delete the original integration
- 
Navigate to the Integrations page and select the Available tab <https://app.vanta.com/integrations?servicecategory=IDENTITY_PROVIDER&state=available>
- 
Search for the new Identity Provider you want to connect and click the Connect button
- 
Follow the prompts to connect the integration to Vanta
