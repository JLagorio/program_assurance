# Migrating HRIS Connection Type in Vanta

## Article text

To migrate your connection type in Vanta, please follow the below steps
- 
Select Integrations from the left-hand navigation panel and locate your Human Resources credential
- 
Click Reconnect
- 
Please do NOT delete your credentials, as you may lose data associated with your HRIS

- 
Follow the instructions shown in the modal for your particular integration
If you're migrating a Zenefits connection with a username and password, please delete any service users that may have been created, if any. The service user's email is [email protected]. 
If you're migrating a Paychex Flex connection that was connected with a username and password, please feel free to delete any Finch service users that may be created
If you're migrating a Justworks connection, please feel free to delete any Finch service users that may be created.
If you're migrating a Workday connection, please delete the integration system user you created.
If you're migrating a Bob HR connection, please delete any Finch service users and permission groups that may have been created.
