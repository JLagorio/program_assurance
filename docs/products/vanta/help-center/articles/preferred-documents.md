# Preferred Documents

Collection: Evidence Collection ; Evidence Collection › Documents
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Customers can opt into their auditor's preferred requirements, removing any previous auditor's preferred requirements. Vanta has scoped down the required documents for your Seamless Auditors audit to lower the cost. The documents on the documents page, filtered by the audit Standard, will show you precisely what you need for your audit.
- 
Open the Documents page from the left-hand navigation panel
- 
Filter by framework
