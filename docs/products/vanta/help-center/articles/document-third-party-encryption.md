# Document Third-Party Encryption

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345994-document-third-party-encryption
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

If an employee cannot encrypt their device with the default encryption option on their device, such as BitLocker (for Windows) or FileVault (for Mac), it will need to be manually documented.
 

## Procedure
- 
Go to test Employee computer hard disk encryption <https://app.vanta.com/monitors?standard=soc2&status=OVERDUE&status=NO_DUE_DATE&status=DUE_SOON&taskType=Test#full-disk-encryption-records>
- 
Go to the computer/user who needs remediation
- 
Click Deactivate monitoring
