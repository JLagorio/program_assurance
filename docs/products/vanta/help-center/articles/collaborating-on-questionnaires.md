## Article text

Feature availability: 
- 
This article discusses Questionnaire Automation, which may require an upgrade or add-on. Refer to Vanta Plans and Pricing <https://www.vanta.com/pricing?utm_source=help_center&utm_campaign=hc_pricing_cta> for details.
- 
This article discusses Automations settings that are currently in Public Preview. Please contact your Customer Success Manager for access.
 
Vanta Questionnaire Automation provides various tools for your cross-functional teams to collaborate on questionnaires and quickly deliver accurate answers.
 
 

## Inviting your team
- 
Vanta has pre-defined permissions to assign to team members to assist you in the platform.
- 
The Trust Admin and Trust Collaborator roles <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345385-assigning-user-roles-and-permissions> are specifically designed for team members only working on security reviews in the Trust Center and Questionnaire Automation and do not need access to the rest of Vanta. 
 

## Assigning questionnaires
A user can be assigned as the primary owner responsible for completing the questionnaire from start to finish.
 
[image 01]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2062788463/19231ac6bd8ecb7eaa859ca5ff4b/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=c3353dad4056e437ecce9ee25963598745e1501f9f7a820a7d1c658afa31d74c&req=diAhFM52lYVZWvMW1HO4zZdrGPTeRLt9QfEWJqTLdZV3njP2EOlrE1EnWW16%0AT2iDFkbQZPv8Tn5M1jc%3D%0A>
- 
Add new questionnaire by selecting +Add questionnaire. From the modal, add the necessary information, including the owner.
- 
Additionally, the owner can be changed anytime by clicking the owner avatar in the questionnaire table or within the questionnaire header.
- 
The owner will be notified of the assignment. 
 

## Assigning individual questions
Individual questions can be assigned to relevant team members to answer or review.
 
[image 02]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2062793872/f5f4b5b059686e4dd59fbebbc545/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=128e6a3d0fe42221225c499b756ca2d8b4573464b1bbcb98125d780ee3d38f21&req=diAhFM53nolYW%2FMW1HO4zcxUoTsjLRvG9Bfao4oH1DPChy6n%2BiBsIwRbhXZQ%0A9QZAg7TvQI5Lwn6oZ%2Fk%3D%0A>
- 
Open on the desired questionnaire.
- 
Locate the appropriate question and select the owner icon.
- 
From here, assign or update the owner.
- 
Admin, Editor, Trust Collaborator, or Trust Admin roles <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345385-assigning-user-roles-and-permissions> can be assigned specific questions. 
- 
Use the toolbar above the question list to filter the questions.
 

## Answering assigned questions in Slack
If your organization has Slack notifications configured <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345744-setting-up-slack-notifications>, users who have been assigned questions will receive a notification in the Vanta Notifications Slack app and can answer without leaving Slack.
 
Assignments are batched into a single notification if questions are assigned within the same 15-minute window.
 
[image 03]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2304334750/9353e8c4033c30fbaf1705cb4cbd/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=703f8d712f59592046c7c4b15f9eb83ed15d125dcce8ff2c7745e22e6f214b88&req=diMnEsp9mYZaWfMW1HO4zXSDWGtPc9CkzUZOElTcPebCYUkVhe89Qkxq9i9c%0ASzdxyRZKXnEaSeIkWxU%3D%0A>
- 
Select Add Answer to open the response form:
- 
Click Close to exit without saving.
- 
Click Save & next to save the response as a draft and move to the next question.
- 
Check Mark answer as approved to mark that individual response as approved—depending on your user role <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345590-understanding-user-roles-and-permissions-in-vanta#h_b0c17a5501>, you might not be able to approve responses.
- 
File attachments are not supported in Slack. To upload evidence for a question,  open the questionnaire in Vanta.
 

## Commenting on individual questions
[image 04]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2144307113/8922d4511c6169c649e802dd16d3/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=8a88fd533c3948452fd2c802b8b25afdee101a51a54c6318d9bc017c3ad67b25&req=diEjEsp%2BmoBeWvMW1HO4zQxlj6p4yImxTPDnC%2BRfJjlXZLOyfPNbxbAVvXgG%0AHv%2BFZx5mUyec7e8dgEU%3D%0A>
- 
Under each question, click to expand the comments section.
- 
Leave relevant comments and optionally @mention other team members to notify them of the comment.
- 
All users who have left a comment or been @mentioned on a question will receive notifications of new comments for that question.
- 
Configure notifications for Slack, email, or both in your account settings to update how and how often relevant users will receive notifications.
 

## Approval workflow
To streamline the approval process, different team members can be assigned to own and approve the questionnaire and track its status. 
 
This approach is particularly useful for enabling customer-facing teams to take an initial pass at the questionnaire before assigning it to a subject-matter expert for review, ensuring accuracy and completeness before it is returned to the customer.
 
[image 05]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2062826305/7f300005b85fc0e60bd51a0de004/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=f93772cb95de6806a865d8bc9814cecfbb6323bf21f7488043c5d2ca5c8db9b4&req=diAhFMF8m4JfXPMW1HO4zavsHsvWv50rdhdLufPVcRK7XC8KtdFicjvdXHZ2%0Af34G8mJHKyoj1NHIMhc%3D%0A>
 
[image 06]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2062830912/f32de38a43a929db54220d10957f/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=d1e1ea46df26923fa76af9c631d96e6e37ab286e21728a22d487674144f18332&req=diAhFMF9nYheW%2FMW1HO4zUFTBdlHx2m20ZJo%2BK4hpFpsZCyJIXzzWgS5PSLU%0Ask107LnAMMKvUFTRRX4%3D%0A>
- 
When ready, an approver can be assigned to the questionnaire. Only the Admin, Editor, and Trust Admin <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345385-assigning-user-roles-and-permissions> roles can be assigned as approver.
- 
When the approver has started the review, they can switch the questionnaire status to In review in the questionnaire header.
- 
The approver can then review all answers and approve each as they are complete.
- 
If the questionnaire needs more work, the questionnaire status can be changed back to In progress and an optional message.
- 
If the questionnaire is approved, the questionnaire status can be changed to Approved.
- 
For each status change, the owner and approver are notified. The user who made the status change will not be notified.
 

## Automations
You can use automations to reduce manual work in the questionnaire review process.
 
To enable automations:
- 
In your account header, click the Settings icon.
- 
Scroll to the Features section, select Customer trust, and go to the Question answering tab. 
[image 07]
 <https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/2144332452/8a263fea2178f1853f01b734499b/image.png?expires=1794967200&signature=6973da683d67cb55c842c9b50edc26f1c4b671807b5d90419deea8d42abd5177&req=diEjEsp9n4VaW%2FMW1HO4zUS7RFal%2FIETgy2A2T0XeMdlWvz0W4ekTMn6YPJ4%0AyABmR2cQT0y%2BrqEKXcg%3D%0A>
 | 
Setting | 
Description
 | 
Automatically approve answers that aren't flagged | 
- 
On: Answers from Vanta AI are automatically marked Approved.
- 
Off: Answers from Vanta AI are marked Draft and require manual approval.
- 
You must always manually review questions Vanta AI marked Flagged or Needs answer.
 | 
Automatically complete questionnaires when all responses are approved | 
- 
On: The questionnaire is automatically marked Complete when all questions are Approved.
- 
Off: You must manually mark the questionnaire as Complete.
