# Changing AWS Roles in Vanta

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345922-changing-aws-roles-in-vanta
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

This article details how to change your ARN in Vanta. Administrators must do this if they change the role Vanta uses in AWS. This article does not detail how to make a new role or how to get its ARN.
 

## Prerequisites
- 
Administrator Access to Vanta
 

## Procedure
- 
Sign in to Vanta and go to the Integrations page:
 
[image 01]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516760781/41280e5eb96a6e07cb3dd98bb0aa/16973861231252?expires=1794967200&signature=23f7c4c3c45ff13c57454f69523b0df61cdb58fc1ffb17824530c968899e6949&req=dSUmEM54nYZXWPMW1HO4zbl7zgt9m1zhFgBS6AQaAxidMJznhyk02gBIqJYt%0ARkXb2Uk9AuwLWma%2BHk0%3D%0A>
 
- 
Click Manage and select Edit on the AWS Integration
 
[image 02]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516760780/0942c7502447c137fd12d05c65f6/16973866692628?expires=1794967200&signature=3576768543975532f3908d144c9527e733f31f4ca9ddf378fb96fc1a4061a743&req=dSUmEM54nYZXWfMW1HO4zQwghxC0LAfuULXQrBCazpprcO2ghzcX6Cu0avPJ%0AFlCHoZFmSjeeMPEq%2FaY%3D%0A>
 
- 
Click Add a new account, then click Console, and then Next on the bottom right
 
[image 03]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516760782/9a92048bbaa1fd761817d658b683/16973945506708?expires=1794967200&signature=ddd8045b08ad082013292afc2b119d02482ce3c27a46f812f24d4884c56d7be3&req=dSUmEM54nYZXW%2FMW1HO4ze7DhemmUVM1an9gv2cz5sPJ1A0TsKGi9QJUTz1d%0ANyCDBa%2BxMUcmwGv6kNY%3D%0A>
 
- 
Click Role ARN under step 4, and on this page you can paste your new role ARN
 
[image 04]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516760784/f3a35df94fb033fc13fd4fe23429/16974050828308?expires=1794967200&signature=625c8927e1d84dc3611d8c20239fd5fcfbe09fae767e4e2ec11906bfa4b9e9b0&req=dSUmEM54nYZXXfMW1HO4zWaOBlCS2RyWfYaZGy%2BESzMJoG1XUeUqp6Qod0Ld%0A0KzcOXeFkRtNwy8xKaU%3D%0A>
 
- 
Once your new ARN is added, click next to go through region selection and configuring scope. Once complete, you will have successfully updated your ARN.
