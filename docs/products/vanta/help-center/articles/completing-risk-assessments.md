## Article text

✅ Feature availability: Risk assessments are included with Core Risk Management. Some features (including multi-step assessment approvals and risk customization) require Advanced Risk Management. Refer to Vanta Plans and Pricing <https://www.vanta.com/pricing?utm_source=help_center&utm_campaign=hc_pricing_cta> for details.
 
Completing a risk assessment means evaluating a risk scenario and getting that evaluation approved. Risk owners review the scenario’s details, score the risk, and document a treatment plan. Approvers then review the assessment and approve it or request changes. You can repeat this whenever your risks change, or turn on recurring risk assessments to repeat it on a set schedule.
 
⚙️ User permissions: Admins and Editors can create, manage, and approve all risks. Collaborators can edit and approve risk scenarios they own or manage. Learn more: User Permissions by Product Area <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345590-user-permissions-by-product-area> 
 
 

## Getting started
Before conducting assessments, make sure you’ve added risk scenarios <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/12005142-adding-and-managing-risks> and configured your risk settings <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345378-risk-management-product-overview#h_dec75fb7b8>.
 
Risk assessments are designed to be collaborative and move through a shared workflow:
- 
Assign risk owners: Assign risk scenarios to the right owners.
- 
Complete risk assessments: Owners score each scenario, document treatment plans, and track any actions needed to reduce the risk.
- 
Approve risk assessments: Owners submit assessments for approval, or approve them directly if they're also the assigned approver.
​
💡 Tip: Use the Comments tab to ask your teammates questions as you review and approve risks. You can @ mention the risk scenario owner and approver, as well as any Admins or Editors.
 
 

## Assigning owners to risk scenarios
Assign each risk scenario to the person responsible for completing the assessment and tracking any follow-up work. You can assign an owner directly from the risk register or within the risk scenario.

## Approval status
Each risk scenario has an Approval status that shows where its risk assessment is in the approval workflow:
 | 
Approval status | 
Description
 | 
Draft | 
The default status when a risk scenario is created. The risk assessment is missing required fields, like an owner, risk scores, or a treatment plan.
 | 
Needs review | 
The risk assessment has all required fields but hasn’t been submitted for approval, or it was returned because changes were requested or made after approval.
 | 
Pending approval | 
The risk assessment has been submitted and is waiting for approval. It is read-only until it is approved or you cancel the approval request.
 | 
Approved | 
The risk assessment has been approved by all required approvers.
 
⚠️ Note: Define risk tasks <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/15089319-tracking-risk-mitigation> before submitting risks for approval. If a risk is Pending approval, you can’t add new tasks. If a risk is Approved, adding a task moves it to Needs review.
 
 

## Completing risk assessments
As a risk owner, review each risk scenario assigned to you, complete the assessment, and submit it for approval.
  
For each risk scenario, you should:
- 
Confirm risk details: Review and update the description of the risk and ensure risk fields are filled out.
- 
Map risks: Link the risk to related controls, assets, and other objects across Vanta to add context before you assess it.
- 
Score risks: Select ratings based on your risk rubric to calculate each risk score you track.
- 
Document treatment plan: Choose a treatment type and track tasks needed to reduce the risk.
- 
Submit for approval: Select an approver and send the assessment for review and sign-off.

### Risk scoring
 
During a risk assessment, you rate each risk once for every risk score you track, using the same risk rubric. Your scoring definitions determine whether you track two risk scores or three, and your scoring method determines how you select the ratings:
- 
Simple scale scoring method: You select a rating for each dimension directly from a defined set of values.
- 
Aggregate factors scoring method: You select a rating for each factor, and those factor ratings roll up into the dimension rating.
​
📖 Learn more: Risk Scoring <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/15089318-risk-scoring-rubric>
 

### Choosing a treatment plan
 
Choose how you want to handle each risk by selecting a treatment type:
 | 
Treatment type | 
Description
 | 
Mitigate | 
Reduce the risk by implementing controls or completing tasks.
 | 
Accept | 
Acknowledge the risk and take no further action.
 | 
Transfer | 
Shift the risk to a third party, such as through insurance or outsourcing.
 | 
Avoid | 
Eliminate the risk by stopping the activity causing it.
 
💡 Tip: To track risk treatment <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/15089319-tracking-risk-mitigation>, manage tasks directly in Vanta or connect a task tracker integration to work in the tools your team already uses.
 

### Adding approval steps
 
Depending on your plan, you can assign one or more approvers to review and sign off on a risk assessment.
 
For multi-step approvals, you can add up to 5 approval steps with up to 3 approvers per step. All approvers in a step must approve before the next step begins.
 
To set up multi-step approvals:
- 
Click Submit for approval.
- 
Add approver(s) to Approval step 1.
- 
Click Add new approval step to add more steps and assign approvers to each.
- 
Submit the workflow.
Once submitted, the risk moves to Pending approval until all required approvers sign off. Approvers are notified by email, and in multi-step workflows, Step 1 approvers are notified first.
 
⚠️ Note: If you need to change approvers after submitting the workflow, cancel the current approval request and restart the workflow with the updated approvers.
 
 

## Approving risk assessments
Once an assessment is submitted, it becomes read-only and moves to Pending approval. You'll receive an email notification when a risk is assigned to you for approval.
 
You can access assessments pending your review from the My work page or by filtering the risk register by approver.
 
To approve a risk assessment:
- 
Open the risk scenario.
- 
Review the assessment details, including the risk scores, treatment plan, and any linked controls or tasks.
- 
Add comments if needed.
- 
Click Submit approval decision, then choose:
- 
Approve: Confirm the assessment is complete. The approval is saved to the assessment's approval history.
- 
Request changes: Send it back to the owner for updates and resubmission.
A risk is only marked Approved once all required approvers sign off. For multi-step workflows, you'll be notified when it's your turn—approvers are notified when the previous step is completed.
 
⚠️ Note: If the assessment is edited after approval, it moves back to Needs review and must go through approval again.
 
 

## Recurring risk assessments
When recurring risk assessments are turned on, owners complete a new risk assessment for each of their risk scenarios during every assessment window and submit them for approval before the due date. An Assessment status is added to each risk scenario indicating which risks need attention.
