# Adding Subprocessors to Vanta Trust Centers

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345937-adding-subprocessors-to-vanta-trust-centers
Collection: Optimizing your Workflow ; Optimizing your Workflow › Troubleshooting
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Article text

Adding subprocessors to your Vanta Trust Center allows you to provide transparency about the third parties you use to deliver your services. This can help build trust with your customers and streamline compliance efforts.
 

## Before You Begin
To get started, make sure your subprocessors are listed as vendors in Vanta. If they haven't been added yet, follow these steps <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345559> to include them in your vendor list. Once that's complete, you're ready to proceed.
 

## Steps to Add Subprocessors
- 
Select Trust Centers
- 
Click Edit
- 
Once in the edit mode, go to Subprocessors and click on the pencil icon

Click the + button to open a list of your vendors.

Check the boxes for all subprocessors you want to include in your Trust Center.
 
[image 03]
 <https://vanta-e0817867462a.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/t4a8cs4z/1516761790/6c692662941a3eb0a34d846c21ff/19440851168020?expires=1794967200&signature=278ba7a9423edd6d20dfb5b4335a92283cf5bdd60a0e4df9fb290d1860027d1d&req=dSUmEM54nIZWWfMW1HO4zY0Q1TWMuvax7huvMAQ%2FZHWbRzDmAp1suwzoMBg6%0A055OOs8E21JArK671SY%3D%0A>

Once you’ve selected the subprocessors, click Save to update your Trust Center.
 
Your subprocessors will now appear in your Trust Center, providing visibility and transparency for your stakeholders.
