## Article text

When using JumpCloud, Vanta will review the list of programs and browser extensions to determine the best antivirus solution. Below is the proper configuration for Vanta.
 
Vanta will recognize if one of our supported antivirus applications <https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11346109> is installed on the machine and listed in the Details section, followed by the Programs list.
