# My Work page

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11997605-my-work-page
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Purpose

- One prioritized list of the work that needs the signed-in person: items assigned to them or to their teams.
- Default landing page for the Collaborator role and custom roles; Admin and Editor land on Home and reach My Work from the side nav.

## Layout

- Left nav (this screenshot's version): Help (⌘+K); Home; My work (current); Roadmap with a "63%" pill; Tests; Reports; Compliance expanded (Frameworks, Controls, Policies, Documents, Audits, Issues with a "Preview" badge, Settings); Customer trust, Risk, Vendors, Privacy (collapsed groups); a collapse arrow (←|) at the foot.
- Page header: title "My Work"; subtitle "View all work items that need your attention".
- Quick filter chips in one row: All (selected), Assigned to me, Assigned to my teams, Needs my approval; a "Type" dropdown at the row's end.
- Groups, each a collapsible heading with an icon and its count: "Urgent (31)" (red calendar icon), "Coming soon (362)" (orange calendar-clock icon).
- Rows under a group: a kind icon (test list or document clipboard), an action sentence with a count, optional grey status meta, and assignee avatars at the end.
- "Show all" under each group's first five rows.
- Type dropdown (open): a Search field over checkboxes: Access request, Access review, Access review system, Control, Document, Policy, Questionnaire, Risk scenario, Risk task.

## How you get here / where it leads

- → Collaborator and custom roles land here on login.
- → Admin and Editor: My work in the left nav.
- → A row opens "a task specific view": for example "three documents that need remediation" opens the Documents page filtered to the reader's assignments.
- → Documents page, Tests page, Policies and access reviews (inferred from the row wording; the article names only the Documents case).

## Actions

- Quick filters: All, Assigned to me, Assigned to my teams, Needs my approval.
- Filter by Type (multi-select with search).
- Collapse a group; Show all.
- Row verbs seen: Resolve, Update, Review, Finish setup of.

## States and numbers

- Groups (article): Urgent = overdue or needs remediation; Coming soon = approaching due date; Unscheduled = no due date; Waiting on others = waiting on someone else's action.
- Urgent (31) rows: Resolve 2 overdue failing tests; Resolve 4 failing tests; Update 1 overdue documents (1 Draft); Update 4 documents that need remediation (2 Draft • 2 Not started); Review 9 policies pending your approval (9 Pending approval).
- Coming soon (362) rows: Review 1 documents pending your approval; Review 5 access requests pending your approval; Review 5 access requests pending provisioning; Finish setup of 348 access reviews; Review 2 policies pending your approval (2 Pending approval).
- One row bundles several records of one kind and state into one sentence with a count (inferred from the rows).
- Status meta words: Draft, Not started, Pending approval.
- Counts differ from Home's "Open Program Tasks": My Work holds only the reader's items; Open Program Tasks holds all items across the program; My Work also holds some items Open Program Tasks lacks (documents that need remediation).

## Vocabulary

- My Work, work items, tasks, assignments.
- Urgent, Coming soon, Unscheduled, Waiting on others.
- Assigned to me, Assigned to my teams, Needs my approval.
- Program elements (owned or approved by you or your teams), Vanta tasks (linked to risks or issues), personal security & compliance tasks (onboarding: policy adherence, installing a device monitor), pending items (policies awaiting approval by others).
- Team (Growth package and above).
- Open Program Tasks.

## For Program Assurance

- My work's Attention could group by urgency (overdue, due soon, unscheduled, waiting on others) with a count on each heading, rather than by record kind.
- One row per kind-and-state as a verb sentence ("Review 9 evidence versions waiting on you", "Resolve 4 overdue POA&M milestones"), opening the register already asked for exactly those records.
- Role decides the landing page: assessors and ISSOs land on their work, administrators on the Portfolio.
