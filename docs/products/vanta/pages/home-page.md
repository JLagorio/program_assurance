# Home page

Source: https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11345372-home-page
Fetched: 2026-11-17

## Purpose

- The "at-a-glance view" of the security and compliance program: what actions are needed, how the business tracks toward its selected frameworks, and shifts in security position over time.
- A launch pad: its cards open other pages "with filtered views to take immediate action".

## Layout

- Global top bar: Vanta mark at the start; help (?), announcements (megaphone), settings (gear) and the account avatar at the end.
- Left nav: Help search (⌘+K); Home (current), Tests, Reports; a divider; Compliance, Customer Trust, Risk, Vendors, Assets, Personnel (each a group with a chevron); a divider; Integrations.
- Page header: title "Home"; one outlined button at the end, "View tasks" (eye icon).
- Alert banner (warning triangle) across the page: "We've detected a connection error with one or more of your integrations. Please go to the Integrations page for more details." with a "View details" button at its end.
- Section "Compliance progress", with "Last updated less than a minute ago" at the heading's end.
- Three framework cards side by side, each with a chevron: name, a large percentage, a progress bar, "N controls complete" at the start and "N total" at the end.
- Link under the cards: "View all frameworks →".
- Section "Monitoring" (heading only in the screenshot; the article: progress and items needing attention across compliance and risk workloads).
- Section "Trust Centre" (article only): recent engagement with the Trust Centre homepage and document downloads.

## How you get here / where it leads

- → Home is the default landing page for Admin and Editor roles (from the My Work article).
- → Frameworks page: "View all frameworks" (the article calls it "View All Frameworks").
- → A framework's details: each Compliance progress card is clickable (chevron) (inferred from the chevron and "click the cards to navigate").
- → The page behind a Monitoring card, filtered to what needs attention: "You can click the cards to navigate to the respective pages where actions can be taken."
- → Trust Centre page: "View Trust Centre page" (view and edit Trust Page settings, approve access requests).
- → Integrations page: the banner's "View details" (inferred from the banner text).
- → Tasks: "View tasks" shows security items with an SLA defined in Vanta that are outstanding or due soon.

## Actions

- View tasks.
- View details (integration error banner).
- View all frameworks.
- View Trust Centre page.
- Hover a Monitoring card's tooltip for a breakdown of items needing attention by category.
- Click a card to navigate.

- SOC 2: 18%, 14 controls complete, 78 total (14/78 = 17.9%).
- ISO 27001:2022: 17%, 21 controls complete, 125 total (21/125 = 16.8%).
- GDPR: 13%, 10 controls complete, 76 total (10/76 = 13.2%).
- The percentage is controls complete over total controls, rounded (inferred from the three cards).
- At most three frameworks in progress are shown.
- Freshness stamp: "Last updated less than a minute ago".
- Integration health shown as a page-level warning banner.

## Vocabulary

- Compliance progress, Monitoring, Trust Centre (Trust Center in settings).
- Framework, controls complete, total.
- Tasks, SLA, outstanding, due soon.

## For Program Assurance

- The Portfolio could open with one card per program's control baseline in this shape: a big percentage, a bar, "N controls satisfied" against "N total", the card opening the program's Control status.
- Reference-data or integration health belongs in one page-level banner with a single action, not scattered through widgets.
- A header action that leads to the reader's own due work (here "View tasks") makes the dashboard a launch pad; every card opens a register already filtered to what it counts.
