# Security Questionnaire Automation (SQA) Beta  Sunset Notice

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/9554898-security-questionnaire-automation-sqa-beta-sunset-notice
Collection: Understand the Drata Platform › Vendors
Updated: 2026-11-14
Fetched: 2026-11-22
Description: Security Questionnaire Automation (SQA) Beta will officially sunset on April 30, 2026.

## Article text

To avoid any disruptions to your questionnaire workflow, please finish any active questionnaires and download any desired content before **April 30, 2026**.

With Drata’s acquisition of SafeBase, we recommend transitioning to Automated Questionnaire Assistance (AIQA).

---

## What This Means for You

After **April 30, 2026**:

- The SQA Beta will no longer be accessible.
- You will not be able to access or retrieve data from the SQA platform so should export anything prior to April 30, 2026.

To retain access to your historical data, you must download your content prior to April 30, 2026.

---

## Transitioning to AI Questionnaire Assistance (AIQA) in SafeBase

If you haven’t already, we recommend transitioning to **AIQA in SafeBase** as soon as possible to avoid any disruption. Learn more about AIQA [here](https://help.safebase.io/en/articles/13608146-ai-questionnaire-aiqa-video-overview)!

AIQA builds on what worked in SQA and takes it further:

- A more powerful, scalable assurance experience
- More advanced functionality than SQA
- Customers see 80% reduction in time spent on inbound questionnaires
- Continuous improvements and feature development

Unlike SQA, AIQA is fully integrated within SafeBase and will continue to receive active product enhancements.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I access SQA after the sunset date?

No. All access will be permanently removed and inaccessible/unrecoverable

### Will AIQA replace all SQA functionality?

AIQA provides enhanced automation capabilities but operates differently within SafeBase.

### Who do I contact for help?

You can reach out to the SafeBase support team at [support@safebase.io](mailto:support@safebase.io) or access in application help by using the speech bubble in the lower right corner when logged into the platform.
