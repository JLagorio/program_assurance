## Article text

Welcome! We're excited to work with you and your company. Customers drive everything we do, and we work hard to make your experience better. We know that many of you were involved in purchasing Drata, but even more of you are interacting with the platform and our team for the first time. So, below, we’ll provide background on what we do, what to expect, who our team is, what resources we offer, and how you can provide us feedback. Let’s dive in!

## What to expect

Drata is a compliance automation platform. We automate your compliance journey from start to audit-ready and beyond. More importantly, we support companies at every stage of GRC maturity from startups just getting started to enterprise companies with established teams and practices. We bring more efficiency to the process.

Not only does Drata provide standard GRC functionality, but we also have multiple avenues to automate evidence collection that fulfill the standards that are most important for your industry or current journey. We connect to your cloud providers, running tests on a daily basis to uncover any gaps in your configurations in accordance with best practices. We also recognize, no platform can automate 100% of any compliance framework, so we deploy streamlined processes for collecting and updating manual evidence as well. In short, we ease the process and create a centralized view of your compliance posture.

## Our team…and now yours!

From day one at Drata, another part of our ethos has been to support you in your journey. You have an entire team behind the scenes to guide you along the way.

### Customer Education

The Customer Education team delivers high-quality learning resources to help you get the most out of Drata. These resources include:

- Technical documentation
- Video-based tutorials
- Product guidance designed to keep you informed and confident as you use the platform

Help Center’s [library of articles](https://help.drata.com/en/) (accessible by selecting the “?” in Drata) is searchable and can help answer common questions you may have.

You can also learn how to best [navigate the help center](navigate-the-drata-help-center.md).

### Technical Support

For technical questions or troubleshooting, our Technical Support team is available through in-app chat to help with:

- Resolving technical issues
- Answering platform-related questions
- Ensuring uninterrupted use of Drata

Select "Live" in the upper right-hand corner of the platform when you are logged into app.drata.com to speak to our Technical Support team or Compliance Advisors as you navigate through the platform. Learn more at [Drata Support](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/13163488-get-support-from-drata).

## Resources

While we have a team of experts to support you, we also know that having resources that allow you to navigate through Drata on your own time, independently are key to your success as well.

Below, we outline a few of our most utilized resources.

### Drata Academy

Note: You must be logged into Drata to access our Drata Academy

Drata Academy is a self-serve training resource for Drata customers designed to help you learn the platform, onboard faster, and build confidence across key workflows. With Drata Academy, customers can learn at their own pace, explore core areas of Drata, stay current on new features and workflows, accelerate onboarding and implementation, and train teammates more efficiently as usage grows.

To learn more, go to **[Getting Started with the Drata Academy.](getting-started-with-the-drata-academy.md)**
