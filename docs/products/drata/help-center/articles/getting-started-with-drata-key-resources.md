## Article text

- Help Center’s [library of articles](https://help.drata.com/en/) (accessible by clicking the “?” in your Drata instance) is searchable and can help answer common questions you may have.

- Join our LIVE Getting Started with Drata Onboarding sessions [here](https://events.zoom.us/ev/AoCvPnibxu-FEhIc_Ray7qpL1zFAtAnPOrXRAY-syyrK9vF2Rdjz~AuXDv8GgYDVEdMDeqvwL7Fyqg3RpkWE55s7nHYt3LyGl6g4ldWNh3nYSjg)
- **Attend our [live training sessions](https://drata.com/customer-webinars)** for interactive, expert-led walkthroughs designed to accelerate your compliance journey.
- New to security and compliance? Check out some of our helpful educational [blog posts](https://drata.com/blog) like [SOC 2 Compliance: A Beginner's Guide](https://drata.com/blog/beginners-guide-to-soc-2-compliance) and [ISO 27001 Checklist: 8 Easy Steps to Get Started](https://drata.com/blog/iso-27001-checklist) to get you familiar with what to expect and how to get started.
- Learn about which system connections are available within Drata and how to get started [here](https://help.drata.com/en/collections/2653772-integrations).

- Learn more about continuous monitoring and why it’s important for your business here: [https://drata.com/blog/moving-to-a-proactive-state-of-compliance](https://drata.com/blog/moving-to-a-proactive-state-of-compliance).
- Watch a [brief tutorial](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/8488381-5-tips-for-getting-started-on-your-drata-policies) from our Manager of Compliance Advisory Services, Rick Stevenson, to guide you through exactly where you should start with your company policies.
