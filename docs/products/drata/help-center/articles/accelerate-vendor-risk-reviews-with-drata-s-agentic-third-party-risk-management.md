## Article text

Drata's agentic TPRM helps your team manage the full vendor risk lifecycle in one place, from vendor intake and inherent risk assessment to evidence collection, residual risk reviews, targeted follow-ups, and final approval. The agent applies your standards consistently, traces findings to source documents, records every action, and syncs decisions back to your procurement tools, so you can assess more vendors with greater speed, coverage, and confidence.
