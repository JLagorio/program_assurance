# Test 83: MSAs Offered to Customers

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4778733-test-83-msas-offered-to-customers
Collection: Monitoring Test Guidance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22
Description: Drata inspects your company records to determine if there is a sample Master Service Agreement (MSA) in place.

## Article text

**ASSOCIATED DRATA CONTROL**

This test is part of the **Commitments Explained to Customers **control that ensures security commitments are communicated to external users, as appropriate.

**WHAT TO DO IF A TEST FAILS**

If Drata is unable to locate a template Master Service Agreement (MSA) the test will fail. To remediate a failed test you will need to upload a Master Service Agreement template to the company document section.

**STEPS TO REMEDIATE**

1. Navigate to Settings > Company Info page.
2. Add a 'Master Service Agreement' document
