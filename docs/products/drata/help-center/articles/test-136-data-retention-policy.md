# Test 136: Data Retention Policy

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4778884-test-136-data-retention-policy
Collection: Monitoring Test Guidance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22
Description: Drata inspects your records to determine if a valid, approved Data Deletion Policy is in place with a data retention period specified.

## Article text

**ASSOCIATED DRATA CONTROL**

This test is part of the **Data Retention **control that ensures your company establishes written policies related to retention periods for the confidential information it maintains.

**WHAT TO DO IF A TEST FAILS**

If Drata finds that a Data Deletion policy either does not exist or has not been approved within the last 12 months the test will fail.

To remediate a failed test, you will need to either upload or build a Data Deletion policy within Drata or notify the owner to click 'Approve Policy' as soon as possible.
