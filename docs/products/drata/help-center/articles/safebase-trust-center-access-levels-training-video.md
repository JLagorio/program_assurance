## Article text

This video explains how to control who can view Trust Center content and when access is granted. It covers the item-level access settings Full, Restricted, and Hidden, and shows how to configure them in the Trust Center Editor. You’ll also see how Preview Mode and organization-level Public Mode Access settings affect the experience for public users, approved users, and specific permission profiles. By combining these settings, teams can make Trust Center content easy to navigate while protecting sensitive information.

**Key takeaways:**

- Understand how Full, Restricted, and Hidden access levels control visibility for different Trust Center items.
- Configure item-level access settings and use Preview Mode to review different user experiences.
- Use Public Mode Access settings and permission profiles to control what public and approved users can see.
