## Article text

**This article is meant to provide examples of evidence for the ‘Non Monitored’ Microsoft SSPA Controls in Drata. For each Control, you’ll find one or more examples of evidence to upload.**

**NOTE: This document should not be interpreted as legal advice. When supplying evidence for Controls, you should always have your legal team review the evidence to ensure that documents are appropriate for your organization’s specific facts and circumstances. In addition, we cannot guarantee that the recommendations provided below will meet specific requirements of the Microsoft SSPA. *When uploading evidence, avoid including unnecessary personal data or other highly sensitive information. Where possible, use redacted documents, limited samples, or exports that exclude full identifiers, and consult with your legal team before sharing HR or customer records.***
