## Article text

This video explains how to configure the branding and URL settings that shape the Trust Center’s public-facing experience. You’ll learn how to manage logos, favicon, Open Graph images, banners, colors, fonts, titles, footers, and legal links. It also covers branded email notifications and custom URL setup, including DNS records and domain validation. Together, these settings help create a polished, branded Trust Center for external users.

**Key takeaways:**

- Customize the Trust Center’s appearance, theme, title, banner, footer, and legal links.
- Configure branded customer emails with a company logo, reply address, and required DNS records.
- Set up a custom URL using a verified domain, add the required CNAME record, and validate the configuration.
