## Article text

Drata AI is designed to help customers accelerate compliance workflows, reduce manual work, and respond faster to trust and security requests — while maintaining strong privacy, security, and responsible AI practices.

This article provides transparency into how Drata AI works, what data it uses, and how customer information is protected.

## What is Drata AI?

Drata AI provides assistive features within the Drata platform that help teams summarize vendor security documents, extract key compliance insights, and accelerate security questionnaire workflows.

## Does Drata Train AI Models Using Customer Data?

No. Drata does not currently train or fine-tune AI models using customer data.

## Is Customer Data Isolated Across Tenants?

Yes. Drata AI does not pull or mix information across customer accounts. Each tenant’s data remains logically separated.

## Can Customers Opt Out of Drata AI?

Yes. Customers can opt out anytime within Drata by accessing Settings → AI Settings.

## How Does Drata Handle Customer Feedback?

Drata AI includes thumbs up/down feedback and logs edits or accepted suggestions to improve outputs over time.

## Responsible AI Practices

Drata is committed to building AI responsibly. Learn more here: [https://drata.com/blog/our-ai-philosophy](https://drata.com/blog/our-ai-philosophy)

## Subprocessors Used for Drata AI

Drata uses authorized subprocessors. More information: [https://drata.com/sub-processors](https://drata.com/sub-processors)

## Frequently Asked Questions

- Does Drata AI use my data to train models? No.
- Can Drata AI access other customers’ data? No.
- Can I disable Drata AI? Yes.
- Is Drata building its own ChatGPT? No.
