Description: In this help article, learn and discover the functionalities available to the Trust Center manager roles within Drata.

## Article text

## Read-Only permissions

If enabled, Trust Center managers can only view the ***Requests*** tab. They cannot modify data or perform certain actions.

## Editor tab

Edit the appearance and add content to your Trust Center to showcase your security posture to current customers and prospects. Learn about the [Editor](https://help.drata.com/en/collections/3492068-trust-center).

## Requests tab

View and manage access requests for documents from your Trust Center. Learn about the [Requests](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/8067477-submitting-and-approving-requests).

## Insights tab

View and analyze usage data and download reports relating to how customers and prospects interact with your Trust Center. Learn about [Insights](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/10192920-trust-center-analytics-dashboard).

## Settings tab

Configure NDA requirements and other settings that control access and functionality of your Trust Center.
