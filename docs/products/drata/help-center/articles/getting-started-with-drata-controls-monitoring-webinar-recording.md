## Article text

In this webinar recording, we connect the dots between frameworks, controls, and continuous monitoring so you can see exactly how Drata structures your compliance program end-to-end.

We begin by explaining the **relationship between frameworks and controls**, including how common requirements map across SOC 2, ISO 27001, and other standards. Then we dive into **types of controls** (monitored vs. not monitored) and the **types of evidence collection** Drata supports—from automated integrations to manual uploads. The session closes with a live demo of the **Frameworks**, **Controls**, and **Monitoring** pages in Drata to show how these concepts come together in the product.

Use this recording to better understand how to manage, track, and continuously validate your controls using Drata.

**Key takeaways: **

- Learn how frameworks, controls, and monitoring connect within Drata’s structure.
- Understand different control types and evidence collection methods Drata supports.
- See how the Frameworks, Controls, and Monitoring pages power continuous compliance.
