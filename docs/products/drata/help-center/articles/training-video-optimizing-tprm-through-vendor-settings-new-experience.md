## Article text

Use this training video to learn how to configure vendor types, customize risk-level labels, and review the thresholds and logic that shape inherent-risk classification. You’ll also learn how to upload a custom risk-classification file or edit classifications manually to align recommendations with your organization’s risk framework. These settings help power AI-generated inherent-risk recommendations, which your team should review and validate.

**Key Takeaways**

- Organize your vendor portfolio using vendor types.
- Customize risk-level labels to match your organization’s risk framework.
- Configure inherent-risk classifications using the default settings, a custom file, or manual edits.
