# Test 58: New Hire Contracts

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4778696-test-58-new-hire-contracts
Collection: Monitoring Test Guidance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22
Description: Drata inspects your company records to determine if there is a sample new hire contract.

## Article text

**ASSOCIATED DRATA CONTROL**

This test is part of the **Formal Recruiting Process **control that ensures your company requires all new hires and/or internal transfers to go through an official recruiting process during which their qualifications and experience are screened to ensure that they are competent and capable of fulfilling their responsibilities.

**WHAT TO DO IF A TEST FAILS**

If Drata is unable to locate a sample new hire contract the test will fail. To remediate a failed test, you will need to upload a sample new hire contract to Drata.

**STEPS TO REMEDIATE**

1. Navigate to the Human Resources page (account-settings/human-resources)
2. Add a 'Sample Employment Agreement'
