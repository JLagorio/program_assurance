# Test 85: Terms of Use Publicly Available

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4778743-test-85-terms-of-use-publicly-available
Collection: Monitoring Test Guidance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22
Description: Drata inspects your company records to determine if a URL to its public Terms of Service has been provided.

## Article text

**ASSOCIATED DRATA CONTROL**

This test is part of the **Maintains a Terms of Service **control that ensures your company maintains a Terms of Service that is available to all external users and internal employees. It is important to note that the Terms of Service must include detail around the company's security and availability commitments regarding the systems.

In cases where a Terms of Service may not apply, a Client Agreement or Master Service Agreement must be in place.

**WHAT TO DO IF A TEST FAILS**

If Drata is unable to locate a URL to a public facing Terms of Service the test will fail. To remediate a failed test you will need to navigate Settings > Company Info in Drata and insert a URL to the publicly-facing Terms of Service.

**STEPS TO REMEDIATE**

1. Go to **Settings → Company Info** in Drata.
2. In the **Terms of Use URL** field, enter the link to your company’s Terms of Use.
3. Select **Update **to save your changes.
