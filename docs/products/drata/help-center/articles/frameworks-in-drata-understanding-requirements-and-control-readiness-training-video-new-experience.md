## Article text

This video introduces compliance frameworks and explains how frameworks, requirements, and controls work together inside Drata. You’ll learn how to navigate the Frameworks page, understand readiness calculations, and manage requirement scope to support your audit goals.

We’ll walk through the framework hierarchy — where frameworks define requirements and controls satisfy those requirements — and demonstrate how readiness is determined based on in-scope controls or requirements. You’ll also see how to filter, bulk update, and manage requirement mappings to keep your compliance program organized and audit-ready.

Key Takeaways:

- **Understand the hierarchy:** Learn how frameworks define requirements, and how controls satisfy those requirements to demonstrate compliance.
- **Manage framework readiness:** See how readiness is calculated by controls or requirements and how scope impacts overall status.
- **Organize and take action efficiently:** Use filters, bulk actions, and control mappings to manage requirements and streamline audit preparation.
