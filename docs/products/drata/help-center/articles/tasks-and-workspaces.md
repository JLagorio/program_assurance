Description: Learn how Drata tasks work across workspaces, including workspace-specific, company-wide, and role-based visibility.

## Article text

If your account uses **Workspaces**, tasks may behave differently depending on where they are created. Some tasks are tied to a single workspace, while others apply across all workspaces.

## Workspace-specific tasks

- Tasks created in one workspace appear only in that workspace’s task timeline.
- Evidence and reports are tied to the workspace where they are uploaded.

## Company-wide tasks

Some tasks are shared across all workspaces because they are tied to company-wide objects:

- **Policies**
- **Vendors**

## Role-based visibility

- Only **Workspace Managers** can view, create, and filter tasks within their assigned workspace.
- **Risk tasks** can only be created and managed in the **primary workspace**.
