## Article text

This video series introduces Policy Center, showing how to create and manage policies with templates, publish finalized versions, and configure approval workflows to streamline reviews and ensure accountability.

## Policies Overview

This video offers a high-level overview of the Policy Center, demonstrating how to effectively build and manage security policies.

Key Takeaways:

- **Create policies your way:** Use built-in templates or upload existing documentation to get started quickly.
- **Streamline approvals:** Configure customizable approval workflows to align with your internal review process.
- **Finalize and publish with confidence:** Publish policies and manage personnel acknowledgements to support compliance requirements.

[Video (arcade): https://app.arcade.software/share/1SWfQIb7VpiV4m1T8jRV](https://app.arcade.software/share/1SWfQIb7VpiV4m1T8jRV)

## Author a Custom Policy

In this video, we’ll walk through the process of creating a custom policy while keeping your control mappings intact.

Key Takeaways:

- **Create custom policies:** Learn how to build a policy from scratch in Drata.
- **Replace or expand templates:** Understand when to replace standard templates or upload external policies.
- **Maintain control mappings:** Ensure controls remain properly mapped when authoring custom policies.
