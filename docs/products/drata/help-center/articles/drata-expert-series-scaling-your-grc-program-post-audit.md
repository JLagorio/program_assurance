## Article text

This webinar recording features Niko Kountroubis, GRC Manager at Syllable, sharing guidance on how to strengthen and scale your GRC program after completing an audit. Drawing on experience across the Big 4, Eden Data, and Syllable, Niko explains how to turn post-audit insights into sustained operational improvements.

You’ll learn actionable strategies to drive continuous improvement, streamline processes in Drata, and strengthen your compliance posture year-over-year—empowering your team to save time, scale responsibly, and stay audit-ready at every stage.

This webinar recording covers how to:

- Understand auditor expectations in subsequent audit cycles
- Turn post-audit insights into a stronger compliance foundation
- Use Drata to simplify policy, vendor, and risk management
- Apply real-world strategies for scaling a GRC program effectively
