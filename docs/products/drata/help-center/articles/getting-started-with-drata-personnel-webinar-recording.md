## Article text

In this webinar recording, we walk through the key components that help you operationalize personnel-related requirements and show how these map directly into the **Personnel** page in Drata.

We start by breaking down the core elements of personnel compliance—**policies**, **background checks**, **device compliance**, and **security awareness training**—and how they work together to support a strong security program.

From there, we jump into the product to demonstrate how Drata centralizes these requirements so you can quickly see which team members are missing critical steps, where follow-up is needed, and how this data flows into your controls and evidence for audit readiness.

Use this recording to understand what “good” looks like for personnel compliance and how to monitor it efficiently in Drata.

**Key Takeaways:**

- Understand the main components of personnel compliance and why they matter.
- See how Drata’s Personnel page tracks policy acknowledgement, background checks, devices, and training.
- Learn how personnel data in Drata supports audit readiness and ongoing monitoring.
