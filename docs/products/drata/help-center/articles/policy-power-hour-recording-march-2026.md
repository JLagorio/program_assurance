## Article text

Whether you’re new to policy management or looking to refine your existing policies, this webinar recording will provide practical guidance and best practices to get your security policies audit-ready.

In this webinar, our Compliance Advisors cover:

- How to approach policy drafting in a way that works across any framework.
- Proven best practices to create clear, effective, and compliant policies.
- Answers to the most common policy drafting questions from customers.
- Practical strategies to start from scratch or refine existing policies with confidence.

During the Q&A portion of the recording, our Compliance Advisors also share insight on following:

- How often policies should be reviewed
- Who should approve policies
- How to incorporate requirements into policies
- Policy length and detail best practices
- Common terminology
- Recommended policy acknowledgement grouping
- Importing policies to Drata
- Policy version control
- Multi-step policy approval
