## Article text

This video explains how to integrate an Okta tenant with Drata by creating a scoped user group, preserving administrator access, configuring a read-only service account and API token, and adding the Drata OIDC app. It then covers entering the required credentials in **Connections** to enable SSO, sync users to **Personnel**, and begin automated compliance checks, including MFA enrollment monitoring.
