## Article text

*If you don’t see Risk insights, your organization may not be using **Risk Management Pro** or you might not have access to the risk module. Contact your Drata admin to confirm your permissions.*

This video provides an in-depth walkthrough of the **Risk Insights** dashboard, a powerful tool for visualizing and reporting on your organization's risk landscape. You’ll learn how to apply granular filters—such as risk category, owner, and type—to focus your data on what matters most.

Learn how to interpret key visualizations, including **Assessment Progress**, **Risk Posture**, and the **Risk Heatmap**, while understanding how to toggle between **Inherent and Residual risk** to showcase the effectiveness of your mitigation efforts. This video also demonstrates how to export these insights into professional formats for board decks, executive reports, and audit packages.

### Key Takeaways

- **Visualize Posture Trends:** Risk Insights allows you to visualize how your risk posture is changing over time.
- **Identify Concentrations:** Gain a clear understanding of where risk is concentrated across your various registers and categories.
- **Streamline Reporting:** Easily export professional charts and PDF snapshots for executive reports and stakeholder updates.
