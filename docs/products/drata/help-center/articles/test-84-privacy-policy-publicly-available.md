# Test 84: Privacy Policy Publicly Available

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4778740-test-84-privacy-policy-publicly-available
Collection: Monitoring Test Guidance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22
Description: Drata inspects your company records to determine if a URL to its public Privacy Policy has been provided.

## Article text

**ASSOCIATED DRATA CONTROL**

This test is part of the **Maintains a Privacy Policy **control that ensures your company maintains a Privacy Policy that is available to all external users and internal employees. It is important to note that the Privacy Policy must include detail around company confidentiality and privacy commitments.

**WHAT TO DO IF A TEST FAILS**

If Drata is unable to locate a URL to a public facing Privacy Policy the test will fail. To remediate a failed test you will need to navigate to Settings > Company Info page in Drata and insert a URL to the publicly-facing Privacy Policy.

**STEPS TO REMEDIATE**

1. Go to **Settings → Company Info** in Drata.
2. In the **Privacy Policy URL** field, enter the link to your company’s Privacy Policy.
3. Click **Update** to save your changes.
