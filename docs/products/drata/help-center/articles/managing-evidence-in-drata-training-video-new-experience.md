## Article text

The Evidence page is your centralized hub for all compliance evidence in Drata. In this video, you’ll learn how manual and test evidence are collected, stored, and linked to controls to support audit readiness.

Key Takeaways

- **Centralize compliance evidence:** Understand how manual and automated test evidence is collected and organized.
- **Manage and link artifacts:** Learn how to assess evidence requirements and add artifacts to remediate controls (DCFs).
- **Stay audit-ready:** Track evidence renewals and maintain up-to-date documentation to support continuous compliance.
