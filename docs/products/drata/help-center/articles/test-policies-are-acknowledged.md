# Test: Policies are Acknowledged

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4776941-test-policies-are-acknowledged
Collection: Monitoring Test Guidance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22
Description: Drata inspects your company security policy records to determine if all employees have acknowledged them.

## Article text

**ASSOCIATED DRATA CONTROL**

This test is part of the **Security Policies **control that ensures your company has approved all policies, and that all employees acknowledge these procedures when hired. This control also checks to make sure that Management has reviewed and approved these policies and that they are made accessible to all employees and contractors.

**WHAT TO DO IF A TEST FAILS**

If Drata finds that there are any policies which have not been acknowledged by all employees the test will fail. With a failed test you will receive a list of personnel that have not acknowledged all policies assigned to them.

To remediate a failed test, you will need to send an email reminder to your personnel asking that they acknowledge all policies assigned to them.
