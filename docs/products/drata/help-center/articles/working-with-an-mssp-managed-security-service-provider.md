Hundreds of Drata customers use Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs) in tandem with the Drata platform to maintain robust security measures and recurring compliance requirements across their IT environments. We’ve curated a network of MSSPs who have experience building world-class security and compliance programs for companies like yours, backed by the power of Drata.

## What’s an MSSP?

Because cybersecurity programs are complex and ever-evolving, the role of MSSPs is to help protect businesses from security threats, whether that means providing software and services or building a network of security experts who can respond to attacks as they happen. The services that an MSSP offers, **like penetration testing, vCISO, and incident response services**, can be tailored to an organization’s specific needs and scale as the company grows.

## Key Benefits

### 🔑 Security Expertise at Your Fingertips

Building and maintaining a security strategy while your business scales is

complicated, ever-changing, and requires many different skill sets. With the right MSSP, you’ll have access to world-class expertise on everything from security and compliance to data privacy at a competitive price.

### 🎉 Effortless Compliance Management

While Drata seamlessly integrates with your existing systems to automate

much of the compliance process, you can pass the remaining efforts to your MSSP partner to ensure that compliance tasks are getting completed and are being documented on time, every time.

### 🕵️ Continuous Monitoring

With Drata’s real-time monitoring capabilities, potential threats and

vulnerabilities are proactively identified; MSSPs are trained to remediate

those threats quickly and work with your team members to mitigate

future risks.

### ⭐ Streamlined Audits

Drata simplifies audits by generating accurate and up-to-date compliance reports at the click of a button. With an MSSP, those pesky audit interviews and evidence collection exercises are no longer your sole responsibility.

Your MSSP will handle your entire audit lifecycle before, during, and in-between audits.
