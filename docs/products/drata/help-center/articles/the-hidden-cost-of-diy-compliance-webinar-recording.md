## Article text

This webinar recording explores the realities of managing compliance in-house, the true cost of long-term compliance, and when outsourcing can help organizations scale more effectively. Drata teams up with Trava Security to share practical guidance for building and sustaining a successful GRC program.

Key takeaways:

- Understand the expectations, responsibilities, and tradeoffs of managing compliance internally versus outsourcing.
- Plan for long-term costs, identify key budget considerations and how to manage compliance as an ongoing program.
- Scale with the right support and understand when Compliance-as-a-Service (CaaS) makes sense.
