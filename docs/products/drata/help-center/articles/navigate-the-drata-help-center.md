## Welcome to the New Drata Help Center

The redesigned Drata Help Center provides fast, intuitive access to all your compliance support needs—from step-by-step guides and best practices to troubleshooting help. Whether you're just getting started with Drata or scaling your governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) program, the Help Center is your central resource for success.

### Drata Help Center gets a new look

We’ve updated the Drata Help Center with a new layout and navigation experience to help you find information more easily.

You’ll now see:

- A persistent sidebar for browsing collections and articles
- Breadcrumbs and a cleaner reading layout
- Improved search with collection filters and keyboard shortcuts

**This update affects only the Help Center layout and navigation**. Your article links, Fin AI experience, ticket management, and support workflows remain unchanged.

## How the Help Center is organized

Articles are grouped into **collections** that follow your journey in Drata — from initial setup, through day-to-day use, to audit prep.

| **Collection** | **Use this when you want to...** |
|---|---|
| [Getting Started](https://help.drata.com/en/collections/16396644-getting-started) | Configure account settings, assign user roles, and set up workspaces |
| [Understand the Drata Platform](https://help.drata.com/en/collections/16396695-understand-the-drata-platform) | Learn how each Drata feature works (Connections, Controls, Monitoring, Evidence, Policies, Personnel, Risk, Vendors, Trust Center, and more) |
| [Framework Information](https://help.drata.com/en/collections/2997147-framework-information) | Understand the compliance frameworks Drata supports |
| [Connection Support](https://help.drata.com/en/collections/16397047-connection-support) | Set up or troubleshoot integrations and use the Drata API |
| [Policy Guidance](https://help.drata.com/en/collections/16397053-policies) | Get expert guidance on Drata's policy templates |

## Help us improve

At the bottom of every article, you'll see three reaction buttons: **😞 😐 😃**.

When you select **😞**, the chat opens and asks what was missing or unclear. Your input goes directly to the technical writing team and shapes future updates. If something is missing, outdated, or hard to follow, please let us know.

## Designed for Self-Service

The new Drata Help Center was built with you in mind to provide quick access to product information, how-to guidance, and troubleshooting resources. Explore the collections, use the search bar, and take advantage of our best-practice content to get the most out of your Drata experience.

Start exploring today — your compliance confidence starts here.
