## Article text

This video provides a guided, high-level overview of the Drata platform and explains how the major sections work together to help organizations build, manage, and monitor a strong compliance program. It covers foundational concepts like frameworks, requirements, and controls, then walks through setup, governance, controls, monitoring, evidence, risk, vendors, audits, and the dashboard so viewers can understand at a high-level how to move from implementation to ongoing audit readiness in Drata.

**Key takeaways:**

- **Understand how frameworks, requirements, and controls** relate to each other in Drata.
- **Learn how key platform areas support setup**, governance, evidence collection, and risk management.
- **See how Drata helps teams progress** from initial configuration to audit readiness.
