# Personnel: who should be accounted for in terms of your audit?

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4793409-personnel-who-should-be-accounted-for-in-terms-of-your-audit
Collection: Personnel Management › Personnel Compliance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22

## Article text

A common question when it comes to audit readiness is which personnel should be included?

Any contractor or employee that will have access to customer data and/or is responsible for developing, monitoring, engineering or implementing your platform is in scope for the audit.
