# Test 49: Contractors Acknowledge the Acceptable Use Policy

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4776953-test-49-contractors-acknowledge-the-acceptable-use-policy
Collection: Monitoring Test Guidance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22
Description: Drata inspects your company records to determine if the Acceptable Use Policy has been acknowledged by all contractors.

## Article text

**ASSOCIATED DRATA CONTROL**

This test is part of the **Contractor Requirements **control that ensures your company requires all contractors to read and acknowledge the Code of Conduct, the Acceptable Use Policy, and pass a background check.

**WHAT TO DO IF A TEST FAILS**

If Drata finds contractors that have not acknowledged the Acceptable Use Policy the test will fail. With a failed test, you will receive a list of contractors that have not acknowledged the Acceptable Use Policy.

To remediate a failed test, you will need to advise these contractors to acknowledge the Acceptable Use Policy.
