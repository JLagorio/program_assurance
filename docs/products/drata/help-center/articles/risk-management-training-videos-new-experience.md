Description: Master risk management in Drata with our three-part training video series.

## Article text

This video series provides a comprehensive guide to managing the end-to-end risk lifecycle within Drata. You will learn how to navigate the risk library, evaluate threats using a quantitative scoring matrix, and document treatment plans to satisfy key compliance requirements. By following this three-part workflow, you can move from initial identification to audit-ready reporting.

Key Takeaways:

- **Build Your Register Fast:** Leverage Drata’s library of over 200 pre-built risk scenarios to quickly identify threats specific to your organization.
- **Standardize Your Scoring:** Use a consistent scoring matrix to calculate inherent and residual risk, allowing for data-driven prioritization.
- **Address Key Controls:** Satisfy DCF-16 and DCF-17 by exporting and uploading finalized Risk Assessment and Treatment reports.

## Part 1: Identifying Risks

In the first video, we explore the foundations of risk management and risk identification. You’ll learn how to use Drata’s library of over 200 pre-built risk scenarios to populate your register and how to use the risk table to assign owners, categories, and perform bulk actions.

[Video (arcade): https://app.arcade.software/share/L89l71XrzrlfCGvj4Il2](https://app.arcade.software/share/L89l71XrzrlfCGvj4Il2)

## Part 2: Scoring Risks

The second video dives into the quantitative analysis of your register. We demonstrate how to assess risks based on Impact and Likelihood using a customizable scoring matrix to calculate an inherent risk score, ensuring your most critical threats get the attention they deserve.

[Video (arcade): https://app.arcade.software/share/cuPU5WJrK01fJLt6gg1m](https://app.arcade.software/share/cuPU5WJrK01fJLt6gg1m)

## Part 3: Treating Risks

The final video covers the four primary risk treatment options: Mitigate, Accept, Transfer, and Avoid. We walk through documenting remediation plans, mapping mitigating controls, and exporting the final reports needed to satisfy DCF-16 and DCF-17.

[Video (arcade): https://app.arcade.software/share/YjZR5jLaheW56XNI492a](https://app.arcade.software/share/YjZR5jLaheW56XNI492a)
