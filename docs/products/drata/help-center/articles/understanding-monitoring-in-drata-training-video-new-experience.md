## Monitoring Overview

Monitoring in Drata provides real-time visibility into the status of your monitored controls (DCFs). This video walks through how monitoring works, how to interpret test results, and how to remediate failed tests to maintain continuous compliance and audit readiness across monitored controls (DCFs).

You’ll learn how Drata’s daily autopilot sync collects evidence, how to investigate test findings, apply exclusions, and re-run tests.

Key Takeaways:

- **Navigate monitoring page:** Understand the test table and filtering options.
- **Assess and remediate failures:** Review findings and corrective actions to resolve failed tests.
- **Manage tests efficiently:** Use filters, bulk actions, exclusions, and re-run tests to maintain 100% passing monitored controls.
