# Does Drata Have a Privacy Policy Template?

Collection: Policy Guidance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22

## Article text

**Background:**

A Privacy Policy is a legal document that outlines how a company or organization collects, uses, and protects personal information from its customers or users. It is a requirement in many countries for businesses or organizations that deal with personal data to have a Privacy Policy in place.

**What is the Privacy Policy for?**

A Privacy Policy is used to keep users informed on how their personal data will be used, who it will be shared with, and what measures are in place to protect their privacy and personal information. The document usually contains details on the types of data being collected such as name, email address, phone number, etc. as well as how the data will be used and whether it will be shared with third parties. The document should also outline how users can control their personal data.

**Does Drata have a Privacy Policy Template?**

***Drata does not provide a template for a Privacy Policy as this is a legal document.*** We highly advise all our customers to consult their legal team in creating/building this policy.
