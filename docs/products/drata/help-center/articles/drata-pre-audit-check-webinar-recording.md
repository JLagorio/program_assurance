## Article text

This webinar led by our Compliance Advisors and the Drata team provides a practical walkthrough of what to review, prepare, and finalize in Drata to ensure a smooth and successful audit.

In this video, we cover the following:

- Making sure evidence is set for controls - monitored and not
- Reviewing the Personnel section for status and key compliance attributes
- Reviewing Policy Center for approvals, versions and assignment
- Ensuring Vendors are set
- Navigating Audit Hub

We also recommend reviewing our [Pre-Audit Checklist](pre-audit-checklist-how-to-prepare-for-your-audit-with-drata.md).
