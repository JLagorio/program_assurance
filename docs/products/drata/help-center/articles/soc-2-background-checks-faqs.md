# SOC 2 Background Checks FAQs

## Article text

What type of background checks are required for SOC 2?

- At a minimum, a criminal background check should be performed for all new hires as a condition of employment; however, this is not an explicit SOC 2 requirement to do so, but rather a best practice.
- While SOC 2 does not mandate background checks, they demonstrate a commitment to maintaining a trusted and secure operational environment, which auditors look for during compliance reviews.
- What needs to be checked by the criminal background check is up to each individual organization to determine. SOC 2 requirements do not specify the type of checks that need to be performed.
- Performing background checks not only aligns with auditors’ expectations but also serves to demonstrate due diligence and strengthen security controls, which enhances trust with clients and stakeholders.

Do I need to perform background checks on existing employees to meet SOC2 requirements?

- As a best practice, we recommend that all employees and contractors complete a background check.
- However, since the control for background checks is only specific to new hires and new contractors, your SOC2 auditor will most likely only check to make sure that new hires and new contractors have a background check completed.

Do I need to perform background checks on contractors?

- If you are hiring contractors as 1099 employees, we recommend performing background checks. If you do not want to perform background checks on contractors, we recommend talking with your SOC 2 auditor to determine if contractors are in scope under the background check control.
- If you are hiring contractors from a third party organization, we recommend ensuring that the third party organization is performing background checks for the contractors working with your organization.
- Clearly outline the scope of compliance obligations for contractors based on their role and access levels. For example, define whether contractors have access to sensitive systems or data.
- Use robust tools or policies to manage exclusions, such as grouping contractors into compliance categories and ensuring regular audits for those exempted from background checks.

What do I do if country-specific laws prohibit me from performing background checks?

- In this situation, SOC 2 would still require that you evaluate and consider the new hires’ background and technical competency prior to hiring them. You can manually upload evidence of how you evaluated the new hires’ background and technical competency for each employee that falls into this category.
- Maintain an audit trail demonstrating the rationale for exemptions and documentation of evaluations conducted for compliance. This ensures transparency and defensibility during audits.

## Best Practices for Compliance Measures

- Conduct risk assessments for all personnel, including contractors, to identify necessary compliance actions.
- Implement robust identity and access management policies, such as role-based access controls, to limit access to sensitive data.
- Keep detailed documentation of compliance processes, decisions, and periodic audit outcomes to maintain transparency.
