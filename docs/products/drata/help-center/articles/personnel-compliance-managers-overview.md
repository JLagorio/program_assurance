## Article text

In this help article, learn and discover the functionalities available to personnel compliance manager roles within Drata.

### Personnel compliance managers access

The side navigation of the Drata app is divided into several sections, each containing pages you can access based on your role.

Note: If your role has the read-only permissions enabled, you can only view, filter, and download the data on the pages available to the role. You cannot modify the data or do certain actions available on that page.

- Governance: Personnel page
- Settings
- Help Menu

### Governance

Personnel compliance managers can access the following pages under the Governance section on the navigation bar:

- Personnel page: Edit personnel details and take actions related to personnel. To learn more, go to [Personnel Overview](https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4785655-personnel-overview).

### Settings

The Settings page can be found as an option under the user menu. Personnel compliance managers have access to 'My Settings' and Human Resources page under 'Company Settings'.

### Help Menu

Select the **Help** icon (**?**) in the top right corner, or choose **Help & Feedback** from the navigation menu after selecting your user account.

The **Help & Feedback** drawer includes:

- Explore our collection of hundreds of articles.
- Preview upcoming features, submit ideas, and explore new features.
- Grant Drata Support temporary access to your environment when requested through an official support case. Access is automatically revoked on the date you select.
