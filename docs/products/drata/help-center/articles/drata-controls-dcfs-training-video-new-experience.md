## Article text

Controls (DCFs) are the backbone of your compliance program in Drata. This video walks through how controls function, how to navigate the Controls page, and how to manage monitored and not monitored controls to bring them to a ready state.

You’ll learn how to review control status, interpret monitoring results, manage evidence, configure approvals, and understand why a control may be marked as not ready.

Key Takeaways:

- **Understand control types:** Learn the difference between monitored (automated) and not monitored (manual) controls and how each reaches a ready state.
- **Review and remediate controls:** Identify failed monitoring tests, missing evidence, unpublished policies, or outstanding approvals.
- **Manage mappings and workflows:** Update framework mappings, associate risks, configure approvals, and use the action panel to quickly resolve issues.
