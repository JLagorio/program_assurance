## Article text

This training video introduces Drata’s Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) module, designed to help you identify, evaluate, and monitor third-party vendors effectively. You will learn how to navigate the Current vendors page, add vendors to Drata, document vendor attributes, and conduct inherent risk assessments to determine the depth of security reviews needed.

**Key Takeaways:**

- **Lifecycle Management:** Learn to identify, evaluate, and monitor vendors to maintain a secure "chain of trust."
- **Impact Assessments:** Understand how to define vendor risk levels to automate the depth and frequency of security reviews.
- **Compliance Automation:** Discover how to set up recurring reviews and automated reminders to satisfy framework requirements like SOC 2 and ISO 27001.
