# How to Allowlist Drata Email Domains and IP Addresses

## Article text

### Overview

To ensure reliable delivery of important product communications and transactional messages, you may need to add Drata’s sending domain and IP addresses to your email server’s allowlist. This helps prevent emails from being marked as spam or rejected by your system.

### Steps

1. Open your email server or gateway configuration settings.
2. Locate the allowlist or approved senders section.
3. Add the following domain to your allowlist:
   - `drata.com`
4. Add the following IP addresses to your allowlist:
   - `50.31.205.111`
   - `50.31.205.112`
5. Save and apply your changes according to your system’s instructions.
