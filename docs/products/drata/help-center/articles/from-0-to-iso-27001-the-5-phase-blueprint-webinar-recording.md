## Article text

Join Drata’s Compliance Advisors in this one-hour recorded session to turn the complex ISO 27001 certification process into a clear, actionable journey. Whether you're starting from scratch or expanding an existing program, this session provides a practical, 5-phase blueprint based on real-world audit experience.

In this webinar recording, you will learn the 5-phase blueprint to:

- Establish your ISMS foundation and secure "Day 1" wins.
- Strategically define your ISMS scope, stakeholders, and objectives.
- Conduct risk assessments, manage treatment, and create your Statement of Applicability (SoA).
- Use internal audits as a "practice run" to find and fix gaps.
- Confidently navigate the final certification audit and demonstrate compliance.
