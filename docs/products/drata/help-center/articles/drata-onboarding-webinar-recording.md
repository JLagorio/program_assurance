## Article text

Whether you're new to the platform or looking to expand your expertise, this 60-minute webinar recording will provide valuable insights into Drata's key features and GRC best practices.

**This webinar recording covers the following: **

- How to navigate the Drata Quick Start Guide
- How to leverage the Frameworks, Controls and Monitoring sections
- How to use, edit and archive out-of-the-box policies and create your own custom policies
- How to plan for next steps after onboarding to maximize your GRC program with Drata
