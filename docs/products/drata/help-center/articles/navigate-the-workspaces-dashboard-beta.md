Description: View compliance health metrics for all the workspaces that you manage within a single account.

## Article text

With Workspace Dashboard, you can manage and understand how each unit or workspace is performing. At a glance, view how many products have frameworks that have requirements or controls that are not satisfied or what is the progress and performance of each unit regarding their compliance journey.

## BEFORE DIVING IN

- You must have workspaces enabled in your account.
- You must be an Admin or an Information security lead.
- We have not changed any scoping or filtering for the accounts listed per guest administrator. Each existing guest administrator should see the same business accounts that they manage within the domain of their MIM account

## Workspace level metrics

Note: The data displayed is gathered hourly. At the top of the page, we display a banner that showcases the time the data was gathered.

With the new workspace dashboard, you can view an overview of key compliance metrics to ensure that the various business units are on track. To access the dashboard, go to your **Dashboard **page, and then select the **All workspaces **tab.

You can view the following metrics:

- **Non-ready controls**: The number of controls that are mapped to a framework within that workspace.
- **Control Readiness**: Calculation of the total readiness of in-scope controls within a given workspace.
- **Tests passing**: The number of passing tests within the given workspace.
- **Tests failing**: The number of failing tests within the given workspace.
   - This does not include tests with errors.
- **Frameworks: **Displays all the frameworks and the total readiness for each framework that the workspace has.

Learn how to use Workspaces to manage multiple products in Drata, with shared operations and separate compliance needs: [Workspaces](https://help.drata.com/en/collections/16396685-workspaces).
