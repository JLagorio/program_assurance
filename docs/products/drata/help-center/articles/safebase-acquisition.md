## General Questions

### What does the acquisition of SafeBase mean for me as a customer?

Drata and SafeBase are coming together to build a seamless ecosystem that integrates trust, governance, risk, and compliance.

With this acquisition, we are uniting to create a best-in-class Trust Management Platform. There will be no disruption to your service—rather, in the coming months, you’ll have the opportunity to explore the Trust Center and AI Questionnaire Assistance (AIQA) products by SafeBase to determine if a migration or upgrade is right for you.

This acquisition brings an even more enhanced experience. Together, we will continue to support:

- **Proactive Trust Building:** Real-time, dynamic trust centers to enhance transparency and streamline self-serve security reviews.
- **Faster Security Reviews: **AI-powered automation to accelerate questionnaire responses and close deals.
- **Effortless Compliance: **Advanced automation to scale compliance and simplify audits.
- **Enhanced Vendor Risk Management:** Greater efficiency and continuous visibility across third-party vendors and suppliers.
- **Scalable GRC Programs:** Meeting enterprise needs as they grow and modernize their programs.

In the coming months, we'll share more details about how the integration of SafeBase will enhance your Drata experience and if you choose, how you can migrate over to their Trust Center and/or AIQA products. Rest assured, your current services will remain uninterrupted. Any changes or new options will be introduced seamlessly, ensuring a smooth and effortless transition.

### Where can I go to find out more?

Your customer point of contact remains the same, and we’ll be in touch via email with more details. Be sure to [sign up](http://drata.com/migration) to receive updates on upgrading to the SafeBase Trust Center and AI Questionnaire Assistance products as soon as details become available.

### How long will it take for me to find details about a migration?

Our team is working hard to make your migration to SafeBase as seamless as possible! We expect to share more details on the next steps within the next 90 days.

## Billing Questions

### Will my existing contracts or terms change?

All valid contracts, terms, and pricing remain in place and are not impacted at this time.

### Are my points of contact for sales, service, and billing and payments the same?

Yes, there are no changes to your contacts at this time.

## Product Questions

### Will there be service disruptions?

We are committed to a smooth transition, and do not anticipate any disruptions in service.

### Do I still have access to the same products and services?

At this time, Trust Center Pro and Essential are not being discontinued. However, you will have an opportunity in the coming months to migrate to an upgraded version of Trust Center and/or AI Questionnaire Assistance with SafeBase. [Sign up](http://drata.com/migration) to be the first to receive updates

### What improvements can I look forward to in the next few months?

We’re incredibly excited about the improvements ahead, including more enhancements to how you’re utilizing Trust Center and deeper AI automation with security questionnaires. Right now, our top priority is developing a seamless integration in addition to ensuring  an effortless transition for those who choose to move to SafeBase for their Trust Center or AI Questionnaire Assistance.

[Sign up](http://drata.com/migration) to stay updated and be the first to receive details on our migration plan.

### How much effort will it take from me to upgrade to Safebase by Drata?

Ensuring a seamless transition to SafeBase is our top priority. We’re actively working on a streamlined migration process to make it easy for you to move your Trust Center to SafeBase. More details will be available soon—[sign up](http://drata.com/migration) to stay updated and be the first to receive migration plan details.

### What if I don't want to use SafeBase for my Trust Center?

Not a problem, if you don’t want to use SafeBase for your Trust Center, you can continue to use Drata’s Trust Center with no disruption to your service at this time. Once we have a seamless way to migrate, we will be encouraging customers to take advantage of SafeBase capabilities as they will give you an even more enhanced experience.

### Will Drata’s Trust Center be Discontinued?

There are no immediate plans to sunset Drata's Trust Center. We are working on an integrated product experience and will communicate any migration plans well in advance. [Sign up here](http://drata.com/migration) to be the first to know about migrating your Trust Center over to SafeBase.

### How much longer will I have access to Drata's SQA Beta?

If you have been participating in Drata’s Security Questionnaire Automaton beta, we are appreciative of all of your continued feedback! We are working on next steps for beta customers. We will share more options on how you can upgrade to SafeBase AI Questionnaire Automation if you would like to choose that route. Please make sure to [sign up](http://drata.com/migration) to stay updated and be the first to receive upgrade plan details.

### What are the benefits of moving over to a SafeBase AIQA Product?

If you decide to migrate to the SafeBase AI Questionnaire Assistance Product in the future, your Security Questionnaire Responses will be even more streamlined with the ability to use a Chrome extension and integrations with Slack, Teams, and Jira to efficiently complete even the most tedious and lengthy security questionnaires.

### What are the benefits of moving over to a SafeBase Trust Center?

If you decide to migrate to the SafeBase Trust Center Product in the future, you'll gain access to expanded features, including:

- **Streamlined CRM & Revenue Integration:** Sync buyer activities, automate Trust Center access, and track security review impact on ARR and sales pipeline in Salesforce and HubSpot.
- **Unlock Actionable Security & Revenue Insights: **Track buyer engagement, prioritize deals, measure Trust Center impact, and benchmark performance against industry peers—all in one powerful dashboard.
- **Configure Your Trust Center: **Easily configure your Trust Center your unique needs with Webhooks, Multi-Language and Mult-Product support.

[Sign up here](http://drata.com/migration) to be the first to learn more on how to migrate over.
