# Test 137: Data Classification Policy

Source: https://help.drata.com/en/articles/4777011-test-137-data-classification-policy
Collection: Monitoring Test Guidance
Updated: 2026-08-05
Fetched: 2026-11-22
Description: Drata inspects your company records to determine if a Data Classification Policy is in place and currently valid.

## Article text

**ASSOCIATED DRATA CONTROL**

This test is part of the **Data Classification **control that ensures your company has established a data classification policy in order to identify the types of confidential information possessed by the entity and types of protection that are required.

**WHAT TO DO IF A TEST FAILS**

If Drata finds that a Data Classification policy either does not exist or has not been approved within the last 12 months the test will fail.

To remediate a failed test, you will need to either upload or build a Data Classification policy within Drata or notify the owner to click 'Approve Policy' as soon as possible.
