# Drata's look, beside Vanta's, for Ledger

Researched 2026-11-22 from 56 Drata screenshots in this folder: help-centre article images, sorted by area under `images/ux/` and listed in `images/index.json`. Colours were read from compressed screenshots, some by sampling a 3×3 patch of pixels, so every hex below is marked (est.). Sizes are CSS pixels estimated from retina captures, also (est.). Image paths are relative to this folder. The images are third-party and stay on this machine; nothing here copies Drata's copy beyond short control labels.

**Two eras show in the set.** The older screens, from 2024 and 2025 and still in many articles, have a dark navy side nav ≈ #1D2A3F (est.) with white text and uppercase group labels, a geometric typeface and a duller blue primary ≈ #295BA2 (est.) [images/ux/frameworks/pci-dss-v4-0--01.png, images/ux/frameworks/a-guide-to-drata-s-fedramp-readiness-framework--03.png, images/ux/evidence/drata-evidence-library-sync--02.png]. The current screens, which the articles call the "new experience" (2026), have a white shell, sentence-case group labels, a neo-grotesque face and a brighter blue-to-indigo primary. Everything below describes the current screens unless it says otherwise.

## 1. Drata's visual language

### The shell and navigation

- The chrome is white and the page sits in an inset grey well ≈ #F5F6F7 to #F7F8FB (est.), with a 1px border ≈ #E3E4E6 (est.) and corners of ≈ 12px, held a few pixels clear of the nav and the top bar. The edge of the well is the only divider, and there is no hairline under the top bar. [images/ux/audits/audits-page-overview-new-experience--01.png, images/ux/audits/audits-page-overview-new-experience--02.png, images/ux/events/events-overview--01.png]
- The top bar is ≈ 64px tall. From the left: a black wordmark in spaced capitals, two small icon controls (collapse the nav, expand every group), a vertical divider, and a bordered workspace switcher ≈ 330px wide with a logo tile and a chevron. At the right sit a blue text link with a megaphone icon, a search icon on newer screens, and a 32–36px avatar. [images/ux/personnel/bulk-import-personnel-training-records-new-experience--01.png, images/ux/risk/manage-multiple-risk-registers-new-experience--06.png, images/ux/assets/bulk-import-assets--01.png]

Small grey sentence-case labels ("Workspace", "Organization") and a hairline head the groups, and a foot group (Connections, Library, Settings) is pinned to the bottom. [images/ux/audits/audits-page-overview-new-experience--02.png, images/ux/monitoring/test-library-new-experience--01.png, images/ux/vendors/vendor-insights--01.png]
- The current item has a grey fill ≈ #EEEFF0 (est.) at radius ≈ 6 with ink text. Its open parent is grey too. There is no accent bar and no blue text. [images/ux/audits/audits-page-overview-new-experience--01.png, images/ux/policies/manage-policy-renewals--02.png, images/ux/settings/personal-settings-notifications-new-experience--01.png]

### Page anatomy

- The header shares the well's grey. It runs, top to bottom: a breadcrumb (an underlined blue link and a slash) or a "← Back to …" link; the title row; a facts strip; line tabs at its foot; and a hairline across the well. [images/ux/controls/manage-required-approval-and-control-readiness--01.png, images/ux/monitoring/view-and-manage-test-details-new-experience--02.png, images/ux/policies/manage-policy-renewals--02.png]
- The title row puts an identifier or a picture before the name (a control code chip, a framework seal, a version chip, an avatar) and sets the name at 26–32px semibold. Actions sit at the right in a fixed order: ⋯, then an outlined secondary, then the solid primary. [images/ux/frameworks/custom-framework--02.png, images/ux/policies/manage-policy-renewals--02.png, images/ux/personnel/personnel-overview--03.png]
- The facts strip sets each fact as a label over a value: 13px medium grey labels, and 16px values or a pill, ≈ 32px apart. Some labels carry a pencil, so that fact is edited where it stands. [images/ux/controls/revert-a-control-to-drata-s-latest-dcf-template--01.png, images/ux/tickets/create-and-manage-workspace-tickets--02.png, images/ux/vendors/customize-the-vendor-questionnaire-email-subject-line-new-experience--02.png]
- A register has a title, optional tabs and a row of stat tiles. Its body splits into a filter-panel card ≈ 300px wide and a table card. [images/ux/policies/view-and-edit-a-policy--01.png, images/ux/controls/manage-required-approval-and-control-readiness--06.png, images/ux/risk/drata-s-risk-library-new-experience--01.png]
- A record's body is a grid of white cards: four readiness cards in a row, then two columns (Info at the left; Owners, Review and approval at the right). [images/ux/controls/manage-required-approval-and-control-readiness--01.png, images/ux/controls/revert-a-control-to-drata-s-latest-dcf-template--01.png, images/ux/workspaces/workspace-manager-overview--01.png]

- A wizard fills the well: back link, title, hairline, a vertical stepper at the left and the form at the right. [images/ux/audits/internal-audits-in-drata-new-experience--02.png, images/ux/policies/create-a-policy--03.png, images/ux/settings/change-your-company-s-email-domain--05.png]

### Borders, radius and shadow

- Every card, the filter panel, the table card, the stat tiles and the sub-cards inside cards have a 1px border ≈ #E3E4E6 (est.) and no shadow. [images/ux/policies/view-and-edit-a-policy--01.png, images/ux/monitoring/view-and-manage-test-details-new-experience--02.png]
- Radii: cards ≈ 8px, the well ≈ 12px, buttons and inputs ≈ 4–6px, modals ≈ 10px. Pills, chips and avatars are fully round. [images/ux/audits/audits-page-overview-new-experience--01.png, images/ux/audits/create-and-add-auditors-to-an-audit--01.png]

## 3. For Ledger

What Drata adds to the Vanta study, in the kit's own terms. None of it takes Drata's name, logo, exact colours or illustrations.

- **An `Id` that carries status.** Let the control code's one representation (`Id` in the kit, wrapped by the app's control-identifier component) take a status tone: the monospaced code inside a round chip on `color.background.<status>.subtler`, with the status glyph in front. A baseline register then shows code and status as one mark (B12), and the separate status column goes. Use it only where the status varies on the page (B14). [images/ux/controls/manage-required-approval-and-control-readiness--06.png]
- **Parameter chips in control text.** Where a control's statement prints the program's parameter values (from `program_control_parameters`), show an unset organisation-defined parameter as an inline outlined chip on `color.border.brand` with `color.text.link`, saying whether a value or a selection is owed. A set value reads as plain text with its source on hover. Vanta's study had nothing for control text, and this is the one Drata pattern built for NIST text. [images/ux/frameworks/a-guide-to-drata-s-fedramp-readiness-framework--03.png]
- **Readiness by stage on a control's Overview.** Four `Card`s, one for each stage of the chain (requirements, evidence, assessment, remediation). Each holds a status word after a tinted glyph and a `Tally` of its counts, and a stage with nothing yet holds one line and the verb `src/lib/next-steps.ts` derives. That is four figures, within the Overview budget, and every number opens its register. [images/ux/controls/manage-required-approval-and-control-readiness--01.png, images/ux/controls/create-edit-and-manage-controls--03.png]
- **A facet panel beside `DataTable`.** For the long registers (control baseline, CCI, objectives), an `AsideLayout` slot holding a facet card of radio, checkbox and `Combobox` groups, each group with its applied count, and above the table a row of applied `Chip`s ending in Clear all. The panel collapses to the toolbar's Filter trigger below a set width, so the seven columns survive. [images/ux/monitoring/test-library-new-experience--01.png, images/ux/access-review/atlassian-integration-guide--03.png]
- **Sunken body, raised cards, on Record and Overview only.** Put `elevation.surface.sunken` behind the cards of a record's Overview tab and of the standing views, with cards on `elevation.surface.raised.default` edged by `color.border.default`, and keep the shell on `elevation.surface.default`. Registers and workspaces stay one surface, as Vanta's do. [images/ux/audits/audits-page-overview-new-experience--02.png, images/ux/controls/revert-a-control-to-drata-s-latest-dcf-template--01.png]
- **Docked panes from a `StickyRail`.** On a record, give the right edge a narrow rail of icon toggles (review thread, notes, tasks) that dock a `WorkPane` beside the page and narrow it, rather than laying a sheet over it. Only one pane is open at a time, its state is in the address, and the chosen icon gets a `color.border.selected` edge mark. [images/ux/tickets/create-and-manage-workspace-tickets--02.png, images/ux/audits/using-the-drata-audit-portal-new-experience--05.png]
- **Plain `Stat` tiles.** Up to four equal tiles above a register: label over an ink numeral at the display size, with no bar, trend or colour. Only a tile that counts a problem leads its figure with the warning glyph (rule 4), so one tile out of four is loud. [images/ux/policies/view-and-edit-a-policy--01.png, images/ux/risk/risk-insights-overview--01.png]
- **A vertical `Stepper` for long Flows.** Program setup and the overlay flows as a full-page Flow: the stepper at the left (done is a filled circle with a check, current a ring, later a subdued circle), a form column ≈ 880px wide, and a footer with the way back at the left and the step's primary at the right, in its trigger's words. [images/ux/settings/change-your-company-s-email-domain--05.png, images/ux/policies/create-a-policy--03.png]
- **An agent's proposal as a Sign.** When an assistant proposes a change, end its answer in a bordered card: one line naming what will change, the role's primary and a secondary to decline, with a collapsible line of its reasoning above. Mark AI with `color.background.accent.purple.subtler`, never a gradient. [images/ux/ai/getting-started-with-the-tprm-agent--03.png]
- **What to leave behind:** blue-outlined secondaries, the seven-tint pill set, a palette per chart, gridlined charts outside a workspace, illustrated empty states, a status as the header's primary, and row ⋯ at the start of the row.
