# What's next

The open list for the design system and the prototype. Josef owns the decisions; whoever is working owns the work. An entry is here only while something in it is open: when it lands or closes, move it to [What landed](next-archive.md) with its date, and leave what it left open here as a "From …" entry named after it. Keep it short: one line per item, the reasoning lives in the spec or the audit it points at.

For screen composition, [the product pattern contract](guides/product-patterns.md) is current. A guide that cites an entry of this list by name may mean one that has since moved to the archive.
