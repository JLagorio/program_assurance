# Family deep dive: charts

Part of the [Ledger audit, 24 September 2026](README.md).

Three units: the foundation (Chart.Frame, the Chart wrapper and the shared plot parts in `_shared.tsx`), the Cartesian charts, and the other charts. The family is careful work for a sighted mouse user, and nothing in the app uses it. Five problems cross the units and are cheapest fixed once:

- The keyboard walk is silent, plots that choose nothing are still tab stops, and Donut and Scatter cannot be chosen from the keyboard at all: CHF-1, CHF-10, CHO-2.
- Forced colours erase the HTML swatches and every colour-scale Heatmap cell, and the forced-colours vitest project covers four parts only: CHF-6, CHO-6.
- The Frame and its part each take data, series, x and size. Expand, the legend, the twin and the CSV drift from the plot, and the twin cannot serve the non-category charts: CHF-4, CHF-5, CHO-4.
- The kit's own locale is bypassed: English literals in the Frame, the tooltip and the Heatmap's screen-reader text, an en-US `formatNumber` export, and time ticks that read one unit early in US time zones: CHF-12, CHX-16, CHO-7, CHF-7.
- The prototype renders no chart, and docs/next.md says it does: CHF-15, CHO-20.

## Chart, Chart.Frame and the shared parts

The foundation works for sighted pointer use: a real table twin with a CSV, an sr-only summary, textures, skeletons per kind and token motion. The non-visual and edge paths do not hold. A screen reader lands on an unnamed `role="application"` that stays silent while the arrow keys move the tooltip, the Table toggle changes its name when pressed, and legend swatches vanish in forced colours. In ordinary use, hiding a series from the legend dims all the others, and Expand never makes the plot taller, because the Frame shares neither size nor data with its part.

Keep: the table twin one toggle away, with extra columns and a CSV of the same rows; the sr-only summary on `aria-describedby`; textures in a fixed order and a categorical order validated for deutan and protan vision; inline and expanded views share hidden-series and table state (18 September deep audit #5, fixed); the details card is a Popover anchored to the mark that closes on Escape and outside click, returns focus to the same leaf and hides the tooltip while open, on touch too; skeletons per kind at the plot's height, so nothing jumps; motion from tokens, with reduced motion drawing marks in place; a focus ring for keyboard focus only; number and category formats from LedgerProvider (`useChartFormat`); reference and band labels placed so they do not overlap, shortened with an ellipsis; a thorough MDX page with a kinds table, content rules, a Don't pair for dual axes, and small multiples on one domain and `syncId`.

**CHF-1, CHX-1 · The keyboard walk of a plot is silent to screen readers, and the focus target is an unnamed role="application"** (high)
[\_shared.tsx:726-812](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L726-L812), [:776](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L776), [:1358-1360](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L1358-L1360), [:1473-1478](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L1473-L1478), [bar.tsx:379](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/bar.tsx#L379), [line.tsx:421](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/line.tsx#L421), [:553](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/line.tsx#L553), [sparkline.tsx:119](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/sparkline.tsx#L119)
Every named plot turns on recharts' `accessibilityLayer`, so the svg becomes `role="application" tabindex=0` with no name ([RootSurface.js:47-52](../../../node_modules/recharts/es6/container/RootSurface.js#L47-L52)). The kit replaces recharts' tooltip, which is `role="status" aria-live="assertive"` ([DefaultTooltipContent.js:133-136](../../../node_modules/recharts/es6/component/DefaultTooltipContent.js#L133-L136)), with a TooltipContent div that has neither. Live on Overview Framed and Bar Details, ArrowRight moves the visible tooltip ("Mar · Open 15 · Closed 8", "AU / Satisfied 18 / … / Total 26") while the page has no live region, and the application reads as its tick numbers. A screen-reader user loses browse mode and hears nothing, and every named plot is a tab stop even when it chooses nothing. This breaks the rule to "preserve the dependency's focus, Escape, outside-click, keyboard and ARIA behavior" ([component-library.md:241-242](../component-library.md#L241-L242)) and the page's "the keyboard gets the same" ([Chart.mdx:119](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.mdx#L119), [:192](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.mdx#L192)).
Fix:

- Give Plot one persistent, visually hidden `aria-live="polite"` region, written from ActiveProbe only when the index changes by keyboard: the category and every visible series value ("AU: Satisfied 18, Partial 4, Other than satisfied 3, Not assessed 1, total 26"). Do not put `role="status"` on TooltipContent: it returns null while inactive, and a region that mounts with its text is often not announced.
- Name the svg (`aria-labelledby` the Frame title), with `aria-roledescription="chart"` and the keys in `aria-describedby` ("Arrow keys move between months; Enter opens details"). RootSurface accepts an explicit `role` and `tabIndex`.
- For a plot that chooses nothing, consider `role="img"` named by the title and summary, with no tab stop, and leave the data to the table twin. Add a play assertion that ArrowRight updates the live text.

**CHF-2 · The Table toggle changes its accessible name when pressed** (high)
[frame.tsx:366-375](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L366-L375)
The Toggle sets `aria-label={showTable ? t("showChart") : t("showTable")}` over the hard-coded visible text "Table", and the Framed play function asserts the flip ([Chart.stories.tsx:293](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.stories.tsx#L293), [:303](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.stories.tsx#L303)). **CHF-3 · Hiding a series from the legend dims every remaining series** (high)
[frame.tsx:118-125](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L118-L125), [\_shared.tsx:411-421](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L411-L421), [:680-687](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L680-L687)
The legend button highlights its key on mouseenter and focus-visible and toggles it on click, and `seriesClass` dims every series that is not the highlighted one. After the click the highlighted key is the series just hidden, so every visible series is dimmed: on Overview Emphasis all four remaining lines turn pale until the pointer leaves, focus-visible plus Space dims everything until blur, and at 390px a tap leaves the rest dimmed indefinitely, because a tap fires mouseenter and never mouseleave. Swatch's line branch also ignores `hollow`, so a hidden line keeps a solid swatch, although [Chart.mdx:92](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.mdx#L92) says "the swatch hollows".
Fix:

- Clear the highlight in `toggle`, and have `seriesClass` ignore a highlighted key that is in `hidden`.
- Highlight only on `pointerType === "mouse"` (onPointerEnter) and on focus-visible.
- Hollow the line swatch in the same change. Add a play step that clicks a legend item and asserts no visible series has opacity-disabled.

**CHF-4 · Expand opens a large dialog, but the plot keeps its inline height** (medium)
[frame.tsx:516-534](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L516-L534), [\_shared.tsx:1427](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L1427)

Live, the ChartMatrix Download/Expand specimen opens an 860px dialog around a 120px plot with the category labels still truncated, and on Downloads at 390px the plot stays 200px. [Chart.mdx:83](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.mdx#L83) says "An expanded chart is the same Frame at `large`".
Fix: carry size and height through FrameContext with part props still winning (the registration in CHF-5 does this), and in the dialog override the context to large or fill the body's height. Replace the map literal at [frame.tsx:517](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L517) with a named constant, and assert the expanded plot height in the Framed play function.

**CHF-5, CHX-9 · Frame and plot each take data, series, x and size, and the two copies drift** (medium)
[frame.tsx:155-188](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L155-L188), [:254-265](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L254-L265), [:484](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L484), [:90-91](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L90-L91), [\_shared.tsx:382-403](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L382-L403), [Chart.stories.tsx:66-67](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.stories.tsx#L66-L67), [:243-271](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.stories.tsx#L243-L271), [ChartLine.stories.tsx:192](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/ChartLine.stories.tsx#L192), [:229](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/ChartLine.stories.tsx#L229), [:282](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/ChartLine.stories.tsx#L282), [:301](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/ChartLine.stories.tsx#L301)
The Frame needs series, data and x for the legend, the twin and the CSV; the part needs them again to draw, and `useFrame` reads only name, hidden, highlight, format, loading, sync and texture. Every story writes the chart twice, and Line stories add `swatch="line"` to the Frame by hand because it does not know the plot is a line. The concrete drift: a format given only to the part is ignored by the twin and the CSV ("45%" in the plot, "45" in the table), the legend's tone and texture index can differ from the plot's, and `toggle()` counts stale hidden keys (`next.size >= series.length`), so after the series set changes, hiding one series can reveal everything.
Fix:

- Have each part register its data, series, x, size, swatch shape and a part-level format with the Frame through context, and make the Frame's props explicit overrides. This also fixes CHF-4.
- Prune hidden keys that are no longer in `series`.

**CHF-6, CHX-6 · Legend, tooltip and card swatches disappear in forced colours** (medium)
[\_shared.tsx:671-703](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L671-L703), [:789-793](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L789-L793), [:1277-1283](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L1277-L1283)
Swatch paints its line, square and dot keys as HTML spans with an inline background or border colour, which forced colours discard, while the SVG marks keep their colours; only textured square swatches are SVG. Live with forced colours active, Framed's "Open" and "Closed" and the stacked Bar's four names have no key, and on Textured the solid first series loses its swatch while the patterned ones stay. A Windows High Contrast reader cannot map names to marks, which undoes the second channel [Chart.mdx:128](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.mdx#L128) and [:196](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/Chart.mdx#L196) promise; lines never take a texture.
Fix:

- Follow [forced-colors.css](../../../packages/design-system/src/styles/forced-colors.css)'s own pattern: a `data-slot="chart-swatch"` with a `forced-color-adjust: none` rule there. Or draw every swatch as SVG, as TextureSwatch does.
- Widen the forced-colours vitest project, which runs only Switch, RadioGroup, Tabs and Progress ([vitest.config.ts:88-91](../../../packages/design-system/vitest.config.ts#L88-L91)), to Framed, Textured and the stacked Bar. Consider turning texture on under `@media (forced-colors: active)`, with the axis and grid in system colours.

**CHF-7 · Time ticks and date-only values read one unit early in any time zone west of UTC** (medium)
[\_shared.tsx:279-346](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L279-L346), [:349-359](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L349-L359)
`timeTicks` computes tick boundaries in UTC (`setUTCHours`, `setUTCDate`, `setUTCMonth`) but formats them in the provider's `timeZone`. A node run of the same logic in America/New_York labels the ticks at 1 February to 1 September 2026 as "Jan" to "Aug", and a date-only "2026-09-04" reads "Sep 3, 2026"; zones east of UTC get correct labels at slightly misplaced ticks. `useChartFormat` already forces UTC for 10-character dates ([:270](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L270)), the time axis does not, and the UTC default hides it in Storybook.
Fix: compute boundaries in the formatting zone, or format date-only data in UTC as `useChartFormat` does. Add a `timeTicks` test and a Line story under `<LedgerProvider timeZone="America/New_York">`.

**CHF-8 · CSV export has no formula guard and no BOM, and loses non-Latin titles** (medium)
[\_shared.tsx:1534-1581](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L1534-L1581)
`csvCell` quotes only commas, quotes and newlines, so a category, series or text cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` (user-entered system, component and owner names) is evaluated by Excel or Sheets: OWASP CSV injection, in an assurance product. Without a UTF-8 BOM, Excel on Windows garbles the en dash in ranges and any non-ASCII name, and `fileName` strips everything outside a–z and 0–9, so a non-Latin title downloads as "chart.csv". Categories go out as display strings ("4 Sep 2026"), and a Date in a series as `String(Date)`.
Fix:

- One shared serializer (`lib/csv.ts`) for DataTable and Chart: an apostrophe before text cells that start with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab or CR (numbers untouched), `\r` quoted, RFC 4180 CRLF, a BOM, and Unicode letters kept in `fileName`.
- ISO 8601 category output is an option, not a defect.

**CHF-9 · PNG export is the bare plot, fails silently, and does nothing on a Heatmap** (medium)
[frame.tsx:301-305](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L301-L305), [:395-397](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L395-L397), [\_shared.tsx:1614-1656](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L1614-L1656)
`savePng` serializes only `svg.recharts-surface`, so the title, description and legend, which are HTML outside it, are missing, and a multi-series PNG has colours with no key. `void savePng()` swallows the rejections `svgToPng` throws, and a Heatmap is HTML with no svg, so its Download PNG does nothing and says nothing. Web fonts are unavailable inside an SVG drawn as an image, so the text falls back to a system font.
Fix: compose the title, description and legend into the canvas (or a wrapper svg) before rasterizing. Hide or disable PNG for kinds with no svg (CHO-15), show an error toast when export fails, and test that the menu item produces a blob.

[\_shared.tsx:1475-1477](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/_shared.tsx#L1475-L1477), [bar.tsx:292](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/bar.tsx#L292), [:379](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/bar.tsx#L379), [:466](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/bar.tsx#L466)

**CHF-11 · Empty and error states are a bespoke dashed box, with no retry slot and no empty detection** (medium)
[frame.tsx:419-434](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L419-L434)

Missing in this family:

`shown` drops hidden slices, the total is recomputed from it and Pie gets `data={shown}`, so the remaining slices expand to 360° and the card's share becomes a share of what is visible. With Satisfied hidden, Partial (40 of 372) fills about half the ring and its card reads "54% of 74", while [ChartDonut.mdx:57](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/ChartDonut.mdx#L57) says "the others keep their angles and the track shows through". No story puts a Donut in a Frame with a legend, so the state is never exercised.
Fix: keep the full total when a slice is hidden (a transparent, or track-coloured and non-interactive, sector), compute share against the full total, and add a Frame and legend story with a play test for angles and share.

**CHO-12 · `label` and `name` mean opposite things on Donut and the other plots** (medium)
[donut.tsx:54](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/donut.tsx#L54), [:66](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/donut.tsx#L66), [scatter.tsx:61](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/scatter.tsx#L61), [:76](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/scatter.tsx#L76), [treemap.tsx:45](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/treemap.tsx#L45), [heatmap.tsx:43](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/heatmap.tsx#L43)
On Donut, `label` is the number in the centre and `name` the accessible name; on Scatter, `label` is the accessible name and `name` the datum key that names a point; on Treemap and Heatmap, `label` is the accessible name. `<Chart.Donut label="Control coverage">` prints the title in the hole and leaves the ring unnamed, and `<Chart.Scatter name="Risks">` looks up `d["Risks"]` and blanks every tooltip and card title. Neither is a type error, so agents copying between pages will ship both.
Fix: make `label` the accessible name everywhere, keeping Donut's `name` as an `@deprecated` alias with a `ledger/no-deprecated-name` fixer. Rename Donut's centre text to `centerLabel` (or a centre part), not `value`, which CHO-8 needs, and Scatter's key to `nameKey`.

**CHO-13 · Scatter has no axis domain or tick control, and one number format covers x, y and z** (medium)
[scatter.tsx:187](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/scatter.tsx#L187), [:198](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/scatter.tsx#L198), [:235-255](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/scatter.tsx#L235-L255), [:356](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/scatter.tsx#L356)
The axes take no domain or ticks, unlike Bar and Line ([bar.tsx:88](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/bar.tsx#L88), [line.tsx:88](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/line.tsx#L88)), so the kit's own 1–5 likelihood × impact example draws both axes 0–8, leaving about 40% of the plot past the scale's maximum. The tooltip and card format every numeric axis with the one `format`, so a dollar format for exposure makes the tooltip read "Likelihood $4K"; x ticks use `String(v)` (12000, not 12K) while y ticks compact, and z is labelled by its raw key.
Fix: add `xDomain` and `yDomain` (ChartDomain) with optional integer or explicit ticks, plus `formatY` and `formatZ` (or a per-axis format map) and `zLabel`, each used in the ticks, the tooltip and the card.

**CHO-14 · Scatter hides coincident points, and its lead example is the ordinal case its guidance sends to Heatmap** (medium)
[scatter.tsx:109](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/scatter.tsx#L109), [:333-335](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/scatter.tsx#L333-L335), [ChartScatter.mdx:10](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/ChartScatter.mdx#L10), [:65](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/ChartScatter.mdx#L65)
The transparent hit circle (r ≥ 12) of a later point covers earlier ones, with no jitter, count or aggregation, so risks that share (3, 4) show one dot and one tooltip, and a click can open the wrong risk. The fixture has no coincident points, so this is proven from the code. Every story plots integer 1–5 likelihood × impact, two ordinal scales the page itself sends to a Heatmap ("two categories are a Heatmap"), and the prototype stores both as text levels ([database.types.ts:10101-10102](../../../src/lib/database.types.ts#L10101-L10102)).
Fix: change the lead example to two real measures (age in days × exposure) and send likelihood × impact to the Heatmap's status matrix. List every coincident datum in the tooltip and card, and optionally offer `jitter` or a count badge.

**CHO-15 · The Heatmap ignores the Frame's status and format** (medium)
[heatmap.tsx:79-81](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/heatmap.tsx#L79-L81), [frame.tsx:302-304](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L302-L304), [:417-418](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/frame.tsx#L417-L418)
Heatmap never calls `useFrame`, so it takes the default format rather than the one the Frame shares with the plot, tooltip and table. With `status="loading"` the Frame renders it as-is: a grey grid of "none" cells with no `aria-busy`, which reads as no data, and [ChartHeatmap.mdx:65](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/patterns/ChartHeatmap.mdx#L65) says "The Frame does not set it". Its Download PNG does nothing, because `savePng` looks for `svg.recharts-surface` (CHF-9).
Fix: read name, format and loading from `useFrame` in Heatmap (its rows and columns already give the skeleton its shape). Hide PNG when the plot has no svg, or render the table to a canvas.

**CHO-16 · Chart.Scale is not tied to the Heatmap's binning, and status scales have no key** (medium)
[heatmap.tsx:115-116](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/heatmap.tsx#L115-L116), [:261-297](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/chart/heatmap.tsx#L261-L297)
The Heatmap cuts [min, max] into five equal bins, from the data's own range unless `domain` is set, while Chart.Scale is a separate part with free-text end labels: the key can say "0 … 40" while the grid is binned 3 … 12, nobody can tell that step 5 means ≥ 9.6, and zero shares step 1 with 1–2, so no findings looks like some. The risk matrix's red, amber, blue and green (`riskTone`) are explained nowhere, so the risk level is carried by colour and position only (1.4.1).
Fix, keeping parts over props rather than a `legend` prop on the Heatmap:

- Let Chart.Scale take `domain` and `format`, or read the sibling Heatmap's computed domain through the Frame context, and print the step thresholds. Add a zero step option.
- Add a status variant of Chart.Scale whose swatches use the cells' `toneClasses[…].subtle` fills, since Chart.Legend's chart colours would not match.
