# Family deep dive: tables and collection controls

Part of the [Ledger audit, 24 September 2026](README.md).

Four groups: the plain Table and its sort and page hooks (TBL), DataTable core (DTC), DataTable's parts (DTP), and Toolbar, ActionBar and Pagination (TLB). When two groups found the same defect, it is written up once, in the group that owns the part, under both ids.

## Table, useSort and usePage

The plain Table is careful markup: native semantics, a scroll frame that becomes a named tab stop only when it overflows, and hairlines that survive sticky scrolling. The prototype uses it only for the contract's two named exceptions. Its real defects are alignment and naming. End-aligned and group-centred headings never move, so every numeric column's heading sits over empty space (TBL-2), and physical `text-left`/`text-right` break RTL (TBL-3); a screen reader hears "Preview row" and a bare "Collapse" on every row, with no row header to place them (TBL-1, TBL-9). Fix first a logical `align` on Header and Cell, a per-row eye label with a row-header cell, and a named, static group toggle.

**Keep:** the scroll frame becomes a named, focusable region only when it overflows, re-measured by a ResizeObserver on the frame and the table; header and pinned-edge hairlines are pseudo-elements, so they stay put under sticky cells, and the checkbox column's edge shows only while scrolled; sortable headers are real buttons in `th` with `aria-sort`, and the sort hint shows on focus and stays visible on touch; `touch-target` gives 24px hit areas under coarse pointers without moving layout, and hover-only controls show under `hover: none`; the eye reserves its slot, so ids end in an ellipsis, with plays at 390px and in a fixed layout; Tree and Disclosure chevrons name their row, and Disclosure keeps a fixed leading column through reorder and hide; `width` doubles as a minimum and `minWidth` gives the name a floor; thorough MDX with anatomy, states, content rules and Do/Don't stories; prototype use limited to version history and profile Source imports.

**TBL-2 · End-aligned and centred headings stay at the start of the column** (medium)

[table.tsx:274](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L274), [:280](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L280), [data-table.tsx:152-153](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L152-L153), [:350-351](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L350-L351)

Th wraps its label in a block-level flex span and the sort button is inline-flex, so the `text-right` that a caller or DataTable's alignClass puts on the `th` never moves the label. The same span defeats DataTable's `text-center` on column-group headings. Live, the Tree story's "Controls" label sits at 1100–1148px while its numbers end at 1172, and in the Metrics story "Open items" sits at 906–969 while its figures end near 1002. Every `c.number` column in the app takes this path ([library-products.tsx:117-118](../../../src/components/prototype/library-products.tsx#L117-L118), [editor.tsx:476-479](../../../src/components/app/profile-tailoring/editor.tsx#L476-L479), [product-configuration-picker.tsx:42-48](../../../src/components/app/product-configuration-picker.tsx#L42-L48)), so on every register with counts the heading floats away from its figures, against the kit's own rule ([Table.mdx:100](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/components/Table.mdx#L100)).

**Fix:**

- Add `align?: "start" | "center" | "end" | undefined` to Table.Header and Table.Cell. It justifies the inner flex and the sort button, puts the sort arrow on the leading side for `end`, and adds `tabular-nums` to end-aligned cells.
- Map DataTable's `meta.align` and its group-heading centring to the prop instead of a class.

**TBL-3 · Physical text-left and text-right put RTL cells on the wrong side** (medium)

[table.tsx:106](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L106), [data-table.tsx:153](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L153)

The kit supports RTL (LocaleProvider sets `dir`, and the scroll edges and resize arrows read direction), but the table root forces `text-left` and end alignment is `text-right`. Live, with `dir=rtl` on the Tree story, the Kind cell's text stays at the physical left while the flex headers move to the right, and the numbers stay right-aligned instead of ending on the left. An RTL reader sees text columns on the opposite side from their own headers throughout.

**Fix:** Use `text-start` on the table root and `text-end` in place of `text-right`, through TBL-2's `align`. Add an RTL Table story with a play assertion, as Shell and Tabs have. ListCell's `text-left` ([table.tsx:900](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L900)) matters little, because its button is a flex container.

**TBL-1 · Every preview eye is "Preview row", and no cell can be a row header** (medium)

[table.tsx:441](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L441), [:425-433](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L425-L433), [:485-491](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L485-L491), [:509-528](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L509-L528), [:379](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L379), [locale-format.ts:125](../../../packages/design-system/src/lib/locale-format.ts#L125)

PreviewButton, PreviewEye and Table.Id take no label, so the eye's name is the fixed string "Preview row", and Td always renders a `td`. Live, `/findings` has 20 buttons named "Preview row" and no row headers; `/vendors` and `/campaigns` have 6 each and none. A screen-reader user tabbing a register hears "Preview row, toggle button" on every row, and table navigation announces the column but never the row's identity. DataTable already names its More fields disclosure with the record ([data-table.tsx:775](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L775)), so the eye is the odd one out. WCAG 2.4.6 and technique H63 apply here as advice, not as failures.

**Fix:**

- Give Table.Id and PreviewEye a `label`, defaulting to "Preview {id}" when `id` is a string, and have DataTable pass the identity it already computes for More fields. DTC-1 names the other row controls.
- Have Table.Id, and DataTable's priority-0 or identity cell, render `<th scope="row">` with the Td styling, or add a `rowHeader` boolean to Table.Cell. Do not reuse Table.Header, which is sticky header chrome.

**TBL-9 · Table.Group's chevron is a bare "Collapse" or "Expand", and its heading is a `td`** (medium)

[table.tsx:619](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L619), [:621-637](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L621-L637), [data-table.tsx:1374-1383](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L1374-L1383)

In the Groups story the band buttons read "Collapse, expanded", "Collapse, expanded" and "Expand, collapsed": the label flips with the state, aria-expanded repeats it, and no group is named. The heading cells are `td`, so rows in a band are not associated with its heading. Table.Tree and Table.Disclosure already name their row ([:689](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L689), [:750](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L750)), and DataTable's `groupBy` draws this part.

**Fix:** Give the title span an id (`useId`) and point the button's `aria-labelledby` at it, so the name stays the group title (which may be a ReactNode) and aria-expanded alone carries the state. Render the band cell as `<th colSpan scope="rowgroup">` with the current styling. No new string prop is needed.

**TBL-8 · Table.List without an opener is a focusable span that announces nothing, and its card never reaches assistive technology** (medium)

[table.tsx:939-949](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L939-L949), [:899-916](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L899-L916), [:918-956](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L918-L956)

Without `onOpen`, the list line is a `span` with `tabIndex=0` and no role. Enter and Space do nothing, and the hover card opens on focus. The accessibility tree reads the cell as "Payments API +2", and the card, with each item's meta and status and the `note` on what needs attention, is a portal popup with no aria relationship. It reaches the app through `c.list` in [requirements-table.tsx:367](../../../src/components/prototype/requirements-table.tsx#L367), [:379](../../../src/components/prototype/requirements-table.tsx#L379), [library-controls.tsx:104](../../../src/components/prototype/library-controls.tsx#L104), [product-structure.tsx:219](../../../src/components/prototype/product-structure.tsx#L219) and [program-library.tsx:167](../../../src/components/prototype/program-library.tsx#L167).

**Fix:** Render the line as a button that opens a Popover on click and Enter (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`), or link the card through `aria-describedby` to visually hidden text that lists every item, its status and the note. Speak "+2" as "and 2 more".

**TBL-7 · Truncated cells keep their full value only when the child is a plain string** (medium)

[table.tsx:380](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L380), [:382](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L382), [:260](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L260), [:535-544](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L535-L544), [profiles.$profileId.tsx:323-325](../../../src/routes/profiles.$profileId.tsx#L323-L325)

Every Td and Th is `max-w-0 truncate`, a title is set only when the children are a string, and Id truncates with no title. On a profile's Derivation tab the Reference cell shows "urn:uuid:5020259a-1767-5221-bb99-85c…" with no title. That href appears nowhere else on the page, although [Table.mdx:132](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/components/Table.mdx#L132) says "the record has it in full". Even where a title exists, keyboard and touch users cannot reach it.

**Fix:**

- Kit: add `wrap` to Table.Cell and Table.Header (see Missing), so a value that must be read whole wraps, as [Table.mdx:103](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/components/Table.mdx#L103) already says. Derive a title from the text content for non-string children, or accept one. A focusable Tooltip on Id is optional.
- App: let the profile's Reference cell wrap and break.

**TBL-12 · Version history does not mark the current version and prints the raw state** (medium)

[library-components.tsx:705-730](../../../src/components/prototype/library-components.tsx#L705-L730), [library-products.tsx:608-633](../../../src/components/prototype/library-products.tsx#L608-L633)

The two Versions tabs render identical markup. Each row's "Open version" calls `onVersion`, including on the version already shown, and no row carries `isSelected`, `aria-current` or a Current badge, although the record knows `revision.id` ([library-components.tsx:760](../../../src/components/prototype/library-components.tsx#L760)). State prints the enum ("published"). The contract says this table "selects the current version with an explicit button" ([product-patterns.md:15](../product-patterns.md#L15)). On the one screen whose job is choosing a version, the reader cannot see which one they are viewing.

**Fix:**

- Mark the current row with `isSelected`, `aria-current="true"` (Tr forwards native props) and a Current badge, and disable or relabel its button.
- Render State with StateBadge, and extract one shared version-history composition.

**TBL-10 · The column resize handle is drag-only for pointers, named the same on every column, and focused with the browser's ring** (medium)

[table.tsx:311-348](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L311-L348), [:315](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L315), [:339](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L339), [columns-menu.tsx:180-238](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/columns-menu.tsx#L180-L238)

In the Keyboard resize matrix story both separators are named "Resize column". On focus they show the browser's `outline: auto 1px`, and the brand guide stays at opacity 0. Apart from a double-click reset, a pointer can set a width only by dragging, and the column menu has no width option, so single-pointer users cannot resize (WCAG 2.5.7). The app enables resizing on [work-table.tsx:116](../../../src/components/prototype/work-table.tsx#L116), [library-controls.tsx:121](../../../src/components/prototype/library-controls.tsx#L121), [library-products.tsx:132](../../../src/components/prototype/library-products.tsx#L132), [:518](../../../src/components/prototype/library-products.tsx#L518), [:804](../../../src/components/prototype/library-products.tsx#L804) and [requirements-table.tsx:407](../../../src/components/prototype/requirements-table.tsx#L407). The 8px hit area is tracked separately.

**Fix:**

- Name the separator with its column ("Resize {column}"), and give it `focus-visible:outline-focused` with the guide shown on focus-visible.
- Add a single-pointer width route to the header menu: Fit to content, Reset width, or a width field.

**TBL-5 · In a two-row header the leaf row covers the column-group row once the frame scrolls** (medium)

[table.tsx:266](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L266), [data-table.tsx:1332](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L1332)

Every header cell is `sticky top-0`, and DataTable renders one row per header group with no offset. Live, in the Column groups story with a 160px frame, "Ownership" sits at top 16 and "ID" at 48. After scrolling 120px both sit at 16 and the group band is gone. This affects any DataTable with `c.group` and `fill` or `maxHeight`; no app screen uses one today.

**Fix:** Pass a cumulative `top` (header row height × row index) from DataTable to Table.Header, or make the `thead` the sticky element so the header rows move as one. Pinned header cells keep their inline-start stickiness.

**TBL-4 · The documented plain treegrid has no keyboard model** (medium)

[Table.stories.tsx:482-568](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/components/Table.stories.tsx#L482-L568), [Table.mdx:42](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/components/Table.mdx#L42), [:129](../../../packages/design-system/src/stories/components/Table.mdx#L129), [table.tsx:653-711](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L653-L711)

Live, Tab lands on "Collapse Ground segment" and ArrowDown leaves focus there. A screen reader switches to focus mode in a treegrid and expects grid keys, so this is worse than a plain table, and anyone who copies the story ships it. DataTable's tree mode is fine, and Table.Tree has no consumer.

**Fix:** Choose one.

- Document the hand hierarchy as a plain table (no role) with named chevron buttons, and reserve `role="treegrid"` for DataTable's tree mode.
- Or export `treeKeys` and the roving tab stop as a hook the hand treegrid uses, and add a keyboard play to the Tree story.

Whether to keep Table.Tree beside Table.Disclosure is a catalogue decision, not part of this fix.

## DataTable and useDataTable

DataTable core is well built: one hook and one renderer over TanStack Table 9, column kinds that carry the rules, states drawn under a stable header, and responsive fitting measured on the container. Its weak edges are the ones the prototype's pickers hit: row controls are named by UUIDs (DTC-1), single-choice pickers ride on multi-select checkboxes whose select-all picks the last row (DTC-2), and a search that matches nothing reads as "Nothing published" (DTC-3). Fix those three first, with `rowLabel`, `selectable: "single"` and a narrowed signal; together they repair four pickers. Then restore row memoization, announce loading, bring back the register's URL state, drop the fixed-width row menu and guard CSV exports against formulas.

**Keep:** one hook and one renderer, with column kinds deciding alignment, sort, filter, minimum width and cell part; states under a stable header (skeleton loading, a filtered-empty state with Clear filters, a no-records state that carries the create action); responsive mode that measures the container, neighbouring panel included, keeps identity and row actions, and moves the rest into a labelled More fields without touching stored visibility, sort or export; pins that give way in narrow frames without changing the reader's pins, with selection, disclosure and handle as pinned leading chrome and actions as pinned trailing chrome; a persisted view that is validated and reconciled (corrupt JSON, bad sizes, unknown ids, denied storage) and tested in [view-state.test.mjs](../../../packages/design-system/test/view-state.test.mjs); a treegrid with one roving tab stop, RTL-aware, with Home and End, and editable Enter that respects validation and IME composition; virtualization that measures each record with its disclosed rows; the eye, kebab and header controls visible under `hover: none` with 24px targets; plays for keyboard resize, stored views, responsive containers and phone row controls.

**DTC-1, DTP-2 · Row controls are named by the raw row id, a UUID in the app** (high)

[data-table.tsx:807-814](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L807-L814), [:830](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L830), [:573](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L573), [:842](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L842), [table.tsx:582](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/table.tsx#L582), [locale-format.ts:51-52](../../../packages/design-system/src/lib/locale-format.ts#L51-L52)

The row checkbox is "Select row {id}" and the reorder handle "Reorder row {id}". The kebab is always "Row actions", the detail chevron "Open" or "Close", and the eye "Preview row" (TBL-1). All 48 `getRowId` calls in `src/` return `.id`, a Supabase UUID. Live, in the Allocate requirements picker for SYS-WSX90, the first checkbox is named "Select row 511a692e-78fd-55da-a207-b983b3bfd192" while the row shows REQ-001, and the requirement code is never in the tab sequence; Add from library reads the same way. A screen-reader user choosing requirements, controls or library items hears a 36-character hex string for every checkbox. The renderer already builds a readable name for More fields from `tree.label` or the identity value ([data-table.tsx:775-779](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/data-table/data-table.tsx#L775-L779)).

**Fix:**

- Add `rowLabel?: (row) => string` to useDataTable. Default it to `tree.label`, then the identity or preview column's string value, and use `row.id` only as a last resort.
- Use it for "Select {label}", "Reorder {label}", "Preview {label}", "Actions for {label}" and "Show details for {label}". Assert the names in the Register and Tree plays, and update the MDX Accessibility bullet that documents "Select" plus the id.

## Toolbar, ActionBar, Pagination

Toolbar's folding and focus hand-off are careful work, and its localised More labels have landed; the fold's stale-width edge case sits in code being rewritten for responsive-2026-09-24 #5. The real defects are in the search and the views slot. Enter in the search submits an enclosing form and saved an allocation in the Allocate requirement dialog (TLB-1), the views trigger's focus ring is fully clipped by the Scroller (TLB-2), and search, filter and page results are never announced (TLB-4). ActionBar is an unused, retired record header and should be deprecated.

**Keep:** a container-measured fold that works the same in a panel as on a phone (the Frame story; `/work` at 390px in two rows with the primary whole); one DOM order at every width, so Tab and screen-reader order match the screen; focus hand-off when the focused control folds into More or comes back out; a More popover that is a named dialog, holds what it took while open and closes after a command from a nested menu; plays for Phone, PhoneLongPrimary, Frame and Constrained that assert rows, order, whole labels and Escape layering; docs that say plainly why the row does not use `role="toolbar"`; Pagination with real anchors, `aria-current="page"`, RTL-aware chevrons and container-query words; every app register toolbar going through ProductCollection or the kit Toolbar, with no Inline-built rows left; distinct "{label} pagination" landmark names when a page has several pagers.

**TLB-6 · Folded filters hide that a filter is active** (medium)

[toolbar.tsx:316-341](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/toolbar.tsx#L316-L341)

When filters fold, the trigger always reads "More" and is named "More filters", with no count or active state, and the popover content is unmounted while closed. In the Constrained story, with Gaps active ("Showing gaps") and the container narrowed, nothing on the row says a filter applies. On a phone or in a panel a reader sees a shortened list with no sign that it is filtered, which misleads assurance work: a list of "all" open issues that is not all of them.

**Fix:** Add `activeFilters?: number | undefined`, supplied by the caller (with a DataTable, `table.state.columnFilters.length`), and render it as a Count on More and in its name through a locale message ("More filters, 2 applied").

**TLB-9 · The search has no clear control, and the native one is hidden** (medium)

[input.tsx:20](../../../packages/design-system/src/components/input.tsx#L20), [toolbar.tsx:276-286](../../../packages/design-system/src/patterns/toolbar.tsx#L276-L286)

Input hides `::-webkit-search-cancel-button`, and the Toolbar adds no clear. Escape clears the field on desktop, but touch keyboards have no Escape, and the only other route is Clear filters, which appears only when nothing matches. A phone reader who searched "AC-2" has to select the text and delete it to get the full register back.

**Fix:** Add a clear IconButton addon to InputGroup or the Toolbar search, not to Input globally: labelled "Clear search" through the locale, shown when the value is non-empty, calling `onSearch("")` and returning focus to the field.

**TLB-10 · PickerSheet builds its own search and filter row** (medium)
