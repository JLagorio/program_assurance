# Decisions

Josef's decisions, one line each: the day, the screen or area, the decision. A rule in [the contract](guides/product-patterns-core.md) may follow from one; this log records what was decided and when, never how a screen is built. Newest first. A "Contract budget" line sets the contract's word caps, which `npm run check:contract-budget` reads from the latest one.

## 2026-11-16

- 2026-11-16 · Budgets · Ceilings rise only where a decision costs words: Component set and Verified on four component set pages (1 or 2 words each), the empty seat's No owner on eight records (one status hue each; two words on a business line and a system), and Sources as a register (8 buttons, 1 primary).
- 2026-11-16 · Contract budget · The contract holds at most 4,000 words and its core at most 1,500 words (D7).
- 2026-11-16 · Contract · A short one, and a lab lane (D7): under 4,000 words with its budgets in the tests, and 30 days to try a form before it becomes a kit part.
- 2026-11-16 · Start · Phase 0 first (D8): one week of ten quick fixes visible on every screen, once the artifacts are approved.
- 2026-11-16 · Direction · The control work is the centre (D1): a system opens on the state of its controls; the pass-one path to the next decision is its Authorization view.
- 2026-11-16 · Centre · Controls first, for every system: the pass-three centre stands.
- 2026-11-16 · Path · Not the front door: the dated path is the Authorization view, not the page a system opens on.
- 2026-11-16 · Standing · One fact in the program header, beside the categorization ("ATO with conditions · PDR 35d late"); no 32px headline.
- 2026-11-16 · Timeline · Two lanes: authorization events and acquisition gates over one time axis; gates stay diamonds.
- 2026-11-16 · Slot · Role-true: the top right holds this reader's next step, or who it waits on; each program records its roles.
- 2026-11-16 · Words · RMF's short words (ATO, ATO with conditions, IATT, ATU, DATO, ATD), defined once in the vocabulary.
- 2026-11-16 · Signing · Held to roles: the assessor judges, the lead assessor publishes, only the authorizing official signs.
- 2026-11-16 · Decision types · IATT and ATU join ATO, ATO with conditions, DATO and ATD, as SP 800-37 allows (D5).
- 2026-11-16 · Operating · Programs say whether they operate (D6): in development reads apart from operating without authorization, lapsed authority apart from never authorized.
- 2026-11-16 · Owed · A variant owes its part of a shared control (D3): Customer A owes 261 of 374, not 234.
- 2026-11-16 · Partial · From mixed objectives (D4): an objective is Satisfied or Other than satisfied; a control with both reads Partially satisfied.
- 2026-11-16 · Inherited · It stands, sourced: an inherited determination reads as such, its row saying where it comes from.
- 2026-11-16 · Versions · Show the count, confirm it: publishing a version shows how many determinations carry forward and asks the owner to confirm.
- 2026-11-16 · Next step · To the component: an inherited control's next step and its chain lead to the component's assessment.
- 2026-11-16 · CVEs · Open until all move: a CVE is counted while any consumer runs an affected version.
- 2026-11-16 · Parts · Ledger's next scope: the component sheets and their seven tokens are built after approval, through the kit's checklist.

## 2026-11-15

- 2026-11-15 · Judgment · A determination on a component names a whole control, never one objective.
- 2026-11-15 · Inherit · Per element, automatically: a component's determination stands for the element built from it; a program's objective counts as judged where its requirements place it only on inherited, unmodified elements.
- 2026-11-15 · Precedence · Where a product assessment and the component's both judge one element and control, the product's own stands.
- 2026-11-15 · Component versions · A new version carries forward every determination except on the controls its owner lists as affected, which read To review until judged.
- 2026-11-15 · Components register · One row a component; the set it belongs to is a column.
- 2026-11-15 · Offers · Reviewed in two places, one Accept: the Review queue's row and the component's Findings tab; Return asks for a note.
- 2026-11-15 · Counting · Satisfied out of every control claimed (42 of 61), the tally showing what nobody judged; never out of the controls judged.
- 2026-11-15 · From above · What a system takes from the library is the Library tab of its System page; no Upstream destination.
- 2026-11-15 · Next · The proposal first: the two artifacts are approved before anything is built; the parked schema and seed wait.

## 2026-11-14

- 2026-11-14 · Navigation · Two scopes, one side nav: an organization scope (Home, Portfolio, Programs, Library) and a work scope under a product or a program's system.
- 2026-11-14 · Unit · The component version is assessed once, not the product as a whole.
- 2026-11-14 · Up · A program's result on an unmodified shared component is offered to its owner, reviewed once, and stands for every consumer.
- 2026-11-14 · Down · Pinned, flagged, findings at once: nothing moves by itself, affected controls are flagged, Take version reviews before it writes.
- 2026-11-14 · Cyber T&E · A kind of assessment under Assessments, on the same chain, reusable on a component.
- 2026-11-14 · Pass two · Carry the working boards onto the new navigation; the dated path and signing brief go under Authorization.
- 2026-11-14 · Page types · Seven page types are the contract; a screen that fits none gets a written exception first.
- 2026-11-14 · Evidence · Reviewed evidence reads Verified; Satisfied stays the assessor's word.
- 2026-11-14 · Findings · A row per fix: the register groups findings by the fix that closes them; one POA&M item answers one row.
- 2026-11-14 · Records · CVEs and SBOM components are records, each tied to its asset, controls and POA&M item.
- 2026-11-14 · Owner · Each shared component has a named owner, first the owner of the business line that publishes it.
- 2026-11-14 · Integration · Component first, then a marked integration set the product's assessor judges.
