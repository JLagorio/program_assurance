11. Asset Owner/ Mission Owner/ Government
Representatives

The program office/ISO will integrate cybersecurity risk
management into their overall systems engineering,
acquisition, test and evaluation, and risk management
processes.

The program office/ISO will complete Risk Management
Framework (RMF) steps to obtain the appropriate approval
or authorization documentation before IT testing or
operation.

For AF IT, the program office/ISO will ensure the IT (as
applicable) is registered in Information Technology
Investment Suite (ITIPS) and Enterprise Mission Assurance
Support System (eMASS) as applicable.

Ensure aggregation of all data, plus the potential impact
and likelihood of a security issue arising from
mishandling or misuse of that data, is factored in the
assessment of all decisions within the ITCSC.
