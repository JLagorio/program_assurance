## The model (RMF's, as eMASS keeps it)
- A **host system** is a separately authorized system that carries this one and offers controls to it (its customer responsibility matrix). A **common control provider** offers controls without carrying the system (here, the Squadron operations facility the Ground Support Subsystem runs in). Both are **providers**.
- Each control of the program's baseline has a **Responsibility**: **Inherited** (the provider implements and assesses it; the program writes no requirement, evidence or narrative for it, and its SSP cites the provider's implementation and authorization), **Hybrid** (the provider does part; the program owes its own part, said in one line, e.g. "F-16 Block 70 isolates the MIL-STD-1553B bus; WS-X90 filters its side"), or **System-specific** (the program's own, as every control is today).
- **By default**, choosing a host makes every control of the baseline it offers Inherited (Hybrid where it offers part). The program may change any control's responsibility, with a reason; the change is recorded (`PA.v2` state) and every screen follows.
- An inherited control's **Control status is the provider's** (the words stay the app's six; no new status word): in this story the providers' current authorizations leave their offered controls Satisfied, perhaps with one exception the provider's own POA&M holds, which the program then inherits as it is. So inheriting moves those controls off the program's to-do: choose them from the baseline's controls that read No requirement today, in families a host aircraft and a facility really provide (PE, PS, MA, AT, parts of CP, IR, MP, SC-7 as hybrid at the bus), so the story is "nobody writes requirements for the jet's physical protection; now they read Satisfied, inherited from F-16 Block 70". Every count of WS-X90's controls follows: the satisfied count rises by the inherited controls ("233 of 560 satisfied · 57 inherited", with the real numbers), in the `PROGRAMS` numbers in `core.js` (you may edit those values), the Home and Programs cards, the Overview, Controls (header, status counts, filters), the baseline, the SSP editor's counts and the SCTM workbook (its rows and its Analysis formulas). Find every place that shows WS-X90's control totals or status counts and make them one number; list them in your notes.
- **The authorization boundary** is the bubble: what is inside is covered by the authorization drawn on it. For WS-X90 there are two: the **ATO** of Sep 18, 2026 covers Increment 1 · Block 1 (the six subsystems); the **IATT** covers Increment 2's test configuration (Block 1.1, `CONFIGS`/`CHANGED` in `mod-v7-program.js`; decide plausibly what else differs in test, e.g. flight test instrumentation inside, a test range ground station outside), dated by G (`PA.v2life.step("iatt-2")` at draw time; fall back to "IATT · Increment 2, planned"). Outside the bubble: the F-16 Block 70 (host), the Squadron operations facility (provider) and the external interconnections the prototype already lists (`mod-v8-boundary.js`; reuse their names, e.g. SIPRNet reachback), with the interfaces that cross the line named (MIL-STD-1553B data bus, MIL-STD-1760 aircraft–store interface through the Payload Interface Subsystem, aircraft power).
