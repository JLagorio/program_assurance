# Section C: colours, widgets and gadgets (folder `noise`)

Question: where have we added too much? Josef: "It looks good, but some of the UI is a little
overwhelming with visual cues."

1. Measure, by script, every nav route (PA.navItems()) plus about ten record pages and two open side
   panels, at 1440 × 900 (and the ten busiest again at 390): inside the page area (not the rail or the
   program menu), count distinct non-grey colours in use (text, fill, border, separately and together)
   and distinct hues; status pills, badges, chips and tags; charts, sparklines, rings, donuts, tallies,
   progress bars, heatmaps and maps; cards; banners and alerts; visible buttons and links styled as
   buttons; icons; distinct font size and weight pairs; and how many of these sit above the fold.
   Save the raw numbers as `metrics.json`.
2. Look at about fifteen Vanta screens of the equivalent pages (Home, My work, Controls, Tests, Documents,
   Policies, Risk, Vendors, Personnel, an audit) and count the same things by eye, approximately.
   `V/style.md` §1 says what Vanta allows; check it against the images.
3. From both: rank our ten most overloaded pages and say what to cut on each; list every gadget type we
   use (where it appears, how many times, keep or cut, why); list colours that carry no meaning (used
   as decoration, or one meaning drawn in several colours, or one colour meaning several things); and
   propose a budget per page type (register, record, overview, home, editor), e.g. "register: at most
   two summary cards, colour only in status pills, no charts", grounded in Vanta's counts.

Judge what is on the page, not the flows; another agent walks those.

report.md structure: the ranking first, then the gadget inventory, then colours, then the budget.
